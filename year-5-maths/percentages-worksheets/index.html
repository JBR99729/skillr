Parent and teacher guide

Percentages make sense when students connect percent to hundredths. Start with 100 grids, money and simple benchmark percentages such as 10%, 25%, 50% and 75%. A common mistake is treating the percent symbol as decoration without understanding that it means out of 100. Link percentages to fractions and decimals using visual models. Ask students to explain whether a percentage is less than, equal to or greater than one whole before solving problems.

Common mistakes to watch for

Ask the student to explain their method before moving to a test. If they can only guess, return to one visual model and one worked example. Short practice with feedback usually works better than a long worksheet completed without review.
