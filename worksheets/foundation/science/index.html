Foundation Science worksheet FAQs

Are these Foundation Science worksheets free?

Yes. These Foundation Science resources are free to access without a learner login. Optional premium books are separate and do not block access to the core online resources.

Can homeschool families use these resources?

Yes. Families can choose a Foundation Science topic, use its explanation and worksheet for a home lesson, then use Practice and Test for review. Choose the topic by the learner's current understanding.

How do I find the matching curriculum topic?

Each topic lists its Australian Curriculum code and links to the matching guide, Classroom View and learning activities. The curriculum hub keeps the complete subject list together.
