Common Year 3 Maths mistakes to review

  • Reading a fraction as a count instead of equal parts.
  • Mixing up hundreds, tens and ones when partitioning numbers.
  • Skipping regrouping steps in addition or subtraction.
  • Reading the minute hand as an hour number on analog clocks.
  • Choosing an operation before understanding the word problem.
  • Forgetting to label units in measurement answers.
