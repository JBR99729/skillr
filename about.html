Australian Curriculum practice • organised by code

SkillrHub helps families, homeschoolers and teachers find exactly what to learn next.

SkillrHub provides free Foundation to Year 10 Maths, English and Science practice organised around Australian Curriculum learning areas and skills. The free site is the core learning engine; optional printable packs add planning, worksheets, answer keys and progress tracking for families who want everything ready to use.

What SkillrHub does

One clear learning system

SkillrHub is built to make curriculum coverage visible. Students can practise online, parents can see the pathway, and teachers can find focused support for specific skills.

📘

Learn

Topic guides and skill pages introduce the key ideas before students move into questions.

⚡

Practise

A large question bank supports repeated practice with feedback, answer checking and explanations.

🎯

Test and review

Separate practice and test pathways help learners revisit weak areas and build confidence before moving on.

For homeschool planning

Free online practice, with optional organised packs

Homeschool families often need more than isolated worksheets. They need a clear year-level path, printable records, review tests and a simple way to show progress across subjects.

🗂️

Mapped by year and subject

Maths, English and Science are organised so families can move from year level to subject to skill without guessing what comes next.

✅

Progress-friendly structure

Practice banks, test banks, worksheets and checklists make it easier to plan learning and keep simple records.

🔗

Print plus online support

Printable packs can link back to matching SkillrHub online quizzes, giving families both paper-based work and instant digital practice.

Our value promise

Free access first, paid convenience second

We do not believe useful education resources should require an expensive subscription. SkillrHub keeps core learning available for free and offers paid products only where they add genuine convenience.

🌱

Free learning stays central

Students can use SkillrHub’s core topic guides, worksheets, quizzes, Practice and Tests without paying or creating a learner account.

💡

Pay for organisation

Paid packs are designed for convenience: printable planners, worksheets, tests, answer keys and coordinated resources that save preparation time.

⚖️

Fair price, substantial depth

SkillrHub aims to keep packs affordable while giving families and teachers enough structured content to make the purchase worthwhile.

Purchases help SkillrHub grow. Revenue from optional products, advertising and affiliate links supports the ongoing development and maintenance of free learning resources. Paid products extend the free platform; they do not replace it or lock core learning behind a paywall.
