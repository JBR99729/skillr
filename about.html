SkillrHub provides Foundation to Year 10 learning resources for teachers, families and students who need clear, useful practice with minimal setup.

What we offer

Useful resources without the usual friction

🎯

Curriculum-aligned practice

Foundation to Year 10 coverage across Maths, English and Science, organised around Australian Curriculum codes.

📄

Printer-friendly worksheets

Clean, ready-to-print activities and PDF resources designed for fast classroom and home-learning setup.

🔒

No student account required

Core resources work without student sign-ups or passwords, while learning progress is saved locally in the browser.

🔒

Teacher and privacy friendly

Designed for classroom privacy

Students can access core learning resources without creating an account. Learning progress is stored in the browser on that device rather than uploaded to a SkillrHub student profile.
