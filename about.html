About SkillrHub

SkillrHub provides free Foundation to Year 10 learning resources, including daily practice for Maths, English and Science and printable worksheet support. Our resources are designed for teachers, parents and students who need practical, curriculum-aligned practice with minimal setup.

What We Offer

Download worksheet packs organised by year level and subject. The resources are free to use, printer-friendly, and published without subscriptions, sign-ups or student data collection.

  • Free daily practice across Maths, English and Science (F-10)
  • Free printable worksheets and curriculum-aligned topic pages
  • No student signup required for core learning resources

How To Use the Site

Find worksheets from the homepage, sitemap, or year pages. The collection is growing steadily, with a focus on quality, clarity and useful learning practice.

Feedback & Support

If you need a worksheet for a specific topic or have suggestions, please contact us at skillrhublearning@gmail.com. Your feedback helps us improve and expand the collection.
