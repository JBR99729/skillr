Useful learning should be accessible—and excellent teaching resources should be affordable.

SkillrHub provides free Foundation to Year 10 Maths, Science and English learning, alongside carefully designed teaching slides and printable packs for teachers and families who want extra convenience. Our aim is simple: deliver high educational value at a fair, accessible price.

Our value promise

A small price for real convenience

We do not believe useful education resources should require an expensive subscription. SkillrHub’s paid products ask for a small amount in return for genuine convenience: polished, ready-to-use materials that save teachers and parents preparation time.

🌱

Free learning stays central

Students can continue to use SkillrHub’s core topic guides, worksheets, quizzes, Practice and Tests without paying or creating a learner account.

💡

Pay for convenience, not access

Paid packs provide polished, classroom-ready slides, structured worksheets and coordinated resources that reduce planning time. The underlying core learning remains free.

⚖️

Small price, substantial value

We aim to keep individual packs and bundles affordable while including enough useful content to make the small purchase worthwhile.

Purchases help SkillrHub grow. Revenue from optional products, advertising and affiliate links supports the ongoing development and maintenance of free learning resources. Paid products extend the free platform; they do not replace it or lock core learning behind a paywall.

Independent and accountable

Trust through transparent standards

SkillrHub is an independent Australian education project. Individual contributors keep their personal identities private, so resources are published under the SkillrHub name. Question banks, topic pages and learning pathways are checked for curriculum fit, clarity, correctness and usefulness.

Content Verified: Foundation Mathematics, Foundation Science, Foundation English, Year 1 Mathematics, Year 1 Science, Year 1 English, Year 2 Mathematics, Year 2 Science, Year 2 English and Year 3 Mathematics question banks have completed our documented review for Australian Curriculum v9 alignment, year-level suitability, answer accuracy, explanation quality and meaningful question variety.

Review is continuing across other subjects and year levels. A verified label is shown only for a learning area that has completed this process.
