AC9M4N01: Tenths, hundredths and decimal notation

Recognise and extend place value to tenths and hundredths and use decimal notation to name and represent decimals.

Topic guideTeacher SlidesWorksheetPracticeTest
What students learn in AC9M4N01

Students connect whole-number place value to tenths and hundredths, model decimal quantities, rename decimals and apply decimal notation to measurements and money.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Content description: recognise and extend the application of place value to tenths and hundredths and use the conventions of decimal notation to name and represent decimals.

  • E1: divide a whole into 10 equal parts and connect tenths to decimal notation such as 0.1 and 0.2.
  • E2: use materials to show the multiplicative relationship between wholes, tenths and hundredths.
  • E3: recognise one as ten tenths and one tenth as ten hundredths and use these relationships to rename decimals.
  • E4: model decimal measures such as 1.5 m and 1.75 units.
  • E5: count mixed notes and coins and write totals using dollars and cents.
  • E6: compare how decimal money amounts and decimal measurements are read and interpreted.

Common mistakes to watch for
  • Treating decimal digits as separate whole numbers.
  • Confusing tenths and hundredths.
  • Reading decimal notation without place-value meaning.
