AC9M4N03: Equivalent fractions and decimals

Find equivalent representations of fractions using related denominators and make connections between fractions and decimal notation.

Topic guideTeacher SlidesWorksheetPracticeTest
What students learn in AC9M4N03

Students model and explain equivalent fractions, connect related denominators and link common fractions with decimal and measurement representations.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Content description: find equivalent representations of fractions using related denominators and make connections between fractions and decimal notation.

  • E1: extend fraction families in collections, for example 3/4 = 6/8 and 2/5 = 4/10.
  • E2: model equivalent fractions by subdividing capacity measures, such as 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8.
  • E3: fold paper to show equivalence and record related fifths and tenths as fractions and decimals.
  • E4: connect fractions of a metre with decimals and centimetres, such as 1/4 m = 0.25 m = 25 cm.
  • E5: use arrays to connect fractions with multiplication and division facts.

Common mistakes to watch for
  • Changing a denominator without changing the numerator proportionally.
  • Comparing fractions only by denominator size.
  • Missing the connection between tenths and decimal notation.
