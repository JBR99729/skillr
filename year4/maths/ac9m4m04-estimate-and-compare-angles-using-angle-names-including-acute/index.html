AC9M4M04: Estimate and compare angles

Use angle names including acute, obtuse, straight, reflex and revolution, and recognise their relationship to a right angle.

Topic GuideClassroom ViewWorksheetPracticeTest
What students learn in AC9M4M04

Angle size measures turn. A right angle is 90°, a straight angle is 180°, a full revolution is 360°, acute angles are less than 90°, obtuse angles are between 90° and 180°, and reflex angles are between 180° and 360°.

Learning routine: Identify vertex and arms → Compare with 90° → Compare with 180° → Name angle → Estimate turn → Justify

Success looks like

  • Use right-angle benchmark
  • Classify named angles
  • Estimate turn size
  • Recognise reflex/revolution
  • Justify comparisons

Concept models and worked thinking

Classify angles using benchmark turns

acute45°
right90°
obtuse135°
straight180°
reflex270°
revolution360°

Estimate by comparing the turn with a quarter, half, three-quarter or full revolution. Arm length and page orientation do not determine angle size.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Content description: estimate and compare angles using angle names including acute, obtuse, straight angle, reflex and revolution, and recognise their relationship to a right angle.

  • E1: classify interior angles of shapes as acute, obtuse, right or reflex.
  • E2: identify and estimate angles in the environment.
  • E3: create and use a right-angle template to compare angles.
  • E4: use tools such as spirit levels or set squares to identify straight, square or perpendicular relationships.

Quick check and feedback
  1. Name an angle of about 70 degrees. Acute, because it is less than a right angle.
  2. Name an angle of about 210 degrees. Reflex, because it is greater than a straight angle but less than a full revolution.
  3. A student says the longer arms make a larger angle. What is the fix? Angle size measures turn between the arms, not arm length.

Mastery evidence: Students compare each angle with 90 degrees, 180 degrees and 360 degrees and explain the benchmark they used.

Common misconceptions
  • Long arms mean large angle — Angle size depends on turn, not line length.
  • Reflex angle identified as smaller interior angle — Specify which turn is being measured; reflex is the larger turn over 180°.
  • Orientation changes angle name — Rotating a diagram does not change the angle.
  • Straight angle called a line only — A straight angle represents a half turn of 180°.
