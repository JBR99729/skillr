AC9M4M04: Estimate and compare angles

Use angle names including acute, obtuse, straight, reflex and revolution, and recognise their relationship to a right angle.

Topic guideTeacher SlidesWorksheetPracticeTest
What students learn in AC9M4M04

Students classify, estimate and compare angles by using a right angle as a benchmark and by recognising acute, obtuse, straight, reflex and full-turn relationships.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Content description: estimate and compare angles using angle names including acute, obtuse, straight angle, reflex and revolution, and recognise their relationship to a right angle.

  • E1: classify interior angles of shapes as acute, obtuse, right or reflex.
  • E2: identify and estimate angles in the environment.
  • E3: create and use a right-angle template to compare angles.
  • E4: use tools such as spirit levels or set squares to identify straight, square or perpendicular relationships.

Common mistakes to watch for
  • Judging an angle by arm length instead of opening size.
  • Confusing obtuse and reflex angles.
  • Forgetting that a straight angle is two right angles.
