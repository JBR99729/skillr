For families and educators

Help more learners discover SkillrHub.

SkillrHub provides free curriculum-aligned learning resources, worksheets, practice, tests and teaching materials. If you find SkillrHub useful, the most valuable way to support the community is to spread the word so more families, homeschoolers, teachers and schools can discover the resources.

Ways to spread SkillrHub

  • Homeschooling families: share SkillrHub with parents and carers looking for structured learning and practice resources.
  • Teachers and friends: recommend SkillrHub to teachers, tutors, colleagues, friends and family.
  • Schools: let your child’s school, local school or teaching team know that SkillrHub offers free learning resources.
  • Facebook: follow the SkillrHub Facebook page and share useful resources with other families and educators.
  • Teachers Pay Teachers: if you have used a SkillrHub resource from our TPT store, an honest positive review helps other teachers understand whether the resource is useful for them.
