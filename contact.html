Contact SkillrHub

SkillrHub provides free Foundation to Year 10 Maths, English and Science practice, short concept refreshers and printable worksheets for students, teachers and families. No student account is required to use the core learning resources.

We welcome messages about resource corrections, technical or accessibility problems, privacy and copyright questions, advertising or partnership enquiries, and suggestions for new topics.

When reporting a problem, please include the page address and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords, sensitive personal information or identifiable student information.

Email: skillrhublearning@gmail.com

Feedback from teachers, parents, carers and students helps us improve the clarity, accuracy and accessibility of SkillrHub resources.

We review all messages and aim to respond when a reply is required. You can also read more about SkillrHub, review our Privacy Policy or see our site policies.

Our Commitment

  • Free core learning resources for Foundation to Year 10
  • No student account required for core practice and worksheets
  • Short online drills and printable learning activities
  • Clear disclosure of analytics, advertising and affiliate links
  • Original educational content designed for classroom and home use
  • Children are not required to submit personal information to access core resources

Our mission is to make focused practice easy to start: read, practise, repeat or print a worksheet without creating a student account.
