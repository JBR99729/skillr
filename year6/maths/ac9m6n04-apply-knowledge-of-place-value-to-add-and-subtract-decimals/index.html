AC9M6N04 • Year 6 Mathematics

Adding and Subtracting Decimals

Use place value, efficient strategies, estimation and digital verification

Topic Guide Teacher Slides Practice Sheet Practice Test
What students learn

Learning goal

Students align place values, rename when regrouping, choose mental or written strategies and compare exact results with purposeful estimates.

Success criteria

  • I can represent or identify the concept.
  • I can explain the underlying relationship.
  • I can select an appropriate strategy or feature.
  • I can apply it in a new context.
  • I can justify and verify the response.

Teaching routine

  1. Represent
  2. Reason
  3. Calculate
  4. Interpret
  5. Verify
Curriculum focus: apply knowledge of place value to add and subtract decimals, using digital tools where appropriate; use estimation and rounding to check the reasonableness of answers
Key concept and central model

Calculate 18.375 + 6.84 by aligning place values

Use the visual model first. Ask students to identify the quantities, structure or conditions before calculating or explaining.

tensonestenthshundredthsthousandths18375684025215

Write 6.84 as 6.840 to align equal place values. Placeholder zeros clarify structure without changing value.

Worked application and transfer

Choose an efficient subtraction strategy

Connect the central relationship to a new context, then verify the conclusion with a second representation, estimate, inverse operation or reasonableness check.

32.00 − 9.875written regrouping
19.95 + 7.60compensation: 20 + 7.60 − 0.05
48.6 − 19.9subtract 20, then add 0.1
digital toolverify after setup and estimation

Estimate before calculating: 18.375 + 6.84 is about 18.4 + 6.8 = 25.2, so 25.215 is reasonable.

Classroom activities

Build and annotate the model

Represent adding and subtracting decimals and label the important parts, quantities or choices.

Compare and reason

Use the application model to compare two cases, explain the relationship and identify a likely error.

Transfer and verify

Apply the idea in an unfamiliar context and use a second method, evidence source or text feature to check it.

Common mistakes and rapid fixes
Decimal points not alignedAlign place values, not final digits.
Zeros added to the left or inside incorrectlyOnly trailing placeholder zeros preserve a decimal’s value.
Estimate omittedUse it to detect misplaced decimal points.
Calculator result accepted without contextCheck operation, input and magnitude.
Quick mastery check

Check understanding

  • Add 18.375 and 6.84.
  • Subtract 32 − 9.875.
  • Use compensation for 48.6 − 19.9.
  • Estimate first.
  • Explain placeholder zeros.

Evidence of mastery

  • Represent or identify the concept
  • Explain the underlying relationship
  • Select an appropriate strategy or feature
  • Apply it in a new context
  • Justify and verify the response

Decision: continue when students can explain the model, apply it to a new example and justify their check. Otherwise return to the central model and reduce the numerical or representational load.
