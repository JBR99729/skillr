How to get started

  1. Teach: open one free topic guide and work through an example together.
  2. Practise: use the matching practice link when that question bank is published.
  3. Review: ask the learner to explain their method, then use a separate assessment when available.

Try this question: Before a Science investigation, identify the variable you will change, what you will measure, and what you must keep the same for a fair comparison.
