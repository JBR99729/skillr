# SkillrHub Topic Page Authoring Contract — Mathematics and Science F–10

## Shared contract
Every topic page must be a useful lesson, not a curriculum-code placeholder. The Australian Curriculum content description is the required learning target; official elaborations are used as a coverage and teaching-opportunity checklist.

Each page must:
- preserve the existing canonical URL where possible;
- explain the topic in age-appropriate language;
- teach all knowledge/skills that the linked Practice normally requires;
- include code-specific examples and misconceptions;
- end in application/transfer rather than only recall;
- link directly to Worksheet, Practice, Test and Teacher Slides;
- use natural SEO titles, metadata and descriptive headings;
- avoid generic filler and keyword stuffing.

## Mathematics lesson contract
**Meaning → representations → worked method → why it works → common errors → strategy choice → application → transfer.**

Substantive codes normally require 2–4 worked examples. Depth increases materially from Year 6 onward, with Years 7–10 requiring stronger formal notation, connected representations, method selection, error analysis, modelling/interpretation where relevant, and unseen transfer.

## Science lesson contract
**Phenomenon/concept → model/mechanism → evidence → worked example → misconception → curriculum elaboration context → application → transfer.**

Depth increases materially from Year 6 onward, with Years 7–10 requiring stronger scientific vocabulary, mechanisms, evidence interpretation, model strengths/limitations, and unfamiliar application. First Nations content must be included where relevant to the curriculum and written respectfully without treating diverse knowledge systems as uniform.

## Assessment contract
### Practice
- Builds from confidence to medium application.
- May scaffold and target misconceptions explicitly.
- Every assessed idea must be taught or reasonably prerequisite knowledge.

### Test
- Uses unseen situations and values.
- Tests transfer, not memory of Practice scenarios.
- Avoids artificial difficulty and content outside the curriculum boundary.

## SEO contract
Search value must come from instructional usefulness. Pages should naturally answer likely student/teacher searches through descriptive headings, worked examples and related internal links. Repeating the curriculum description in multiple paragraphs is not an SEO strategy.
