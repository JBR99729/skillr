## Question-bank audit

- Mathematics: 648 Practice and 432 Test questions.
- Science: 456 Practice and 304 Test questions.
- Total: 1,104 Practice and 736 Test questions; 1,840 questions overall.
- Every code has at least 24 Practice and 16 separate Test questions.
- The shared runtime enforces exactly eight questions and unseen-question cycling in every Practice and Test attempt.
- Science banks pass the corrected schema-aware validator.
- Mathematics banks are generally accurate and substantial. AC9M8A04, AC9M8M06 and AC9M8N03 contain repeated stems; AC9M8N01, N03 and N04 contain limited cross-bank prompt overlap. These are targeted P1 edits, not grounds for bulk rewriting 1,080 strong Maths questions.
- Some AC9M8A03 binary suitability questions are clear but repetitive. Future replacements should ask students to identify the violated domain assumption or improve the model, not merely answer yes/no.

## Topic Page implementation

All 27 Mathematics Topic Guides now include:

- clearer, grammatically complete student success criteria
- two code-specific worked examples or applications
- a direct explanation/check prompt attached to the examples
- concise embedded Revision Notes
- three important questions with answers, including a misconception or boundary check
- preserved curriculum elaborations, misconception guidance, differentiation, resources and fixed Teacher Slides

The 19 Science Topic Guides were retained. Existing diagrams for cells, body systems, energy, rocks, matter, chemical change and plate tectonics remain because they materially improve understanding. No decorative images were added.
