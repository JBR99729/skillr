## Scope and method decisions

| Code | Source basis and meaningful teaching demands | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LA01 | Asking/providing relevant information; offers/requests/commands/exclamations; words, expression, gesture and clarification. Closed/open questions remain explicit. | A sign language is a language; facial/gesture clues do not prove hidden feelings. No eye-contact, accent or fixed-body-language gate. Community-specific signs require a named endorsed account and preparation. |
| LA02 | State a genuine preference, connect a relevant reason, compare useful features for an explicit purpose and respond to another view. Oak's reason/relevance methods and comparative support are adequate. | Do not mark a taste wrong. Better needs a stated purpose; a picture alone cannot prove sound, comfort or taste. |
| LA03 | Recount/narrative organisation; informative/report grouping; opinion/reason and explanation versus procedure. Source methods explain why order/grouping helps the purpose. | Purpose categories can overlap. A time word, heading or because alone is not proof of one exclusive genre. Use complete tiny texts. |
| LA04 | Repetition, spoken rhyme and rhythm/beat supporting cohesion, with original short performed examples and a fresh pattern. Later ABC glossary closure distinguishes pulse from word timing. | No one-word-one-beat rule, rhyme-scheme formalism or claim that every poem rhymes. Static choices do not demonstrate real timing. |
| LA05 | Titles/headings/contents/page location; images/layout; controls/links/scroll/swipe and available spoken commands; compare routes for a stated target. | Define model-specific behaviour. A paper button or command label does not prove device operation; swipe direction and voice support vary. |
| LA06 | Complete independent idea, whole naming/happening-state parts, meaningful ordering and expansion within one independent clause. | Neither length, visible movement nor the number of printed verb words decides simple-sentence status. Questions and commands can be complete. |
| LA07 | Contextual nouns/pronouns; doing, saying, thinking and being verbs; qualities and when/where/how details, with precise word choice. | Supplied pronouns and clear referents; no assumed gender. A detail phrase is not automatically one adverb; adverbs do not all end in -ly. |
| LA08 | Compare picture jobs/types, words-plus-images, labelled diagrams and ordered sequences. Targeted PETAA/Oak label/map pedagogy closes the old partial benchmark. | Actual images must accompany image-reading prompts. Drawings are not photographs. Use published First Nations artwork and its guidance, not generated imitation or guessed symbol meanings. |
| LA09 | Topic groups, taught contextual meanings, precise vocabulary and cross-context meanings; respectful Country/Traditional Owners/Acknowledgement vocabulary. | Do not assess untaught subject facts or imply one weather/season pattern across Australia. Country has cultural meaning beyond map location; do not invent local names. |
| LA10 | Meaning-led . / ? / ! choices and actual phrased reading; proper names including places and familiar holidays; sentence-start/I editing and writing. | A calm command can use a full stop. Punctuation is not decided by volume or one universal intonation. No advanced dialogue/comma/apostrophe demand. |
