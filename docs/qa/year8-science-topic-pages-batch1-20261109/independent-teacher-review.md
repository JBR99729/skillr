1. **U04 retained formation wording — fixed/PASS.** Four statements incorrectly made complete melting the separator between metamorphic and igneous formation. Actual final prose now states solid-state metamorphism without melting the material being metamorphosed, and crystallisation of a melt that may arise through partial source-rock melting. Rechecked both Key concept paragraphs, the retained Q&A answer, the remaining Common misconceptions dependency and the new no-melting process model. The final local dependency recheck found and closed that last misconception-line occurrence without restarting the full review. The exact four changes are recorded in `retained-corrections.json`, including the markup-specific source pin.
2. **U06 retained activity materials — fixed/PASS.** “Classify four particle diagrams” did not specify the four stimuli. It now explicitly requires four teacher-supplied diagrams. This remains a supported activity, not a claim that this page supplies a four-diagram set. The three new A–C panels and separate retained three-panel illustration are unchanged. The precise fifth prose correction is ledgered.
3. **U05 new energy-flow ambiguity — fixed/PASS.** Initially the 20 J outward arrow appeared to leave the final 60 J motion box, risking a reading of a later second loss. The final branch starts at the descent connector and is labelled “20 J outward during descent.” Re-viewed the actual final individual PNG and source: final motion remains 60 J and the outside allocation 20 J. The table, prose and double-counting question agree.
4. **Two author-found retained visual issues — fixed/PASS.** The author identified direction strokes without arrowheads in the U03 boundary model and poorly positioned balls in U05. Independently inspected both exact final replacements. Six heads now show apart/together/opposed-lateral directions. The ramp's first ball touches the high track and second touches the trough; the later hill is lower, with the existing high-potential/high-kinetic labels preserved. These are author-found candidates independently verified here, not a claim this reviewer discovered them first.
