## AC9S8U01

model: Two original labelled typical-cell schematics: leaf cell has wall, membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, chloroplasts, large vacuole and mitochondria; animal cell has membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus and mitochondria. Not to scale; colours are a legend, not observation. Root-cell counterexample uses supplied textual features, not a claimed micrograph.

worked: Compare photosynthetic leaf cell L with root cell R: R has wall/membrane/nucleus/mitochondria but no chloroplasts. Explain why both are plant cells and why mitochondria matter to both. Add intestinal microvilli and muscle mitochondrial abundance for structure-to-function reasoning.

boundary: No senior biochemical pathways, universal all-cells-have-nucleus claims or source-pixel identification.

## AC9S8U02

model: Original paired transport paths: soil→root hair→root/stem xylem→leaf; human right heart→lungs→left heart→body→right heart. Cell/tissue/organ/system hierarchy explicitly mapped. Flow model, not anatomy or scale.

worked: Root hair extension increases exchange area; hollow strengthened conducting xylem links organs. Cardiac muscle cells→cardiac tissue→heart→circulatory system; capillary thin walls permit exchange. Compare a damaged xylem pathway with reduced heart pumping without diagnosing illness.

boundary: No all-xylem-dead or all-veins-deoxygenated shortcut; plant source-to-sink phloem; retained optional artificial-organ example must be scientifically checked.

## AC9S8U03

model: Three independent relative-motion panels: divergence arrows apart with ridge; oceanic subduction arrows together with descending oceanic plate/trench; transform opposed lateral arrows in plan view. Solid slowly deforming mantle, no global magma ocean.

worked: Original hypothetical symmetric seafloor records: distance from ridge 0,40,80 km each side, age 0,2,4 Ma. Age increases away, supporting spreading; same-age magnetic bands on either side plus earthquake/volcano belts are converging evidence. Distinguish collision mountains from subduction volcanism.

boundary: Do not infer a particular real location/map, use dataset to claim measured rate, or represent every convergence as volcanic.

## AC9S8U04

model: Original branching rock-process map with explicit paths magma→cooling→igneous→weathering/transport/deposition→sediment→compaction/cementation→sedimentary; either solid rock→heat/pressure without melting→metamorphic; all rock may weather or melt. Arrows are processes, not a compulsory sequence.

worked: Choose sample A interlocking coarse crystals for an abrasion-resistant path when tested suitability supports it; sample B carbonate marble unsuitable for an acid-exposed feature despite polish. Compare seconds-long fragment transport with long sediment accumulation and variable thick-lava cooling, without assigning universal rock ages.

boundary: No universal igneous strength, all sedimentary weak or all metamorphic banded claims; no pupil acid/heating/chipping practical.

## AC9S8U05

model: Original energy-accounting diagram for a hypothetical ramp run: initial gravitational store 80 J; bottom combined translational/rotational kinetic 60 J, transfer to surroundings 20 J. Total 80 J; no energy disappearance. Simple store/pathways model.

worked: Classify moving ball kinetic, raised stationary ball gravitational potential, stretched stationary band elastic potential. Compare ramp runs released from rest at matched heights; repeated safe tabletop model records, not an assertion that NASA experiment was performed. Higher later hill requires input or suitable starting height; include friction/rotation.

boundary: No KE/GPE equations required; thermal energy is store, heating transfer; light/sound/electrical are pathways in explanation; no launch/explosion activity.

## AC9S8U06

model: Original atom-labelled particles: A three O2 pairs (one element); B two H2O groups (one compound); C one O2 pair plus one H2O group (mixture). H/O explicit letter legend; circles and joining lines are conventions. Separate labelled sketch for a 3D physical model described by formula, not a formula-implies-shape claim.

worked: Count atoms: 2H2O has 4 H and 2 O. O2 has two atoms but one element; H2O has two element types but one compound. NaCl denotes a crystal ratio, not a required discrete molecule. Formula alone does not show 3D geometry; physical clay/paper model preserves atom inventory, limitations explicit.

boundary: No stoichiometry/moles, molecular bond-order recall or claim every compound molecular; no copied source model art.

## AC9S8U07

model: Original evidence comparison cards: ice melts retaining water identity; iron rusts producing different substances; bubbles alone could be boiling or reaction. Energy arrows use system boundary.

worked: Original hypothetical, separately controlled teacher-run records: mixture X 22→17°C with independently established new gas product; mixture Y 22→28°C with independently established new solid. Explain net energy absorption/release, with surroundings and multiple evidence; temperature alone cannot prove reaction. No adopted ACS expected values as measured results.

boundary: No actual chemicals/procedure prescribed, no bond-breaking-releases-energy claim; reversibility alone insufficient; no hazard demonstration.

## AC9S8H01

model: Original evidence timeline separating earlier continental drift, later seafloor/polarity evidence and modern GPS. Concept links and approximate periods; no sole-inventor claim. Contrast inference versus observation.

worked: Earlier outline/fossil matches suggest past connection but do not settle mechanism. Later ocean ages increasing away from ridges and magnetic symmetry support moving lithospheric plates; GPS tests movement now, not in Wegener's era. A hypothetical contrary local age prompts sampling/method checks, not automatic erasure of all evidence.

boundary: No fake historical quote/date, public-source model pixels or equal-weight-every-opinion framing; cell microscopy as contrasting example retained.

## AC9S8H02

model: Original semantic co-production route: locally defined purpose→knowledge-holder-led observations/categories→agreed documentation→authorised use/credit. Public Gulumoerrgin/Larrakia/CSIRO project metadata only; no seasonal knowledge, calendar, motifs, art or invented Indigenous names.

worked: Explain how locality, priorities, who participates and who is credited influence questions/methods. Compare long-term local ecological relationships for local management with a school temperature log whose purpose is instrument comparison; neither represents all knowledge. Case questions ask leadership/locality/purpose and explicit permission-scope reasoning, not reproduction of knowledge.

boundary: No commercial calendar derivative, consultation, permission or cultural endorsement claimed; publicly visible does not imply reproduction authority. No legal procedural deadlines.

## AC9S8H03

model: Original four-lens decision matrix for a hypothetical school reusable-foodware proposal: environmental production/washing/litter; ethical access/future generations; social convenience/inclusion; economic purchase/washing/labour costs. No invented exact real-policy costs or legal claims.

worked: Compare reduce-supply-only with reusable option plus accessible alternatives and measured review. Original hypothetical pilot: 100 baseline single-use pieces per lunch vs 30 pilot pieces, 70 fewer/70% lower count, does not prove 70% lower total impact. Public historical 2020 Queensland consultation used only as attributed method case: self-selection, distribution of impacts, unknown costs, distinguish proposals from current rules.

boundary: No current ban/compliance guidance or unsupported causal percentages; recommendations conditional, alternatives accepted with reasoning.
