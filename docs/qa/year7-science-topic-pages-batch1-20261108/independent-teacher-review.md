## Findings and targeted rechecks

1. **U04 new guided answer — fixed/PASS.** The initial generic answer allowed rest despite the explicitly left-moving cart. Actual HTML now states that this already moving cart continues left at constant velocity while balanced forces persist, then separately explains another initially stationary object. Rechecked table, worked explanation, model label and activity dependencies.
2. **U01 retained key scope and missing leaf stimuli — fixed/PASS.** The restricted practice table now supplies four matching animal cards and excludes general animal identification. Four hypothetical leaf cards supply all combinations of toothed/smooth margins and netted/parallel veins, with a valid worked first split and route. All old correct teaching remains.
3. **U02 retained drought prompt — fixed/PASS.** It now names the retained E1 worked food-web model and requests assumptions and qualified predictions. That model supplies two direct and two indirect pathways. A separate preliminary concern about the fox/frog link was not a defect: the retained same-section diagram visibly supplies it.
4. **H01 retained cultural claims — fixed/PASS.** Ten exact paragraph, answer, list and dependent-prompt changes replace unsourced generic dugong/turtle rules with particular knowledge holders, Country/Place, documented observations, evidence limits, attribution and permitted use. All thirteen entries in `retained-corrections.json` were independently found and read in actual HTML; no remaining dependent answer assumes the removed generic rules.
5. **Retained U01 classification illustration — fixed/PASS.** The fourth branch ends only “8 legs”, rather than an identification. The replacement original SVG now identifies bird, furry mammal, insect and arachnid through complete yes/no paths. It explicitly supplies feather/fur/six-leg/eight-leg traits and restricts itself to these four example cards; the caption and worked bird answer remain consistent.
6. **Retained U03 seasons illustration — fixed/PASS.** December is on the left and June on the right while the north axes lean upper-right. This made north tilt toward the Sun in the December position, opposite the actual seasonal interpretation. The replacement now uses June left and December right with parallel axes, north toward the Sun in June and away in December; labels and Southern Hemisphere answers agree.
7. **Retained U03 spring/neap illustration — fixed/PASS.** The neap panel places the Moon vertically above Earth but keeps the main bulge ellipse horizontal. The replacement shows positions only: full-Moon alignment for the larger spring range and a quarter-Moon right angle for the smaller neap range, with no misleading bulge outline. Its explicit limit excludes local heights/times; retained spring/new/full/quarter answers remain consistent.
8. **Retained U04 orbital velocity illustration — fixed/PASS.** The old illustration drew velocity diagonally at the ellipse's rightmost point. The replacement is an explicitly circular model: its rightmost velocity arrow is vertical, tangent to the orbit; gravity points horizontally inward. The continuous-free-fall explanation and orbit answer agree.
9. **Retained U04 lever/pulley illustration — fixed/PASS.** The old lever had upward effort left of the fulcrum, giving the wrong lifting torque, and the old pulley paths were incomplete. The replacement has downward effort on the left: 20 N × 1.2 m = 60 N × 0.4 m at balance. Fixed pulley rope passes continuously over the supported wheel, with one load-supporting segment; the anchored movable-pulley rope passes under its wheel, over a fixed guide and down to the effort end, supplying two upward tensions. Ideal effort equals the load or half the load respectively. Friction/component weights are explicitly neglected, and force is traded for distance. Rechecked E5 explanation, numerical lever/pulley worked answers, caption and alt dependencies.
