- **U04 PASS:** 12 N right minus 8 N left gives net 4 N right; support/gravity of 20 N each balance vertically. An initially leftward-moving object initially slows rather than instantly moving right. Equal net force, duration and initial state give the 2 kg object a larger speed change than the 4 kg object. Given 2 kg and rounded fields 10 N/kg and 1.6 N/kg give weights 20 N and 3.2 N; mass stays 2 kg. These are supplied model values, not measured fields or data.
- **U02 PASS:** the six supplied feeding links are grass→grasshopper, grass→snail, grasshopper→frog, grasshopper→bird, snail→bird and frog→bird. Those links permit multi-path reasoning; they do not supply exact population changes. The plan discusses detrital, inorganic and heat flows separately rather than falsely treating every ecological relation as a food arrow. Matter cycling and energy dissipation remain distinct.
