## Accepted page handoff and checks for the authored version

Use the approved illustrated page format: one original worked example, three distinct teaching focuses with guided question/answer pairs, three reasoning checks, two FAQs, misconception support and a differentiated safe ten-minute activity per code. Retain the useful baseline eight educational sections in each U code and nine in each H/I code: 104 sections total, subject to any separately documented factual repair. Remove obsolete Classroom View descriptions, mapping/reference/related/process duplication while keeping actual code-matched Classroom View and written-resource access. Preserve metadata, every structured-data payload, static semantic teaching and native disclosures. The single app entrance must use verified availability and an exact canonical return; a catalogue link must not claim an unavailable code bank is published.

The explicitly invented plant dataset is mathematically coherent: twelve already-emerged seedlings begin at 20 mm, with four per 2/6/12 mL daily treatment. Final heights at day 9 are 30/32/31/31, 42/44/43/43 and 36/35/37/36 mm, giving means 31/43/36 mm. Mean height increases are therefore 11/23/16 mm. All groups have four of four alive, so this record shows different height responses without an observed survival-count difference during those nine days. It does not establish a universal optimal dose, guarantee later survival, isolate every natural habitat factor or show that more water always produces more growth. State matched starting size, species/batch and pot/soil/light/temperature/time/drainage conditions and preserve every supplied value.

Label any bar graph as mean final height or mean height increase, then use the corresponding values and a correctly scaled axis; do not mix the two. A hypothetical comparison-group method must identify what differs and must not invent unprovided observations. The graduated-water-cylinder model must show 2 mL intervals and a 36 mL reading consistently; use the lower water meniscus at eye level and no unsupported decimal precision. Deterministic HTML/SVG must carry all exact data and answer-bearing information.

Context artwork is not a scientific record. A bench scene with six pots must not be represented as the actual twelve-seedling dataset; captions, alternative text and learner tasks must make that separation clear. Earth diagrams must hold the tilted axis approximately parallel through the annual orbit and separate rotation from revolution; circuit paths must visibly include both cell terminals and the load rather than imply a short circuit. Chemical-change images must not be the sole proof of reaction identity.

Prefer paper/supplied-data alternatives. Any real plant work requires teacher-approved care, collection and disposal; circuit activities require teacher-selected school cells/loads and never mains or intentional shorting; food/rust work must avoid tasting, unsafe handling and unsupervised heat. Actual fieldwork needs relevant local permissions and risk management before starting. No mould-growing or medicine practical accompanies the historical case.
