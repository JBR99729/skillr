## Original model and answer boundaries

- **LA01:** Explicit speaker/listener identities and a fixed help purpose support comparison without inferring relationships from politeness, appearance or wording alone. Question/request/opinion cards have different functions. A clear fitting response can be expressed several ways; the same wording may fit both listeners. Retain the existing home/school connection as well as friend/teacher scenarios. No home-language/register/community ranking, compulsory eye contact, accent or courtesy formula.
- **LA02:** Original apple/pear pictures and stated speaker preferences provide checkable facts; the learner’s own taste has no fixed key. The stated names/preferences must be visible or read aloud, rather than guessed from an expression. Speaking, pointing, drawing, sign/AAC and another/none/decline routes can demonstrate an intended message. A reason can be modelled or invited, not imposed as proof that a preference is valid.
- **LA03:** A real original sign, cover and screen notice can show form versus message. The library-closed message can remain constant across sign, adult speech and screen; this is not a claim the screen picture works as a device. Supply the actual two-panel whale comparison and the named read-aloud material when a retained exercise uses them. An object is not automatically a text merely because it appears in the choices.
- **LA04:** Cover/title/start, two exact short print lines and labelled Home/Food/Homes controls provide distinct print/screen tasks. Start/line-return arrows must actually point to the correct word locations. English’s usual print direction is not universalised to every language; controls vary across devices. Any clickable local Food control must reach its actual supplied example; a static control illustration must be labelled/styled as a picture.
- **LA05:** The small pup is a naming group; The pup naps. and The bag is red. give complete statements, including a state rather than only a visible action. Cat-follows-dog versus dog-follows-cat changes the follower/followed relation; it does not establish chasing. Meaning, oral rehearsal and useful word order lead; full stops, word counts or a rigid subject/action rule do not define every possible sentence.
- **LA06:** Grouped naming/happening/details strips can be changed while preserving a complete meaningful message. Swapping the animal or location changes a supported meaning. The learner need not parse every word class or obey an invented universal two-group limit; internal word order matters as well.
- **LA07:** Four specified actual quick-check illustrations, the open box/three shells scene and a separate labelled plant diagram support story and informative-text coverage. Words can supply Rae’s identity/action; actual art supplies visible contents. A prose image description is not a substitute for the image being assessed. Counts, under/beside relations, labels and colour claims must match final pixels. Do not infer a name, exact thought or private feeling from a picture alone.
- **LA08:** Familiar home/pretend-shop/play/classroom diagrams support learning and transferring meanings such as receipt, rolling/sliding and fragile. Adult modelling supplies unfamiliar meanings; assess contextual meaning rather than spelling, word length or untaught science knowledge. Use pictures/pretend materials rather than broken glass, sharp objects or required food handling.
- **LA09:** Visible lower-/upper-case letters and separate ending marks allow the intended distinctions. We met Ava. supports sentence-start W/name A/full stop. Capitals remain letters, not punctuation marks. A new printed line is not automatically a new sentence; names and sentence starts can both use capitals. Question/statement purposes are given rather than inferred from one universal voice contour.
- **LE01:** Actual attributed story, poem and illustration material supports text clues first, then one’s own similarity/difference or supported response. Personal experience/feeling is not graded against an adult’s life or taste; children may choose a familiar imagined example or pass. The original family microstories are supplementary original practice and must not be labelled First Nations traditional stories or imitations of named creators.

The complete exact student-facing givens, guided questions/checks, images, response keys and alternative response criteria remain to be authored and independently checked. A request to draw/say/point is an opportunity for actual communication, not evidence that a child has already performed it. Adult read-aloud, re-reading, explanation, AAC/sign/gesture and drawing can remove incidental decoding or access load while the intended competence remains clear. A fixed written sentence or compulsory because is not required for every response. Historical word/step/timing quotas in reused app records are not automatically carried into these public pages.
