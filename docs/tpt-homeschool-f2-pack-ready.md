# SkillrHub F-2 Homeschool Planner Packs for TPT

## Product Strategy

Launch as a free or low-cost entry product that sends homeschool parents to SkillrHub's free online learning path. The pack should feel immediately useful even before a parent creates any account anywhere: open the PDF or page, choose a year, tick progress, print records, and use SkillrHub links for quizzes, tests, worksheets and topic guides.

Recommended launch setup:

| Offer | Suggested Price | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Foundation Homeschool Planner Pack | Free-$3 | Lead magnet for early-years homeschool searches |
| Year 1 Homeschool Planner Pack | Free-$3 | Lead magnet and proof of coverage |
| Year 2 Homeschool Planner Pack | Free-$3 | Lead magnet and proof of coverage |
| Foundation-Year 2 Bundle | $9-$19 | Paid printable bundle once PDF polish is ready |
| Progress Records + Reporting Helper | $19-$29 | Later upgrade for state reporting confidence |

Keep SkillrHub core free. Sell convenience, printable organisation, parent confidence and done-for-you planning.
