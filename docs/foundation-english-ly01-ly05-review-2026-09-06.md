# Foundation English LY01-LY05 topic and worksheet review

Text-only review of AC9EFLY01, AC9EFLY02, AC9EFLY03, AC9EFLY04 and AC9EFLY05. No classroom-view, Practice or Test content changed.

## What changed

- Added 20 Explain and apply teaching activities across the five topic pages.
- Tuned 15 worksheet questions across the base worksheet, Topic Practice 1 and Topic Practice 2 routes.
- Preserved the existing nine-question worksheet structure and warm-up/core/challenge spread.
- Removed only exact duplicate worked-example cards where present.

## IXL benchmark notes

The IXL Australia Foundation English alignment maps AC9EFLY03 to real-life/imaginative text decisions, AC9EFLY04 to phonics, decoding and context checks, and AC9EFLY05 to picture meaning, feelings and prediction. AC9EFLY01 and AC9EFLY02 have no direct mapped IXL skill in the public alignment, so their updates focus on the same instructional standard: short prompts, observable evidence, clear listening or text-purpose decisions, and original wording.
