# SkillrHub Visual Quality Policy

Visual investment should follow conceptual difficulty, curriculum importance and future STEM transfer value.

- Simple or mainly descriptive topics: 0–1 instructional visual where it materially improves understanding.
- Important, high-yield topics with strong STEM transfer: target 2–4 purposeful instructional visuals, each teaching a distinct concept.
- Do not add decorative or stock imagery.
- Keep core assessed understanding fully teachable on SkillrHub; do not rely on external reference links to fill teaching gaps.
- Prefer reusable lightweight WebP diagrams, typically 500–700 px wide, lazy-loaded, with descriptive alt text, width and height.
- Reuse diagrams across relevant curriculum codes when they teach the same relationship.
- A model-limitation or misconception point may remain a concise callout when a dedicated image would add little learning value.

For multi-concept STEM pages, visual coverage should be mapped to the major conceptual subtopics rather than applying a one-image-per-page quota.
