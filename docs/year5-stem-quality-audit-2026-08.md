# Year 5 STEM quality pass — August 2026

Scope: 24 Year 5 Mathematics and 12 Year 5 Science codes. Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 content descriptions and every elaboration recorded in the curriculum manifest were checked before implementation.

## Audit before implementation

All 36 codes had accurate curriculum identity, working resource routes and strong authored teaching specifications. The 1,440 canonical assessment items comprised 864 Practice and 576 Test questions. Every item passed schema, identity, answer-key, distractor-count, audio, structured-feedback, visual-path, uniqueness and Practice/Test-separation checks. The 24-question Practice and 16-question Test pools are large enough for multiple eight-question attempts, with shuffling and no-repeat cycling. Existing shared Year 5 drills already use eight-question attempts, large 200–260-item pools, difficulty bands and cycle-aware selection.

The material weakness was the live Topic Page. Strong authored explanations, models, applications, vocabulary, activities, misconceptions and revision prompts existed in the data layer but the canonical pages exposed generic summaries instead. Science pages also retained obsolete runtime lesson-renderer scripts. Tests launched 12 questions, contrary to the hard eight-question requirement.

## Implementation report

Every Topic Page now contains its authored explanation, learning routine, success criteria, two worked concept/application models, precise vocabulary, three guided activities, exact curriculum description and all elaborations, Revision Notes, important questions and topic-specific misconception corrections. Existing lightweight HTML/CSS visuals provide number lines, fraction bars, hundred grids, arrays, tables, graphs, coordinate grids, transformations, nets, rays, particles, process sequences and evidence models. These visuals directly explain relationships; no decorative raster images were added.
