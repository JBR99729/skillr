# Worker 6 Year 9 Topic Page Audit

Scope: Year 9 Maths, Science and English topic pages.

Findings:
- Maths pages already mostly use the static curriculum v2 details structure with worked examples, misconceptions, differentiation, mastery evidence and resource links. No broad rewrite was needed.

Changes made:
- Added support/core/extend and mastery evidence to targeted thin Science pages.
- Added deeper analysis and mastery evidence sections to targeted English language pages.
- Did not touch Practice or Test banks.
