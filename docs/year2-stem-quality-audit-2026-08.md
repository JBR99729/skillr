# Year 2 STEM quality pass — August 2026

Scope: 18 Year 2 Mathematics and 10 Year 2 Science codes. Alignment was checked against Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 content descriptions and the elaborations recorded in the repository curriculum manifest. Scores are demanding editorial judgements.

## Audit before implementation

Common strengths: every code had a working topic route, fixed Teacher Slides and Practice Sheet. The standard banks contain 24 Practice and 16 separate Test questions; AC9S2U01 has a protected, deeper live bank of 49 Practice and 32 Test questions. All 1,120 standard-bank assessment questions passed answer-key and required-feedback integrity checks. Eight Mathematics pages and AC9S2U01 were already substantial authored lessons.

Common weaknesses: 19 pages still used a generic introduction, a curriculum list and generic mistakes rather than exposing the authored model, application and activities. Revision Notes were absent from 27 pages. Nine codes with First Nations elaborations lacked consistent cultural-safety guidance. Test launches used 12 questions and AC9S2U01 used 10/15-question Practice/Test attempts, contrary to the hard eight-question product rule. AC9M2M04 has repeated stems paired with different clock visuals; these are valid visual variants but should be spaced by the selector.
