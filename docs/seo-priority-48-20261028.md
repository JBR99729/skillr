# SkillrHub: 48 priority search topics — 28 October 2026

Owner request: select and improve 40–50 strong curriculum codes. This batch improves **48 existing guides**, concentrated on coherent primary-school topics with existing authored lessons and resources: 43 Maths, two Year 1 English and three Year 1 Science.

## Selection and evidence

This is a practical search-potential shortlist, **not a claim that these are the 48 highest-volume keywords**. No keyword-volume service was available. Search Console impressions measure this site's exposure, not total search demand. The current canonical manifest contains 676 codes; the owner's expected 687 remains an unresolved inventory difference.

Selection combines the saved 20 September–17 October Search Console guide inventory, the native 28-day comparison inspected on 28 October, existing authored teaching/resource availability, and specific parent/teacher searches rather than broad year/subject terms. Existing lessons were read before writing their short introductions. Search-result checks validated resource-intent vocabulary around time, fractions, decimals, percentages and multiplication, without treating competitors' listings as volume data.

The native comparison (1–28 September versus 29 September–26 October) found telling time AC9M3M04 at 450→10 impressions, duration AC9M3M03 at 355→2, odd/even AC9M4N02 at 118→4, and shapes AC9M1SP01 at 118→6. These recovery candidates are included. Year 1 skip-counting's separate supporting article also lost exposure; this batch improves the canonical code guide, not that article's route or canonical.

## Changes

- Write distinct natural-language search titles and descriptions, keeping exact curriculum codes.
- Replace generic or promotional hero openings with topic-specific explanations and small worked examples that are available without JavaScript.
- Add contextual prerequisite/next-topic links within each guide's existing related-learning container. Retain its previous links and declare existing navigation ownership where needed to prevent a competing injected pathway.
- Archive public source-development/process panels in recoverable HTML comments, following the approved Year 1 pattern. Research status and verification badges are unchanged.
- Preserve all existing authored lesson sections, examples, answers, diagrams, H1s, resource destinations, product claims, canonicals, robots directives, styles and script dependencies.
- Update lastmod for only these 48 changed pages and their six child sitemap entries. No inventory, sitemap architecture or blanket date refresh.
