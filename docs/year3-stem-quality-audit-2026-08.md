# Year 3 STEM quality pass — August 2026

Scope: 23 Year 3 Mathematics and 12 Year 3 Science codes. Alignment was checked against Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 content descriptions and all elaborations stored in the curriculum manifest. Scores use a demanding launch-readiness standard.

## Audit before implementation

The assessment foundation was strong: the canonical banks contained 1,416 questions with valid identities, answer keys, distinct options, structured hints/explanations, visual references and separate Practice/Test prompts. No weak curriculum-definition wording was detected; no normalised template family exceeded three items. Question pools used no more than eight skill/coverage keys, allowing the balanced selector to represent every eligible key in an eight-question first attempt. Daily Drills were also structurally sound.

The principal weakness was the Topic Page: 33 of 35 pages were generic curriculum summaries even though high-quality authored models existed in the data layer. Students could see what the curriculum required but were not explicitly taught how the concept worked. AC9M3M03 and AC9M3M04 were already deep standalone lessons and were protected. A second P0 issue affected assessment launches: standard Tests selected 12 questions; AC9M3M03 and AC9M3M04 used legacy 10/12 settings.

Existing lightweight HTML/CSS diagrams were used because they directly teach place value, fractions, arrays, measurement scales, time, angles, maps, graphs, chance, life cycles, soils, heat, states and inquiry processes. No decorative images were added. AC9M3M03 and AC9M3M04 teaching remained unchanged.
