# Year 9 educational-quality re-audit

Status: IN PROGRESS — do not mark Year 9 complete from structural QA alone.

## Hard pass rule

A topic page passes only when a Year 9 student who is new to the topic can use the page to learn the core concept, see it applied, avoid common misconceptions, check mastery and move into independent practice without needing another explanatory resource.

Minimum score: 8.0/10 overall, with no pass if Teaching clarity is below 3.0/4.0.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Pass evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Teaching clarity and conceptual completeness | 4.0 | Defines the idea in student language, explains relationships/mechanisms, teaches required vocabulary and boundaries, and contains enough substance for first learning. |
| Worked examples / models | 2.0 | At least 2 concrete, topic-specific worked examples or model analyses with reasoning, not generic teacher prompts. |
| Visual / representational support | 1.0 | Uses a diagram, model, table, number line, flow or other representation when it genuinely reduces conceptual load; no decorative imagery. |
| Misconceptions and corrective feedback | 1.0 | Names likely misconceptions and explains the correction with evidence/example. |
| Mastery questions | 1.0 | Contains a useful retrieval/application set (target 8–10 questions for substantive topics), with answers or expected ideas and at least one transfer item. |
| Structure and usability | 1.0 | Clear sequence from Learn → Worked examples → Check → Practice/Test; links work and content is readable on mobile. |

## Automatic fail conditions

- Curriculum wording is merely repeated rather than taught.
- Worked examples say “explore”, “discuss”, “build a model” or similar without actually showing the reasoning.
- Generic prompts such as “what changes, what stays invariant?” substitute for topic-specific instruction.
- A diagram-dependent concept has no useful representation.
- Important questions are mostly repetitions of the page summary or curriculum descriptor.
- A student must already understand the concept to make sense of the page.
- Technically correct navigation/metadata is treated as educational quality.

## Required remediation pattern

For every failing Year 9 page:

1. Replace descriptor restatement with a concise student-facing explanation of the concept.
2. Add the minimum vocabulary and conceptual relationships needed to understand it.
3. Add concrete worked examples/model analyses with visible reasoning.
4. Add a teaching representation where it improves understanding (diagram/flow/table/number line rather than decorative imagery).
5. Add misconception → correction pairs specific to the topic.
6. Add 8–10 high-value mastery questions for substantive topics, including recall, reasoning, application and at least one transfer question.
7. Keep practice/test/worksheet/homework/teacher-slide wiring intact.
8. Re-score using this rubric. Do not pass below 8.0/10 or with weak teaching clarity.

## Completion rule

Year 9 is complete only when all canonical Year 9 Maths, English and Science topic pages have been individually checked against this gate and every failing page has been remediated. Duplicate/legacy routes should not be mistaken for separate curriculum codes; canonical pages are the scoring unit.
