# Year 4 STEM quality pass — August 2026

Scope: 23 Year 4 Mathematics and 12 Year 4 Science codes. Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 content descriptions and every elaboration recorded in the curriculum manifest were checked before implementation.

## Audit before implementation

All 35 codes had accurate curriculum identity, working supporting-resource links and strong authored concept/application models in the data layer. The canonical assessment banks contained 1,400 separate questions: 840 Practice and 560 Test. Every item passed schema, identity, answer-key, three-option, audio, structured-feedback, visual-path, SVG-symbol, alt-text, uniqueness and Practice/Test-separation checks. No generic curriculum-definition prompts were found; no normalised question family exceeded two items. Each bank used at most eight skill/elaboration coverage keys, so the balanced eight-question selector can cover all eligible keys on a first attempt.

The major weakness was teaching delivery. Topic Pages ranged from generic curriculum summaries to short content-description shells; none exposed the complete authored model, application, vocabulary, guided activities, misconception support and Revision Notes needed for independent learning. Standard Tests also launched 12 questions, violating the hard product rule.

## Implementation report

Existing lightweight HTML/CSS instructional diagrams were used for decimals, fractions, arrays, scales, area, timelines, angles, grids, symmetry, graphs, chance, food chains, water cycles, forces, materials and inquiry processes. No decorative images were added.
