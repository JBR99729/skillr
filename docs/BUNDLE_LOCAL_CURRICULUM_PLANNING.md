# Bundle local curriculum planning notes

Added 9 October 2026 to the 21 available bundles in the catalogue. Individual resources are unchanged. Planning notes are stored as curriculumPlanningNote and rendered in static pages and the fallback product view. They retain the AC9 references and do not claim verified NESA/VCAA code equivalence or complete local syllabus coverage.
