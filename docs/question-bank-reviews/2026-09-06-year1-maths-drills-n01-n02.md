# Year 1 number and place-value Daily Drill review — 6 September 2026

## Release scope

First maths **Daily Drill** batch after the Year 1 English Practice/Test sweep: AC9M1N01 and AC9M1N02. The other five Year 1 maths drill topics are not reviewed by this release.

Replace the active 288-item number/place-value production bank (236 distinct exact prompts, with repeated template families) with **80 original authored tasks, 40 per code**. There are 45 three-choice questions, with keys 15/15/15, and 35 number-entry questions. Twenty-four simple SVGs supply mathematical evidence. The drill remains eight questions per attempt, with ten completed attempts exhausting the bank before a new shuffled round.

These are Daily Drill tasks, not a replacement or relabelling of the separate 24 Practice + 16 Test banks. Curriculum Practice/Test files, topic pages, teacher presentations and existing worksheet assets are unchanged. Drill page counts and its two listing cards are corrected. No shared quiz or rotation runtime changes are required; numeric difficulty metadata uses the existing progressive ordering.

## Official authority and coverage map

**AC9M1N01**: “recognise, represent and order numbers to at least 120 using physical and virtual materials, numerals, number lines and charts”. [Official ACARA v9 descriptor, WS03](https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/resources/work-samples/mathematics/year-1/ws03-place-value).

**AC9M1N02**: “partition one- and two-digit numbers in different ways using physical and virtual materials, including partitioning two-digit numbers into tens and ones”. [Official ACARA v9 descriptor, WS02](https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/resources/work-samples/mathematics/year-1/ws02-donuts).

The descriptors, not IXL's catalogue associations, set the boundary. In particular, N02 does not require three-digit partitioning. The former drill description conflated that with N01's range; it now distinguishes the two.

Every item stores its code, component, task type, cognitive demand, targeted misconception, review version/date and this review path. N01 has 8 recognition / 16 application / 16 reasoning tasks; N02 has 9 / 14 / 17. Existing runtime sequencing orders the selected eight by difficulty; this is mixed retrieval practice, not an adaptive mastery system.

Physical materials are represented in the original diagrams and concrete situations. Automatic answers assess the stated numeric result or representation, not a child's unobserved handling of objects. A teacher can accompany these drills with actual counters and bundles; successful quiz marking is not claimed as verification of hands-on performance. The full teaching/worksheet journey is preserved.

## Direct IXL inspection method and limits

The [Year 1 v9 skill plan](https://au.ixl.com/maths/skill-plans/australian-curriculum-version-90-year-1) supplied candidate links. The full Year 1 maths catalogue was also checked for direct matches beyond those alignment links. **All 54 relevant skill pages below were opened individually (34 alignment links plus 20 additional references).** On each, the live question region and the page's “Learn with an example” question/solution were read. This was not a catalogue-only comparison.

Public question/example access worked in the existing browser. The visible session was not signed in; no credentials were requested or handled. Two listening pages exposed their prompt and worked solution, but the live spoken number was not independently heard. Those pages therefore provide evidence for the response structure and worked explanation only, not a fully inspected live audio item.

Representative initial questions and worked examples showed low/high number ranges, model-supported and unsupported formats, same/different leading digits, equality, three/four-number ordering, and standard/non-standard parts. A words-to-digits answer was submitted and the following question inspected. An equivalent-partition item with two number fields was answered and its next item inspected. This is **representative progression evidence**, not inspection of every generated question or every SmartScore level. No exhaustive adaptive-progression or measured equivalence claim is made.

The two catalogue suggestions excluded as non-essential/outside the direct descriptor coverage were [Roman numerals](https://au.ixl.com/math/year-1/roman-numerals-i-v-x) and [ordinal numbers](https://au.ixl.com/math/year-1/ordinal-numbers). They were not used as benchmarks or counted among the 54 inspected pages.

## Differences, originality and validity

- N02's broad requirement for different partitions includes single-digit wholes and non-standard additive parts. Its IXL mapping concentrates heavily on teen and place-value strands. The wider Year 1 catalogue supplied direct small-number partition, systematic-pair and make-ten references; these were inspected and used to improve the counter, ten-frame and part-whole coverage. No absence of a direct small-number equivalent is claimed.
- N01's materials, numerals, lines and charts all receive coverage. Relative-value tasks support ordering; unrelated Roman numeral notation, formal rounding and operations beyond the descriptor are not imported.
- The model-supported/unsupported progression, direct number entry, short contexts and explicit place-value reasoning informed the task design. SkillrHub's MCQ misconceptions were authored independently, including for IXL formats that supply no distractors.
- **No IXL question wording, names, stories, answer-choice sets, diagrams, media, worked examples or question sequences were copied.** All drill stems, solutions and SVGs are original. Common mathematical facts and curriculum vocabulary are not proprietary examples.
- This is an internal instructional reference review. It does not establish IXL endorsement, formal equivalence, identical outcomes or equal measured difficulty.
- The smaller bank removes repetition rather than preserving a volume claim. The source has 80 distinct exact and number-normalised stems, but those checks are only mechanical screens; the row-by-row editorial review also checks task purpose, changed representation, boundary cases and reasoning demand.
