This is an editorial judgement from representative examples, not a measurement of cognitive-load equivalence, a review of every adaptive IXL level, or IXL endorsement. ## Changes and review

- Replaced three 40-Practice/16-Test legacy source banks with 24-Practice/16-Test banks: 120 independently authored items, 24 original evidence diagrams/cards.
- Practice has 8 recognition, 8 application and 8 reasoning items. Existing random rotating attempts remain 8 questions; Test retains its existing 12-question draw from 16. The owner-specified counts are bank sizes.
- Replaced distractors such as unrelated toys/television or magical effects with competing needs, reversed changes, inappropriate actions and observed-versus-inferred differences. Some basic force items retain colour/material alternatives to distinguish what actually changed.
- All questions have three alternatives, one intended answer, short feedback and read-aloud support. Correct positions rotate across the authored bank and the runtime also shuffles alternatives.
- Newly authored visual evidence is necessary to answer card/arrow questions; equivalent alt text makes the same evidence available without sight. No answer label is supplied by alt text.
- New question IDs and result/best-score keys isolate previous attempts. Cache keys refreshed. Topic guides, teacher slides and worksheets remain outside this release.
- Source text: `scripts/year1_science_ixl_review/u01.txt`, `u02.txt`, `u03.txt`. `build.py` materialises only these three canonical JSON banks and their diagrams. The older `year1_science_actual` files remain legacy material and must not be used to overwrite this reviewed batch.
- Published with `publish_production_question_bank.mjs --reviewed` for each code; review ledger check required. Existing ledger already records these codes, so recording may produce no ledger diff.
