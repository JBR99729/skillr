# Foundation Science review — AC9SFI01–AC9SFI03

Reviewed 6 September 2026. Scope covers the next three Foundation Science Practice/Test banks and a purposeful SVG enrichment pass across AC9SFU01–AC9SFI03.

## Curriculum authority and coverage

| Code | Official Australian Curriculum v9 focus | Assessable coverage used |
|---|---|---|
| AC9SFI01 | Pose questions and make predictions based on experiences | distinguish questions, predictions and observations; ask observable questions; predict from a relevant prior experience; use tentative language rather than certainty |
| AC9SFI02 | Engage in investigations safely and make observations using their senses | follow instructions and boundaries; use sight, hearing, touch and permitted smell appropriately; avoid tasting unknown materials; protect living things; use simple and digital tools safely |
| AC9SFI03 | Represent observations in provided templates and identify patterns with guidance | place observations in tables, charts and organisers; sort by observable features or materials; interpret repeated movement, animal-feature, material-use and weather patterns |

Fair-test design, formal measurement and independent graph construction remain outside the Foundation scope.

## Actual IXL task evidence inspected

| Curriculum need | Representative Foundation IXL skills | Design evidence used |
|---|---|---|
| Questions and predictions | Weather patterns; Investigate shadows; Investigate sunlight and shade | short narrated contexts, observation before inference, staged movement from description to a simple pattern and “most likely” application |
| Safe sensory observation | The five senses; Investigate sunlight and shade; Investigate pushes and pulls | direct sense/body-part matching, picture-supported observation, concise choices and explicit worked feedback for every distractor |
| Recording and patterns | Weather patterns; Sort objects by material; Classify objects by shape, colour, material and texture | provided tables, visual sorting, one visible rule at a time, then combined-property and repeated-pattern reasoning |

The IXL Foundation Science catalogue, representative actual questions and worked feedback were inspected. IXL does not expose direct Australian Curriculum inquiry-code mappings, so multiple relevant skills were combined. ACARA controls the scope. No IXL wording, answer combinations, images or sequence was copied.
