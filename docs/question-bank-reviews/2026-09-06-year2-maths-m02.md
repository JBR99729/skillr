## Review and validation

All 64 legacy question stems and keys were inspected. They largely repeat a few templates with substituted settings; one asks to fold a garden bed. Replaced with substantive contexts and representations. No IXL wording, illustrations, media, values or question sequence was copied.

Every new stem, option, key and explanation was reviewed individually. Visual QA caught a mismatch between “squares” and a rectangular mat diagram; the wording was corrected to rectangles. The explanation of unequal event durations was tightened so it does not claim equal stage lengths are logically necessary for one particular stage to happen to occupy an eighth of the total.

Production validator passes: 40 unique IDs; Practice 24 and Test 16; position counts 8/8/8 and 6/5/5 including adult-review metadata. MCQ-only counts are 7/7/8 and 5/5/5. All eleven SVG panels rendered and were visually inspected for correct fractions, labels and clipping.
