# Foundation Science review — AC9SFU01–AC9SFU03

Reviewed 6 September 2026. Scope is limited to the first three Foundation Science Understanding Practice/Test banks. Topic guides, worksheets and teacher slides were not rebuilt.

## Curriculum authority

- **AC9SFU01:** observe external features of plants and animals and describe ways they can be grouped based on these features.
- **AC9SFU02:** describe how objects move and how factors including their size, shape or material influence their movement.
- **AC9SFU03:** recognise that objects can be composed of different materials and describe the observable properties of those materials.

The QCAA Australian Curriculum v9 Prep alignment and content-description sequence were checked alongside the canonical curriculum wording already used on SkillrHub.

## Actual IXL task evidence inspected

| Code | Representative IXL Foundation Science skills | Design evidence used |
|---|---|---|
| AC9SFU01 | Identify plant parts; How do animals use their body parts?; Compare plants and animals | Short read-aloud directions, direct visual observation, two-choice early recognition, comparison language and one-feature explanations |
| AC9SFU02 | The ways things move; Investigate pushes and pulls; Design a race car track | Movement vocabulary, direction caused by a push/pull, picture-supported paths and staged application from naming movement to interpreting a simple design |
| AC9SFU03 | Classify objects by material; Classify objects by texture; Describe objects | Object/property discrimination, material-based sorting, precise texture vocabulary and choosing a description supported by observation |

Actual questions and worked feedback were inspected in the IXL skill player. The public science player was available even though it did not retain the dashboard identity display. No IXL wording, answer combinations, images or sequences were copied.

## SkillrHub decisions

- Preserved strong existing authored questions rather than manufacturing unnecessary rewrites.
- Added missing ACARA coverage for foods as plant parts and comparisons between humans and other animals.
- Strengthened overlapping-group reasoning and required every member of a group to satisfy its rule.
- Replaced uncontrolled or weak motion comparisons with clearer one-factor comparisons involving size, shape and material.
- Strengthened materials questions by separating object, material and observable-property language.
- Retained three answer choices because SkillrHub's distractors diagnose misconceptions; the early questions remain as concise as the inspected IXL tasks.
- Added editorial-review metadata, teaching hints, explicit difficulty progression and stable sequence priorities.
- Practice attempts draw 8 from 24; Test attempts draw 12 from 16. Question cycling exposes the whole bank over repeated attempts.

These are editorial comparisons, not claims of IXL endorsement or measured equivalence.
