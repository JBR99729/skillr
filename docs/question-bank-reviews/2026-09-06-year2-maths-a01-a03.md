## AC9M2A03

Official ACARA v9 descriptor: **Recall and demonstrate proficiency with multiplication facts for twos; extend and apply facts to develop the related division facts using doubling and halving.**

Assessable components: recall twos facts; interpret pairs, arrays and equal jumps; connect multiplying by 2 with doubling; divide by 2 through sharing and grouping; connect halving to related multiplication/division facts; use inverse operations to find unknowns and check errors.

IXL pages inspected:

- https://au.ixl.com/math/year-2/multiply-by-2
- https://au.ixl.com/math/year-2/divide-by-2

Observed task structures: direct ×2 and ÷2 facts, arrays, number lines, equal sharing, grouping into pairs and retrieval of related multiplication facts. Worked explanations move between an array, skip-counting and inverse facts. SkillrHub adds contexts, unknown-factor/dividend tasks, interpretation, error checking and comparison while remaining within twos facts.
