# Year 1 English review — LY06–LY10

Reviewed 2026-09-06. Five banks contain 24 Practice and 16 Test tasks each; attempts remain 8 and 12. All 200 tasks are text-only. There are 32 automatically marked editing choices and 168 tasks requiring a parent/teacher to check actual work. Coverage reaches 25/30 codes.

## Curriculum and IXL evidence

Official descriptors checked on page 5 of https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/aciqv9/english/curriculum/ac9_english_p-6_cd_sequence.pdf. The live mapping at https://au.ixl.com/english/skill-plans/australian-curriculum-version-90-year-1 lists related tasks for LY06, LY09 and LY10, and no direct tasks for LY07 or LY08.

- LY06: https://au.ixl.com/ela/year-1/complete-the-sentence-with-the-best-verb. The displayed task contrasts verb forms agreeing with a plural subject; its worked example contrasts forms for a singular subject. The inspected demand informs original sentence editing. Our coverage additionally includes text focus, vocabulary, boundaries, spelling and eight actual writing/editing responses.
- LY09: https://au.ixl.com/ela/year-1/does-the-word-start-with-a-consonant-blend. Displayed question and example distinguish a word with a beginning blend from one without it, using a picture and two word choices. The explanation separates the consonant sounds within the blend. We require children to segment spoken words, including beginning and ending clusters, rather than only identify printed blends.
- LY10: https://au.ixl.com/ela/year-1/identify-each-sound-in-a-word. The displayed task identifies an initial sound, and its worked explanation orders phonemes with separate sound positions. This is related component evidence, not direct coverage of every manipulation operation. Our original oral tasks cover adding, deleting and substituting initial, medial and final sounds.

The visible questions and explanations were inspected publicly without login. Audio was not independently verified as a calibrated recording, and the full adaptive progression was not explored. No IXL wording or media is reproduced. These are editorial comparisons, not measured cognitive-load equivalence, endorsement or equal-outcomes claims.

## Performance alignment

| Code | What the child does | How the work is checked |
|---|---|---|
| LY06 | Chooses precise edits, then creates and re-reads reports, opinions and recounts | 32 choices plus 8 actual writing responses with criteria for meaning, vocabulary, grammar and sentence boundaries |
| LY07 | Delivers short talks with opening, relevant middle and conclusion; uses vocabulary, gesture and suitable volume/pace | A grown-up hears the talk and checks the supplied task-specific guidance; clicking a choice is not accepted as speaking evidence |
| LY08 | Writes words on paper in unjoined lower/upper case, progressing to case changes, word spacing and checking recognisable forms | Actual handwriting is checked; spelling support and the school's taught letter forms are allowed. Typing or letter recognition alone is not evidence of handwriting |
| LY09 | Segments spoken phonemes, distinguishing blends/clusters from single digraph sounds | A grown-up keeps the screen out of view, says the word normally and observes segmentation; no letter-counting substitution |
| LY10 | Produces new spoken words through sound addition, deletion and substitution | A grown-up gives the instruction orally, without displaying spellings, and checks the produced word and operation |

Speech-sound labels in post-response guidance are teaching notation, not a promise of reliable synthetic phoneme audio. Use natural spoken cues; avoid adding an extra vowel to consonants. Allow ordinary accent variation, and distinguish phonemes from letters and syllables. Cue words for manipulation are different from the expected output so the child must perform the operation rather than merely repeat the cue.

The handwriting tasks need paper, and presentations need a listener. Short, familiar tasks keep the demand appropriate; these are focused practice opportunities, not psychometric tests. Recognise/apply/reason metadata remains the existing three-block bank organisation; it does not turn productive tasks into multiple-choice recognition or introduce an adaptive engine.
