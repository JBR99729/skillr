# Foundation Maths Daily Drills review — AC9MFN01–AC9MFN03

Reviewed 2026-09-06. Scope is the existing Foundation `numbers-to-20` Daily Drill route only. Topic guides, teacher slides, worksheets, curriculum-code Practice banks and Test banks are unchanged.

The old 240-item drill contained 148 AC9MFN01 items and 92 AC9MFN03 items, no AC9MFN02 item, no rendered visual, and heavy repetition. The rebuilt bank contains 80 original items per code, retains 30 rounds of 8, and places all three codes in every stored round. It includes 188 purposeful inline SVG models. Correct positions are exactly balanced at 80/80/80 across the bank and differ by no more than one within each code.

## Official Australian Curriculum v9.0 requirements

The exact descriptors were checked against ACARA's Foundation work samples: [Knowing numbers](https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/resources/work-samples/mathematics/foundation/ws01-knowing-numbers) and [Let's pack a picnic](https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/resources/work-samples/mathematics/foundation/ws04-lets-pack-a-picnic).

| Code | Official descriptor | Assessable components used in this drill |
|---|---|---|
| AC9MFN01 | name, represent and order numbers including zero to at least 20, using physical and virtual materials and numerals | connect collections, number names and numerals; include zero; recognise and represent quantities to 20; continue forward/backward sequences; locate missing positions; find one more/less; order numerals; use ordinal positions from a marked starting end |
| AC9MFN02 | recognise and name the number of objects within a collection up to 5 using subitising | instantly recognise 1–5 in standard and non-standard arrangements; see a whole or two familiar parts without one-by-one counting; keep the total invariant when colour, orientation, spacing or layout changes |
| AC9MFN03 | quantify and compare collections to at least 20 using counting and explain or demonstrate reasoning | count organised and less-organised collections to 20; compare more, fewer and equal; compare by one-to-one matching and by counting; separate mixed collections; select an explanation supported by both totals or unmatched objects |

The worked pages use very short directions, large visual targets, audio support, direct input or picture selection, adjacent-number distractors, and explicit count-check explanations. Difficulty increases mainly through the quantity range, less regular arrangements, reversing the count, missing interior sequence values, switching among numeral/name/model representations, and comparing mixed or unmatched collections. Early tasks isolate one action; later tasks require two counts or a stated relationship. Explanations model one-to-one counting, full-ten recognition, counting order, and leftover matching rather than merely restating the answer.

## ACARA boundary and SkillrHub decisions

- IXL's AC9MFN02 mapping is largely worded as counting. The descriptor requires subitising, so the SkillrHub items use familiar and unfamiliar arrangements, whole/part recognition and “look quickly” directions. The explanations show structural recognition instead of teaching one-by-one counting as the target strategy.
- IXL maps count-beyond-20, skip-counting, teen-number partitioning and abstract numeral comparison under AC9MFN03. Those were excluded here: quantities stay at or below 20; decomposition belongs more directly with AC9MFN04; the N03 comparisons always concern visible collections.
- IXL's number-line-to-30 and hundred-chart variants were excluded from this drill. AC9MFN01 items stay within 0–20.
- IXL provides drag, click and audio interactions that a static Daily Drill cannot reproduce exactly. SkillrHub uses accessible inline SVGs and three plausible choices. It adds explicit reasoning choices for AC9MFN03 because the official descriptor requires explanation or demonstration, not only selecting the larger picture.
- Public pages exposed the initial task and complete worked example on every inspected page. No active signed-in state was available in the browser, so deeper adaptive SmartScore sequences were not claimed as inspected.
