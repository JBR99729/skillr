## Actual IXL evidence

The live Year 1 plan was inspected: https://au.ixl.com/english/skill-plans/australian-curriculum-version-90-year-1. All five codes have mapped tasks, but the samples only cover selected aspects.

- LA06 — https://au.ixl.com/ela/year-1/is-it-the-naming-or-action-part-of-the-sentence. Visible question asks learners to classify a highlighted sentence part. The worked explanation distinguishes the subject from what is said about it and includes states as well as physical actions. The text snapshot does not preserve which words were bold in the current question, so no claim is made about its submitted answer.
- LA07 — https://au.ixl.com/ela/year-1/identify-adjectives. Visible task selects a descriptive word in a short sentence; its worked example treats number words as adjectives. Our bank uses unambiguous quality adjectives and covers the descriptor's additional word roles without adopting every grammar convention from the reference.
- LA08 — https://au.ixl.com/ela/year-1/which-feeling-matches-the-picture. Actual illustrated event, facial expression and two feeling alternatives inspected in both the text snapshot and screenshot. This supports inference from depicted clues, but does not cover the entire cross-text image-comparison descriptor. All Skillr visuals are independently authored; no IXL pictures, characters or scene copied.
- LA09 — https://au.ixl.com/ela/year-1/use-context-to-identify-the-meaning-of-a-word. Visible task uses a contrast in a short sentence to infer word meaning. Our examples apply contextual vocabulary decisions to learning-area topics instead of copying that sentence or its geography.
- LA10 — https://au.ixl.com/ela/year-1/choose-the-right-end-mark. Visible question selects an ending mark for a direct question. The worked explanation distinguishes statements, questions, exclamations and commands and allows a full stop or exclamation for commands depending on force. Our items state calmness, urgency or surprise when needed to make the intended answer defensible.

Public tasks were available, so login was not needed. Only representative tasks and their displayed worked examples were inspected. No complete adaptive progression, measured cognitive-load equivalence, IXL endorsement or student outcome equivalence is claimed.

## Changes and limits

Legacy banks had 40 Practice and 16 Test items with many generic prompts that omitted the required sentence or image information. New items supply context, replace synthetic distractors and explain the actual decision. A pronoun alternative was revised because singular they could make the original options overlap. Punctuation-only choices have explicit spoken labels for read-aloud.

Four codes are text-only. LA08 retains eight small SVG evidence pairs because images are the assessed focus. They contrast story personification with labelled factual features, a route, a gauge, a direction arrow and timed weather panels. The remaining LA08 items describe image situations in words; those items assess interpretation of described image choices rather than direct visual recognition. This limitation is explicit, and no text-only substitute is claimed to measure all visual comprehension. All generic hint-card SVG references are removed from revised questions. Old unused SVG assets and historical QA logs are not current review evidence.

Authored Practice blocks are eight recognise, eight apply and eight reason items. Test has separate examples. Existing rotation is not IXL-style adaptive progression. Unrelated topic guides, teacher slides and worksheets remain unchanged.
