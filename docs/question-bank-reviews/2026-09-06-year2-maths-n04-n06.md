# Year 2 Maths review — AC9M2N04–AC9M2N06

Reviewed 2026-09-06 against the official Australian Curriculum v9 descriptors and the actual IXL Australia skill pages linked from the [Year 2 skill plan](https://au.ixl.com/maths/skill-plans/australian-curriculum-version-90-year-2). The comparison used one browser tab sequentially. All 106 mapped pages were opened; each live entry question and each “Learn with an example” worked explanation was read. Pages whose explanation loaded separately were opened again and read directly. The adaptive bank beyond the displayed entry question was not exhausted.

The released banks contain exactly 24 Practice and 16 independently sampled Test questions per code. All questions, names, contexts, choices and explanations are original SkillrHub writing. No IXL wording, sequence, illustration or media was copied. IXL is an instructional-quality reference only; ACARA remains the alignment authority.

The IXL pages demonstrate short reading loads, explicit part-part-whole diagrams, equation selection, unknown-start/change/result variations, comparison language and ordered two-step solutions. Distractors are most effective when they represent the wrong operation, reverse the quantities, solve only one step or report a number without its meaning. SkillrHub combines those structures with original additive, multiplicative and mixed models, then adds ACARA-required whole-dollar transactions, strategy choice, reasonableness, incomplete-information decisions, remainder interpretation and adult-reviewed written communication.

**Important difference and limitation:** the IXL pages mapped to AC9M2N06 contain strong word-problem modelling but no direct money-transaction strand in this mapping. Money totals, equal costs, payment/change and contextual communication were therefore aligned directly to the ACARA descriptor rather than inferred from IXL. Productive communication cannot be validly reduced entirely to multiple choice, so the bank includes original adult-review tasks. No equivalence, identical outcome or IXL endorsement is claimed.
