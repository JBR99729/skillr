# Year 2 Maths review — AC9M2N01–AC9M2N03

Reviewed 2026-09-06 against the official Australian Curriculum v9 descriptors and the actual IXL Australia Year 2 skill pages listed below. The released source banks contain 24 Practice and 16 independently sampled Test questions per code. All questions, choices and explanations are original SkillrHub writing; no IXL wording, sequence, illustration or media was copied. IXL is an instructional reference only, not an endorser or curriculum authority.

## AC9M2N01

**Official descriptor:** Recognise, represent and order numbers to at least 1000 using physical and virtual materials, numerals and number lines.

**Assessable coverage:** recognise and read numerals; connect numerals, number words and hundreds–tens–ones materials; count across decade/hundred boundaries; locate values and interpret scale on number lines; compare and order numbers to 1000; reason from bounds and multiple representations.

The active questions and worked examples use short directions, number-chart movement, missing entries, forward sequences, number-line plotting/comparison, ordered-card tasks, base-ten models and numeral/word conversion. Explanations model one explicit step at a time: locate, count, compare the first differing place, or translate hundreds/tens/ones. Distractors commonly reverse tens and ones, omit a zero or confuse a neighbouring mark. SkillrHub combines these forms and adds scale reasoning, boundary crossings, middle-value decisions and explanations of comparisons. IXL's mapped Roman-numeral and numbers-to-10,000 pages were excluded because they are not required by this descriptor; isolated numbers-to-20 work was not used as the target demand.

## AC9M2N02

**Assessable coverage:** identify the place and value of digits; partition in standard form; rename using non-standard numbers of hundreds, tens or ones; exchange 10 ones for 1 ten and 10 tens for 1 hundred in both directions; regroup across more than one place; verify or reject equivalent groupings; explain zero as a placeholder in different positions.

The IXL pages move from identifying a place/value to typed conversions, alternative regroupings and multi-clue number reasoning. Worked explanations use place-value charts and explicit equal exchanges. Plausible errors include naming the digit instead of its value, reversing places, exchanging unequal amounts and stopping before all places are standardised. SkillrHub samples each descriptor verb, includes zero in the tens and ones positions, and asks learners to diagnose apparently valid and invalid regroupings. The IXL “Guess the number” page also uses odd/even and skip-counting clues outside the narrow descriptor; SkillrHub uses only its process-of-elimination structure with place-value and regrouping clues.

## AC9M2N03

**Official descriptor:** Recognise and describe one-half as one of 2 equal parts of a whole and connect halves, quarters and eighths through repeated halving.

**Assessable coverage:** distinguish equal from unequal parts; recognise and make halves; recognise quarters and eighths; describe repeated halving from whole to halves to quarters to eighths; connect 1 half with 2 quarters and 4 eighths and 1 quarter with 2 eighths; recognise different valid partitions; reason about the same fraction of differently sized wholes.

- [Make halves, quarters and eighths](https://au.ixl.com/maths/year-2/make-halves-quarters-and-eighths)
- [Make halves and quarters in different ways](https://au.ixl.com/maths/year-2/make-halves-and-quarters-in-different-ways)

The active IXL tasks select equal partitions, identify one shaded part, and draw a cut between supplied points. Worked examples repeatedly state that the named parts must be equal, count total and shaded parts, and contrast a correct partition with an unequal or wrong-denominator distractor. The progression moves from recognition to construction and alternate orientations. SkillrHub preserves that progression in an auto-markable format and extends it to the ACARA connection that matters most: every part is halved in the sequence 1 → 2 → 4 → 8. It also prevents the common misconception that a fraction has a fixed physical size when the wholes differ. Direct drawing is better observed off-screen; these banks test the decision and explanation, while topic/worksheet activities remain the place for physical folding and drawing.

## Quality and limitations

All 120 items use concise Year 2 Australian English, three plausible choices, balanced answer positions, explicit misconception-based explanations, and a recognise → apply → reason sequence. Practice and Test use different numbers, decisions and reasoning prompts. Exact and normalised question duplication is prohibited by local validation. The live IXL pages exposed an active question and a “Learn with an example” worked solution for every listed page. Where the explanation was not initially present in the page text, the example panel was opened and read directly. Some diagram-only answer choices were visually rendered rather than named in the accessibility text. The adaptive sequence beyond the displayed entry level was not exhausted, so no empirical equivalence, identical outcome or IXL endorsement is claimed.
