# Final Year 2 Science bank release

The owner approved publishing AC9S2U01 and AC9S2I04–AC9S2I06 and activating the Content Verified badge. The adjacent CSV is the exact source for these 160 items, with 24 Practice and 16 Test questions per code. This supersedes the earlier unpublished 48-Practice U01 draft, selecting 24 items for coverage rather than simply taking its first 24.

## Coverage

- U01: Earth, Sun, Moon and planets; observable Sun and shadow patterns; changes in Moon, star and planet positions; interpreting model and sky records; limitations of observations.
- I04: sorting and ordering records; reading and completing supplied tables; chronological and magnitude sequences; equal-unit visual and physical models; representing patterns and missing observations. Six adult-reviewed tasks require actual representations.
- I05: comparing observations with predictions and others' records; partial agreement; checking conditions and units; suggesting fairer comparisons; asking guided follow-up questions. Six adult-reviewed tasks require explanation and question generation.
- I06: communicating procedures, predictions and findings; accurate labels, captions, units and scientific vocabulary; audience clarity; appropriate audio/video; separating observation from unsupported claims. Six adult-reviewed tasks require creating text, drawings or explanations.
