# SkillrHub growth plan: one million qualified page views

## Fixed business rules

- Core online learning remains genuinely free.
- Learners do not need accounts or subscriptions.
- Learner progress stays local to the device; SkillrHub does not build a central student-results database.
- Free curriculum content targets dependable, useful coverage rather than workbook-level mastery depth.
- Premium subject packs and workbooks provide the structured sequence, cumulative practice and mastery pathway.
- Affiliate recommendations must be relevant, adult-facing, clearly disclosed and must not obstruct free learning.

## North-star outcome

Reach one million qualified page views from students, families, teachers and tutors, then convert trust into sustainable subject-pack sales and relevant affiliate revenue.

Qualified traffic means a visitor completes or meaningfully uses a learning action: opens a topic, Classroom View, worksheet, Practice, Test, Daily Drill, dashboard, product preview or relevant affiliate recommendation.

## Funnel

1. Discovery through Google, AI answers, Pinterest, teacher communities or referrals.
2. Immediate free value through a topic guide, worksheet, Classroom View or practice activity.
3. A completed learning loop: Learn, Practise, Test and Review.
4. Repeat use through Daily Drills, local progress and relevant next-step links.
5. Commercial continuation through a clearly relevant mastery workbook, subject pack or disclosed affiliate resource.

## Measurement

### Acquisition

- Organic clicks and impressions by query and landing page
- AI and referral traffic
- Pinterest and community-referral traffic
- New versus returning users

### Learning engagement

- Topic, worksheet, Classroom View, Practice, Test and Daily Drill starts
- `learning_activity_complete` by mode, curriculum code and score band
- Dashboard use and repeat activity
- Resource sharing

### Commercial intent

- `subject_pack_interest`
- Product preview opens
- `affiliate_click` by product type and source page
- Resource-request clicks
- Subject-pack purchases and net revenue, reconciled from marketplace reports
- Affiliate orders and commission, reconciled from affiliate reports

### Core ratios

- Learning completions per 1,000 page views
- Returning users as a share of active users
- Product-page visits per 1,000 learning completions
- Pack purchases per 1,000 product-page visits
- Affiliate clicks and revenue per 1,000 page views

## Delivery sequence

### Phase 1: measurement and commercial foundations

- Track learning completions without learner names or answers.
- Track affiliate clicks, subject-pack interest, resource requests and sharing.
- Establish a monthly scorecard using GA4, Search Console, marketplace and affiliate reports.
- Create the canonical subject-pack catalogue when the first mastery pack is ready.

### Phase 2: traffic concentration

- Use Search Console evidence to select high-impression pages ranking outside strong click positions.
- Improve a small number of high-intent parent/teacher landing pages rather than expanding curriculum-code volume.
- Connect each target page to the complete free learning pathway.
- Build external distribution through useful free packs, teacher groups, community organisations and Pinterest.

### Phase 3: conversion without degrading free access

- Add relevant workbook continuation panels only where the product matches the learning topic.
- Always preserve the free Practice, Test and worksheet routes alongside a paid option.
- Show sample pages, exact contents, answers, year level, format and printing information before purchase.
- Test messaging based on structure and convenience, never failure pressure.

### Phase 4: retention

- Improve local dashboard next steps, subject/strand progress and review suggestions.
- Use Daily Drills and continue-learning links to increase useful return visits.
- Provide local backups and printable progress summaries without central learner storage.

## Human inputs required

- Monthly Search Console query and page exports
- Monthly GA4 acquisition and event exports
- Final product files, sales URLs, prices and preview images
- Marketplace sales and fee reports
- Affiliate click, order and commission reports
- Genuine user feedback and permission for any testimonial used
- Approval of pricing, marketplaces and new affiliate categories

## Decision rule

Prioritise work that improves one of four outcomes: qualified discovery, completed learning actions, useful repeat visits or trusted commercial continuation. Do not add new free-content depth merely to chase completeness when the existing resource already meets the dependable 7/10 usefulness target.
