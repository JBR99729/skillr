# Foundation comparing and sharing review

6 September 2026. Scope: AC9SFI04 and AC9SFI05 topic pages and worksheets. No new images, classroom revisions or Practice/Test changes.

## Benchmark limits
The available IXL example at https://au.ixl.com/science/year-1/weather-patterns asks learners to combine a described experience and a pictured weather scene to select a description. The question text and scene were visible, but the session subsequently displayed a sign-in dialog. No answers were submitted and no authenticated progression was claimed for this batch.

This is an indirect benchmark for using relevant evidence. No exact IXL counterpart for the full Foundation requirements of comparing predictions with guidance or sharing one's own science ideas was established. The earlier observed IXL description and investigation tasks provide additional context, while the Australian Foundation descriptors govern these changes. All new wording and scenarios are original.

## Changes
Ten teaching/check activities and ten specific elaboration expansions. Twenty explained worksheet answers and teaching hints added in static printable adult guides, retaining the student PDF renderer and 8 core / 2 enrichment structure.

Comparing now includes matched results, different results, partly matching predictions and insufficient information about the predicted feature. Sharing now requires an accurate message that distinguishes prediction from observation, a representation that matches the evidence, and a relevant listener response. Adult guidance, oral answers and drawings remain appropriate.

## Validation
All nine Foundation science static pages pass. Only target worksheet data changed; original question types, options and visuals preserved. Each answer guide renders to one Letter page and was visually inspected without clipping. Links, topic image preservation and guide counts checked.

This completes the topic-page and worksheet review for all nine Foundation science codes. Classroom views remain a separate review scope.
