# Year 6 STEM quality pass — August 2026

Scope: 24 Year 6 Mathematics and 12 Year 6 Science codes. Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 descriptions and all elaborations recorded in the curriculum manifest were checked before implementation.

## Audit before implementation

The 23 standard Mathematics Topic Pages were already strong static lessons with accurate models, worked transfer, misconception support and mastery checks. AC9M6N01 was an independently authored deep lesson with two essential diagrams, revision notes and 20 worked questions. These pages were kept rather than rebuilt. Their main gap was that exact elaborations were not visible on 23 pages. The 12 Science pages were generic curriculum summaries despite having strong authored models and activities in the data layer, so students could not yet learn independently from the canonical page.

The canonical banks contained 1,440 questions: 864 Practice and 576 Test. All items were retained. The only material question-bank issue was AC9M6N01's highly uneven correct-answer position distribution; its 40 four-option questions were rebalanced without changing stems, answers or explanations. The previous validator incorrectly treated legitimate four-option questions, spoken-form audio and questions needing no visual as defects. All Test sessions used 12 questions, and AC9M6N01 Practice/Test used 12/16, breaching the hard eight-question rule.

## Implementation report

All 36 Topic Pages now meet the standalone-learning threshold. Mathematics pages preserve their strong existing content and expose every available elaboration plus Revision Notes. Science pages now contain learning goals, worked models, vocabulary/representations, guided activities, exact curriculum coverage, Revision Notes, important questions and misconception corrections. Existing HTML/CSS models cover integers, fractions, number lines, graphs, angles, coordinates, tessellations, habitats, the solar system, circuits, energy, matter and scientific inquiry. No decorative raster imagery was added.
