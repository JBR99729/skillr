- Four additional activities per guide: 20 total, each with expandable answer/teaching guidance.
- LA05: completeness based on meaning, changing word order, and completing an idea.

- LA08: familiar contextual meanings, near-synonyms, role-play vocabulary and transfer to a new example.
- LA09: visible editing examples, capitals inside sentences, and the different jobs of letters and punctuation.
