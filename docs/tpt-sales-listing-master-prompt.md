# SkillrHub TPT Sales Listing Master Prompt

Use this operating framework whenever preparing or optimising a Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT) title, description, metadata, freebie, or bundle for SkillrHub Learning.

## Objective
Maximise legitimate TPT discovery, followers, conversions, bundle sales and revenue while accurately representing the resource.

## Required listing workflow
1. Identify Year/Grade, subject, topic, Australian Curriculum v9 code, format, page/slide count, free/paid status, related products and bundle opportunities.
2. Use TPT Keyword Finder and Search Analytics when available. Prefer actual TPT demand, competition, Opportunity Score, related keywords and SkillrHub search/conversion data over guessed keywords.
3. Prefer high-intent, high-demand, lower-competition long-tail phrases.
4. Title: strongest teacher search phrase first, then Grade/Year, resource type, Australian Curriculum/code where useful. Use title space efficiently without irrelevant keyword stuffing.
5. Description opening: naturally include primary keyword, Grade/Year, topic, resource type and classroom benefit.
6. Keep copy teacher-scannable. Include contents, skills, classroom uses, curriculum alignment, answers and page/slide count when known.
7. Select every truthful relevant TPT grade, subject, format, resource type and standards/curriculum filter.

## Commercial ladder
FREE SAMPLE -> SINGLE-SKILL PRODUCT -> TOPIC BUNDLE -> STRAND BUNDLE -> FULL-YEAR BUNDLE.

Check every new resource for legitimate bundle membership. Do not wait for the whole year before creating coherent topic/strand bundles.

## Freebie CTA
For free samples, include:

**Enjoyed this free resource?**

If this resource helped save you planning or prep time, please **follow the SkillrHub Learning TPT store** and consider leaving feedback. It helps us understand which resources teachers find most useful.

We release **new FREE classroom samples regularly**, so follow the store to see the next one.

— SkillrHub Learning

## Post-publication optimisation
Monitor TPT search impressions, product views, search terms, free downloads, followers, paid orders, conversion rate, revenue and bundle performance. Use Search Analytics to revise weak listings and double down on demonstrated teacher demand.

Consider TPT Newsletter submissions, legitimate seller discounts/coupons, cross-selling and bundle expansion when appropriate.

## Rules
Never manufacture reviews, fake purchases or engagement; misrepresent curriculum alignment; stuff irrelevant keywords; duplicate identical products to manipulate search; or circumvent TPT controls.

Out-research, out-package, out-merchandise and out-operate competitors legitimately.

## Operating target
Build toward a sustained run rate of **10 paid TPT product orders per day**.

Treat every completed SkillrHub PPT as commercial inventory and every listing as an optimisation experiment.
