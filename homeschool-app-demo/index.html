See the actual workflow

A captioned walkthrough assembled from recorded live screens. All learning entries are demonstrations; pack page references are illustrative.

1 · Choose

Your child and learning year

Create or select a learner. Set their current year and jurisdiction. Use Learning year to view Foundation–Year 10 topics or revisit earlier learning.

2 · Use the worksheets

Paper learning, once

Buy the matching pack through the printable button, or use one you already own. Topic guides help you explain the skill. There is no need to repeat the worksheet online.

3 · Record

What your child could do

Choose the pack, pages and support level. The date is filled in; minutes are optional. Add a short observation. Each entry keeps its learning year.

4 · Keep evidence

A paper folder, not photo uploads

Keep selected dated samples and add their folder or page reference. Record a new session for further learning; edit an entry to correct it.

5 · Review

Your dates and two short notes

Choose the reporting period. Add your reflection and next goals, save them, and review the prompts for the learner’s state or territory.

6 · Print

A useful supporting summary

Print or save PDF with all pages. Check the End of record line. Supply selected work samples and required official forms separately.
