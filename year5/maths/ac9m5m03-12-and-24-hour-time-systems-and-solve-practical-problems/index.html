AC9M5M03: Compare 12- and 24-hour time systems and solve practical problems involving the conversion between them

What students learn in AC9M5M03

Students convert times around noon and midnight, read schedules and calculate elapsed time using timelines while distinguishing a clock time from a duration.

In 24-hour time, hours after noon continue 13–23; midnight begins 00:00 and noon is 12:00.

For pm times after 12:59, add 12 to the hour. For 13:00–23:59, subtract 12 and label pm.

Do not subtract time digits as ordinary decimals. Bridge through whole hours and check whether the interval crosses noon or midnight.

Learning routine: Represent → Reason → Calculate → Interpret → Verify

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Content description: compare 12- and 24-hour time systems and solve practical problems involving the conversion between them.

  • E1: using timetables written in 24-hour time, such as flight schedules, to plan an overseas or interstate trip, converting between 24- and 12-hour time
  • E2: converting between the digital and analog representation of 24-hour time, matching the same times represented in both systems; setting the time on an analog watch using a digital alarm clock

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • 12 am and 12 pm reversed — 12 am is midnight; 12 pm is noon.
  • Add 12 to every pm hour — Do not add 12 to 12 pm.
  • Minutes treated as hundredths — An hour has 60 minutes.
  • Leading zero omitted in formal 24-hour time — Write 07:05 for schedule clarity.
