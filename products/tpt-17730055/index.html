Show & Explain Your Science | Year 1 Science Communication Lessons | AC9S1I06

Teach Year 1 students how to communicate science observations, findings and ideas with this Australian Curriculum v9 aligned teaching resource for AC9S1I06. Students learn to choose important observations, use clear everyday and science words, create labelled drawings and diagrams, record findings in a science journal, consider their audience and share their ideas clearly.
