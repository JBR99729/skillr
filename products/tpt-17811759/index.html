Foundation Position & Directions | AC9MFSP02 | Australian Curriculum v9

Teach Foundation students to describe position, follow routes and give clear directions with this 103-slide resource aligned to Australian Curriculum v9 code AC9MFSP02. Six strands cover position words such as in, on, under, beside, between, in front of and behind; left and right; moving and turning; following routes; giving directions; and fixing an incorrect route or instruction.
