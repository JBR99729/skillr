Year 3 Fractions Lesson Slides | Unit Fractions | AC9M3N02 Australian v9

A 30-slide visual teaching presentation for unit fractions and their multiples, with worked examples, guided practice, misconception checks and answers.

Australian Curriculum: AC9M3N02

What’s included

  • Learning intention and success criteria
  • Equal and unequal parts; halves, thirds, quarters, fifths and tenths
  • Numerator and denominator meaning
  • Unit fractions and multiples of unit fractions
  • Fractions of collections and lengths
  • Fraction strips, number lines and completing a whole
  • Eight check-for-understanding and misconception questions with answer slides
  • Guided practice, Think-Pair-Share prompts and an exit ticket with answers
  • Focused on AC9M3N02; not a complete Year 3 mathematics program
