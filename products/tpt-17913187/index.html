Five Teaching Focuses

  • Build and read number models: hundreds, tens and ones; zero as a placeholder; 10 hundreds make 1000.
  • Match numbers and number names: connect words and numerals, including teen numbers and empty places.
  • Read and use number lines: count equal gaps, find missing positions and distinguish exact locations from estimates.
  • Compare and order numbers: compare hundreds, then tens and ones; order numbers and arrange digit cards.
  • Use charts and cross boundaries: explore a ten-column chart, count in ones, tens and hundreds, and explain counting back across a hundred.

How To Use

Present a short section, talk through the worked examples and pause for learners to try a question using mini whiteboards, paper or manipulatives. Reveal the worked answer and discuss the reasoning. Use the mixed recap to check understanding.

Suitable for whole-class teaching, small-group support, tutoring and homeschool lessons. Students can then practise the same concepts using your chosen activities.
