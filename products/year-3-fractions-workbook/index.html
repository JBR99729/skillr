Year 3 Fractions Workbook | 80 Questions & Answers | AC9M3N02

A 43-page printable fractions workbook with 80 questions, visual models, mastery checks and complete answers with worked methods and teaching tips.

Australian Curriculum: AC9M3N02

Australian curriculum planning

Australian primary teachers can use this Australian Curriculum v9 (ACARA) Year 3 Mathematics resource for local planning, including NSW teachers using the NSW Curriculum / NSW Mathematics Syllabus (NESA Stage 2) and Victorian teachers using Victorian Curriculum 2.0 Mathematics (VCAA Level 3). Retain the listed AC9 code as the curriculum reference. Check the relevant local syllabus content and achievement standards when selecting activities; this planning guidance does not establish a code-by-code equivalence.

What’s included

  • 64 written-response practice questions plus 16 mastery and extension questions
  • Halves, thirds, quarters, fifths and tenths; unit fractions and their multiples
  • Fraction strips, area models, collections, lengths and number lines
  • Completing a whole, drawing models, explaining reasoning and correcting mistakes
  • Complete answer key with worked methods and teaching or checking tips
  • Focused on AC9M3N02; not a complete Year 3 mathematics program
  • 43-page non-editable PDF; US Letter pages can be scaled to A4
