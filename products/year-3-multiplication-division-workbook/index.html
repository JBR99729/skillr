Year 3 Multiplication & Division Workbook | 144 Questions | Australian v9

A 70-page workbook with 144 multiplication and division questions, including practice, application, mastery checks and detailed answer sheets.

Australian Curriculum: AC9M3A03 · AC9M3N04

Australian curriculum planning

Australian primary teachers can use this Australian Curriculum v9 (ACARA) Year 3 Mathematics resource for local planning, including NSW teachers using the NSW Curriculum / NSW Mathematics Syllabus (NESA Stage 2) and Victorian teachers using Victorian Curriculum 2.0 Mathematics (VCAA Level 3). Retain the listed AC9 codes as the curriculum references. Check the relevant local syllabus content and achievement standards when selecting activities; this planning guidance does not establish a code-by-code equivalence.

What’s included

  • 144 questions: 112 practice and application questions plus 32 mastery checks
  • 3, 4, 5 and 10 multiplication facts and related division facts
  • Equal groups, arrays, sharing, grouping and fact families
  • Missing factors, inverse operations and efficient calculation strategies
  • Word problems, reasoning and error analysis
  • Detailed answer sheets with methods and explanations
  • Australian Curriculum v9.0 alignment: AC9M3A03 and AC9M3N04
