Foundation Plants & Animals Slides | AC9SFU01 | Australian Curriculum v9

Help Foundation students observe the visible features of plants and animals with this photograph-rich, 103-slide resource aligned to Australian Curriculum v9 code AC9SFU01. Six strands teach children to look closely, name plant and animal features, compare what is the same and different, and group plants and animals using a visible rule.
