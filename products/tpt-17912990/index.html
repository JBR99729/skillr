Build confidence with numbers to 1000 through a clear teach–practise–assess sequence. This 37-page printable Year 2 maths workbook includes worked examples, 50 practice questions, 15 separate test questions and teacher answer keys.

Designed for teachers, tutors and homeschool families, the pack helps learners connect number models, numerals, number names, number lines and place-value reasoning.

What'S Included

  • 50 numbered practice questions — 10 for each of five teaching focuses.
  • 15 separate numbered test questions covering the five focuses.
  • 5 additional vocabulary and dictation prompts, counted separately.
  • Worked teaching examples with labelled mathematical models.
  • Complete teacher answer keys, explanations and guidance for acceptable responses.
  • Teacher guidance for support, challenge, assessment and marking.
  • Contents and page ranges for selective printing.

Skills Covered

  • Build and read number models: hundreds, tens, ones and the role of zero.
  • Match numbers and number names: read and write numerals and words.
  • Read and use number lines: equal intervals, missing numbers and estimates.
  • Compare and order numbers: place-value comparisons, ordering and digit arrangements.
  • Use charts and cross boundaries: neighbouring numbers, chart patterns and counting across tens and hundreds.

Activities include drawing, matching, completing, ordering, locating numbers, correcting errors and explaining reasoning.

How To Use

Teach one focus at a time, model a worked example and assign the matching practice. Use the separate test after teaching the five focuses. Suitable for classroom lessons, small groups, tutoring, supported homework and homeschool learning.

Curriculum And Scope

Supports Australian Curriculum V9 Year 2 code AC9M2N01. Printed activities focus on numbers through 1000. Use physical or virtual models alongside the pages and extend beyond 1000 where needed for the descriptor's “at least” scope. Australian spelling and number-name conventions are used.
