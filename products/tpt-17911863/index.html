Description

Help Year 1 students understand what they hear, see and read with this focused 71-page printable pack and accompanying eight-slide teaching PowerPoint. Short original texts, clear teaching models and consistent black-and-white illustrations support five essential comprehension strategies.

Five teaching focuses

  • Visualising — make a picture in your mind and draw the details the words tell you.
  • Predicting — use clues to make a guess, then check it after reading more.
  • Connecting — link the text to personal experience and background knowledge.
  • Summarising — identify the main idea and briefly retell key events.
  • Questioning — ask and answer questions, find evidence, and notice what the text does not say.
