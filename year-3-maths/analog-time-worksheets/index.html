Parent and teacher guide

Analog time can feel tricky because students need to read two pieces of information at once: the hour hand and the minute hand. Start by checking that the hour hand shows which hour the time is near, then use skip-counting by fives around the clock face for the minutes. Many Year 3 students rush to read the longer minute hand first and then choose the wrong hour. A good teaching sequence is to practise o'clock and half past, then quarter past and quarter to, then five-minute intervals. Ask students to say the time in words before writing it digitally. This helps them connect the clock face to real language such as quarter past three or twenty minutes to six.

Common mistakes to watch for

Ask the student to explain their method before moving to a test. If they can only guess, return to one visual model and one worked example. Short practice with feedback usually works better than a long worksheet completed without review.
