Curriculum alignment

These Foundation maths and science resources are primarily aligned to the Australian Curriculum Version 9.0, while also offering useful equivalent references for families and educators in the US, UK, Canada, New Zealand and India.

Equivalent curriculum references: US CCSS early numeracy and science inquiry, UK EYFS, Canada Ontario Kindergarten, NZ Early Level Mathematics and Science, and India NCERT Class 1 Mathematics and Environmental Studies.

Visible curriculum codes: Foundation Mathematics — VIC VC2MFM, NSW MAE; Foundation Science — VIC VC2SFS, NSW STe-1.

For more detailed curriculum mapping, visit the individual topic pages in the Foundation Maths and Foundation Science sections.
