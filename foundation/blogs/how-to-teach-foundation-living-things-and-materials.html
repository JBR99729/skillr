How to Teach Foundation Living Things and Materials

Foundation science becomes more meaningful when children compare real objects and talk about what they observe.

Use everyday examples

Children can explore living things by looking at plants, animals and people around them. Materials can be compared by texture, hardness, flexibility and use.
