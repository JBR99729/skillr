How to Teach Foundation Science Observations and Questions

Science in the early years works best when children can look closely, ask questions and talk about what they notice.

Keep questions simple

Use questions such as “What do you notice?” or “What do you think will happen?” to build curiosity. Young children often learn more when they are given time to explore before being told the answer.
