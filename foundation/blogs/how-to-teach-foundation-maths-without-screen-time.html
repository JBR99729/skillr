How to Teach Foundation Maths Without Screen Time

Screen-free maths can be rich, playful and highly effective when children learn through movement, objects and conversation.

Use real objects first

Start with counters, toy animals, blocks or buttons. Children can sort, compare and count them before moving to written work. This keeps the learning concrete and meaningful.

For early number work, try building a number with objects, then matching it to a numeral and a picture.

Use the current topic pages to choose printable work

Each current topic page connects the explanation to its printable worksheet and, when screens are appropriate later, online practice and testing.

Why this approach works

Hands-on learning gives children time to explore, build confidence and develop language before they move to more formal recording.
