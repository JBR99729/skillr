How to Explain Foundation Maths to Children

Children learn early maths best when ideas are explained in simple, familiar words and supported by objects they can touch and move.

Keep the language simple

Instead of saying “equivalent”, try “the same amount” or “the same number”. For young children, short phrases and clear examples are much more effective than formal vocabulary.

Use examples from everyday life

Counting snacks, sorting toys, comparing shoes or matching socks all make maths feel natural. These moments are a great way to introduce number ideas without making learning feel like a lesson.
