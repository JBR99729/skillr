Teach, ask and checkTopic-aligned

AC9EFLA04 · How Books and Screens Are Organised

Learning focus: I can use book and screen clues to know where to start and what to do next.

Model it: indicating the title of a book and where to start reading

Ask the learner: What is the main idea of print and screen conventions?

Look for: The learner can I can use book and screen clues to know where to start and what to do next in a fresh example and explain the evidence or reasoning without copying the model.

If they are stuck: If the learner guesses or repeats a rule without evidence, return to the concrete or visual model and ask them to explain what each part shows.
