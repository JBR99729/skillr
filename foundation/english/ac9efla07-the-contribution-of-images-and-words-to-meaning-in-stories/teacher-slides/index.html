AC9EFLA07 · AC9EFLA07: Explore How Images and Words Build Meaning

Learning focus: Explore the contribution of images and words to meaning in stories and informative texts. Students identify what each mode adds, then combine picture and word clues to explain the fuller meaning.

Model it: Use the skill in reading, speaking or writing: Explain what the words tell and what the picture adds. Picture card: dark clouds Words: It might rain. Meaning: weather is changing

Ask the learner: What is the main idea of images and words work together?

Look for: What is the main idea of images and words work together? Which example shows the skill?

If they are stuck: If the learner guesses or repeats a rule without evidence, return to the concrete or visual model and ask them to explain what each part shows.
