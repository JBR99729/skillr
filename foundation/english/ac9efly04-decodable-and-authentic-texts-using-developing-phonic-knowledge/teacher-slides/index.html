Elaboration expansion and examples 1

  • E1: navigating a text correctly, starting at the right place and reading in the right direction, returning to the next line as needed and matching one spoken word to one written word
  • E2: attempting to work out unknown words by using phonic decoding and knowledge of high-frequency words
  • E3: pausing or asking for support when meaning breaks down

Elaboration expansion and examples 2

  • Decode and check: c-a-t → cat Does it make sense? Reread if it sounds wrong
  • Use the skill in reading, speaking or writing: Blend sounds and check the word makes sense in the sentence. c-a-t → cat Does it make sense? Reread if it sounds wrong
