Teach, ask and checkTopic-aligned

AC9EFLY02 · listening and voice in interactions

Learning focus: I can listen when others speak and use a voice volume that suits the situation.

Model it: Worked example: change volume for the audience Worked-model method: listen, wait, respond on topic and choose an audible volume. 1. Nora explains a picture to one partner with a quiet, clear voice. 2. Later, Nora reports the same idea to the class and speaks clearly enough for the back row. 3. Her message stays the same, but her volume changes because the audience changes. Model answer: A suitable volume is not always the loudest or quietest voice; it lets the intended listeners hear comfortably.

Ask the learner: Ask the learner to use a new example and explain how it demonstrates I can listen when others speak and use a voice volume that suits the situation..

Look for: The learner can complete a fresh example and explain the reasoning without copying the model.

If they are stuck: If the learner guesses or repeats a rule without evidence, return to the concrete or visual model and ask them to explain what each part shows.
