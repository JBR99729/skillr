Elaboration expansion and examples 1

  • E1: using drawing and beginning forms of writing to express personal responses to stories, poems or films
  • E2: discussing events and characters in texts, and connecting them to their own experiences
  • Character + event + response: The bear lost his hat. I felt worried because he looked sad. I think the rabbit should help.

Elaboration expansion and examples 2

  • Use the skill in reading, speaking or writing: Say what happened and how you feel or think about it. The bear lost his hat. I felt worried because he looked sad. I think the rabbit should help.
  • Teacher model: Read or say the first card. Ask: what does this show?
