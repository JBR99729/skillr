role playing or actively engaging in situations that involve quantifying or comparing collections of items or simple money transactions; for example, “Do we have enough scissors for our group so that each person has their own pair?”; role-playing using $1 coins to pay for items in a shop where items are priced in whole dollars Learning connection: Use objects, actions, drawings and numbers to represent what happens when quantities join or separate.

Example 3

Example 4

representing addition and subtraction situations found in leaf games involving sets of objects used to tell stories, such as games from the Warlpiri Peoples of Yuendumu in the Northern Territory Learning connection: Use objects, actions, drawings and numbers to represent what happens when quantities join or separate.

Questions

Review hints

  • separate: take objects away from a group
