Elaboration expansion and examples 1

  • E1: describing the position of an item in relation to other items in the space using language like “inside”, “underneath” and “on top of”; for example, when asked “Where are the scissors kept?”, responding with “They are in a box, on the bottom shelf at the back of the classroom” Learning connection: Position words describe where something is in relation to another person, object or place.

Elaboration expansion and examples 2

  • E2: describing where they have moved themselves and items in relation to other items within a space, using familiar terms; for example, playing a hiding game and when asked “Where did you hide the ball?”, responding, “I hid it behind the garbage bin over there near the bench” Learning connection: Position words describe where something is in relation to another person, object or place.
  • E3: describing the position of a robotic toy in relation to other objects as it moves around a familiar space; for example, describing the position of a robotic car as being under the desk or next to the chair Learning connection: Position words describe where something is in relation to another person, object or place.

Elaboration expansion and examples 3

  • E4: exploring First Nations Australians’ instructive games; for example, Thapumpan from the Wik-Mungkan Peoples of Cape Bedford in north Queensland, describing position and movement of self in relation to other participants, objects or locations Learning connection: Position words describe where something is in relation to another person, object or place.
  • Always name the reference object: INSIDE [ ● ] The ball is inside the box. UNDER TABLE ───── ● The ball is under the table. BESIDE CHAIR ● The ball is beside the chair. Think aloud: Build or point to the model first, explain what each part shows, then state the answer.

Elaboration expansion and examples 4

  • Use more than one clue: “The scissors are in the box, on the bottom shelf, at the back of the classroom.” Specific relational words make a location easier to find.
