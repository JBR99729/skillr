What the Foundation English strands teach

Language

Explore how language changes with purpose and audience, how print and screen texts work, and how sentences, words, images, vocabulary and punctuation create meaning.

Literature

Enjoy and respond to stories, poems and images; recognise different literary texts; play with rhythm and sound; and retell or adapt familiar texts.

Literacy

Listen, speak, read, view and create short texts while developing comprehension, handwriting, phonological awareness, letter–sound knowledge, spelling and high-frequency words.
