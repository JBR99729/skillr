What the Foundation Maths strands teach

Number

Build number sense to 20 by connecting real collections, visual models and numerals. Students count, compare, partition and model addition, subtraction, sharing and grouping.

Algebra

Notice, copy, continue and explain repeating patterns made with objects, shapes, sounds and movements.

Measurement

Compare length, mass, capacity and duration fairly, then sequence familiar events, days and times of day.

Space

Name and describe familiar shapes using their features, and use precise position words to locate and move objects.

Statistics

Ask a familiar question, collect and sort responses, display them with objects or pictures, and compare the results.
