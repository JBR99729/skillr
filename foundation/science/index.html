Foundation Science Learning Overview

Foundation students learn how plants and animals meet their basic needs, investigate daily and seasonal environmental changes, and explore how pushes and pulls affect objects.

Students also learn to ask scientific questions, make predictions, conduct investigations safely, record observations, sort information, recognise patterns, compare results and communicate their findings.
