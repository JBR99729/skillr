AC9SFI05 - AC9SFI05: Share Science Ideas and Evidence
Example 1

sharing questions, making predictions and describing observations to others through discussions and circle groups Learning connection: Scientists share what they asked, predicted and observed so other people can understand the investigation and discuss the evidence.

Example 2

recounting stories and posing questions about their own experiences learning about the natural world, such as when gardening or observing plants and animals at home or visits to conservation areas or centres Learning connection: Scientists share what they asked, predicted and observed so other people can understand the investigation and discuss the evidence.

Example 3

acknowledging and exploring First Nations Australians’ ways of communicating information about anatomical features of plants and animals Learning connection: Scientists share what they asked, predicted and observed so other people can understand the investigation and discuss the evidence.

Example 4

showing or describing how objects can be moved in different ways and responding to questions Learning connection: Scientists share what they asked, predicted and observed so other people can understand the investigation and discuss the evidence.

  • Share a short science story: Question → What did we want to find out? Prediction → What did I think would happen? Observation → What actually happened? Idea → What do I think now? Think aloud: Describe what is observed first, then use that evidence to explain the idea.
  • Use a picture, object, words or simple record: Foundation students can share by speaking, pointing to a drawing, showing a simple table or demonstrating with an object.
