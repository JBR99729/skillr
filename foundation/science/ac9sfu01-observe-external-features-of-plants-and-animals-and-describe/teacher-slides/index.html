Elaboration examplesCopied content

Elaboration expansion and examples 1

  • E1: observing fruits and vegetables and identifying them as parts of plants such as roots, flowers, fruits or leaves.
  • E2: recognising humans as animals, describing external features of humans and exploring similarities and differences compared with other animals.
  • E3: using magnifying glasses or digital cameras to observe and identify external features of plants and animals.

Elaboration expansion and examples 2

  • E4: sorting collections of model animals and explaining different grouping strategies.
  • Learn: Observe → Name features → Compare → Group → Explain Goal: identify external features of plants and animals and explain a sensible grouping rule using what can be seen.
  • Teach It: Look closely at visible features

Elaboration expansion and examples 3

  • Apply the Idea: Group by an observable rule: Body covering Group animals as feathers / fur / scales . Movement feature Group animals by visible features such as wings / fins / legs . Plant feature Group plants by visible features such as flowers / no flowers or broad / narrow leaves . Important: more than one sensible grouping rule can be correct. The student should state the rule and use visible evidence.

Assessment-style questions and review hints 1

Questions

  • What is the key idea when learning about living things and external features?
  • Which model or example can show living things and external features?

Review hints

  • An external feature is visible on the outside of a plant or animal.
  • Observe first: describe only what you can actually see.

Assessment-style questions and review hints 2

Questions

  • What common mistake should be corrected when learning about living things and external features?
  • What routine can students use for living things and external features?

Review hints

  • Compare by naming one similarity and one difference.
  • Group living things using one clear visible rule, such as body covering, wings, fins, leaves or flowers.
