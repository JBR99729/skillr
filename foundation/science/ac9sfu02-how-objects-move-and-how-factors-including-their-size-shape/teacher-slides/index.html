AC9SFU02 - AC9SFU02: How Objects Move
Example 1

observing how toys move, and grouping them based on their movement Learning connection: Objects can move in different ways such as rolling, sliding, spinning and bouncing. Their size, shape and material can affect how they move.

Example 2

observing and describing ways different and unusually shaped objects such as blocks, tubes or eggs move when rolled down a slope Learning connection: Objects can move in different ways such as rolling, sliding, spinning and bouncing. Their size, shape and material can affect how they move.

Example 3

comparing the way different-sized, similar-shaped objects such as tennis balls, golf balls, marbles or basketballs roll and bounce Learning connection: Objects can move in different ways such as rolling, sliding, spinning and bouncing. Their size, shape and material can affect how they move.

Example 4

exploring how the material a ball is made from affects the way it moves, such as plastic, foam, cloth or rubber balls on a surface Learning connection: Objects can move in different ways such as rolling, sliding, spinning and bouncing. Their size, shape and material can affect how they move.

Important questions and answers 1

What is the key idea when learning about how objects move?
describe how objects move and how factors including their size, shape or material influence their movement
Which model or example can show how objects move?
Shape can change the movement: round marble → rolls easily flat block → tends to slide spinning top → turns around one point Think aloud: Describe what is observed first, then use that evidence to explain the idea.

Important questions and answers 2

What common mistake should be corrected when learning about how objects move?
Make a fair movement comparison: Same surface + same start → change ONE feature Compare two objects fairly by keeping the start and surface the same.
What routine can students use for how objects move?
Best self-check: explain one new example without copying the worked model.
