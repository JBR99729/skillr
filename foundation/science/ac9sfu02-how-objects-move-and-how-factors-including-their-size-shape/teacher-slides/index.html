AC9SFU02 · AC9SFU02: How Objects Move

Learning focus: describe how objects move and how factors including their size, shape or material influence their movement

Model it: Pause after each worked example.

Ask the learner: Ask the learner to use a new example and explain how it demonstrates describe how objects move and how factors including their size, shape or material influence their movement.

Look for: Learning routine: Observe → Move → Describe → Change one feature → Compare

If they are stuck: If the learner guesses or repeats a rule without evidence, return to the concrete or visual model and ask them to explain what each part shows.
