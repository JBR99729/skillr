Networks and Connectedness

Networks represent entities as vertices and relationships as edges, allowing connectedness and routes to be analysed.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

Networks represent entities as vertices and relationships as edges, allowing connectedness and routes to be analysed.

Learning intention: Networks represent entities as vertices and relationships as edges, allowing connectedness and routes to be analysed.

Success criteria

  • Explain translate a situation into nodes and edges using the approved model.
  • Apply represent kinship relationships respectfully to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • interpret networks and network diagrams used to represent relationships in practical situations and describe connectedness

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 9 space representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • A diagram is trusted by appearance instead of stated conditions — Mark givens and name the theorem or transformation used.
  • The networks and connectedness model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Explore how networks and network diagrams can be used to model authentic situations, recognising what real world quantity is represented by the nodes (vertices).’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
