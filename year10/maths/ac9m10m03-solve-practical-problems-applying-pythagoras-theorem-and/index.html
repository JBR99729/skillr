Navigation and Right-Triangle Trigonometry

Bearings, elevation and depression problems become solvable when directions and right triangles are labelled before calculation.

What students learn

Bearings, elevation and depression problems become solvable when directions and right triangles are labelled before calculation.

Learning intention: Bearings, elevation and depression problems become solvable when directions and right triangles are labelled before calculation.

Success criteria

  • Explain navigate with bearings using the approved model.
  • Apply connect cultural technologies and trigonometry to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • solve practical problems applying Pythagoras’ theorem and trigonometry of right-angled triangles, including problems involving direction and angles of elevation and depression

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 9 measurement representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Dimensions, units or accuracy are mixed — Label every measure and convert before calculating.
  • The navigation and right-triangle trigonometry model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Determining the bearing and estimating the distance of the final leg of an orienteering course.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
