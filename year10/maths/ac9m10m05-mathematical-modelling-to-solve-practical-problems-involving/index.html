Proportion and Scaling Models

A valid scale model preserves corresponding ratios and is reviewed against physical, design and contextual constraints.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

A valid scale model preserves corresponding ratios and is reviewed against physical, design and contextual constraints.

Learning intention: A valid scale model preserves corresponding ratios and is reviewed against physical, design and contextual constraints.

Success criteria

  • Explain read plans and elevations using the approved model.
  • Apply preserve aspect ratio in generated images to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • use mathematical modelling to solve practical problems involving proportion and scaling of objects; formulate problems and interpret solutions in terms of the situation; evaluate and modify models as necessary, and report assumptions, methods and findings

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 9 measurement representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Worked examples

Read plans and elevations

Use plans and elevation drawings to investigate making changes to building designs, employing appropriate scales and converting to actual measurements within the context to make decisions about changes.

Teacher prompt: For read plans and elevations, which units, bounds or accuracy statement belong in the result?

Scale a production prototype

Using a 3D printer to produce scaled versions of actual objects.

Teacher prompt: For scale a production prototype, which quantities must scale or compare by the same factor?

Estimate an object's scale

A toy car, by measuring a linear dimension and using a typical car dimension to work out the scale factor.

Teacher prompt: For estimate an object's scale, which quantities must scale or compare by the same factor?

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Dimensions, units or accuracy are mixed — Label every measure and convert before calculating.
  • The proportion and scaling models model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Use plans and elevation drawings to investigate making changes to building designs, employing appropriate scales and converting to actual measurements within the context to make decisions about changes.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
