Conditional Probability Simulations

A valid conditional simulation preserves dependency, replacement rules and the restricted sample space before interpreting frequencies.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

A valid conditional simulation preserves dependency, replacement rules and the restricted sample space before interpreting frequencies.

Learning intention: A valid conditional simulation preserves dependency, replacement rules and the restricted sample space before interpreting frequencies.

Success criteria

  • Explain compare sample size and replacement using the approved model.
  • Apply model infection risk with simulation to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • design and conduct repeated chance experiments and simulations using digital tools to model conditional probability and interpret results

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 9 probability representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Worked examples

Compare sample size and replacement

Use samples of different sizes with and without replacement from a population to identify when the difference in methods becomes negligible.

Teacher prompt: For compare sample size and replacement, which outcomes belong in the denominator, and why?

Recognise dependent events

Recognise that an event can be dependent on another event and that this will affect the way its probability is calculated.

Teacher prompt: For recognise dependent events, which outcomes belong in the denominator, and why?

Teacher prompt: For simulate a counter-intuitive problem, which outcomes belong in the denominator, and why?

Use simulation for decisions

Supply and demand of product, insurance risk and queueing.

Teacher prompt: For use simulation for decisions, which outcomes belong in the denominator, and why?

Model infection risk with simulation

Use simulation to predict the number of people likely to be infected with a strain of flu or virus.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • The sample space or dependency changes without being noticed — Rebuild the outcomes and label replacement or conditioning explicitly.
  • The conditional probability simulations model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Use samples of different sizes with and without replacement from a population to identify when the difference in methods becomes negligible.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
