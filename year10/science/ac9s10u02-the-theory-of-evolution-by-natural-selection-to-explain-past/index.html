Natural Selection Explains Diversity

Heritable variation, selection pressure and differential reproduction change populations over generations.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

We are learning to explain natural selection explains diversity using models and evidence.

Success criteria

  • Identify and connect heritable variation, selection pressure, survival and reproduction.
  • Use a model to explain rather than only describe the concept.
  • Apply the central idea to a curriculum example and justify the connection.
  • Evaluate one model limit, misconception or evidence boundary.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • Populations contain heritable variation.
  • Selection pressures affect survival and reproductive success.
  • Fossils, anatomy, biogeography and molecular evidence support evolutionary theory.

Prerequisites

  • Read a labelled scientific diagram or data display.
  • Distinguish an observation from an explanation.
  • Use evidence to support a short scientific statement.

Do not overteach

  • Do not describe evolution as progress toward perfection.
  • Do not claim organisms change traits deliberately in response to need.
