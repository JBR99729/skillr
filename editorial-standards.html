Transparent publishing

Editorial standards and corrections

SkillrHub is an independent Australian education project. Individual contributors keep their personal identities private, so resources are published under the SkillrHub name. Trust is earned through transparent standards, clear scope, quality checks and an open correction process.

Report a correctionView site updates

Scope and alignment

What our curriculum claims mean

SkillrHub resources are designed around Australian Curriculum learning areas and year-level expectations. Curriculum codes and topic mappings are used to organise practice, but SkillrHub is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian Curriculum, Assessment and Reporting Authority (ACARA), any state curriculum authority or any school system.

References to overseas grades, standards or curricula are practical comparison guides only. They do not claim exact equivalence and should be checked against the learner’s local requirements.

Quality review

How resources are checked

🎯

Curriculum fit

Topic pages and question banks are checked against their stated year, subject and curriculum focus.

✅

Clarity and correctness

Questions, answers and explanations are reviewed for accuracy, age-appropriate wording and useful feedback.

🧪

Technical checks

Automated checks help detect missing content, broken pathways and structural problems before updates are published.

Use of digital tools

Tools assist; published content remains accountable

Digital and automated tools may assist with drafting, formatting, checking and maintaining resources. They do not remove the need for review. SkillrHub remains responsible for correcting published material when an error is identified.

Corrections

How to report an issue

If you find an incorrect answer, unclear explanation, broken link, accessibility problem or questionable curriculum mapping, email skillrhublearning@gmail.com. Include the page URL, curriculum code if shown, and a short explanation. Reports are assessed and material corrections are published as soon as practical.

Site-wide improvements and significant resource updates are documented on the Updates page.
