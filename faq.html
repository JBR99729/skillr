FAQ

Answers to common questions about using SkillrHub and supporting free learning resources.

How can I help support SkillrHub?

If you find the resources helpful, here are simple ways you can support SkillrHub:

  • Share the link: Tell friends, schools, tutoring networks and families about SkillrHub so more learners can find free educational resources.
  • Send us a testimonial: Email us a brief message describing how SkillrHub helped you, including which region you are from. We'd love to hear your story at skillrhublearning@gmail.com.
  • Spread the word: Recommend SkillrHub to educators, families and learning communities that may find the resources useful.

Why should I bookmark this site?

Bookmark SkillrHub for quick access to warm-up activities, topic guides, online practice and printable worksheets. Keeping the site handy makes it easier to return to useful resources for teaching, homework, revision and independent learning.
