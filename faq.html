FAQ

Answers to common questions about using Skillr Education and supporting free learning resources.

How can I help support Skillr Education?

If you find this resource helpful, here are simple ways you can support us:

  • Share the link: Tell your friends, schools, tutoring networks, relatives and social media followers about Skillr. Education is a basic right and everyone should have access to free quality resources.
  • Send us a testimonial: Email us a brief message describing how Skillr helped you, including which region you are from. We'd love to feature your story on the site at skillrhublearning@gmail.com.
  • Spread the word: Tag us on social media, recommend us in forums, or mention us in education blogs and newsletters.

Why should I bookmark this site?

Bookmark Skillr so it becomes part of your daily routine. Whether you need a quick warm-up activity, a topic guide or a printable worksheet, having Skillr bookmarked means you can return instantly without searching. Make free, quality education part of your everyday teaching and learning journey.
