Frequently asked questions

How can SkillrHub help your family?

Understand the topic. Practise the skill. Build confidence.

Built around Australian Curriculum V9, with explanations and available practice that families around the world can explore by topic and suitable learning level.

What does SkillrHub help my child with?

SkillrHub helps families turn “What should we practise?” into a clear next step. Find a topic your child is learning or finding difficult, read an explanation, then use available practice to review mistakes and strengthen the skill. It supports school learning, revision and learning at home, one skill at a time.

How does SkillrHub help Australian families?

Resources are organised around Australian Curriculum V9.0 topics and year levels, so parents can find explanations and practice for a particular skill without starting from scratch. Use them to reinforce school learning or support a home-education plan. Check the available resources for your chosen topic; local syllabus requirements and school teaching sequences can differ.

Can families outside Australia use SkillrHub?

Yes. Families in the US, UK, New Zealand, Canada, India and elsewhere can explore explanations and available practice by topic and suitable learning level. Start with what your child needs to learn, then check the examples and question difficulty. Australian year levels are not automatic equivalents to local grades, and complete coverage of overseas curricula is not claimed.

Where should we start?

Choose one topic your child is learning or would like to revisit. Read the topic guide and worked examples together, try available practice, then review mistakes before moving on. Keep sessions manageable and revisit a skill when needed. You can use topic guides on their own or add optional paper practice.

Are all year levels and subjects fully available?

The website has Maths, English and Science topic guides across Foundation–Year 10. Online practice and assessments are available for selected skills, with more content added progressively. A topic guide does not mean every matching online activity or printable pack is available; check the resource page or app for current options.

How does free practice work?

Topic guides are free to read without an account. Guests can try 20 practice questions each day in supported banks. Register and confirm your email for 500 free questions total across Maths, English and Science, including guest questions already used. An account saves online progress in separate learner profiles. Some banks, including available Year 6 banks, require a free account.

Do I need to buy a worksheet pack?

No. Free topic guides and the 500-question registration offer do not require a worksheet purchase. Printable packs are optional, separate purchases on Teachers Pay Teachers. Check each listing for its included resources and any free sample.

Can I save progress for more than one child?

Yes. An adult account lets you create and select separate learner profiles, so each child’s saved online work stays separate. Saved activity helps you review what has been practised and where another attempt may help.

Can we use SkillrHub for home education?

Yes. Use topic guides to help explain an idea, choose available practice or optional worksheets, and review the work together. Families choose the sequence and pace. SkillrHub can support your learning plan; check your state or territory’s requirements separately when planning and keeping home-education records.

How can teachers use SkillrHub?

Use topic guides, available classroom resources and focused practice to support explanations, revision or homework. Choose resources that match your lesson and learners. Optional printable packs are separately purchased, with contents and licence terms shown in each listing.
