What students learn in AC9S3I05

Students learn to compare findings with those of others, consider if investigations were fair, identify questions for further investigation and draw conclusions. They use the visual model, precise vocabulary and evidence to explain and apply the concept.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

The content description and elaborations below show the curriculum ideas taught in this unit. Items marked as teaching context support lesson planning.

  • Content description: compare findings with those of others, consider if investigations were fair, identify questions for further investigation and draw conclusions
  • E1: comparing findings, such as about best insulators, with those of others and identifying further questions based on differences in findings
  • E2: discussing the factors that make investigations fair and evaluating the fairness of their own and others’ investigations
  • E3: drawing conclusions based on consideration of their own and others’ findings
  • E4: identifying further questions for investigation based on observations, differences in findings or new ideas

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Different result means someone is wrong — Chance variation or method differences may explain it.
  • Feature named without effect — Explain how the feature changes meaning or outcome.
  • One example treated as a rule — Test the idea with another example.
