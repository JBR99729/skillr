What students learn in AC9S3I03

Students learn to follow procedures to make and record observations, including making formal measurements using familiar scaled instruments and using digital tools as appropriate. They use the visual model, precise vocabulary and evidence to explain and apply the concept.

Learning routine: Observe → Model → Investigate → Record → Explain

Success looks like

  • Recognise qualitative observation
  • Use quantitative observation
  • Explain scaled instrument
  • Apply in a new context
  • Justify with evidence

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

The content description and elaborations below show the curriculum ideas taught in this unit. Items marked as teaching context support lesson planning.

  • Content description: follow procedures to make and record observations, including making formal measurements using familiar scaled instruments and using digital tools as appropriate
  • E1: using appropriate equipment to make and record observations, such as digital cameras, video, voice recorders and scaled instruments with appropriate increments
  • E2: exploring how to use equipment such as thermometers or measuring cylinders and making readings with guidance
  • E3: collaboratively designing a table to collect observations in the form of numerical data, written descriptions, drawings or photos
  • E4: identifying and taking on roles in group work, such as setting up the equipment, making observations, recording observations and ensuring safe behaviours

Common misconceptions
  • Unit omitted — A number without its unit may be unclear.
  • Feature named without effect — Explain how the feature changes meaning or outcome.
  • One example treated as a rule — Test the idea with another example.
