AC9M3M03 • Year 3 Maths

Time Units and Duration

Use 60 seconds = 1 minute, 60 minutes = 1 hour and 24 hours = 1 day to estimate, convert and compare event durations.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

Students choose suitable time units, estimate duration, use fixed unit relationships and compare events after expressing durations in compatible units.

Worked examples and teaching sequence
  1. Choose seconds, minutes, hours or days.
  2. Estimate a reasonable duration.
  3. Use the known unit relationship to convert when needed.
  4. Compare durations in the same unit.

Example: 2 minutes = 120 seconds.

Common misconceptions and quick check
  • Treating time as base 10.
  • Comparing numbers while ignoring units.
  • Using an unsuitable unit for an event.

Quick check: Which is longer: 90 minutes or 2 hours?

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Contexts include timing classroom activities, estimating familiar events, converting between related formal units and justifying comparisons using unit relationships.
