Time Units and Duration

What students learn

Time units have fixed relationships: 60 seconds = 1 minute, 60 minutes = 1 hour and 24 hours = 1 day. Suitable units help compare event durations. This matters because use multiplication or division when changing between related units.

Choose the unit that makes the comparison clear. Use this idea when a problem asks you to compare event durations, and explain the result with duration, formal unit, convert.

Learning intention: We are learning to recognise and use the relationship between formal units of time including days, hours, minutes and seconds to estimate and compare the duration of events.

Success criteria

  • I can relate seconds and minutes.
  • I can relate minutes and hours.
  • I can relate hours and days.
Key vocabulary
  • duration — how long an event lasts
  • formal unit — a standard time unit such as second, minute, hour or day
  • convert — express the same duration using a different unit

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Using 100 minutes per hour — Time uses 60 minutes per hour.
  • Wrong unit for event — Seconds suit short events; days suit long events.
  • Comparing numbers without units — 2 hours is not shorter than 90 minutes.
Teaching sequence and quick check

Quick check: How many seconds in 2 minutes?

Expected response: 2 minutes equals 120 seconds.

If incorrect: Time uses 60 minutes per hour.
