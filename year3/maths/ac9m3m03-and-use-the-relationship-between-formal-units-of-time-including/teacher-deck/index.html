What Are Formal Units of Time?

Time units help us measure how long things take.

  • Days: long events
  • Hours: medium events
  • Minutes: short events
  • Seconds: very short events
  • 1 day = 24 hours
  • 1 hour = 60 minutes
  • 1 minute = 60 seconds

Why Do We Need Different Units?

We do not measure everything the same way.

  • A movie → hours
  • A game → minutes
  • A blink → seconds
  • A school week → days
  • Teacher prompt: Which unit makes sense?

Reading Digital Time
