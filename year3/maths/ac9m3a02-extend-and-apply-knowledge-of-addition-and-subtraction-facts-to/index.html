AC9M3A02: Efficient Mental Addition and Subtraction

Students choose efficient mental strategies for larger numbers by using known facts, place value, compensation, bridging and partitioning.

Topic guideTeacher SlidesWorksheetPracticeTest
What students learn in AC9M3A02

Students extend facts to 20 into larger-number calculations and explain why a strategy is efficient.

  • Use known facts to solve related facts with tens and hundreds.
  • Partition numbers by place value to add and subtract mentally.
  • Use compensation to adjust numbers and then correct the answer.
  • Choose a strategy that fits the numbers.
Key models and vocabulary
  • compensation — adjust a number to make a friendlier calculation, then correct
  • partition — split a number into useful parts
  • bridge — make the next ten or hundred before continuing
  • mental strategy — a planned way to calculate without a written algorithm
Teaching progression and worked examples
  1. Compensation: 68 + 29 = 68 + 30 − 1 = 97.
  2. Subtract near a ten: 143 − 58 = 143 − 60 + 2 = 85.
  3. Use related facts: 260 + 170 uses 26 + 17 = 43, so the answer is 430.

Teacher language: “What known fact helps? What did you adjust? How will you check?”

Curriculum coverage and elaborations
  • Content description: extend and apply knowledge of addition and subtraction facts to 20 to develop efficient mental strategies for computation with larger numbers without a calculator
  • E1: extending addition and subtraction facts to 20 to larger numbers
  • E2: using place value, partitioning, compensation and bridging strategies
  • E3: explaining why a chosen mental strategy is efficient

Common mistakes to watch for
  • Forgetting to correct after compensation: mark the adjustment and reverse it.
  • Partitioning by digits instead of value: 143 is 100 + 40 + 3, not 1 + 4 + 3.
  • Using one strategy every time: compare two methods and choose the efficient one.
Support, core and extend
  • Support: use number lines and base-ten blocks.
  • Core: solve mixed two- and three-digit mental calculations and explain the strategy.
  • Extend: create two solutions and justify which is more efficient.
Quick check and mastery evidence
  1. Solve 76 + 28 using compensation.
  2. Solve 132 − 49 using an efficient mental strategy and explain it.

Mastery means students can solve accurately, name the strategy and check the result.
