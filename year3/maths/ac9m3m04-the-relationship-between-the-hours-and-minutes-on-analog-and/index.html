AC9M3M04 • Year 3 Maths

Analog and Digital Time

Connect the 60 minutes in an hour with minute-hand movement, hour-hand position and matching digital time notation.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

Students read analog and digital clocks, connect 5-minute intervals to clock-face numbers and explain how the hour hand moves continuously as minutes pass.

Worked examples and teaching sequence
  1. Locate the hour hand between hour markers.
  2. Count minute intervals around the clock.
  3. State the analog time.
  4. Write the matching digital notation.

Example: A minute hand on 6 means 30 minutes past the hour.

Common misconceptions and quick check
  • Reading the hour hand as the next hour before it reaches it.
  • Treating each clock-face number as one minute.
  • Writing 3:5 instead of 3:05.

Quick check: Show 4:35 on an analog clock and explain both hands.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Teaching contexts include matching analog and digital displays, using 5-minute counting, describing minute/hour hand relationships and reading times in schedules and everyday routines.
