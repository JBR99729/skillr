Analog and Digital Time to the Minute

What students learn

The minute hand moves around 60 minute marks while the hour hand moves gradually between hours. Analog and digital clocks represent the same time in different ways. This matters because count minute marks from 12; the hour hand is slightly past 3.

Use two digits for minutes in digital time, such as 8:05. Use this idea when a problem asks you to match analog and digital representations, and explain the result with analog clock, digital time, minute hand.

Learning intention: We are learning to describe the relationship between the hours and minutes on analog and digital clocks, and read the time to the nearest minute.

Success criteria

  • I can read minute hand.
  • I can read hour hand.
  • I can read nearest minute.
Key vocabulary
  • analog clock — a clock that shows time with hands on a dial
  • digital time — time written using digits separated by a colon
  • minute hand — the longer hand that shows minutes past the hour

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Counting only by fives — Use individual minute marks for exact times.
  • Hour hand read as next hour — The hour hand stays between the current and next hour.
  • Missing digital zero — Write 7:04, not 7:4.
Teaching sequence and quick check

Quick check: Read 3:27.

Expected response: Five minutes past eight is 8:05.

If incorrect: Use individual minute marks for exact times.
