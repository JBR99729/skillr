AC9M3M02 • Year 3 Maths

Measuring with Metric Instruments

Measure and compare objects accurately by choosing an instrument, starting at the correct point, reading labelled intervals and recording the unit.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

Students measure length, mass and capacity with rulers, scales and measuring containers. They identify interval values, read scales carefully and compare measurements expressed in the same unit.

Worked examples and teaching sequence
  1. Choose the correct instrument and unit.
  2. Locate zero or the starting reference.
  3. Determine the value of each interval.
  4. Read the endpoint, pointer or liquid level.
  5. Record the value with its unit and compare.

Example: A ruler endpoint at 13 cm represents a length of 13 cm when the object starts at zero.

Common misconceptions and quick check
  • Starting at the ruler edge instead of zero.
  • Counting marks rather than intervals.
  • Omitting units.

Quick check: Explain how to read a scale whose labelled marks increase by 200 mL.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Teaching contexts include measuring familiar classroom objects, reading graduated instruments, comparing results, estimating before measuring and explaining why a recorded measurement is reasonable.
