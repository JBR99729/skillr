Natural Numbers and Place Value

Read, represent, compare and order natural numbers by using place value, number names, numerals and number-line position.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

Students connect spoken names, written numerals, expanded representations and positions on number lines. They compare digits from the greatest place value and explain ordering decisions.

Worked examples and teaching sequence
  1. Build a number with place-value materials.
  2. Write it in numeral, word and expanded form.
  3. Locate it relative to benchmark numbers.
  4. Compare from the highest place value.
Common misconceptions and quick check
  • Ignoring zeros as place holders.
  • Comparing only the last digit.
  • Confusing number name order.

Quick check: Order 4,208; 4,028; 4,280 and justify the first comparison.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Contexts include number lines, place-value charts, regroupable materials, reading and writing numbers, and ordering quantities from familiar real-world collections.
