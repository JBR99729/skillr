What students learn in AC9E3LA05

This unit helps students build a clear, usable understanding of Identify the purpose of layout features in print and digital texts and the words used for navigation. The goal is not to memorise one answer pattern. Students should be able to explain the idea, recognise it in a new example and apply it in a short practice or worksheet task.

  • Start with the main idea: identify the purpose of layout features in print and digital texts and the words used for navigation.
  • Use concrete examples, pictures, oral explanation and short written responses before moving to independent practice.
  • examining the words used as hyperlinks discussing words used as headings and subheadings in digital and print information texts
  • Finish with a mixed activity so students must choose the correct strategy rather than copy the last example.
