## Research basis

The reauthoring used representative Year 4 IXL examples supplied during the research session as a depth benchmark for:

- inferring word meaning from context
- selecting exact evidence that supports an inferred meaning
- base words and affixes
- prefixes `re-`, `pre-`, `mis-`
- suffixes `-ful`, `-less`, `-ly`, `-ness`, `-able`, `-ment`
- applying morphology in sentence context
- remediation pattern: rule → simple example → apply to target word → reread/check

The curriculum requirement for accurate/fluent reading, rereading and self-correction was treated as the controlling target, so the authored resources also explicitly teach print/sound checking, grammatical monitoring, text-structure monitoring, fluency and self-correction.

No IXL passage, question, answer set or explanation was copied. SkillrHub examples and stimuli are newly authored.
