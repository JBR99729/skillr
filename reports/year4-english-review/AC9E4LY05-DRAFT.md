## Authoritative scope
AC9E4LY05: use comprehension strategies when listening, viewing and reading to build literal and inferred meaning, expand content knowledge, integrate/link ideas and understand texts.

Elaboration coverage used in this draft:

- E3 main and supporting ideas
- E4 evidence and reasoning supporting points/arguments
- E5 self-monitoring and self-correction: rereading, questioning, pausing, confirming and cross-checking

The previous static page listed E6/E7; those are not treated as AC9E4LY05 elaborations in this draft.

## IXL research supplied by owner
Primary observed Year 4 IXL Australia references:
- H.3 / 6J3 — Draw inferences from a text
- H.4 / HEQ — Make predictions about a story
- A.1 / 7SD — Use key details to determine the main idea

Observed design principles carried forward without copying IXL wording:
- inference: textual clue → what it suggests → supported conclusion
- prediction: story evidence/current intention → most likely next event
- main idea: cover all important details; reject too-narrow, unrelated and unsupported-new-information distractors
- incorrect-answer feedback should reconnect an exact clue to the reasoning, not merely say “look again”

Rules for the bank:
- no stem-only shuffled duplicates
- four plausible options where MCQ is used
- exact clue/evidence reasoning in explanations
- supported inference distinguished from merely possible guesses
- main-idea distractors include too narrow, too broad/off-target and unsupported new information
- argument questions separate claim, evidence, reasoning and evidence strength
- self-monitoring items require noticing, rereading/questioning/cross-checking and correction
- fresh contexts in Test; no Practice passage reused in Test
- no IXL item wording copied
