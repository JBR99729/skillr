Eight written response tasks

Read, write or dictate your own responses. Share when asked. An adult checks the completed comparisons or explanations using the answer guidance in the printable worksheet.

  1. Read: “A broad-shouldered sailor with a rain-soaked hat smiled at the child.” Write an opinion of this description. Identify one noun group and explain the picture it creates.

  2. Read: “The classroom was a beehive before the concert.” Explain the metaphor and give your opinion of its effect.

  3. Read: “Once more we try the knot. / Once more we pull it tight. / Once more—and now it holds!” Explain the repeated phrase and share your response.

  4. Read: “Today Freya led the team onto the court. Last season she had been too shy to join a game.” Explain the flashback and say how it affects your opinion of Freya’s present action.

  5. Read: “A footstep sounded outside. I held the lantern still. Then Gran called, ‘It is only me.’” Explain how tension builds and is released.

  6. Compare “The storm hammered the roof” with “The rain tapped softly on the roof”. Which would you choose for a peaceful scene? Explain how the words affect your choice.

  7. Read: “The evening stretched like a slow, sleepy cat.” Share your opinion of the simile with an adult or partner. Write their response and one point of agreement or difference.

  8. Write a short response to this ending: “After searching all day, Theo found the missing sketchbook under his coat. He laughed, then opened to a clean page.” Explain the resolution and whether you find it effective. Suggest one small wording change and explain its effect.
