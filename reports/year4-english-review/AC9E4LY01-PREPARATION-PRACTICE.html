Before you practise: compare dated accounts

Read both supplied texts. Identify their dates, purpose and audience, then compare the same feature: event details, starting emphasis, feelings or ways of showing the event. Support a similarity and difference with clues from A and B.

The bank’s labelled fictional extracts are original teaching examples, not historical archives. A transcript or described photo is not the original audio or image. Some tasks ask for actual written comparisons; an adult checks your response.
