## Specific content direction

### AC9E4LY02
Listening instructions, useful notes, clarification/follow-up questions, seeking help when required, acknowledging a contribution, linking responses to topic and extending another idea. Actual discussion/listening tasks are adult-reviewed.

### AC9E4LY03
Purpose-linked features of imaginative, informative and persuasive texts: imaginary-world language, headings/bold/italics/navigation, informative images/layout, persuasion and evidence of purpose. Original microtexts replace generic feature spotting.

### AC9E4LY04
Word identification through word parts, sound/meaning/grammar cross-checking, topic and text-structure knowledge, rereading, self-correction, accuracy, phrasing and expression. Sixteen Practice and eight Test tasks require actual reading aloud to an adult.

### AC9E4LY05
Literal and inferred meaning, main/supporting ideas, print/image/sound connections, topic vocabulary, prediction, summarising, monitoring, evidence/reasoning and evaluation of arguments. The First Nations elaboration is handled through actual source-aware reading rather than invented cultural meaning.

### AC9E4LY06
Research/source responsibility, paragraph planning, imaginative/informative/persuasive structure, precise/topic vocabulary, verb/noun/adverb groups, complex sentences, tense, multisyllabic spelling, punctuation, cohesion, multimodal planning and genuine drafting/editing.

### AC9E4LY07
Report/story/recount/argument structure, reliable support for factual claims, audience-appropriate vocabulary, subjective/objective language, visuals, tone, pace, pitch, volume, real rehearsal, genuine peer feedback and authentic oral/multimodal delivery.
