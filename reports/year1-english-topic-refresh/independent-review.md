## Corrections requested from author

1. **LA03, preserved See-it model, Topic and Classroom:** replace `What features prove it?` with `What features give clues?`. The old absolute conflicts with the new accurate warning that headings/time words/pictures are clues, not proof of a text type.
2. **LA01, preserved Explain-and-apply repair answer, Topic and Classroom:** replace the compulsory calm voice plus open-hand gesture criterion with a clear respectful request using speech/signing/AAC and an optional appropriate gesture. This must agree with the new access guidance and should judge clarity of the message/response, not a fixed bodily performance.

Local cultural limitation: LA01 lists the First Nations elaboration and gives appropriate community-endorsed preparation guidance, but the supplement itself does not provide an actual named local cultural teaching example. Do not claim a fully observed cultural lesson or watched short-film activity. The site remains a teacher-led guide at that point; actual local guidance and a suitable scene must be selected before classroom delivery.
