## LY09

The bank uses 16 MCQs and 48 adult-observed reading-and-writing tasks. The lexical range includes compounds, consonant-l-e, open/closed syllable patterns, vowel teams, consonant clusters and longer words. The adult tasks require oral reading, segmentation/blending and an actual written sentence, checked against the printed targets. Natural accent differences and reasonable time to blend are accepted.

Corrected findings:

- P001 originally asked which option retained two b letters; three options met that condition. The question now asks for the conventional animal spelling.
- P005 originally represented a bedroom pronunciation as bed-rum. This was replaced with an unambiguous omitted-word-part task. Cambridge lists both /ruːm/ and /rʊm/ for room: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/room . Written pseudo-pronunciation was unsuitable for rejecting a natural accent.
- P012 incorrectly claimed ee occurred in both green and house. It now asks which option preserves both printed parts; the explanation correctly distinguishes ee and ou.
- P021 now distinguishes the written groups ngl and ndl without suggesting an inaccurate spoken split for jungle.

Limitation: these are supported reading-and-writing tasks with printed word checking. They do not establish unseen spelling or dictation mastery. Repeated activity instructions accompany different substantive lexical targets; this is not eight target prompts recycled to inflate the count.

## LY10

All 64 original MCQs were read. After the requested addition of actual word formation, all 16 converted adult tasks were read again. The final intended mix is 48 MCQs and 16 formed-word, sentence and meaning-explanation tasks.

Corrected findings:

- P023, P027 and T011 now specify changing only final y after a consonant, rather than wording that could tell a learner to delete the consonant as well.
- P020 explains careful as taking care; P047 consistently explains enjoyable as able to be enjoyed.
- P034 explicitly says boxes look identical, avoiding an apparent contradiction with their different weights.
- Converted P002 no longer falsely says precooked has two c letters at the prefix boundary.
- Converted P011 no longer describes an n/n boundary in kindness; it now preserves the whole base kind before ness.
- Adult marking criteria require a formed word and sentence plus the requested oral or written explanation. Completion ticks alone are insufficient.

Limitation: affixes and bases are supplied for the formation tasks. This supports word formation and meaningful use; it is not unseen dictation. Final response instructions were rechecked and now consistently require the formed word, sentence and requested explanation.

## LY11

All 64 questions were checked, including 48 written spelling-and-pattern responses. Less-common patterns include silent initial/final letters, ph, gh with different jobs, vowel teams and consonant endings. Word-length clues and sentence fit were inspected.

Corrected findings:

- P026 no longer states that bees collect honey from flowers; the sentence now concerns a beekeeper obtaining honey from a hive.
- T008 now has people carrying a boat across the beach, rather than a boat crossing the beach unaided.
- P047 no longer supplies od plus our, which revealed the complete target spelling.
- Generic instructions no longer reveal the target whole in P039.
- P004 explicitly asks for a correctly spelt correction, excluding a distractor that restored initial k but added another error.
- Test clues for fleece, yacht, gnome, knock and thorn were reduced so they do not supply the principal unusual letter groups.
- Completion wording permits an actual typed or paper response consistently.

Limitation: clues sometimes supply partial spelling or a rhyme. These are scaffolded production tasks, not independent dictation or independent recall of every named pattern. Adult review must inspect the actual word and pattern explanation; uncertainty about vocabulary is not automatically a spelling failure.

## LY12

Corrective checks included target visibility in the question, audio prompt, response instructions and completion label. The final draft has no complete target-word matches in those pre-response fields for its 48 production tasks.

Corrected findings:

- P032 now reads the socks are the ___, making the target same grammatical.
- P039 no longer reveals four inside four-letter.
- P043 no longer reveals write in the generic task instruction.
- Shared instructions no longer reveal the targets before, here or its in P024, P026 and P041.
- P017 no longer misdescribes the stressed middle of another as unstressed.
- P019 avoids a needless always/today exception; P036 qualifies the ate/eight pronunciation clue for accent variation.

Limitation: these tasks assess writing from contextual and partial clues, not unseen dictation. A copied model is supported practice and cannot establish independent spelling. Actual written responses remain subject to adult inspection.
