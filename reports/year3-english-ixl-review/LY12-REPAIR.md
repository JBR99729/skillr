## Authority and actual comparison

ACARA v9 requires recognition and knowledge of writing high-frequency words, including some homophones. Official Year 3 column, QCAA page 6: https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/aciqv9/english/curriculum/ac9_english_p-6_cd_sequence.pdf . Shared research log refreshed to v17 for the research cycle; details are preserved in `LY05-LY12-RESEARCH.md`.

All four actual IXL Year 3 mappings had full worked help and actual entry inspection, in Parent mode with results explicitly not recorded:

- https://au.ixl.com/english/year-3/spell-the-sight-word — letter-box spelling production. Actual entry audio-only; no audio listened to or target inferred, so actual word range/progression is unobserved.
- https://au.ixl.com/english/year-3/homophones-with-pictures — meaningful picture-to-spelling choice. Worked picture and two actual entry pictures visually checked. One correct submission, counter 1/10; next numeral picture inspected only.
- https://au.ixl.com/english/year-3/identify-homophones — choose a word within a continuous sentence that matches the target sound. One correct submission, counter 1/10, followed by a different pair inspected only.
- https://au.ixl.com/english/year-3/use-the-correct-homophone — select a spelling according to sentence meaning. One correct submission and next different contextual pair inspected. No later mastery claim.

These observations distinguish recognition from spelling production. The replacement includes both; it does not treat a two-choice answer as evidence of independent spelling.

## Bank and coverage

64 original items: 48 Practice + 16 Test; all 56 existing IDs retained plus eight Practice IDs. There are 16 recognition/context MCQs (12 / 4) and 48 written spelling tasks (36 / 12). Replaces generic strategy questions with specific original contexts, distinct words or homophone uses, and prompt-specific explanations.

| Component | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| High-frequency recognition | come, under, wonder, open, after, already; direct word and sentence checks rather than generic advice |
| High-frequency production | could/would/should, another/every/always, people/yours/beside/before, warm/behind/next/above/same/stop/close, from/into/were/when/because/show and other familiar words |
| Contextual homophones | their/there/they're, two/too/to, knew/new, whole/hole, hear/here, see/sea, four/for, its/it's, write/right, threw/through, tail/tale, pair/pear, buy/bye, one/won, ate/eight; contextual read/red recognition |
| Predictable and less predictable spelling | Explanations name useful patterns and unusual letter sequences, rather than calling every high-frequency word wholly irregular |
| Writing | Learner must supply the actual word. Adult checks spelling and meaning before marking; copying a model is practice, not independent evidence. |

The word set samples important high-frequency and familiar words. It is not an exhaustive frequency inventory and does not prove a learner has mastered “most” such words from a single session. Meaning, initial/final letters and sometimes length scaffold production; this is not unseen oral dictation. Each Test sentence is newly authored. No answer target is printed in its own production question or shared response instruction, including the easily leaked targets four and write.
