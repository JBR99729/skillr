## Replacement and coverage

The previous bank repeated generic questions about a displayed cue word and spelling strategies. Replacement: 64 original tasks, 48 Practice + 16 Test. Retains all 56 existing IDs and adds eight Practice IDs. There are 16 applied MCQs (12 Practice / 4 Test) and 48 actual written spelling responses (36 / 12), not 64 versions of eight underlying questions.

| Component | Representative coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Less-common consonant spellings | ph in phone/photo/elephant/trophy; tch in sketch/patch/kitchen; dge in bridge/edge; gh pronounced f in laugh/cough |
| Silent letters and contrasting patterns | kn in knife/knot/knee/knight/knock; wr in wring/wrench/wrist/written; mb in lamb/thumb/plumber; gn in gnome; mn in autumn; silent letters in sword/yacht/chalk |
| Vowel spelling variation | ea contrasts bread/bead, feather/heavy versus beach; igh and eigh; ey endings; our in flour/odour; ai/oi/ow/ue; selected consonant-l-e and unstressed endings |
| Meaning and spelling together | Homophonous spellings such as knight/night, whole/hole and flour/flower; a context must select the intended written word |
| Production and checking | Learner supplies the missing word and identifies a relevant spelling feature. Adult checks the actual response, not a completion claim. |

Production is **clue-scaffolded spelling**, not independent dictation. Some Practice prompts supply a beginning, ending or length; those supplied parts cannot be counted as independently recalled. Test prompts reduce several such clues, while retaining enough meaning to identify the intended word. No full target is printed in its production prompt. This is a finite sample of patterns, not proof that a learner can spell every word with those patterns.

## Editorial review and response handling

Author read every prompt, key and explanation. A separate agent read all 64 and root also reviewed all prompts/keys. Corrections include replacing false bee-collects-honey wording with a beekeeper and hive; carrying a boat across a beach; avoiding fully supplying odour by concatenated chunks; tightening the knife correction; and removing generic wording that revealed the target whole. No IXL question or illustration is copied into the bank.

Adult responses use the existing helper and remain pending until reviewed. Model answers identify the exact target and spelling feature. Alternative sensible vocabulary should prompt clarification, not a false claim of a spelling error. Capitalisation is accepted; odour explicitly targets Australian spelling. Accent differences are respected, especially wh and chalk. No fixed speech speed, audio recording or new runtime feature is introduced. Model answers must remain hidden until Practice response or Test review under the existing adult-review flow.

Validation: production validator passes 64 unique IDs and 48/16 split; MCQ positions 3/3/3/3 in Practice and 1/1/1/1 in Test. All 56 previous IDs retained; all 64 full prompts distinct. Numeric letter-count clues were checked against targets. These structural checks supplement, not replace, editorial review.
