The official QCAA text extraction was read and checked against the actual IXL v9 alignment plan. The following is a coverage paraphrase; source URLs provide the exact official wording.

- LA06: clauses as grammatical units, usually with an agreeing subject and verb. Preserve “usually”; clauses are not synonymous with complete sentences.
- LA07: verb meanings for doing, feeling, thinking, saying and relating. Action verbs alone do not close coverage.
- LA08: tense locates verbs in time. Include past, present and future, with familiar regular and irregular forms.
- LA09: identify information and meaning added by images to accompanying text. Actual useful images are required, not descriptions pretending to be images.
- LA10: expand topic/technical vocabulary and distinguish meanings across contexts.
- LA11: apostrophes for omitted letters and for both singular and plural possession.
- LE01: discuss character, event and setting across contexts in literature by First Nations Australian, wider Australian and world creators. Original generic fiction cannot by itself meet the creator-range component.
- LE02: connect personal and character experiences and share preferences. A marked single-answer choice cannot substitute for a learner's own response.
- LE03: explore how language and illustrations portray characters/settings and how setting/events affect narrative mood. Include actual illustrations and supported interpretation.
- LE04: effects of literary devices in poetry and prose, explicitly rhythm and onomatopoeia. Naming devices alone is insufficient.
