## Candidate coverage

64 tasks: 48 Practice and 16 Test. All 56 existing IDs retained, with eight extra Practice IDs. Twelve Practice MCQs and four Test MCQs use explicit written observations, not unseen images. They identify specific issues with formation, relative size, joining, spacing and evidentiary limits. Answer positions balance 3/3/3/3 and 1/1/1/1. These supplementary decisions cannot establish handwriting competence.

The other 48 tasks require real pen/pencil writing on paper, an adult viewing the sample, and comparison/revision where needed. Thirty-six Practice samples and twelve Test samples vary the actual letter combinations: repeated small shapes, m/n distinction, round forms, tall forms, descenders, model-specific s/f/t/x/z treatment, doubled letters, longer words and short labels/phrases. Each task names the words and a concrete feature for inspection. Repeated formation practice is purposeful motor practice with different letter demands, not a claim that changing only a topic title creates new literary content.

The adult evaluates joins, recognisability and consistent relative size in the actual sample. A typed copy or checkbox cannot substitute. Comfortable pace is supported; no arbitrary speed, decorative style or single grip is graded. Learners using alternative transcription accommodations still need their normal educational support; this narrowly scoped bank does not relabel typed transcription as handwriting evidence.
