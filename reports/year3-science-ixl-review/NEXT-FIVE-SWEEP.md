### Use data to describe world climates
URL: https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/use-data-to-describe-world-climates

One correct response, then deliberate incorrect response for actual Remember/Solve help. Help explains reading axes/scales, comparing graph heights and checking each statement against both graphs. H01 transfer: scientists organise measurements and use patterns to support descriptions. Seasonal/climate taxonomy is not adopted as core content. Stage 1 of 2 only; Stage 2 unobserved.

### Solve problems using magnets
URL: https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/solve-problems-using-magnets
Three linked Stage 1 tasks inspected: read a crane-sorting diagram; identify material properties from the observation table; predict which new objects can be sorted. Two correct responses then an incorrect response opened Review/Solve worked help. Help explicitly transfers observed material behaviour to new objects to solve the sorting need. H02/I01 transfer is science-based practical choice and evidence-based prediction, not memorising new magnetism facts. Stage 1 of 3 only; later stages unobserved.

### Evaluate multiple design solutions to prevent erosion
URL: https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/evaluate-multiple-design-solutions-to-prevent-erosion
Eight tasks inspected: four Stage 1 questions identifying the dirty-pond need, sediment source, rain transport and erosion; four Stage 2 tasks choosing a relevant solution goal, roots holding soil, placement between source and pond, and before/after pond observations. Seven correct responses; normal incomplete-answer submission opened actual Review/Solve help on the eighth. Correct-answer explanatory panels on erosion, engineering and planting were also read. Help distinguishes improvement from complete removal of the problem. H02 transfer: need→scientific explanation→placement/design→observed evaluation. I02 inference: use a provided sequence and compare outcomes; this sequence is not itself evidence of a controlled fair test. Stage 2 of 3 inspected, Stage 3 and later competing-design evaluation unobserved.

### Evaluate multiple design solutions to prevent flooding
URL: https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/evaluate-multiple-design-solutions-to-prevent-flooding
Three Stage 1 tasks inspected: conditions causing flooding, consequences for a building, and several solutions acting at different points in the water pathway. Two correct responses followed by partial selection to inspect actual Solve/Explore help. Help connects uphill relocation, river barriers and door gaps to how water reaches a building; supporting raised-house example. H02 transfer: one explanation can support several practical responses. Stage 1 of 5 only; later design evaluation stages unobserved. Do not import emergency instructions or claim this represents fair-test instruction.

### Use climate data to make predictions
URL: https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/use-climate-data-to-make-predictions
Two Stage 1 tasks inspected: identify lowest monthly bars in a Victoria precipitation graph, then identify a seasonal pattern in Vienna. One correct response then incorrect selection opened actual Remember/Solve/Explore help. Help compares bar heights and groups months into observed wetter/drier periods. This directly supports pattern reading; future-prediction progression on this page remains unobserved. I01 prediction evidence also reuses the directly observed prior heat-prediction skills. Stage 1 of 3 only; stages 2–3 unobserved.

### Collect and graph temperature data
URL: https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/collect-and-graph-temperature-data
Two linked Stage 1 tasks inspected: read three 1-degree thermometer scales and enter 26, 27, 27 degrees into corresponding time rows; transfer those observations to graph points. First response correct; empty graph submitted through normal incomplete-answer dialog to inspect Remember/Solve help. Help explains time/temperature axes, each time-value pairing, and flat versus falling segments. I03 transfer: follow measurement procedure and preserve matching time, reading and unit when recording. Formal graph construction is supporting context, not a substitute for I03 scaled-instrument tasks. Stage 1 of 2 only; Stage 2 unobserved. Prior first-four thermometer evidence includes Stage 2 scale reading and worked help.

### Choose units of time / Choose units of distance
URLs: https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/choose-units-of-time and https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/choose-units-of-distance
Each inspected through two Stage 1 contexts plus actual Remember/Solve help (one correct response then a deliberate incorrect response). Time: bathtub drainage seconds/minutes, scarf knitting minutes/hours. Distance: bench metres/kilometres, river centimetres/kilometres. Help makes number-plus-unit meaning explicit and compares unit sizes using familiar references. I03 transfer: select an appropriate unit and record it. Original items use investigation contexts rather than culturally variable everyday speed estimates. Both Stage 1 of 2 only; later stages unobserved.

### Abbreviate time and length units / Abbreviate mass and volume units
URLs: https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/abbreviate-time-and-length-units and https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/abbreviate-mass-and-volume-units
Two Stage 1 tasks per page plus actual Remember/Solve help. Time: hours then minutes; help lists s/sec, min, h/hr and retains number before unit. Mass: grams then milligrams; help lists mg, g, kg. One correct response then a deliberate notation error on each page. I03 transfer: use conventional unit labels in records, not advanced conversion. Length progression on first page and volume progression on second remain unobserved; do not claim otherwise. Stages observed: 1 of 3 and 1 of 2 respectively.
