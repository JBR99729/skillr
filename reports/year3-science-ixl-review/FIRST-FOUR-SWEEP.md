# Year 3 Science first-four strict sweep

Started 7 September 2026. ACARA v9 is the alignment authority. IXL observations below come from the signed-in Year 3 skill pages, not the catalogue. These IXL science skills use staged “Get started” questions and did not provide a “Learn with an example” panel; that absence is recorded rather than replaced or claimed.

## AC9S3U01 — BLOCKED: inspection in progress

Official descriptor: “compare characteristics of living and non-living things and examine the differences between the life cycles of plants and animals”.

Components: living/non-living characteristics; compare living and non-living; stages and order in animal cycles; stages and order in plant cycles; compare plant and animal cycles; examine similarities and differences.

Directly inspected live Stage 1 questions:

- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/identify-living-and-non-living-things` — select the living item from photographed examples.
- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/read-animal-life-cycle-diagrams` — identify the stage immediately after a named stage.
- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/construct-animal-life-cycle-diagrams` — place missing stages in a horse cycle.
- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/compare-stages-of-an-animals-life-cycle` — sort traits into tadpole-only, both and egg-only columns.
- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/compare-different-animals-life-cycles` — compare horse and lion events, with distractors that belong to neither.
- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/read-and-construct-flowering-plant-life-cycle-diagrams` — identify the stage before fruit production.
- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/how-do-flowering-plants-make-new-plants` — identify that seeds grow into new plants.

No drafting until representative later stages and the final coverage allocation are recorded.

## AC9S3U02 — BLOCKED: inspection in progress

Official descriptor: “compare the observable properties of soils, rocks and minerals and investigate why they are important Earth resources”.

Components: observe and compare soils; observe and compare rocks; observe and compare minerals; distinguish using observable evidence; investigate uses and importance as Earth resources.

Directly inspected live Stage 1 questions:

- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/classify-rocks-and-minerals-by-color-shape-and-texture` — select a photographed rock using relative colour.
- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/identify-minerals-using-properties` — distinguish a manufactured, non-pure foam from naturally formed pure minerals.
- `https://au.ixl.com/science/year-3/identify-rocks-using-properties` — distinguish a human-made brick from naturally formed rocks.

IXL’s listed skills do not yet cover soil properties or the importance/use of Earth resources; those remain ACARA-driven gaps. No drafting until later-stage inspection and coverage allocation are complete.
