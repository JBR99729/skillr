M01 scale questions must distinguish intervals from marks and state the resolution and unit. Clock estimates need a sensible tolerance; their drawings must put the hour hand between numerals when minutes have elapsed. M02 perimeter follows boundary lengths; area counts coverage. Approximate part-square results must admit defensible alternatives. A drawing printed on an arbitrarily scaled screen or PDF cannot support a claimed physical centimetre measurement without a stated calibration.

M03 questions must make the day and any midnight transition clear. A cultural account must name its public source/community and teach the particular account without presenting one community's knowledge as universal. M04 must indicate the intended opening or turn, especially for reflex angles. Angle size is independent of arm length and page orientation. Formal protractor measurement and angle-sum theorems are not added as core requirements. A spirit level's level/plumb check and a set square's right-angle comparison have different purposes.

Construction and investigation elaborations need actual doing tasks, models and review criteria across the code's resources. Multiple-choice recognition and a structural coverage tag alone do not satisfy them. The separate per-code independent reports must identify and recheck the final banks, all visuals, Topic/Classroom teaching, written homework and rendered production PDFs before content approval.
