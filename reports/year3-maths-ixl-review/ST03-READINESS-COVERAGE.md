# AC9M3ST03 readiness and coverage map

Official ACARA v9 descriptor: “conduct guided statistical investigations involving the collection, representation and interpretation of data for categorical and discrete numerical variables with respect to questions of interest”. Source: official ACARA v9 workbook, Learning areas sheet.

Components: conduct a guided investigation; pose/use a question of interest; collect, represent and interpret data; include categorical and discrete numerical variables.

Direct IXL observations: [Interpret tally charts](https://au.ixl.com/maths/year-3/interpret-tally-charts) used grouped tallies and totals; [Interpret data in tables](https://au.ixl.com/maths/year-3/interpret-data-tables) used contextual totals and comparisons; the four Year 3 graph pages recorded in ST02 demonstrated representation and interpretation. Higher adaptive stages were not completed. IXL did not directly demonstrate planning or conducting a complete guided investigation.

Existing gap: 24/16 legacy bank with limited whole-investigation sequencing and variable-type coverage. Planned original 48/16 coverage: questions, fair collection, categorical/discrete variables, recording, representation, interpretation, conclusions and limitations. **READY FOR DRAFTING.**
