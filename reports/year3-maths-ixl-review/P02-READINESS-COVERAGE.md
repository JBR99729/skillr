# AC9M3P02 readiness and coverage map

Official ACARA v9 descriptor: “conduct repeated chance experiments; identify and describe possible outcomes, record the results, recognise and discuss the variation”. Source: official ACARA v9 workbook, Learning areas sheet.

Components: conduct repeated experiments; identify possible outcomes; record results; recognise variation between trials; discuss variation without assuming short-run equality.

Direct IXL observations: the two Year 3 probability pages inspected for P01 established outcome-based likelihood language and representations. The Year 3 catalogue contained no repeated-experiment skill, and targeted search found none; repeated trials, recording and variation are therefore ACARA-driven gaps, not claimed as IXL observations. Higher adaptive stages were not completed.

Existing gap: 24/16 legacy bank, three options and insufficient experiment-design/variation depth. Planned original 48/16 coverage: coins, dice, spinners and bags; tally/frequency records; totals and missing counts; compare repeated trials; explain natural variation; improve experimental reliability. **READY FOR DRAFTING.**
