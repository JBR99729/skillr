# AC9M3SP01 strict readiness and coverage map

Date: 2026-09-07

## Official curriculum

Australian Curriculum v9 descriptor: **make, compare and classify objects, identifying key features and explaining why these features make them suited to their uses**.

Official source: Australian Curriculum v9 curriculum workbook, Learning areas sheet, AC9M3SP01, at <https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/content/dam/en/curriculum/ac-version-9/downloads/curriculum-workbook.xlsx>.

Analyst decomposition: make objects; compare objects; classify objects using stated features; identify key flat/curved surfaces, faces, edges and vertices; connect features to fitness for a use.
