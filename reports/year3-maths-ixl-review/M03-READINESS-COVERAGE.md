## Official scope

Exact ACARA descriptor: “recognise and use the relationship between formal units of time including days, hours, minutes and seconds to estimate and compare the duration of events”. [Official ACARA workbook](https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/content/dam/en/curriculum/ac-version-9/downloads/curriculum-workbook.xlsx), Learning areas sheet; exact extraction/hash in official-curriculum-components.json.

Components (analyst decomposition): C1 recognise time-unit relationships, including day/hour, hour/minute and minute/second; C2 use those relationships; C3 estimate event duration; C4 compare event duration. Official elaborations were also read directly from workbook rows AC9M3M03_E1–E5: estimate reading time, plan sequences of activities using estimates, use device timers, check short estimates by timing, and explore cultural time cycles. Elaborations are illustrations rather than additional mandatory descriptors. No cultural scenario will be invented as a token context.
