# AC9M3M01 Choosing Metric Units and Estimating

## ACARA descriptor
identify which metric units are used to measure everyday items; use measurements of familiar items and known units to make estimates

## IXL benchmark signals used
- Estimate lengths with centimetres and metres
- Which metric unit of length is appropriate?
- Which metric unit of mass is appropriate?
- Which metric unit of volume is appropriate?

## Coverage map
- choose length unit
- choose mass unit
- choose capacity unit
- estimate with benchmark

## Additional free-resource benchmark
- Vedantu free Class 3 measurement worksheets: printable practice, conversions, word problems and answer-key style review were used as a broad variety benchmark. Content remains SkillrHub-original and Australian Curriculum-aligned.
