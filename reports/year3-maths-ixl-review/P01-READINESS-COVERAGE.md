# AC9M3P01 readiness and coverage map

Official ACARA v9 descriptor: “identify practical activities and everyday events involving chance; describe possible outcomes and events as ‘likely’ or ‘unlikely’ and identify some events as ‘certain’ or ‘impossible’ explaining reasoning”. Source: official ACARA v9 workbook, Learning areas sheet.

Components: recognise chance activities/events; describe possible outcomes; classify likely/unlikely/certain/impossible; explain using the possible outcomes.

Direct IXL observations: [More, less and equally likely](https://au.ixl.com/maths/year-3/more-less-and-equally-likely) compared spinner regions, including equal likelihood; [Certain, probable, unlikely and impossible](https://au.ixl.com/maths/year-3/certain-probable-unlikely-and-impossible) classified a marble outcome and explained impossibility from no matching marbles. Higher adaptive stages were not completed.

Existing gap: 24/16 legacy bank, three options and narrow contexts. Planned original 48/16 coverage: spinners, bags, dice and everyday events; complete outcome sets; classifications; comparisons; misconception-rich explanations. **READY FOR DRAFTING.**
