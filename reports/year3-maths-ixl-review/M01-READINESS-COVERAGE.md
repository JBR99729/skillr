# AC9M3M01 strict readiness and coverage map

Date: 2026-09-07

## Official curriculum

Australian Curriculum v9 descriptor: **identify which metric units are used to measure everyday items; use measurements of familiar items and known units to make estimates**.

Official source: Australian Curriculum v9 curriculum workbook, Learning areas sheet, AC9M3M01, published at <https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/content/dam/en/curriculum/ac-version-9/downloads/curriculum-workbook.xlsx>.

Analyst decomposition (not additional ACARA wording):

1. Identify centimetres and metres as suitable length units for everyday items.
2. Identify grams and kilograms as suitable mass units for everyday items.
3. Identify millilitres and litres as suitable capacity units for everyday items.
4. Use a known measurement of a familiar item as a benchmark.
5. Use the benchmark and the size of an item to choose a reasonable numerical estimate with its unit.

## Existing SkillrHub gap audit

The production source already contains 48 practice and 16 test IDs, but the prompts are derived from 16 base tasks repeated four times with artificial context suffixes. The bank therefore has the right shape but inadequate substantive variety. It also overweights isolated unit choice and underweights explicit benchmark reasoning and estimate justification.
