# AC9M3M02 Measuring with Metric Instruments

## ACARA descriptor
measure and compare objects using familiar metric units of length, mass and capacity, and instruments with labelled markings

## IXL benchmark signals used
- Measure using a centimetre ruler
- Compare metric units of length
- Compare metric units of mass
- Compare metric units of volume
- Compare metric units

## Coverage map
- read ruler
- read labelled scale
- compare measurements
- choose instrument

## Additional free-resource benchmark
- Vedantu free Class 3 measurement worksheets: printable practice, conversions, word problems and answer-key style review were used as a broad variety benchmark. Content remains SkillrHub-original and Australian Curriculum-aligned.
