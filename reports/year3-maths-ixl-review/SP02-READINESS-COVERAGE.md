# AC9M3SP02 strict readiness and coverage map

Date: 2026-09-07

## Official curriculum

Australian Curriculum v9 descriptor: **interpret and create two-dimensional representations of familiar environments, locating key landmarks and objects relative to each other**.

Official source: Australian Curriculum v9 curriculum workbook, Learning areas sheet, AC9M3SP02, at <https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/content/dam/en/curriculum/ac-version-9/downloads/curriculum-workbook.xlsx>.

Analyst decomposition: interpret a 2D map or plan of a familiar environment; create or complete a usable representation; use a title, symbols or legend where useful; locate landmarks; describe one landmark relative to another.

| Objects on a coordinate plane | <https://au.ixl.com/maths/year-3/objects-on-a-coordinate-plane> | Live visual coordinate-reading prompt. Worked example defined the horizontal x-axis, vertical y-axis, origin and ordered pair, then traced left from a star to read a y-coordinate of 8. |

Direct-observation boundary: initial forms and worked examples were inspected; higher adaptive stages were not completed. IXL's formal first-quadrant coordinates are an observed representation, not an inference that ACARA narrows this code to Cartesian coordinates.

## Existing-bank gaps and planned coverage

The current 24-practice/16-test bank repeats a small group of positional frames. It gives too little coverage to selecting/completing map features and evaluating whether another person could use a representation.

| Component | Practice | Test |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interpret a familiar map or plan | 14 | 4 |
| Locate landmarks relative to each other | 14 | 5 |
| Use grid references/coordinates as one representation | 8 | 3 |
| Select or complete a clear 2D representation | 12 | 4 |
| **Total** | **48** | **16** |

Supported-assessment boundary: selected-response items can assess planning, completion and critique of a map, but not independently prove freehand map creation. Adult guidance must include an unscored draw-and-explain task.
