### 60-second Quick Read

A digit's position tells you its value. In **35 042**, the 3 means 30 000, the 5 means 5000, the 0 means no hundreds, the 4 means 40 and the 2 means 2. We read it as **thirty-five thousand and forty-two**. Its expanded form is **30 000 + 5000 + 40 + 2**.

For larger numbers, group digits in threes from the right: **218 036** is **two hundred and eighteen thousand and thirty-six**. Keep zeros inside a group so the other digits stay in their places.

To compare numbers, compare their highest places first. **35 042 < 35 402** because, after the matching 35 thousands, 0 hundreds is less than 4 hundreds. Do not start with the ones digit. A number further right on an increasing number line is greater.

You can rename an amount without changing it: **2 thousands = 20 hundreds**. In counting, **9999 + 1 = 10 000** because the lower places regroup.
