# AC9M3ST01 strict readiness and coverage map

Date: 2026-09-07

## Official curriculum

Australian Curriculum v9 descriptor: **acquire data for categorical and discrete numerical variables to address a question of interest or purpose by observing, collecting and accessing data sets; record the data using appropriate methods including frequency tables and spreadsheets**.

Official source: Australian Curriculum v9 curriculum workbook, Learning areas sheet, AC9M3ST01, at <https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/content/dam/en/curriculum/ac-version-9/downloads/curriculum-workbook.xlsx>.

Analyst decomposition: identify categorical variables; identify discrete numerical variables; frame a question/purpose; acquire data by observation; collect data by asking/measuring/counting; access an existing dataset; record raw observations accurately; organise frequencies in a table; use rows, columns and headings appropriately in a spreadsheet.

Direct-observation boundary: initial formats and worked examples were inspected; higher adaptive stages were not completed. The visible Year 3 IXL set did not directly assess choosing observation/survey/existing-data acquisition methods or spreadsheet structure. Those are ACARA-driven gaps, not observed IXL coverage.
