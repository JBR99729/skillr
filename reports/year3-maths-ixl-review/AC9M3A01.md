## Quality and limits

48 practice and 16 test items, with four options and one correct answer. Correct positions are balanced. IDs, curriculum ownership, distinct answer options and practice/test prompt overlap were checked. Numeric equation answers were checked by substitution independently of the authoring formulas. All SVG diagrams were rendered and visually reviewed.

The bank mixes repeated fluency structures with varied reasoning tasks. It does not assess independently written explanations or timed automatic recall; multiple choice provides scaffolding. No claim of full IXL adaptive parity is made.

The launch pages use five-question shuffled attempts with repeats allowed, and do not advertise bank totals. Repeated attempts offer access to the full source bank without guaranteeing complete coverage after a fixed number of attempts.

Publication uses the complete current main tree and the review ledger.
