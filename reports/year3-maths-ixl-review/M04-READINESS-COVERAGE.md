## Official authority

Exact ACARA v9 descriptor: “describe the relationship between the hours and minutes on analog and digital clocks, and read the time to the nearest minute”. Source: [official ACARA curriculum workbook](https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/content/dam/en/curriculum/ac-version-9/downloads/curriculum-workbook.xlsx), Learning areas sheet, retrieved 7 September 2026; exact extraction and source hash in official-curriculum-components.json.

Analyst decomposition: C1 describe hours/minutes on analogue clocks, including 60 minutes per hour, five minutes between numbers, one minute per tick and continuous hour-hand movement; C2 describe digital hour/minute fields and their relationship to analogue representation; C3 read both representations to individual minutes. Past/to and quarter/half language support these components. Formal 24-hour conversion, elapsed-time calculations, calendar work and seconds conversion are not core M04.
