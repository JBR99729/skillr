Their per-code INDEPENDENT-REVIEW reports record corrections and final artifact identities. All 30 pages of the six actual production worksheet PDFs were rendered and inspected. A following table-based question was observed but not answered. Full independent graph creation and later progression were not observed; original construction tasks extend this bounded benchmark to the official science requirement.
