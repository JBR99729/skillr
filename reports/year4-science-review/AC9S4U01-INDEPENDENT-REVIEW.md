## Baseline substantive audit

Read all 56 canonical questions: P001–P040 and T001–T016, including every answer option, correct flag, explanation and hint. Baseline is 40 Practice +16 Test, not the requested48+16. No canonical question contains a visual.

- P026 is malformed: “roo grass → dingo” and an answer about a “clearer arrow” do not cleanly test the missing kangaroo step.
- T003 calls termite activity decomposition without distinguishing animal detritivores from fungi/bacteria. Use an unambiguous decomposer organism or explicitly explain that termites help break material into smaller pieces.
- P002/P007 use decomposers as wrong consumer distractors. The three primary-school roles should be framed explicitly (for example animal consumers) to avoid conflicting with the broader heterotroph use of consumer.
- Many distractors are absurd rather than plausible misconceptions (organisms turning into other species, animals making sunlight, rocks being created). Substantial improvement is needed especially in independent Test questions.
- Strong existing ideas worth preserving: P006 excludes dead leaf litter from the living-producer label; P038 separates energy movement from nutrient recycling; P039 uses repeated direct feeding observations; predictions commonly use may/could rather than ecological certainty.

Read complete static Topic Guide and Classroom View teaching text. Findings:

- Food-chain models contain generic “feeds / causes / connects” arrows, including Sun→plant; sunlight energy transfer and feeding must be distinguished.
- One model places decomposers only at the end despite text correctly stating they act on dead material from every level.
- All seven elaborations are listed, but listing is not explicit teaching. Human food sources, local observations, habitat-chain comparison, First Nations perspectives, introduced-fox effects on native small mammals and evidence concerning food-source removal need direct treatment.
- Important questions have generic “Explain using the model or evidence above” rather than answers.
- Classroom View repeats these shortcomings and must copy corrected canonical teaching content.
- Worksheet baseline is a link shell advertising a separate nine-question sheet; it is not eight independently authored homework tasks with acceptable answers/evidence.

All above findings sent directly to the author and root coordinator. No source evidence is implied by baseline inspection. Exact external ACARA elaborations, IXL/Khan inspection, final64-item audit, eight-task rubric review, visual inspection and artifact hashes remain pending.
