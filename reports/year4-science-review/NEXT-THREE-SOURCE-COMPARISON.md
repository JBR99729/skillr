## Khan Academy: supplementary, not Australian scope authority

Both articles were opened in the browser after successful public search, and their rendered instructional text was read.

- [Controlled experiments](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/biology/intro-to-biology/science-of-biology/a/experiments-and-observations): supports matching comparison conditions, checking predictions, repeating observations and acknowledging limitations of observational evidence. Its advanced biology, chemical notation and statistical testing are excluded from Year 4 requirements. Used for H01 evidence reasoning and H02 testing ideas.
- [Physical properties and changes](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ms-chemistry/xc370bc422b7f75fc:physical-properties-of-matter/xc370bc422b7f75fc:intrinsic-and-extrinsic-properties/a/physical-properties-and-changes): observable/measurable properties provide a useful introductory comparison for U04. Particle formulas, intrinsic/extrinsic terminology and chemical-change classification are outside this batch's core learning.

Named First Nations examples are verified separately against ACARA's primary background material; they are not inferred from IXL or Khan. Classroom results are expressly labelled invented models. H01 includes primary research-report structure and source-checking tasks; H02 includes practical scientific mechanisms across all six elaborations. These source-specific details and review corrections appear in the code reports.
