## Khan Academy: actual rendered lessons

- [Food chains and food webs](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/biology/ecology/intro-to-ecosystems/a/food-chains-food-webs): read the article's role, feeding-arrow and decomposer sections. These support food-to-eater arrows and nutrient return from dead material at multiple feeding levels. Its trophic-level terminology, chemical detail and quantitative energy-transfer rule are teacher background, not Year 4 requirements. Discussion comments were not evidence.
- [Key points: The water cycle](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/middle-school-earth-and-space-science/x87d03b443efbea0a:weather-and-climate/x87d03b443efbea0a:the-water-cycle/a/the-water-cycle-ms): read the complete key-points article and diagram description. It confirms liquid-to-gas evaporation, gas-to-liquid condensation, precipitation, runoff and groundwater. Transpiration is explanatory background; the authored core remains the ACARA Year 4 descriptor.
- [Forces](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ms-physics/x1baed5db7c1bb50b:motion-and-forces/x1baed5db7c1bb50b:forces/v/forces): opened About and Transcript; read the contact/friction and non-contact gravity/magnetism explanations, and the later net-force discussion to establish the boundary. The video itself was not played. Newton calculations, perpendicular-vector formalism and electromagnetic unification are excluded from required Year 4 learning.
