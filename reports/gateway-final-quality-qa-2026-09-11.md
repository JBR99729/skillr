## QA standard

Each code was reviewed as a complete learning pathway rather than as an isolated SEO page. The required surfaces were Topic Page, Classroom View, Homework/Worksheet, Practice bank and Test bank. Acceptance criteria were:

1. Australian Curriculum v9 descriptor fidelity and age-appropriate scope.
2. Explicit teaching progression, worked thinking and misconception handling.
3. Practice progression comparable to current IXL Australia patterns without copying proprietary questions.
4. Explanation and transfer depth comparable to strong Khan-style worked learning.
5. Practical resource usefulness comparable to Australian teacher-resource expectations (including Twinkl/Education Perfect style coverage where relevant).
6. Separate Practice and Test purposes; tests must use unseen transfer rather than repeat practice wording.
7. No generic placeholder definitions, template-only questions, vague “human error”/“use evidence” answers or content that merely names a skill without requiring it.
8. Learner routes must point to the reviewed bank and preserve indexability/canonical behaviour already established for gateway pages.

## Corrections made in this closeout

### AC9E5LY06
The previous route was not acceptable as a gateway-quality learning resource even though it had been marked finished. The problems were substantive:

- vocabulary definitions were generic placeholders rather than definitions;
- worked examples repeatedly told students to “identify the decisive clue” instead of modelling writing decisions;
- Classroom View listed questions without useful worked answers;
- Practice/Test used repeated template stems such as “Which response best demonstrates the skill?” rather than authentic writing tasks;
- the topic title/meta repeated the full curriculum descriptor and obscured the human-readable skill.

The replacement now provides:

- a purpose/audience-led writing cycle;
- distinct imaginative, informative and persuasive structures;
- paragraph modelling with weak→revised examples;
- real connective relationships;
- expanded noun groups, precise verbs and specialist vocabulary;
- explicit dialogue-punctuation teaching;
- multimodal feature selection based on meaning;
- revision versus editing instruction;
- common misconceptions and a guided revision task;
- 48 authentic Practice items and 16 separate unseen Test items spanning the complete descriptor;
- refreshed Practice/Test cache keys so the new banks are served immediately;
- a stronger 12-question printable worksheet path based on the reviewed practice source.

### AC9M1M03
The existing content was pedagogically sound, but the learner-facing Practice route still exposed only 28 questions while the gateway standard is 48 Practice + 16 Test. The Practice bank is now 48 original, age-appropriate questions covering:

- choosing appropriate units (hours/days/weeks/months/years);
- comparing named durations;
- daily event sequence and before/after language;
- days of the week and weekly rollover;
- months and the repeating yearly cycle;
- common Australian four-season sequence while avoiding a claim that every cultural seasonal calendar has four seasons;
- calendar rows and simple date/day reasoning;
- sensible duration estimation and reasoning traps.

The compatibility Practice bank was synchronised and the route cache/version was refreshed. The QA log now records 48 Practice + 16 Test and gateway-final status.
