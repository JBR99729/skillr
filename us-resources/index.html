A teaching sequence for busy adults

Spend less time searching for activities and assembling lessons. Choose a skill, explain it with a clear model, provide manageable practice, then check what needs another look.

1

Explain with visual slides

Teaching packs use simple explanations, visual models and guided examples to introduce a concept one step at a time. Check each preview for the exact contents.

2

Practice with an organized workbook

Our workbook style brings teaching examples, focused practice and checks into a planned sequence. Answer keys help adults review work without preparing every solution themselves.

3

Check, revisit and move on

Use the included checks or tests where provided to spot skills that need more practice. Follow the learner’s pace and revisit a concept before moving forward.

Teachers can reduce lesson preparation; tutors can choose a focused session; parents and homeschoolers can follow a clear next step. Pack contents vary—review the listing before purchasing.

Using these resources in a US classroom or homeschool

Start with the skill your learner needs—such as counting, addition or place value—then review the sample pages and teaching models. Common Core and state expectations can differ from Australian year-level sequences. Australian packs keep their original curriculum labels, spelling, examples and print format; they are supplements rather than complete US units.

Check each product’s preview for page size, answer keys, tests, classroom use and household licensing. Worksheets are for paper practice; teaching slides provide the explanation and guided examples. You can use either format on its own or select resources covering the same skill.

Are the digital resources and printable packs both free?

Digital learning and teaching notes on SkillrHub are currently free. Some TPT samples are free; paid workbooks and slide packs are optional purchases. The listing shows the current price and included files.

Is there a complete US curriculum or question bank?

No. This page is a focused resource selection. The question bank is being developed over time, and the current free digital library primarily follows the Australian Curriculum. Check a resource’s actual scope against your local requirements.

Where do I get a workbook or teaching pack?

Open a resource above to inspect its details, then follow its TPT link for the preview and any checkout or download. TPT may require an account. There is one SkillrHub brand and one TPT store.
