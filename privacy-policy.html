Privacy Policy

Skillr Education shares free worksheet and learning resources for schools, teachers, families and students. Our resources are available without login, account creation or collection of student information. This page explains, in general terms, how information may be collected and used when you visit the site.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed and general location (for example, country or region).
  • Website usage information from analytics tools to understand which resources are most useful.
  • Information you choose to send us directly, such as details in an email or contact request.

Learning Progress Stored on This Device

Skillr does not require a learner account and does not sync learning progress to a Skillr learner account. When you use supported learning activities, your browser may save progress on the device you are using so the Dashboard and activities can remember your work.

Depending on the resources and features used, this device-local information may include:

  • An optional learner profile name and chosen avatar, including an avatar image selected from the device.
  • Completed activity attempts, topic or curriculum code, scores, pass status, completion date and activity duration.
  • Best scores, practice or daily streak information and site preferences used to personalise the experience on that browser.

This local learning record is separate from the analytics and advertising services described below. Analytics and advertising providers may process website usage, device, cookie and consent information under their own services, but the on-device Dashboard record is not a synced learner profile.

You can remove local learning progress by clearing Skillr's site data in your browser. You can also use the Dashboard to save a progress backup and load it on another device or browser. A backup file is only created or selected when you choose those actions, so keep it somewhere appropriate for your family or school.

Google Analytics, Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use Google Analytics on this site. Analytics tools use cookies and similar technologies to help us understand traffic patterns and improve the website. Cookies are small files stored by your browser. You can manage cookie settings in your browser at any time.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

This website may display advertising through third-party services such as Google AdSense. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to provide ads, measure performance, and improve the overall experience. The use of such services may vary depending on your location and consent settings.

Some pages may also include affiliate links (for example, selected classroom resources). If a purchase is made through an affiliate link, we may receive a small commission at no additional cost to you.

Child-Focused and Educational Use

Skillr Education is an educational website intended for teachers, parents and students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising purposes. Parents, carers and teachers should supervise children using online services and manage privacy settings where appropriate.

Advertising notice: if you see ads on this site, details about advertising and cookies are explained on this privacy page.

Managing Cookies and Ad Personalisation

You can manage privacy choices by:

  • Adjusting browser settings to block, clear or limit cookies.
  • Managing ad personalisation settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Reviewing cookie and privacy controls provided by your device or browser.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect how some website features work.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and improve the website and worksheet resources.
  • Understand usage trends and maintain site performance.
  • Respond to questions, requests or feedback.

Third-Party Links and Services

This site may include links to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies.

Children and School Use

Skillr Education is designed for educational use. Parents, carers and teachers should supervise children using online services and apply school or family privacy preferences where needed.
