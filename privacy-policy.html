Privacy Policy

Skillr Education shares free worksheet and learning resources for schools, teachers, families and students. This page explains, in general terms, how information may be collected and used when you visit the site.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed and general location (for example, country or region).
  • Website usage information from analytics tools to understand which resources are most useful.
  • Information you choose to send us directly, such as details in an email or contact request.

Google Analytics, Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use Google Analytics on this site. Analytics tools use cookies and similar technologies to help us understand traffic patterns and improve the website. Cookies are small files stored by your browser.

Advertising and Google Ads

Some pages may include advertising now or in the future, including Google Ads. Ads may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure advertising. We aim to keep advertising practical and non-intrusive for learning use.

Advertising notice: if you see ads on this site, details about advertising and cookies are explained on this privacy page.

Managing Cookies and Ad Personalisation

You can manage privacy choices by:

  • Adjusting browser settings to block, clear or limit cookies.
  • Managing Google ad personalisation settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Reviewing cookie and privacy controls provided by your device or browser.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect how some website features work.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and improve the website and worksheet resources.
  • Understand usage trends and maintain site performance.
  • Respond to questions, requests or feedback.

Third-Party Links and Services

This site may include links to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies.

Children and School Use

Skillr Education is designed for educational use. Parents, carers and teachers should supervise children using online services and apply school or family privacy preferences where needed.
