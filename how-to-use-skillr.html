How to use Skillr: a simple 5-step learning routine

Skillr works best as a practical learning assistant alongside normal teaching, explanation and discussion. The aim is to make it easy to move from finding a skill to practising it, reviewing mistakes and reinforcing it again later.

1

Choose the learner's level

Start from the homepage or year-level pages and choose the most suitable year, grade or early-years level.

2

Choose Maths or Science

Open the subject and find the current topic, curriculum skill or learning intention you want the student to practise.

3

Practise online or print

Use an online practice activity when available, or download a printable worksheet for screen-free learning, written working and classroom use.

4

Review answers

After online practice, review the result, the student's answer, the correct answer and the explanation so mistakes become useful learning opportunities.

5

Repeat and reinforce

Repeat the same skill across the week when useful. Different attempts can expose students to more examples and question types rather than relying on a single worksheet or quiz.

Students can use Skillr to revisit a topic, practise before a test, review a difficult concept, strengthen foundational Maths and Science skills, or complete a short daily learning routine. Using Skillr internationally

Skillr is designed to be useful to educators and families beyond one school system. School year names, curriculum sequences and terminology vary internationally, so the most important guide is the learning intention and the actual questions on the page. Where curriculum references are provided, they are intended to help educators identify matching or closely related learning rather than suggest that different curricula are identical.

This makes Skillr useful for teachers, parents, tutors and students in Australia and for families using comparable primary or elementary learning expectations in countries such as the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, New Zealand and India. Early-years labels can also differ; for example, a similar age group may be described as Foundation, Prep, Reception, Kindergarten or another local equivalent.

For international users: check the topic title, learning intention and question difficulty first. Use curriculum codes as a reference point, not as a claim that every national, state or provincial curriculum follows the same sequence or depth.

Skillr Frequently Asked Questions

These FAQs explain online practice, printable worksheets, repeated learning, classroom use, home learning, curriculum alignment and access to Skillr resources.

Getting started and access

What is Skillr?

Skillr is a free learning resource designed to complement classroom and home learning. Students can practise curriculum skills online, receive immediate marking and explanations, or use printable worksheets.

Is Skillr free to use?

Yes. The core learning resources are designed to be freely accessible. Teachers, parents and students can open a resource and begin using it without a paid subscription for the core experience.

Do students need an account or login?

No. The core Skillr experience is designed to work without a login so students can move quickly from finding a resource to practising or printing it.

Who can use Skillr?

Skillr is designed for students, teachers, parents, carers and tutors. It can be used for classroom practice, revision, warm-ups, relief-teacher activities, homework support, tutoring and independent learning.

Does Skillr replace classroom teaching or a teacher?

No. Skillr is designed to complement teaching. Teachers provide instruction, discussion, demonstrations, professional judgement and support; Skillr provides focused opportunities for practice and review.

Online quizzes, practice and revision

Why does an online practice set use a small number of questions?

Short sets keep practice manageable. Students can complete a set, review their answers and then try another set when appropriate instead of facing one long assessment. Where a Skillr activity uses eight questions, the goal is focused practice rather than exhaustive testing.

Will students get the same questions every time?

Not necessarily. Practice is designed to provide different questions and question types across attempts where the question bank supports variation. Repeating a skill can therefore give broader exposure to the underlying learning intention.

Why should a student repeat the same skill during the week?

One attempt cannot show every possible question type. Repeating a skill across the week gives students more opportunities to encounter, practise and reinforce different aspects of the same concept.

What happens after a student finishes an online quiz?

The quiz can be marked at the end so the student can review the result, compare their answer with the correct answer and read a short explanation that helps them understand mistakes.

Why does Skillr use short practice followed by review?

The aim is not simply to produce a score. Students practise a small set of questions, review mistakes and explanations, and then have the opportunity to practise again. This creates a cycle of practice, feedback and reinforcement.

What is the Skillr Daily Routine?

The Daily Routine is short, grade-appropriate practice intended to keep essential knowledge active. In Maths, this can include addition, subtraction, multiplication facts, division, number knowledge, mathematical vocabulary and other fundamentals appropriate to the learner's level.

How is Daily Routine different from curriculum practice?

Daily Routine maintains essential skills through regular mixed practice. Curriculum practice focuses on the specific skill or learning intention a student is currently studying. The two forms of practice are designed to complement each other.

Why does Skillr include mathematical vocabulary?

Understanding the language of Maths helps students interpret questions and explain reasoning. Regular vocabulary practice helps learners become familiar with grade-appropriate mathematical terms as well as calculations.

How much Skillr practice should a student do?

Skillr is designed for flexible use. A student might complete one short set, repeat a current skill several times during a week, or use Daily Routine regularly. Teachers and parents can choose an amount appropriate to the learner and available time.

Printable Maths and Science worksheets

Can I print Skillr questions and worksheets?

Yes. Printable worksheets are a core part of Skillr and are intended for classroom, tutoring and home use. They are designed to be clear, practical and easy to print.

Why are many Skillr worksheets kept to one page?

A one-page format keeps printing simple and economical. Students can complete the structured questions on the front and use the reverse side for extra working when needed.

Does a printable worksheet always contain every online question?

No. Some online activities, such as interactive arranging or drag-and-drop tasks, do not translate well to paper. Printable versions prioritise paper-friendly questions and a clean layout.

Are printable worksheets different each time?

Where the question bank supports variation, a new practice or printable set can present different questions. This helps teachers and parents provide repeated practice without relying on one fixed worksheet.

Can Skillr worksheets be used for homework or tutoring?

Yes. Printable resources can be used for homework support, tutoring sessions, revision, intervention, independent work and additional practice at home or school.

Teachers, parents and school use

Can teachers use Skillr with a whole class?

Yes. A teacher can direct students to online practice or print a worksheet for the class. A consistent routine can help students become familiar with the workflow and work independently when appropriate.

Can Skillr be used by a relief or substitute teacher?

Yes. Familiar Daily Routine and curriculum practice can provide ready-to-use independent learning when a relief or substitute teacher is taking a class, helping reduce setup and explanation time.

Can parents use Skillr at home?

Yes. Parents can choose the child's year level and a relevant skill, then use online practice or a printable worksheet. The learning intention helps parents understand what the activity is practising.

Can tutors and learning centres use Skillr?

Skillr can support one-to-one tutoring, small-group practice and targeted revision. Tutors can choose a specific topic or foundational skill and use the resource to structure focused practice.

Curriculum alignment and international use

How is Skillr aligned with different curricula?

Skillr teaches underlying Maths and Science skills and can display matching or closely related curriculum references from different education systems. These references help teachers identify where a resource may fit their local curriculum.

Does a curriculum reference mean different curricula are identical?

No. Curriculum sequence, terminology, depth and expectations can differ between education systems. Teachers and parents should use the learning intention and actual questions to decide whether a resource is suitable for the student.

Can Skillr be used outside Australia?

Yes. The underlying Maths and Science skills can be useful internationally, although local year-level names, curriculum order and terminology can differ. International users should match the learning intention and difficulty to their own school system.

What if my country uses “math” instead of “maths”?

Skillr uses Australian and British-style “Maths” wording on many pages, but the learning resources are also intended to be understandable to users who search for “math worksheets”, “math practice” or “elementary math” in other countries.

What if my school uses Kindergarten, Reception, Prep or Grade instead of Foundation or Year levels?

Year-level names vary by country and education system. Use the topic title, learning intention and question difficulty to identify the best match rather than relying only on the label used for the school year.

Purpose and learning approach

What is Skillr trying to achieve?

Skillr aims to make high-quality practice of essential Maths and Science skills easy to access. The focus is on helping students build strong foundations through manageable, repeated practice that can be used at school or at home.

Is Skillr suitable for revision before a test?

Skillr can be used to revisit taught concepts, strengthen foundational knowledge and practise common question types. It should be used alongside the student's class notes, teacher guidance and the exact content required for the assessment.

Can I request a new worksheet or topic?

Yes. If you need a worksheet for a topic that is not yet available, use the Skillr contact page to send the year level, subject, topic and relevant curriculum information.

What can Skillr help you find?

Skillr's growing resource library is designed around the kinds of learning support teachers and families commonly need: free maths worksheets, free math worksheets, free science worksheets, printable worksheets, online maths practice, online math practice, primary school worksheets, elementary learning activities, classroom resources, homework help, home-learning resources, tutoring worksheets, numeracy practice, science practice, curriculum-aligned resources, revision activities, Daily Routine practice and no-login learning resources.

These terms are included naturally because they describe how people actually use the site. The best way to find a suitable resource is still to begin with the learner's year level, subject and current skill.
