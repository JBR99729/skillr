Quick guide

How to use Skillr

Skillr is designed to help teachers, parents and students find printable maths and science resources quickly. The site is organised by year level, learning area and topic so you can start with the part that matches your learner.

Choose a starting point

Different visitors use Skillr in different ways. These are the easiest paths to follow:

  • Teachers: choose a year level, open a topic page and use the worksheet or guide in class.
  • Parents and carers: browse by your child’s year level and pick a topic that needs extra practice.
  • Home educators: use the topic guides, worksheets and practice pages together as a simple lesson plan.
  • Students: start with a topic you are learning and work through the practice pages at your own pace.

What you will usually find on each page

  • Topic guides explain the idea and give teaching support.
  • Worksheets offer printable practice that can be used at school or at home.
  • Practice and quiz pages help with revision and quick checks.

A simple three-step start

  1. Choose the year level that matches your learner.
  2. Select the maths or science area you want to explore.
  3. Open the topic page and begin with the worksheet, guide or practice activity that looks most useful.
