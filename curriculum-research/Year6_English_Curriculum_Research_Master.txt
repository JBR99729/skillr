==================================================
CODE: AC9E6LA01
DESCRIPTOR: Understand that language varies as levels of formality and social distance increase.
STATUS: RESEARCHING
==================================================
1. Curriculum intent: Students select and explain language choices that fit formality, relationship and communicative purpose; a language variety is not inherently better or worse outside its context.
2. Required knowledge: register, formality, social distance, audience, purpose, tone, vocabulary, terms of address, modality, sentence choice.
3. Prior-year foundations: identifying purpose and formality; fact/opinion and connotation.
4. IXL evidence: Year 6 v9 mapping links “Which sentence is more formal?”, “Compare passages for tone”, “Distinguish facts from opinions”, “Describe the difference between related words”, and “Positive and negative connotation”. Task families use short contextual excerpts and contrasting options; key traps are judging a sentence by one sophisticated word or treating informal language as incorrect.
5. Khan Academy evidence: authenticated Khan Academy 6th Grade Reading & Vocabulary, Unit 1, “Making inferences in informational texts” opened. It models conclusions from multiple textual clues plus relevant world knowledge, and explicitly distinguishes an inference from a guess. This supports the evidence-based explanation component of register/tone analysis, but is not a direct AC v9 code mapping.
6. Authoring implications: use paired messages/email, dialogue, notices and spoken prompts; require students to identify language evidence and explain its effect on relationship/formality. Include revision tasks that improve suitability without erasing a speaker’s identity.
7. Boundaries: do not prescribe one “correct” dialect or add advanced sociolinguistic theory.
8. Source references: IXL Australia Year 6 English v9 alignment; Khan Academy 6th Grade Reading & Vocabulary, Unit 1, Close reading: informational text.
RESEARCH STATUS: CONTINUING.
