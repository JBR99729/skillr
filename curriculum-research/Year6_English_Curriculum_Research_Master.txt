CODE: AC9E6LA01
DESCRIPTOR: Understand that language varies as levels of formality and social distance increase.
STATUS: AUTHORING-READY
==================================================
1. Curriculum intent: Select and explain language choices that fit formality, relationship and purpose; a language variety is not inherently better or worse outside its context.
2. Required knowledge: register, formality, social distance, audience, purpose, tone, vocabulary, terms of address, modality and sentence choice.
3. Prior-year foundations: identifying purpose/formality; fact/opinion and connotation.
4. New Year 6 extension: explain a pattern of choices and its relationship effect, not just label a sentence formal/informal.
5. IXL evidence: Year 6 v9 mapping links Which sentence is more formal?, Compare passages for tone, Distinguish facts from opinions, Describe the difference between related words, and Positive and negative connotation. Task families use short contextual excerpts and contrasting options. Traps: judging by one sophisticated word; treating informal language as incorrect.
6. Khan evidence: 6th Grade Reading & Vocabulary, Unit 1, Making inferences in informational texts, models conclusions based on multiple textual clues plus relevant knowledge and distinguishes inference from guessing. Supplementary only, not an AC v9 map.
7. Other sources: ABC Education author/media resources support audience and language-choice discussion; use for teaching context only.
8. Required representations: paired email/message, dialogue, notice, meeting excerpt, spoken prompt, revision comparison.
9. Vocabulary: register, formal, informal, social distance, audience, purpose, tone, modality, address.
10. Easy patterns: identify a suitable wording for context; match key terms.
11. Medium patterns: compare two choices and identify evidence.
12. Hard patterns: revise an unsuitable message and justify changes without denigrating language varieties.
13. Practice coverage: 48 original items across recognition, comparison, revision, evidence explanation and transfer.
14. Test coverage: 16 independent items including an unfamiliar multi-message context.
15. Boundaries: no prescriptive “correct dialect” claims; no advanced sociolinguistics.
16. Source references: IXL Australia Year 6 English v9 mapping; Khan Academy 6th Grade Reading & Vocabulary Unit 1.
RESEARCH STATUS: AUTHORING-READY
