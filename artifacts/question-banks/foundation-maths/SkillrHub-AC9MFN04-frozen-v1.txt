Foundation Maths — AC9MFN04
Partition and combine collections up to 10

Status: APPROVED FOR FREEZING by user review, with visual separation and wording edits applied.
Version: frozen-v1

Adult guidance: Read aloud. Children may use counters, fingers, drawings, or dot cards. The focus is seeing a whole collection split into two parts, or seeing two parts combined into one whole, up to 10. A vertical bar | separates the two parts of a collection.
