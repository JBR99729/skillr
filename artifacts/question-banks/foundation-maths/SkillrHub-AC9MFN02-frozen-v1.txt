Foundation Maths — AC9MFN02
Recognise and name collections up to 5 without counting

Status: APPROVED FOR FREEZING by user review.
Version: frozen-v1

Adult guidance: Read the question aloud. Show the dot collection briefly, then cover it before the child answers. The goal is to see whether the child can recognise the quantity quickly, not count each dot one by one.
