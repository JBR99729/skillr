FOUNDATION MATHS — AC9MFN07
SkillrHub/Internal Descriptor: Connect days of the week, seasons and familiar events to everyday experiences.

Status: Frozen v1
Note: Adult read-aloud support is expected for Foundation students.
Production note: AC9MFN07 is treated as a SkillrHub/internal label. The day/time content overlaps with official Foundation Maths time sequencing expectations; season content is retained because this SkillrHub/internal descriptor includes seasons.

PRACTICE QUESTIONS 1–24

PRACTICE 1
Which day comes after Monday?

A. Sunday
B. Tuesday
C. Friday

Answer: B
Explanation: Tuesday comes after Monday.


PRACTICE 2
Which day comes before Friday?

A. Thursday
B. Saturday
C. Monday

Answer: A
Explanation: Thursday is the day before Friday.


PRACTICE 3
Which day is usually part of the weekend?

A. Wednesday
B. Tuesday
C. Saturday

Answer: C
Explanation: Saturday is part of the weekend.


PRACTICE 4
Which day comes after Sunday?

A. Friday
B. Monday
C. Saturday

Answer: B
Explanation: Monday comes after Sunday. The week starts again.


PRACTICE 5
Which order is correct?

A. Wednesday, Monday, Tuesday
B. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday
C. Tuesday, Sunday, Friday

Answer: B
Explanation: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday is the correct order.
