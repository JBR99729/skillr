Foundation Maths — AC9MFN03
Quantify and compare collections to at least 20 using counting

Status: APPROVED FOR FREEZING by user review, with minor wording edits applied to T15 and T16.
Version: frozen-v1

Adult guidance: Read the question aloud. Children may count the objects. Encourage them to explain how they know, for example: “I counted each one,” “I made a group of ten,” or “I saw one more than twelve.” Use consistent dot rows or five-rows for visual cards. Adult read-aloud support is expected for longer comparison and reasoning choices.
