FOUNDATION MATHS — AC9MFSP02
Describe the position and location of themselves and objects in relation to other people and objects within a familiar space.

Status: Frozen v1
Note: Adult read-aloud support is expected for Foundation students.
Production note: Keep this bank focused on everyday positional language in familiar spaces: in, on, under, beside, next to, between, behind, in front, above, below, near and far. Do not introduce maps, grids, coordinates or formal direction systems.

PRACTICE QUESTIONS 1–24

PRACTICE 1
The teddy is on the chair.
Where is the teddy?

A. Under the chair
B. On the chair
C. Behind the chair

Answer: B
Explanation: The teddy is on top of the chair.


PRACTICE 2
The ball is under the table.
Where is the ball?

A. Under the table
B. Beside the table
C. Above the table

Answer: A
Explanation: Under means below the table.
