FOUNDATION MATHS — AC9MFST01
Collect, sort and compare data represented by objects and images in response to given investigative questions that relate to familiar situations.

Status: Frozen v1
Note: Adult read-aloud support is expected for Foundation students.
Production note: Keep data displays simple and visual. Use objects, pictures, dots or marks only; avoid formal bar graphs, scaled axes, tables with numbers beyond Foundation counting expectations, or abstract statistical language.
