Foundation Maths — AC9MFN06
Equal sharing and grouping

Status: APPROVED FOR FREEZING by user review, with group-boundary and option-length guidance applied.
Version: frozen-v1

Adult guidance: Read aloud. Children may use counters, blocks, drawings, toy food, cups, plates or boxes. The focus is representing fair sharing and equal groups, not formal multiplication or division symbols. In production, show each group as a clearly separate plate, mat, child, bag, box or cluster rather than relying only on a thin divider.

PRACTICE QUESTIONS 1–24

P01. There are 4 apples. They are shared equally between 2 children. How many apples does each child get?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 4

Answer: B
Explanation: Sharing 4 apples fairly between 2 children gives each child 2 apples.

P02. There are 6 biscuits. They are shared equally onto 2 plates. How many biscuits go on each plate?

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4

Answer: B
Explanation: 3 biscuits on each plate makes 6 altogether.

P03. There are 8 counters. They are shared equally between 4 mats. How many counters go on each mat?

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4

Answer: A
Explanation: Each mat gets 2 counters because 2, 2, 2 and 2 make 8.

P04. There are 10 grapes. They are shared equally between 2 children. How many grapes does each child get?

A. 4
B. 5
C. 6

Answer: B
Explanation: 5 grapes and 5 grapes make 10 grapes.

P05. Which picture shows fair sharing?

A. Child 1: ● ●    Child 2: ● ●
B. Child 1: ● ● ●    Child 2: ●
C. Child 1: ● ● ● ●    Child 2: none

Answer: A
Explanation: Fair sharing means each child gets the same number.

P06. Which picture does not show fair sharing?

A. Plate 1: ● ●    Plate 2: ● ●
B. Plate 1: ● ● ●    Plate 2: ● ● ●
C. Plate 1: ● ● ●    Plate 2: ●

Answer: C
Explanation: Plate 1 has 3 and Plate 2 has 1, so the shares are not equal.

P07. There are 9 shells. They are shared equally between 3 children. How many shells does each child get?

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4

Answer: B
Explanation: Each child gets 3 shells because 3, 3 and 3 make 9.

P08. There are 6 blocks. Put 2 blocks in each box. How many boxes can be filled?

P09. There are 8 toy cars. Put 4 toy cars in each garage. How many garages are needed?

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4

Explanation: 4 cars in one garage and 4 cars in another garage use all 8 cars.

P10. Which shows 3 equal groups of 2?

P13. There are 5 stickers. Can they be shared equally between 2 children with none left over?

A. Yes
B. No
C. Hide one

Answer: B
Explanation: 5 cannot be shared equally between 2 children with none left over.

P14. There are 7 counters. Can they be put into groups of 2 with none left over?

A. Yes
B. No
C. Add a group

Answer: B
Explanation: Groups of 2 use 2, 4, 6 counters, with 1 counter left over.

P15. There are 10 socks. Put 2 socks in each pair. How many pairs can be made?

C. Child 1: ● ● ● ●    Child 2: ●    Child 3: ●

Answer: A
Explanation: Each child gets 2 pencils, so the sharing is fair.

P24. Which answer gives a good reason?

Story: 8 flowers are shared equally between 2 vases.

A. 4 and 4 make 8.
B. There are 2 vases.
C. Each vase gets 8.

Answer: A
Explanation: Fair sharing means both vases get the same number, and 4 plus 4 is 8.
