FOUNDATION MATHS — AC9MFA01
Recognise, copy and continue repeating patterns represented in different ways.

Status: Frozen v1
Note: Adult read-aloud support is expected for Foundation students.
Production note: For action patterns such as clap/tap/sit/stand, pair text with simple icons or audio/read-aloud support in the app interface. Keep option formatting visually consistent.

PRACTICE QUESTIONS 1–24

PRACTICE 1
What comes next?

red, blue, red, blue, red, ___

A. red
B. blue
C. green

Answer: B
Explanation: The pattern repeats red, blue. After red comes blue.


PRACTICE 2
What comes next?

B. circle
C. triangle

Answer: A
Explanation: The repeating part is circle, square. After circle comes square.
