PRACTICE QUESTIONS 1–24

P01. A plate has no counters on it. Which number shows how many counters there are?
A. 1
B. 0
C. 10
Answer: B — 0
Explanation: Zero means there are none.

P02. Point to the number seven.
A. 7
B. 1
C. 17
Answer: A — 7
Explanation: We write seven as 7. Seventeen is 17.

P03. What is the name of 12?
A. Twenty
B. Two
C. Twelve
Answer: C — Twelve
Explanation: The numeral 12 is read as twelve.

P04. Which numeral means sixteen?
A. 6
B. 16
C. 19
Answer: B — 16
Explanation: Sixteen is written 16. Six is written 6.

P05. A number card shows 20. What should you say?
A. Twelve
B. Two
C. Twenty
Answer: C — Twenty
Explanation: The numeral 20 is read as twenty.

P06. Which numeral matches these counters?
● ● ● ●
A. 4
B. 3
C. 5
Answer: A — 4
Explanation: Touch each counter once: 1, 2, 3, 4. The collection represents 4.

P07. Your number card says 6. Which collection matches it?
A. ● ● ● ● ●
B. ● ● ● ● ● ●
C. ● ● ● ● ● ● ●
Answer: B — Six counters
Explanation: The matching collection has one counter for each count from 1 to 6.

P08. Each row has five counters. Which numeral matches the whole collection?
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ● ●
A. 5
B. 15
C. 10
Answer: C — 10
Explanation: Count both rows. The first ends at 5 and the second ends at 10.

P09. Which collection represents 13?
A.
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ●
B.
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ● ●
● ●
C.
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ●
Answer: A — The first collection
Explanation: Its first two rows show 10 counters. Continue counting: 11, 12, 13.

P10. These counters represent a number. Which number?
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ● ● ●
● ● ●
A. 13
B. 18
C. 20
Answer: B — 18
Explanation: The three full rows reach 15. The last row takes the count to 18.

P11. You need to show 20 with counters. Which arrangement should you make?
A. Two rows with five counters in each row
B. Three rows with five counters in each row
C. Four rows with five counters in each row
Answer: C — Four rows of five
Explanation: Counting every counter in four full rows of five takes you to 20.

P12. You have counted a collection correctly. The last number you said was eleven. Which card labels the collection?
A. 11
B. 1
C. 12
Answer: A — 11
Explanation: Eleven is written 11, so that card matches the collection.

P13. Start at zero and count forwards. Which number is missing?
0, ___, 2, 3
A. 0
B. 1
C. 4
Answer: B — 1
Explanation: The sequence begins 0, 1, 2, 3.

P14. What comes immediately after 8 when counting forwards?
A. 7
B. 10
C. 9
Answer: C — 9
Explanation: Say 7, 8, 9. Nine comes immediately after eight.

P15. What comes immediately before 14?
A. 13
B. 15
C. 12
Answer: A — 13
Explanation: The sequence is 12, 13, 14. Thirteen is immediately before fourteen.

P16. Which number belongs in the gap?
9, ___, 11
A. 12
B. 10
C. 8
Answer: B — 10
Explanation: Ten sits between nine and eleven in the counting sequence.

P17. Count backwards. Which number is missing?
7, 6, ___, 4
A. 7
B. 3
C. 5
Answer: C — 5
Explanation: Counting backwards gives 7, 6, 5, 4.

P18. Start at 17. Count forwards two steps. Where do you finish?
A. 19
B. 18
C. 20
Answer: A — 19
Explanation: After 17, the first step is 18 and the second is 19.

P19. Which list goes from smallest to largest?
A. 5, 2, 8
B. 8, 5, 2
C. 2, 5, 8
Answer: C — 2, 5, 8
Explanation: Two comes before five, and five comes before eight.

P20. Put these cards in order from smallest to largest: 16, 11, 14.
A. 11, 14, 16
B. 11, 16, 14
C. 16, 14, 11
Answer: A — 11, 14, 16
Explanation: In the counting sequence, eleven comes first, then fourteen, then sixteen.

P21. Which list goes from largest to smallest?
A. 0, 3, 9
B. 9, 3, 0
C. 3, 9, 0
Answer: B — 9, 3, 0
Explanation: Nine is the largest of these numbers. Zero is the smallest.

P22. These cards are in order from smallest to largest:
4, 8, 15, 20
Where should 12 go?
A. Before 4
B. Between 4 and 8
C. Between 8 and 15
Answer: C — Between 8 and 15
Explanation: Twelve comes after eight but before fifteen.

P23. Someone counted:
“Fourteen, fifteen, seventeen, eighteen.”
Which number did they miss?
A. Sixteen
B. Thirteen
C. Nineteen
Answer: A — Sixteen
Explanation: Sixteen belongs between fifteen and seventeen.

P24. These cards should count backwards by one:
20, 19, 18, **16**, 16
Which number should replace the bold card?
A. 15
B. 17
C. 19
Answer: B — 17
Explanation: The correct sequence is 20, 19, 18, 17, 16.
