FOUNDATION MATHS — AC9MFM02
Sequence days of the week and times of the day including morning, lunchtime, afternoon and night time, and connect them to familiar events and actions.

Status: Frozen v1
Note: Adult read-aloud support is expected for Foundation students.
Production note: Keep this bank focused on daily and weekly sequencing only. Do not add seasons here; seasons are covered only in SkillrHub/internal AC9MFN07.

PRACTICE QUESTIONS 1–24

PRACTICE 1

A. Sunday
B. Tuesday
C. Friday

Answer: B
Explanation: Tuesday comes after Monday.

C. Saturday

Explanation: Wednesday is the day before Thursday.
