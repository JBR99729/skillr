FOUNDATION MATHS — AC9MFSP01
Sort, name and create familiar shapes; recognise and describe familiar shapes within objects in the environment, giving reasons.

Status: Frozen v1
Note: Adult read-aloud support is expected for Foundation students.
Production note: Use clear shape visuals/icons in the app for circle, triangle, square and rectangle items. Keep the focus on familiar shapes, obvious features, everyday objects and simple reasons.

PRACTICE QUESTIONS 1–24

PRACTICE 1
Which shape is round?

A. Triangle
B. Square
C. Circle

Answer: C
Explanation: A circle is round and has no corners.


PRACTICE 2
Which shape has 3 sides?

A. Rectangle
B. Circle
C. Triangle

Answer: C
Explanation: A triangle has 3 sides.


PRACTICE 3
Which shape has 4 equal sides?

A. Circle
B. Triangle
C. Square

Answer: C
Explanation: A square has 4 equal sides.


PRACTICE 4
Which shape looks like a door?

A. Circle
B. Triangle
C. Rectangle

Answer: C
Explanation: A door is usually shaped like a rectangle.


PRACTICE 5
Which object is like a circle?

A. Ball
B. Book
C. Plate

Answer: C
Explanation: A plate has a round circle face.


PRACTICE 6
Which object is like a rectangle?

A. Orange
B. Button
C. Book

Answer: C
Explanation: A book often has a rectangle face.


PRACTICE 7
Which object is like a triangle?

A. Clock face
B. Lunchbox lid
C. Sandwich cut in half

Answer: C
Explanation: A sandwich cut corner to corner can make triangle shapes.


PRACTICE 8
Which shape has corners?

A. Circle
B. Round ball
C. Square

Answer: C
Explanation: A square has corners. A circle does not.


PRACTICE 9
Which shape does not have straight sides?

A. Triangle
B. Rectangle
C. Circle

Answer: C
Explanation: A circle is curved and has no straight sides.


PRACTICE 10
Which two shapes belong together because both have 4 sides?

A. Square and rectangle
B. Circle and triangle
C. Triangle and circle

Answer: A
Explanation: Squares and rectangles both have 4 sides.


PRACTICE 11
Which shape does not belong?

circle, circle, square, circle

A. First circle
B. Last circle
C. Square

Answer: C
Explanation: The square does not belong because the others are circles.


PRACTICE 12
Which shape does not belong?

triangle, square, rectangle, circle

A. Triangle
B. Circle
C. Rectangle

Answer: B
Explanation: The circle does not have straight sides or corners.


PRACTICE 13
Which group has only circles?

A. plate, coin, wheel
B. book, door, window
C. roof, flag, sandwich

Answer: A
Explanation: A plate, coin and wheel are round like circles.


PRACTICE 14
Which group has only rectangles?

A. ball, plate, coin
B. book, phone, door
C. cone, hat, roof

Answer: B
Explanation: A book, phone and door often have rectangle faces.


PRACTICE 15
Which shape could you make with 3 straight sticks?

A. Triangle
B. Circle
C. Oval

Answer: A
Explanation: Three straight sticks can make a triangle.


PRACTICE 16
Which shape could you make with 4 equal straight sticks?

A. Circle
B. Square
C. Triangle

Answer: B
Explanation: Four equal straight sticks can make a square.


PRACTICE 17
Which reason best describes a square?

A. It has no corners.
B. It has 3 sides.
C. It has 4 equal sides.

Answer: C
Explanation: A square has 4 equal sides and 4 corners.


PRACTICE 18
Which reason best describes a circle?

A. It is round.
B. It has 4 corners.
C. It has 3 sides.

Answer: A
Explanation: A circle is round and has no corners.


PRACTICE 19
A child sorts shapes into “round” and “not round”.
Where should the circle go?

A. Not round
B. Round
C. Both groups

Answer: B
Explanation: A circle belongs in the round group.


PRACTICE 20
A child sorts shapes into “has corners” and “no corners”.
Where should the triangle go?

A. Has corners
B. No corners
C. Round shapes

Answer: A
Explanation: A triangle has corners.


PRACTICE 21
Which object could be sorted with a ball?

A. Orange
B. Book
C. Door

Answer: A
Explanation: An orange and a ball are both round objects.


PRACTICE 22
Which object could be sorted with a box?

A. Coin
B. Dice
C. Plate

Answer: B
Explanation: A dice and a box have flat faces and corners.


PRACTICE 23
Which shape is best for drawing a simple house roof?

A. Circle
B. Triangle
C. Rectangle

Answer: B
Explanation: A simple roof is often drawn as a triangle.


PRACTICE 24
Why could a window and a book be sorted together?

A. Both are usually round.
B. Both are usually rectangular.
C. Both have no corners.

Answer: B
Explanation: A window and a book often have rectangle shapes.
