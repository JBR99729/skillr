This page supports steady Year 2 growth by building on early skills with more structure and a wider range of maths and science topics. It is especially useful for children who are ready to practise place value, measurement, simple multiplication and early scientific observation with more confidence.

Explore free printable Year 2 worksheets for Maths and Science. These resources are designed to support classroom learning, home practice and skill development through clear, structured activities that feel manageable and purposeful.

If you need worksheets for a particular topic, please contact us at skillrhublearning@gmail.com. We are a small team working part-time, but we will do our best to prioritise useful worksheet requests.

If a child needs a refresher first, revisit the Foundation worksheets for counting, sorting and simple science, or the Year 1 worksheets for number patterns and measurement.
