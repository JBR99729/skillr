Start with these Year 2 Maths topics

Choose the skill your learner needs, or work through these lessons in order. Each guide starts with a worked example and has a short check with an answer to reveal.

  1. Addition and subtraction facts. Use a known addition fact to find a missing part.
  2. Addition and subtraction strategies. Split numbers into tens and ones and show the jumps on a number line.
  3. Equal groups, arrays and division. Work out how many groups you can make or how much goes in each group.
  4. Practical problems and money. Draw a model, choose an operation and explain what the answer means.
