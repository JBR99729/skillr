Choose a synonym that fits the context

Name: __________________________ Date: ______________

Explain your choices using evidence from the example. Give reasons where requested.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

Choose a suitable antonym for ‘generous’ when describing a person who will not share: noisy, selfish or cheerful.

2. Try the skill

In ‘a brief visit’, can ‘brief’ be replaced with ‘tiny’ without changing how natural the sentence sounds? Explain.

3. Apply and explain

Replace ‘walked’ with a more precise word in ‘The exhausted hikers walked slowly to camp.’ Explain the effect.

4. Apply and explain

Write a sentence using ‘bright’ to describe light, then name an antonym that fits that sentence.

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9E4LA11

  1. Selfish. It contrasts with generous in this context.
  2. No. ‘Brief’ describes a short time; ‘tiny’ normally describes size.
  3. For example, ‘trudged’ suggests slow, effortful walking and fits the hikers' exhaustion.
  4. For example, ‘The lamp gave a bright light.’ ‘Dim’ is an appropriate antonym here.

If help is needed: Words with related meanings may differ in strength or fit different contexts. Check the whole sentence before substituting a synonym.
