AC9E4LY09 • Year 4 English

Reading and Writing Complex Multisyllabic Words

understand how to use and apply phonological and morphological knowledge to read and write multisyllabic words with more complex letter combinations, including a variety of vowel sounds and known prefixes and suffixes

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students analyse long words by spoken syllables, grapheme patterns and meaningful morphemes, blend for pronunciation and check the result against sentence meaning and known word families.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Model → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for syllable and morpheme boundaries must match: They serve different purposes and may divide differently.
