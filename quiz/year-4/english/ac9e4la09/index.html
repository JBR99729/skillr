AC9E4LA09 • Year 4 English

Tense and Its Impact on Meaning

understand past, present and future tenses and their impact on meaning in a sentence

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students identify verb tense and aspect, maintain logical time relationships, use tense shifts deliberately and explain how tense influences immediacy, sequence and certainty.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Model → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for every verb in a text must use one tense: Tense can shift when the time frame changes clearly.
