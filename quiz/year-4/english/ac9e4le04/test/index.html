Literary Devices and Deliberate Word Play

Read the quick preparation notes, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Students identify devices such as metaphor, simile, personification, alliteration, rhythm, repetition, onomatopoeia, pun and double meaning, then explain how each contributes to imagery, sound, emphasis, humour or theme.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Model → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for naming device completes analysis: Connect evidence to local and whole-text meaning.
