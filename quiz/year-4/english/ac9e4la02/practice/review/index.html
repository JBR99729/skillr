Before you begin

Report: “The new playground opened on Monday.” Evaluation added: “The brilliant new playground opened on Monday.” Both preserve the same opening and day. “Brilliant” adds approval; it does not add a checked detail. “The playground is exciting” also evaluates but leaves out the opening day, so it is not a complete replacement for this report.
