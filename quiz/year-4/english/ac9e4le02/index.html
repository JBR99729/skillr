AC9E4LE02 • Year 4 English

Responding to Literary Structure and Language

describe the effects of text structures and language features in literary texts when responding to and sharing opinions

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students move beyond liking or disliking a text by identifying structural and language choices, explaining their effects and using precise evidence to support a personal response.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Model → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for opinion counts as analysis by itself: Name the feature, evidence and effect.
