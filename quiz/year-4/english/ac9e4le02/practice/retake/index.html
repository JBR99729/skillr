Before you restart

  • Students move beyond liking or disliking a text by identifying structural and language choices, explaining their effects and using precise evidence to support a personal response.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Model → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for opinion counts as analysis by itself: Name the feature, evidence and effect.
