Before you practise

Read each supplied story extract or poem. Notice a particular structure or language choice, explain its effect and use evidence to support an opinion. Useful terms include noun group, repetition, simile, metaphor, personification, exaggeration, flashback, tension and resolution.

The full Practice bank includes eight tasks that ask you to write or dictate and share a real response. An adult checks those responses separately from the multiple-choice score. Different opinions can be valid when the text supports them.
