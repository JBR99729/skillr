Before you restart

Keep your message clear while choosing language that suits the listener, purpose and situation. Formal and informal language are not the same as polite and rude.

Check your choice: Explain an exact word or phrase. Keep the original meaning when revising. Welcome different ways to contribute, and check preferred names and local guidance instead of guessing.
