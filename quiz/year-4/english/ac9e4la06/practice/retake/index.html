Before you begin

“We sheltered because the hail began.” Main clause: “We sheltered.” Dependent clause: “because the hail began.” The dependent clause gives the reason. In “The shelter that has a green roof is open”, “that has a green roof” describes shelter; the main clause is “The shelter is open”.
