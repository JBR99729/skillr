Before the test

The full Test bank includes editing decisions and four writing or revision tasks. Your shuffled attempt may include a selection of these. Have paper or a writing device ready, or use your usual dictation support. Submit your actual scene, revisions and explanations for adult review.

Some tasks need a peer exchange; follow the selected question’s instructions. If a peer is unavailable, leave that collaboration evidence unassessed; an adult-supported rehearsal is useful practice. Do not count finishing a self-check as an automatically correct answer.
