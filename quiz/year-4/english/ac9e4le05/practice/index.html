Creating and Editing Literary Texts

The full Practice bank has 48 questions. Each shuffled attempt contains five questions.

Before you practise: create and edit stories

Read each supplied draft and the purpose for the change. Compare how an edit develops events, character or setting. Then complete the actual writing tasks; multiple-choice answers alone do not show that you can create a story.

Have paper or a writing device ready. You may dictate using your usual support. Keep the original and revised versions. Some tasks need a peer: exchange specific comments, ask a question and record the reply. An adult checks the completed writing and genuine peer evidence.
