AC9E4LE05 • Year 4 English

Creating and Editing Literary Texts

create and edit literary texts by developing storylines, characters and settings

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students plan a coherent narrative, develop characters through goals and choices, make setting influence events and revise structure, language, cohesion and conventions rather than treating editing as spelling correction only.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Model → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for editing means fixing spelling only: Revision addresses storyline, character, setting, pacing and clarity.
