AC9M4SP02 · Before you begin

Grid references identify positions consistently. A system must define how letters, numbers or coordinates are read, and pathway instructions must use a fixed orientation and clear sequence.

AC9M4SP02 • Homework

Grid References, Directions and Pathways worksheet

Use this printable homework sheet for written working, application and reflection alongside the online learning activities.

Open practice

Eight independent written and practical tasks

Use separate paper for drawings and save screenshots of digital work. An adult checks the actual work using the answers below.

Task 1

Draw columns A–F and rows 1–4 increasing upward on a north-up map. Put a gate at B1 and a garden at E3. Draw and describe a route between them, recording every visited cell.

Task 2

Create a spreadsheet with Day in A1 and Cups of water in B1. Observe three days or use three clearly labelled classroom observations. Enter labels in A2–A4 and measured counts in B2–B4. Identify the second count cell.

Task 3

Draw a bird’s-eye playground scene with two swings and a bench. Give a partner a description of one swing without a grid. Then add a labelled grid and give a reference-based description. Record what became clearer or what still needed detail.

Task 4

Draw a picture in four columns and three rows of 1 cm squares. Copy it to a matching grid of 2 cm squares. Mark three corresponding points and give the new grid width and height.

Task 5

On a north-up A–F,1–6 grid with rows increasing upward, start a token at D2 facing west. Act out forward two,turn right,forward three. Record the finish cell and facing direction.

Task 6

On the same north-up grid, a route from A2 to D4 is written east two,north two. Trace it, find the actual finish and repair the instructions in one way.

Task 7

Make two grids: a map with row 1 at the bottom and a spreadsheet with row 1 at the top. Both have columns A–D and four rows. Mark C3, then mark the cell directly above it in each system.

Task 8

On a north-up A–E,1–5 grid with rows increasing upward, mark C2 as blocked. Draw two routes from B2 to D2 that avoid it, give compass instructions and compare their number of moves.

Answers and adult review checks

Task 1

Answer: One route: B1,C1,D1,E1,E2,E3; east three, then north two.

Inspect labelled map, accurate placement and consistent route. Accept alternative legal paths with all cells recorded.

Task 2

Answer: The second count is in B3. Values depend on the actual observations.

Inspect saved spreadsheet/screenshot with headings, actual recorded data and correct cells; distinguish observations from invented data.

Task 3

Answer: A cell reference supplies a shared location; objects sharing one cell still need extra detail.

Inspect before/after scene and actual comparison with the partner’s attempt, labels and a specific explanation.

Task 4

Answer: The enlarged grid is 8 cm wide and 6 cm high.

Inspect both drawings, consistent cell-by-cell placement, three matched points and both correct dimensions.

Task 5

Answer: D2,C2,B2,B3,B4,B5; finish B5 facing north.

Require actual token simulation or a traced record, turn from west to north and correct final location/direction.

Task 6

Answer: Actual finish C4. Change east two to east three, or add one eastward move at the end.

Inspect traced route, correct C4 diagnosis and a fully corrected path to D4.

Task 7

Answer: Map: C4; spreadsheet: C2.

Inspect labels, C3 positions, correct above cells and explanation that the row-number directions differ.

Task 8

Answer: Examples: north one,east two,south one; south one,east two,north one. Both use four moves.

Inspect two valid distinct routes and counts; accept other valid routes with an accurate comparison.
