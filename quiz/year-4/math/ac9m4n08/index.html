AC9M4N08 • Year 4 Maths

Mathematical Modelling in Multi-step Problems

use mathematical modelling to solve practical problems involving additive and multiplicative situations including financial contexts; formulate the problems using number sentences and choose efficient calculation strategies, using digital tools where appropriate; interpret and communicate solutions in terms of the situation

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • A mathematical model simplifies a real situation while preserving the relationships needed to answer the question. Strong solutions explain assumptions, operations, units and the meaning of the result.
  • Use this routine: Understand context → Define quantities → Represent → Formulate operations → Solve → Validate → Communicate.
  • Watch for all numbers used automatically: Include only quantities relevant to the question and model.
