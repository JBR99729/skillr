Unit focus

  • The equals sign says both sides have the same value. The missing number can be anywhere in an addition or subtraction equation.
  • Use a part-part-whole or balance model. Addition and subtraction undo each other, so identify whether the unknown is a part or the whole.
  • You may swap addends without changing a sum. You cannot generally swap the numbers in a subtraction. Substitute your answer to check both sides.
