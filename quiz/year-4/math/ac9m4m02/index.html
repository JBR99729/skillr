Perimeter and Area of Shapes and Enclosed Spaces

recognise ways of measuring and approximating the perimeter and area of shapes and enclosed spaces, using appropriate formal and informal units

Choose a learning activity. Practice and Test use short shuffled attempts. Repeat quiz practice to access more of the full question bank.

Unit focus

  • Perimeter measures the whole boundary using length units. Area measures the enclosed surface using square units or other equal area units.
  • Count boundary lengths for perimeter and covered tiles for area. Fit area units without gaps or overlaps; combine part units when a boundary cuts through a tile.
  • Choose formal or informal units that suit the space. State when a result is approximate, and explain your measuring method.
