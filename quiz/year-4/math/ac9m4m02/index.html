Perimeter and Area of Shapes and Enclosed Spaces

recognise ways of measuring and approximating the perimeter and area of shapes and enclosed spaces, using appropriate formal and informal units

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Perimeter measures distance around a boundary in linear units. Area measures surface coverage in square units. Shapes can share an area but have different perimeters, or share a perimeter but have different areas.
  • Use this routine: Identify boundary or surface → Choose unit → Measure/count → Calculate or approximate → Label units → Compare.
  • Watch for perimeter and area formulas confused: Ask whether the problem concerns around or covering.
