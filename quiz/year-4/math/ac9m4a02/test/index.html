Multiplication Facts to 10 × 10 and Derived Strategies

Read the quick preparation notes, then start when you are ready.

SkillrHub quiz practice is built for curriculum-aligned revision with answer feedback and a clear review path.

Quick preparation

  • Use multiplication facts to 10 × 10 and their related division facts. An array shows equal rows and columns; turning it preserves the total.
  • Build a less familiar fact from known facts: double, halve, split groups, or add or remove one whole group. Explain what each part counts.
  • Extend facts to larger mental calculations without a calculator. Keep track of tens and hundreds, and check using the inverse relationship.

SkillrHub Test guide

Each attempt draws five questions from the full bank. Repeat an attempt to meet more questions and use the feedback to improve.

  • Worked example: Use 6 × 7 = 42 to find 60 × 7: sixty is six tens, so the result is 42 tens, or 420. The inverse check is 420 ÷ 7 = 60. For 23 × 4, split 23 into 20 and 3: 80 + 12 = 92.
  • Common mistake: One more group of 8 adds 8, not 1. When you use a fact with tens, keep the tens unit in the answer.
  • Materials and practical tasks: Have paper, a pencil and small paper cards ready. Drawing and game tasks require your actual work; an adult checks the array, calculations, rules and recorded play before awarding credit.
  • Review: Name the known fact that helped, explain your strategy and check a related division fact. Accept a different strategy when every step is correct.
