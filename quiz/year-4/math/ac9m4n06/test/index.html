Quick preparation

  • Choose a strategy that fits the numbers: partition, bridge a ten or hundred, compensate, or use a known fact. Explain why it helps.
  • Use arrays or equal-group models for multiplication and exact division. Identify the total, number of groups and number in each group.
  • A calculator or spreadsheet can help with larger calculations or checking. First write the correct calculation and estimate its result.

SkillrHub Test guide

  • Worked example: For 348 + 197, add 200 to get 548, then subtract the extra 3: 545. For 96 ÷ 6, share 60 and 36 into 6 equal groups: 10 + 6 = 16 in each group.
  • Common mistake: If you round a number to calculate more easily, undo the change. In a division story, check whether the answer counts groups or objects in each group.
