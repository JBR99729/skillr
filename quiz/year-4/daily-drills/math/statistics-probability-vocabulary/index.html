Statistics, Probability and Vocabulary

Read axes and scales carefully. Probability describes likelihood from impossible to certain.

Key ideas

  • tables and graphs
  • mode and frequency
  • chance language
  • simple probability
  • maths vocabulary

Worked example: probability 0 is impossible; probability 1 is certain.

Common trap: Most frequent means mode, not necessarily the largest numerical value.

Key words

frequency — number of occurrences
mode — most frequent value
probability — measure of likelihood
certain — will happen
impossible — cannot happen
