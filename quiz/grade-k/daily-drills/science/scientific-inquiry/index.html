Observe, Ask and Investigate

A simple investigation starts with a question. Predict, observe safely, record what happens and use observations as evidence.

What to remember

  • A clear question guides an investigation.
  • Predictions come before results.
  • Observations should record what actually happened.
  • Repeating an observation can check it.
  • A conclusion uses evidence.

Key words

question — what you want to find out
prediction — what you expect
observation — what you notice
record — store what happened
conclusion — answer based on evidence
How to use this drill: review the short notes when learning, then close them on later attempts and practise from memory. The rotating bank delays a full set repeat for 25 attempts on the same browser.

What this Foundation / Kindergarten science drill practises
