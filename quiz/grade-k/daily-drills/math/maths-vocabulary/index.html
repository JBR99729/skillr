Maths Vocabulary

Mathematical vocabulary helps you understand instructions and explain thinking. Read the definition carefully and connect each word to an example.

Key ideas

  • number words
  • comparison words
  • addition and sharing words
  • measurement words

Worked example: equal means the same amount.

Common trap: Choose the mathematical meaning, not just an everyday meaning.

Key words

numeral — a written number symbol
equal — the same amount
share — divide fairly
longer — having more length
capacity — how much a container can hold
pattern — an arrangement following a rule
position — where something is
data — collected information
