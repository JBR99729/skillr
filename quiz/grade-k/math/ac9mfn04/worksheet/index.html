1. 7 is the whole. The parts are 5 and 2.

Summary: 7 is the whole. The parts are 5 and 2. The whole includes both parts, using the same counters.

Hint: The whole includes both parts, using the same counters.

2. 7. Five and two combine to make seven.

Summary: 7. Five and two combine to make seven. Bring the two parts together to check.

Hint: Bring the two parts together to check.

3. 6 → 4 + 2; 7 → 5 + 2; 8 → 5 + 3.

Summary: 6 → 4 + 2; 7 → 5 + 2; 8 → 5 + 3. Check that each pair combines to make the named whole.

Hint: Check that each pair combines to make the named whole.

4. 4 and 2 make 6. The other pairs make larger wholes.

Summary: 4 and 2 make 6. The other pairs make larger wholes. Build six counters and split that same collection.

Hint: Build six counters and split that same collection.

5. For example, 4 and 1, then 3 and 2. Yes: 5 and 0 also make 5.

Summary: For example, 4 and 1, then 3 and 2. Yes: 5 and 0 also make 5. Move counters between the parts without adding or removing any.

Hint: Move counters between the parts without adding or removing any.

AC9MFN04 — Topic Practice 2 Answer Key

Teacher copy • this sheet only

6. 5 is missing because 3 and 5 make 8.

Summary: 5 is missing because 3 and 5 make 8. Build the whole of 8 and separate the visible part of 3.

Hint: Build the whole of 8 and separate the visible part of 3.

7. whole → 7; part → 2; same whole, different order → 2 + 4 and 4 + 2.

Summary: whole → 7; part → 2; same whole, different order → 2 + 4 and 4 + 2. Name both parts and the whole before matching.

Hint: Name both parts and the whole before matching.

8. 4 and 3 make 7, not 6. Keep the part of 4 and change 3 to 2; alternatively keep 3 and change 4 to 3.

Summary: 4 and 3 make 7, not 6. Keep the part of 4 and change 3 to 2; alternatively keep 3 and change 4 to 3. Both parts together must match the known whole.

Hint: Both parts together must match the known whole.

9. 5 are hidden. Four and five make nine. Uncover and recombine the same counters to check the whole.

Summary: 5 are hidden. Four and five make nine. Uncover and recombine the same counters to check the whole. Use the known whole and visible part instead of guessing.

Hint: Use the known whole and visible part instead of guessing.
