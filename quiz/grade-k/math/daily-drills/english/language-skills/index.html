Language Skills

Focus on clear, correct sentences. Read the whole sentence before deciding which grammar, punctuation or word choice is best.

Useful reminders

  • Sentences begin with a capital letter.
  • Statements usually end with a full stop.
  • Questions end with a question mark.
  • A noun can name a person, place, animal or thing.
  • A verb can show an action.

Key words

sentence — a group of words expressing a complete thought
grammar — rules for how words and sentences work
punctuation — marks that help organise meaning
spelling — correct arrangement of letters in words
edit — check and improve writing
Daily practice: these drills focus on useful basics to intermediate English skills. They are designed to build confidence through repeated practice, not to teach every English curriculum outcome.
