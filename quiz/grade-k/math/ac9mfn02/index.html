About This Foundation Subitising Quiz (AC9MFN02)

Subitising is the ability to instantly recognise the number of objects in a small group without counting them one by one. This interactive quiz directly addresses the Australian Curriculum (Version 9.0) Foundation Mathematics content description AC9MFN02 .

Australian Curriculum Alignments

  • Australian Curriculum (V9): AC9MFN02 (Foundation Year)
  • NSW Syllabus: MAE-RWN-01 (Representing Whole Numbers)
  • Victorian Curriculum 2.0: VC2MFN02 (Subitising)

Global Equivalents

This quiz also supports related early-years number learning goals in other curricula.

  • United States: Common Core CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.K.CC.B.4 and K.CC.B.5 (Kindergarten)
  • United Kingdom: EYFS / Key Stage 1 Number Milestones
  • New Zealand: NZC Number & Algebra Level 1

Skills Practised

  • Perceptual Subitising: Instantly naming small quantities from 1 to 3 using dice faces, dots and finger patterns.
  • Visual Arrays: Identifying quantities arranged in standard configurations, ten-frames and card patterns.
  • Conceptual Subitising: Combining smaller groups, such as seeing 3 and 2 and recognising 5 without sequential counting.
