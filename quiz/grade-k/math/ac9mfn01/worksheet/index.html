1. 12: ten and two more make twelve.

Summary: 12: ten and two more make twelve. Build the ten first, then add the two extra blocks.

2. 13 comes after 12 and before 14.

Summary: 13 comes after 12 and before 14. Say the count from 11 to 14 in order.

Hint: Say the count from 11 to 14 in order.

3. 0 matches the empty collection; 7 matches seven counters; 12 matches ten and two.

Summary: 0 matches the empty collection; 7 matches seven counters; 12 matches ten and two. Check how many objects each representation shows.

Hint: Check how many objects each representation shows.

4. Tray A is empty, so it shows zero objects.

Summary: Tray A is empty, so it shows zero objects. Zero describes a collection with no objects.

Hint: Zero describes a collection with no objects.

Summary: 2, 5, 11, 16. The number 11 belongs between 5 and 16 in this list. Use the existing 0–20 number line to check the order.

Hint: Use the existing 0–20 number line to check the order.

AC9MFN01 — Topic Practice 2 Answer Key

Teacher copy • this sheet only

6. 13 is one less than 14; 15 is one more.

Summary: 13 is one less than 14; 15 is one more. Move one step left and one step right from 14.

Hint: Move one step left and one step right from 14.

7. five → 5; twelve → 12; twenty → 20.

Summary: five → 5; twelve → 12; twenty → 20. Say each number name, then find its numeral.

Hint: Say each number name, then find its numeral.

8. No. On the tray, 0 tells how many objects there are. On the bus, 12 can identify its route; it need not count the people.

Summary: No. On the tray, 0 tells how many objects there are. On the bus, 12 can identify its route; it need not count the people. A numeral can show a quantity or act as a label.

Hint: A numeral can show a quantity or act as a label.

9. 0, 5, 11, 16. Swap 16 and 11 because 11 comes before 16 on the number line.

Summary: 0, 5, 11, 16. Swap 16 and 11 because 11 comes before 16 on the number line. Keep 0 and 5 in place and compare the last two numbers.

Hint: Keep 0 and 5 in place and compare the last two numbers.
