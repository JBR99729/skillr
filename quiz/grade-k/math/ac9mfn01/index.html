Foundation Mathematics • Number

Name, represent and order numbers to 20

Build confidence with numbers from zero to 20. Practise reading numerals, matching numbers to quantities, finding numbers before and after, and putting numbers in order.

Five questions are selected for each practice attempt.

What this practice covers

  • Read and recognise numerals from 0 to 20.
  • Connect a number name, numeral and quantity.
  • Find the number before, after and between.
  • Order numbers and use first, second, third, fourth and fifth.

Curriculum alignment

Australian, state and international equivalents

This practice is designed primarily for the Australian Curriculum, NSW Early Stage 1 and Victorian Foundation Mathematics. International rows show the closest comparable learning expectations.
