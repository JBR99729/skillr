AC9MFN01 Numbers to 20 Curriculum Alignment

This Foundation Mathematics quiz develops understanding of number names, numerals, representations and number order from zero to at least 20.

It is aligned with Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 Foundation Mathematics AC9MFN01 and closely related outcomes from New South Wales, Victoria and international curricula.

Australian Curriculum

  • AC9MFN01 — name, represent and order numbers including zero to at least 20 using physical and virtual materials and numerals.

New South Wales Curriculum

  • MAE-RWN-01 — demonstrates an understanding of how whole numbers indicate quantity.
  • MAE-RWN-02 — reads numerals and represents whole numbers to at least 20.

Victorian Curriculum

  • VC2MFN01 — name, represent and order numbers, including zero to at least 20, using physical and virtual materials and numerals.

Curriculum structures differ between countries. The standards below are the closest matches to the number recognition, representation and ordering skills developed in AC9MFN01.

Skills Practised

  • Recognising numerals from 0 to 20.
  • Matching number names with numerals.
  • Representing numbers with objects and pictures.
  • Understanding zero as representing no objects.
  • Counting forwards and backwards.
  • Identifying numbers before and after a given number.
  • Finding missing numbers in a sequence.
  • Ordering numbers from smallest to largest.
  • Connecting numerals with quantities.

How This Quiz Works

Each attempt selects 5 random questions from the AC9MFN01 question bank. Students can repeat the quiz to practise different combinations of number skills.

Questions may include number recognition, visual representations, missing numbers, number entry, true or false, ordering and other interactive question formats.
