About This Addition and Subtraction Quiz (AC9MFN05)

Early addition and subtraction begin with meaningful actions. Children join collections, add more objects, take objects away and compare what remains.

This quiz supports Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 Foundation Mathematics AC9MFN05 , which asks students to represent practical situations involving addition, subtraction and quantification using physical or virtual materials and counting or subitising strategies.

Australian Curriculum Alignment

  • Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 — AC9MFN05: represent practical situations involving addition, subtraction and quantification with physical and virtual materials and use counting or subitising strategies.

New South Wales Curriculum Alignment

  • MAE-CSQ-01: reasons about number relations to model addition and subtraction by combining and separating, and comparing collections.
  • MAE-RWN-01: supports quantification by developing understanding of how whole numbers indicate quantity.

Victorian Curriculum Alignment

  • Victorian Curriculum 2.0 — VC2MFN05: represent practical situations, including simple financial situations, involving addition, subtraction and quantification with physical and virtual materials and use counting or subitising strategies.

Skills Practised

  • Joining collections: combine two groups and determine how many objects there are altogether.
  • Adding more: recognise that adding objects increases a quantity.
  • Taking away: remove objects from a collection and determine how many remain.
  • Quantification: determine how many objects are present or needed in a practical situation.
  • Counting on: start from a known quantity and count forward as more objects are added.
  • Counting back: count backwards when objects are removed.
  • Subitising: recognise small quantities quickly and use them when joining or separating collections.
  • Story problems: connect mathematical actions to familiar situations involving toys, animals, food and classroom materials.
  • Addition and subtraction language: understand words such as altogether, more, left, take away, join and remain.

How This Quiz Works

The AC9MFN05 quiz can use a large bank of addition and subtraction questions ranging from simple visual joining and taking-away situations to more challenging missing-part and reasoning problems.

Each attempt randomly selects 5 questions from the full question bank so students can repeat the quiz and receive a different mixture of questions each time.

Question formats can include multiple choice, true or false, number entry, fill in the blank, multiple selection, ordering and drag-and-drop activities.
