Australian Curriculum Connection

This vocabulary quiz supports language used throughout Foundation Mathematics rather than assessing one individual content description.

Vocabulary is drawn from Foundation learning across:

  • Number
  • Algebra
  • Measurement
  • Space
  • Statistics

It can therefore be used alongside Foundation curriculum quizzes as regular vocabulary revision.

How to Use This Quiz

Students complete 5 randomly selected questions in each attempt.

Encourage children to read the word aloud, explain it in their own words and connect it with an example.

Repeated short practice helps children become familiar with the language they will see in classroom questions and maths activities.

Suitable for Daily Practice

This activity can be used as a two-to-five-minute warm-up at the beginning of a maths lesson, during numeracy rotations, for home practice or for quick revision.
