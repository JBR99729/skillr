AC9EFLY12 · Adult copy

Observe the method as well as the answer. Offer a smaller collection, counters, letter cards or a real object when a child needs support.

  1. Adult says dog. Expected d-o-g. Adult may repeat the spoken word without spelling it.
  2. man. The first sound changes; a and n remain.
  3. hen. Add e for the middle vowel sound.
  4. Adult says bed. Expected b-e-d. Listen for the child blending the completed word.
  5. pit. Only the first letter/sound changes. Accept oral explanation.
