AC9EFLY03 — Imaginative and informative texts

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

Which text is imaginative?

[A] A story about a talking wombat flying to the moon.[B] A fact sheet about wombat habitats.[C] A labelled diagram of a wombat.
2.

Which text is informative?

[A] A story about a cloud that goes to school.[B] A poem about a dancing star.[C] A page explaining how rain forms.
3.

A book says, ‘Koalas sleep for many hours each day’ and has labelled photos. What kind of text is it most likely?

[A] A shopping list.[B] Informative.[C] Imaginative.
4.

A story shows a fox wearing boots and speaking to a rabbit. What is a clue that it is imaginative?

[A] Animals are acting like people in an invented story.[B] The book uses words.[C] The pages have numbers.
5.

You need to find out what bees eat. Which text should you choose?

[A] A fairy tale about a bee king.[B] A rhyme about buzzing.[C] An information book about bees.
6.

Which image is more likely in an informative animal text?

[A] A talking fish driving a bus.[B] A labelled photo showing body parts.[C] A dragon wearing a crown.
7.

Which image is more likely in an imaginative story?

[A] A mouse sailing in a teacup.[B] A labelled map of Australia.[C] A chart showing frog growth.
8.

Two books are both about dinosaurs. One tells a made-up adventure and one gives facts. What is different?

[A] Only one can have pages.[B] Only one can use pictures.[C] Their purpose and content.

Which question helps you decide the text type?

[A] How tall is the reader?[B] Is this mainly telling an imagined story or giving information?[C] What colour are my shoes?
