Words work together to make a message

Name: __________________________ Date: ______________

Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

Use these words to tell a message: bird / sings / The.

2. Try the skill

Change ‘The dog sleeps’ to tell about a cat. Which word changes?

3. Apply and explain

Say a sentence about someone jumping. Draw what your sentence means.

4. Apply and explain

A sentence says ‘The frog hops.’ Which words tell who it is about, and which word tells the action?

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9EFLA06

  1. The bird sings.
  2. Change dog to cat: The cat sleeps.
  3. For example, ‘The child jumps.’ Accept a meaningful spoken sentence matched by the drawing.
  4. ‘The frog’ tells who; ‘hops’ tells the action.

If help is needed: A row of familiar words does not automatically make a meaningful sentence. Say the complete message and check that the words work together.

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

In ‘The cat ate’, which word names the animal?

[A] cat[B] ate[C] the
2.

In ‘The cat ate’, which word tells the action?

[A] cat[B] the[C] ate
3.

In ‘The black cat slept’, which word describes the cat?

[A] the[B] black[C] slept
4.

Which words belong together to name the animal?

[A] the small dog[B] ran quickly[C] small ran
5.

In ‘Birds sing’, which words show who and what they do?

[A] Birds / birds[B] sing / sing[C] Birds / sing
6.

Which sentence connects a describing word to a naming word clearly?

[A] Ball the bounced red.[B] The red ball bounced.[C] The bounced red quickly.
7.

In ‘My wet shoes squeak’, what does ‘wet’ describe?

[A] shoes[B] squeak[C] my
8.

In ‘The baby laughs’, what does ‘laughs’ tell us?

[A] What colour the baby is.[B] Where the baby lives.[C] What the baby does.
