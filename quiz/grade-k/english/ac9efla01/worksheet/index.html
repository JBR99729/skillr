AC9EFLA01 — Choose Words That Fit

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

You cannot find your hat at school. What could you ask your teacher?

[A] Where is my hat?[B] My hat is blue.[C] I like hats.
2.

You need a pencil from your teacher. What is the best thing to say?

[A] Pencil![B] Give me one.[C] Can I please have a pencil?
3.

Which sentence tells what you think?

[A] Where is the book?[B] I think this book is funny.[C] Can I have the book?
4.

Match each person to words that suit the situation.

A. friend

B. teacher

C. shopkeeper

1. Can I play too?

2. Excuse me, can you help me, please?

3. Can I please have an apple?

Matches: __________________________

5.

Mia says to her teacher, ‘Give me that book!’ Write a better way for Mia to ask.

6.

Your friend has the glue. You need it next. What could you say?

[A] Can I use the glue when you're finished?[B] That's mine now.[C] Give it!
7.

Your parent asks which story you liked. Write or say an opinion.

8.

You are in a shop and cannot find the milk. What could you ask the shopkeeper?
