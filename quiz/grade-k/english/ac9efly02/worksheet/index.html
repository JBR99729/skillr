Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

Your classmate is sharing an idea. What should you do?

[A] Talk over them.[B] Listen until they finish.[C] Start a different conversation.
2.

Which voice suits a quiet classroom conversation with one partner?

[A] A playground shout.[B] No voice at all when you need to answer.[C] A soft speaking voice.
3.

You are speaking to the whole class from the front. What should you do?

[A] Use a clear voice that everyone can hear.[B] Turn away and mumble.[C] Whisper so only one person hears.
4.

Which behaviour shows listening without interrupting?

[A] Call out while the speaker is talking.[B] Wait, then respond when the speaker finishes.[C] Start humming loudly.
5.

A teacher says, ‘Put your book away and sit on the mat.’ What shows good listening?

[A] Keep reading and ignore it.[B] Do a different activity.[C] Do the two steps in the instruction.
6.

Outside on the oval, your friend is several metres away. What might change?

[A] You may need a louder voice than indoors.[B] You should stop using words.[C] You must always whisper.
7.

In a small-group discussion, how can you contribute?

[A] Speak for the whole time.[B] Listen to others and add an idea when it is your turn.[C] Ignore every other idea.
8.

Which is an informal speaking situation?

[A] A printed road sign.[B] A silent worksheet.[C] Pretend shop play with a friend.

9.Extension

If looking directly at the speaker is not culturally appropriate, what is still important?

[A] Show listening in a respectful way that suits the context.[B] Walk away every time someone speaks.[C] Interrupt constantly.
10.Extension

Which choice uses volume well?

[A] Use the same shout everywhere.[B] Speak softly beside a friend and more clearly to a large group.[C] Whisper across a noisy playground.
