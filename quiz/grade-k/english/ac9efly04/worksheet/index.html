Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

You see the word ‘sat’. Which sounds help you read it?

[A] s-o-p[B] s-a-t[C] m-a-p
2.

After saying /m/ /a/ /p/, what word do you read?

[A] mop[B] mat[C] map
3.

Where should you begin reading a simple page of English text?

[A] At the first word in the reading order.[B] At the last letter of the page.[C] At a random word near the bottom.
4.

The sentence says ‘The cat sat.’ You read ‘The cat sun.’ What should you do?

[A] Keep going without checking.[B] Stop and check the letters because the sentence does not make sense.[C] Change every word in the sentence.
5.

You come to an unknown CVC word ‘fin’. What is a good first step?

[A] Guess from the page colour.[B] Skip every unknown word.[C] Say the sounds /f/ /i/ /n/.
6.

Which word is a familiar high-frequency word that may be recognised quickly?

[A] the[B] vup[C] zog
7.

A picture shows a dog, but the printed word begins with c. Should you guess ‘dog’?

[A] Yes, pictures always replace decoding.[B] No, check the letters and sounds.[C] Yes, the first letter does not matter.
8.

You read ‘A red hen ran.’ Which reading behaviour shows one spoken word matched to one written word?

[A] Say two extra words between every printed word.[B] Skip all spaces.[C] Point or track each word as you say it.

9.Extension

What should you do if a sentence still does not make sense after decoding a word?

[A] Pause and ask for support or reread.[B] Replace the whole sentence with a guess.[C] Pretend it makes sense.
10.Extension

Which strategy uses both phonics and meaning?

[A] Look only at the picture.[B] Blend the letters, then check whether the word fits the sentence.[C] Say the first word that comes to mind.
