AC9EFLY05 — Comprehension strategies

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

The cover shows dark clouds and a child holding an umbrella. What is a sensible prediction?

[A] The story must be about baking a cake.[B] It may rain in the story.[C] Nothing can happen next.
2.

A story says, ‘The puppy ran to the gate when Dad came home.’ What is the key event?

[A] The gate has hinges.[B] Dad wears shoes.[C] The puppy ran to greet Dad.
3.

An information text says, ‘Penguins have feathers and cannot fly.’ Which fact can you tell?

[A] Penguins have feathers.[B] Penguins can all fly.[C] Penguins are made of ice.
4.

While listening to a story, which question helps you understand a character?

[A] What colour is my pencil?[B] Why did the character hide?[C] How many chairs are here?
5.

The story describes warm sand under bare feet. What strategy is being used when you picture it in your mind?

[A] Skipping.[B] Alphabetising.[C] Visualising.
6.

A character moves to a new house. You remember moving house too. What strategy are you using?

[A] Connecting.[B] Rhyming.[C] Counting.
7.

Which is the best short summary of ‘A bird builds a nest, lays eggs and feeds its chicks’?

[A] The word bird has four letters.[B] A bird cares for its nest and chicks.[C] The nest is somewhere.
8.

You are listening for what a character is wearing. What should you do?

[A] Ignore every description.[B] Think only about the cover colour.[C] Listen for details about the character's clothes.

Optional Enrichment Extension

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page only for students who are ready for extension.

9.Extension

A sequence of pictures shows seed → sprout → plant. What comprehension action helps explain it?

[A] Tell the ideas in order.[B] Name only the page number.[C] Mix the pictures randomly.
10.Extension

After reading, you realise you do not understand why the event happened. What could you do?

[A] Pretend you understood.[B] Ask a question and reread or discuss the clue.[C] Forget the whole text.
