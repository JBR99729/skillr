AC9EFLA05 — Sentences express ideas

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

Which is a complete sentence?

[A] The dog is sleeping.[B] The sleeping.[C] Dog the is.
2.

Which group is only a sentence fragment?

[A] Dad will pick me up after school.[B] The bus stops here.[C] After school.
3.

Which sentence makes sense?

[A] Sat chair the on boy.[B] The boy sat on the chair.[C] The chair sat on the boy.
4.

Which words express a complete idea?

[A] Mia found her hat.[B] Found her.[C] Mia hat found her.
5.

Which choice is a sentence?

[A] Can fly.[B] Birds the can.[C] Birds can fly.
6.

Why do ‘The dog chased the cat’ and ‘The cat chased the dog’ mean different things?

[A] One sentence has no words.[B] The word order changes who does the chasing.[C] The words have different colours.
7.

Which group needs more words to become a sentence?

[A] In the garden.[B] The children play in the garden.[C] A bee lands on a flower.
8.

Which sentence tells one clear idea?

[A] Red kite high my.[B] Flew high.[C] My red kite flew high.
