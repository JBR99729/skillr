AC9EFLE02 — Responding to stories

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

The puppy finds its lost owner. Which response connects a feeling to the event?

[A] I felt happy when the puppy found its owner.[B] Puppies have four legs.[C] The page has a picture.
2.

A character shares lunch with a friend. Which response is about the character?

[A] Lunch can be in a box.[B] The story has ten pages.[C] I think the character was kind.
3.

The bear loses its hat. Which response fits the event?

[A] Bears are animals.[B] I felt worried because the bear could not find its hat.[C] Hats can be red.
4.

A character keeps trying to climb after falling. What might you think?

[A] The character is determined.[B] The book is heavy.[C] The cover is shiny.
5.

Which drawing would be a personal response to a story about a storm?

[A] A random row of numbers.[B] A picture unrelated to the story.[C] A drawing of the storm scene with a worried face beside it.
6.

A story ends with two friends making up after an argument. Which response fits?

[A] Friends always agree.[B] I am glad they listened to each other.[C] The last page is paper.
7.

A character refuses to help and another child feels sad. Which response gives a thought?

[A] I think the character could have helped.[B] The sentence has six words.[C] The picture uses green.
8.

Which sentence connects a story event to your own experience?

[A] Camping has seven letters.[B] The book is square.[C] The camping scene reminds me of camping with my family.

9.Extension

A character is scared of the dark. Which response is suitable?

[A] The page should be brighter.[B] I understand because I sometimes feel scared in the dark too.[C] Dark is a colour word.
10.Extension

Which response tells both what happened and what you think?

[A] Mina returned the lost toy, and I think that was honest.[B] Mina is a name.[C] The toy is small.
