1. enormous

Summary: Enormous is the vocabulary choice whose meaning is very big because we connect the word to its meaning and use it in a fitting familiar context.

3. enormous → very big; observe → look carefully; prefer → like one choice more

Summary: Each useful word is connected to the idea it communicates because we connect the word to its meaning and use it in a fitting familiar context.

Hint: Put each word into a short sentence to test the match.

Summary: The explanation gives the topic word's meaning inside a familiar sport situation because we connect the word to its meaning and use it in a fitting familiar context.

Hint: Say what the player does and where the player is usually found.

AC9EFLA08 — Topic Practice 2 Answer Key

Teacher copy • this sheet only

6. shy or not confident

Summary: The hiding behaviour provides context for the unfamiliar word's meaning because we connect the word to its meaning and use it in a fitting familiar context.

Hint: Use what the kitten does after the word.

7. describing a very small seed → tiny; watching a leaf closely → observe; choosing one game over another → prefer

Summary: Each word fits the meaning needed in its familiar context because we connect the word to its meaning and use it in a fitting familiar context.

Hint: Decide whether each situation needs a size, looking or choosing word.

8. Enormous fits when we need to say something is very big, while red fits when we need to name its colour, so length does not decide which word is best.

Summary: The comparison chooses vocabulary by precise meaning instead of word length because we connect the word to its meaning and use it in a fitting familiar context.

Hint: Ask whether the speaker needs to describe size or colour.

9. Habitat means the place where a living thing lives; a pond is a frog habitat; for example, ‘The pond is a habitat for frogs.’

Summary: The response develops new vocabulary through meaning, example and contextual use because we connect the word to its meaning and use it in a fitting familiar context.

Hint: State the meaning, give a familiar example and then say a new sentence.
