Familiar Text Types and Their Purposes

Review the key ideas, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Stories are usually created to entertain or imagine; informative texts are created to share facts and explanations.
  • Book covers, headings, pictures, labels and familiar symbols can give clues about a text’s purpose.
  • Texts can be grouped by purpose or topic, even when they use different formats.
  • Common trap: Deciding from one picture only instead of using several clues from the text.
