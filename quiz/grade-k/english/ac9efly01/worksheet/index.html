AC9EFLY01 — Topic Practice 1 Answer Key

Teacher copy • this sheet only

1. entertain with characters and events

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; the story clue points to an imaginative sequence designed for enjoyment.

Hint: Think about the job of a text with a character and an adventure.

2. inform readers about wombats

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; the title promises factual information about the topic.

Hint: The word facts tells you which job the page is trying to do.

3. exit sign → guide people towards a way out; animal story → entertain with characters and events; frog fact page → inform about frogs

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; each text is paired with the practical or communicative job its clues support.

Hint: Match the exit sign first by asking what action it helps people take.

4. a labelled photograph and the title Animal Facts

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; the label, photograph and fact title are clues that the book is designed to inform.

Hint: Look for evidence that promises real information rather than an adventure.

5. Both share the topic farm animals, but the story mainly entertains and the fact book mainly informs.

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; topic and purpose are identified separately so two related texts can do different jobs.

Hint: First say what both texts are about, then say what each one is for.

AC9EFLY01 — Topic Practice 2 Answer Key

Teacher copy • this sheet only

6. guide people towards an exit

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; the doorway, moving figure and arrow combine as a familiar guidance symbol.

Hint: Follow the arrow and ask where the pictured person is going.

7. instruct

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; numbered action steps tell the reader what to do and in what order.

Hint: Choose the purpose word for giving directions to complete a task.

8. The story entertains using characters and events, the fact page informs using facts or labels, and the care instructions guide actions using ordered steps.

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; each purpose is justified by a distinct content or layout clue within texts on the same topic.

Hint: For each text, answer two questions: What is its job and what feature shows that job.

9. A valid response creates a fitting cover feature, names the text as a story or informative book and explains whether the clue signals entertaining or informing.

Summary: Use this method: identify the text from its clues, name what it is for, then explain the purpose; the designed cover feature communicates a plausible text type and purpose before the reader checks inside.

Hint: Choose character and adventure clues for a story or photograph, label and fact clues for an informative book.
