AC9EFLY01 — Familiar texts and their purposes

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

What is the main purpose of a story book?

[A] To tell or share a story.[B] To show a road rule.[C] To list the price of food.
2.

What is the purpose of an EXIT sign?

[A] To tell a fairy tale.[B] To teach a rhyme.[C] To show people where to leave.
3.

Which text would help you learn facts about frogs?

[A] A pretend menu with no frog facts.[B] An information book about frogs.[C] A birthday card.
4.

A school crossing sign is used to do what?

[A] Help people cross safely.[B] Tell a bedtime story.[C] Show someone's favourite food.
5.

Which cover is most likely an imaginative text?

[A] A cover titled Facts About Weather.[B] A bus timetable.[C] A cover showing a talking dragon on an adventure.
6.

Which cover is most likely informative?

[A] The Lost Fairy Crown.[B] Amazing Facts About Sharks.[C] The Magical Moon Rabbit.
7.

Three texts are all about farm animals. Why might we group them together?

[A] They share the same topic.[B] They must all have the same purpose.[C] They must all be stories.
8.

A school badge appears on a uniform. Why is the symbol used?

[A] To tell the weather.[B] To give cooking steps.[C] To show the school or group it belongs to.

9.Extension

Which clue helps you work out a text's purpose?

[A] The colour of the table nearby.[B] What the words and images are trying to help the reader know or do.[C] The reader's shoe size.
10.Extension

A recipe and a story both have words. What is different?

[A] Their purposes are different.[B] Only the story is a text.[C] Only the recipe can have pictures.
