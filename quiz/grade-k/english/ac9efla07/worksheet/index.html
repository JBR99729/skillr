AC9EFLA07 — Images and words build meaning

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

The words say, ‘The puppy hid.’ The picture shows the puppy under a bed. What does the picture add?

[A] Where the puppy hid.[B] The puppy's name.[C] The day of the week.
2.

The words say, ‘A storm is coming.’ The image shows dark clouds and bending trees. What do they do together?

[A] They tell two unrelated stories.[B] The image cancels the words.[C] They both help show stormy weather.
3.

A picture shows three chicks, but the words say, ‘The hen watched her babies.’ What does the picture add?

[A] It shows the book price.[B] It shows how many chicks there are.[C] It tells the author's address.
4.

The words say, ‘Sam wore boots.’ The picture also shows a yellow raincoat. What does only the picture tell?

[A] Sam wore a yellow raincoat too.[B] Sam's name is Sam.[C] Sam wore boots.
5.

Which is the best way to understand a picture book page?

[A] Ignore every picture.[B] Read only the first word.[C] Use clues from both words and pictures.
6.

The image shows a smiling child, but the words say, ‘I felt nervous inside.’ What should a reader understand?

[A] Pictures always tell everything.[B] The picture and words give different clues about the feeling.[C] The words must be wrong.
7.

An information page says, ‘Bees collect nectar.’ The image labels a flower and a bee. What does the image help with?

[A] Seeing the bee and flower connected to the information.[B] Finding a story ending.[C] Choosing a favourite colour.
8.

A story says, ‘The room was messy.’ The picture shows toys, books and clothes on the floor. What does the picture add?

[A] A new main character.[B] The title of another book.[C] Examples of what made the room messy.

9.Extension

If you cover the picture on a page, what might you lose?

[A] The ability to turn the page.[B] Visual details that add to the words.[C] Every written word.
10.Extension

[A] Information the image does not show.[B] The colours in the image.[C] The shape of the page.
