Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

Which is a good short sentence to record an event?

[A] Went park the.[B] We went to the park.[C] Park we the to.
2.

You write ‘the dog ran.’ What should you edit?

[A] Remove every space.[B] Change dog to a random word.[C] Change the first t to a capital T.
3.

You write ‘I can hop’ at the end of a sentence. What is missing?

[A] An ending punctuation mark.[B] A picture of a bus.[C] Another capital in the middle of every word.
4.

You want to spell ‘cat’. Which letters match the sounds /c/ /a/ /t/?

[A] c-o-g[B] c-a-t[C] m-a-t
5.

Which action is shared editing?

[A] Hide the writing and never reread it.[B] Replace the whole idea with a drawing only.[C] Read the sentence together and circle the missing capital.
6.

A child writes about a class trip and draws the bus beside the sentence. What are they doing?

[A] Using writing and drawing to communicate an event.[B] Avoiding communication.[C] Making an unrelated pattern.
7.

Why might a child read their own sentence back to an experienced writer?

[A] To make the paper heavier.[B] To check that the writing says what they meant.[C] To avoid thinking about meaning.
8.

Which spelling is the best attempt for the spoken word /d/ /o/ /g/?

[A] dig[B] fog[C] dog

9.Extension

Which sentence has clear boundaries?

[A] Mia ran. She stopped.[B] Mia ran she stopped[C] mia ran she stopped
10.Extension

You reread ‘I lik mi dog.’ Which edit uses sound-letter knowledge?

[A] Delete the whole sentence because it is not perfect.[B] Change ‘lik’ to ‘like’ if that spelling has been learnt, while keeping the intended sentence.[C] Change dog to sun for no reason.
