Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

A story child feels nervous on the first day of school. Which response makes a connection?

[A] I felt nervous on my first day too.[B] The book has pages.[C] School starts in the morning.
2.

A story family eats together outside. Your family usually eats inside. What could you say?

[A] The picture has a table.[B] All families eat the same way.[C] That is different from my family because we usually eat inside.
3.

Which response shares an idea about a poem?

[A] The page is made of paper.[B] The poem reminds me of playing in the rain.[C] The poem has black letters.
4.

A picture book shows a child visiting grandparents. Which response shows a similar experience?

[A] I visit my grandparents too.[B] Grandparents are adults.[C] The cover is blue.
5.

A story is set in a remote community you have not visited. Which response is thoughtful?

[A] It must be exactly like my home.[B] I should ignore the setting.[C] This place is different from where I live, so I can notice what is new to me.
6.

A story character eats a food that is new to you. What could you say?

[A] Everyone eats the same food.[B] That food is different from what I usually eat, and I would like to learn about it.[C] The character is wrong to eat it.
7.

Which question helps you connect a story to your own life?

[A] Has something like this happened to me?[B] How many letters are on the cover?[C] Is the chair beside me red?
8.

A character helps a younger child. You have helped your sibling before. Which response fits?

[A] The character is on page five.[B] Helping is a six-letter word.[C] That reminds me of helping my sibling.

Optional Enrichment Extension

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page only for students who are ready for extension.

9.Extension

You hear a story told aloud and also see pictures with it. What can you share?

[A] Nothing because it was spoken.[B] An idea about the story and something the pictures helped me notice.[C] Only the number of chairs in the room.
10.Extension

Which response shows that two experiences can both be valid?

[A] The character's family does it differently from mine, and both are ways families can live.[B] My way is the only right way.[C] Different means wrong.
