AC9EFLA02 — Likes, dislikes and preferences

Core Class Worksheet

Name: ____________________    Date: ____________

Print this page for the whole class. Enrichment is on a separate optional sheet.

1.

Which sentence tells that the speaker likes drawing?

[A] I enjoy drawing pictures.[B] The paper is square.[C] Where are the crayons?
2.

Mia likes apples more than pears. What could she say?

[A] Apples are fruit.[B] Put the pears away.[C] I prefer apples.
3.

Which sentence politely tells a dislike?

[A] Everyone hates jumpers.[B] I do not like scratchy jumpers.[C] Scratchy jumpers are silly.
4.

A child smiles, claps and says, ‘Again!’ What feeling is most likely?

[A] The child enjoyed it.[B] The child is asking where it is.[C] The child cannot see it.
5.

Which gesture could show that someone likes a performance?

[A] Turning away and covering their ears.[B] Pointing at the floor to find a shoe.[C] Smiling and clapping.
6.

In an animation, a character frowns and pushes a plate away. What does this most likely show?

[A] The character is counting the food.[B] The character does not like the food.[C] The character wants a bigger plate.
7.

An advertisement shows a child smiling while holding a toy. What feeling is the picture trying to show?

[A] The child is happy with the toy.[B] The child has lost the toy.[C] The child is asleep.
8.

Which sentence shows a favourite choice?

[A] The swings are outside.[B] Are the swings wet?[C] The swings are my favourite.

9.Extension

Leo likes both blocks and puzzles but chooses puzzles first. What could he say?

[A] Blocks are made of wood.[B] I like both, but I prefer puzzles.[C] Puzzles are the only good game.
10.Extension

A friend likes a different story from you. Which reply is friendly?

[A] I like a different story best, and that is okay.[B] Your story is bad.[C] Everyone should choose mine.
