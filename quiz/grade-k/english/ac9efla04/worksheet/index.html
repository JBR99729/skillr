6. Hearing the words aloud

Summary: The familiar speaker icon signals an audio action because we identify the text, find its starting point and follow its navigation clues.

Hint: Match what each feature helps the reader know or do.

8. Look for a label, outline or familiar icon, listen to the teacher's instruction, and only select the feature meant for navigation.

Summary: The response checks visible and teacher-provided clues before taking a screen action because we identify the text, find its starting point and follow its navigation clues.

Hint: Compare a picture made to show information with a feature made to be selected.

9. Open the text, find the title at the top, select the speaker icon to hear the fact, then select Next to move on.

Summary: The directions use screen organisation clues in a clear sequence because we identify the text, find its starting point and follow its navigation clues.
