AC9SFH01 • Foundation Science

Observing and Asking About Nature

Explore how people make and use observations and questions to learn about the natural world.

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from a 56-question learning bank; Test uses a separate 24-question auto-marked bank.

Unit focus

  • Make careful observations using safe senses and simple tools.
  • Ask useful questions that grow from what was observed.
  • Record evidence using drawings, photographs, tallies and simple tables.
