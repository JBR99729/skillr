Pose Questions and Make Predictions

Pose clear questions and make sensible predictions based on experiences.

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from a 56-question learning bank; Test uses a separate 24-question auto-marked bank.

Unit focus

  • Ask a focused question that can be answered using observations.
  • Make a prediction before seeing the result.
  • Use prior experience to give a relevant reason and compare the prediction with evidence later.
