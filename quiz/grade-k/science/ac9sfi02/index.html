Safe Science Investigations

Engage in investigations safely and make observations using appropriate senses and simple tools.

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from a 56-question learning bank; Test uses a separate 24-question auto-marked bank.

Unit focus

  • Follow instructions, boundaries and adult-supervision rules.
  • Choose safe senses and suitable observation tools.
  • Protect both the investigator and the living things or materials being observed.
