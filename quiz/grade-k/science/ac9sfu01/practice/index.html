Living Things and External Features

Use this short visual recap, then try the questions.

60-second Quick Read

Plant: look for roots, stem and leaves. Flowers, fruits or seeds may also be visible.

Bird: feathers, wings, beak, eyes and legs are external features you can observe.

Fish: scales, fins, eyes, mouth and tail are visible external features.

Remember: compare what you can see, then group living things using one clear visible feature and explain your rule.
