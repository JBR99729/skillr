60-second Quick Read

  • Focus: plan, create, rehearse and deliver presentations for purposes and audiences in ways that may be imaginative, reflective, informative, persuasive and/or analytical, by selecting text structures, language features, literary devices and visual features, and using features of voice including volume, tone, pitch and pace
  • Approach: Read the supplied words, image or structure closely. Name a precise choice, then explain its effect on meaning, purpose or audience.
  • Common trap: Naming a feature is not analysis: support the interpretation with exact evidence.
