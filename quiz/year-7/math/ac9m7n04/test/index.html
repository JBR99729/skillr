Equivalent Rational Numbers and Number Lines

Review the key ideas, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Equivalent fractions represent the same value: multiply or divide the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.
  • For example, 2/3 = 4/6 = 6/9, and 2/3 < 5/6.
  • Convert among fraction, decimal and percentage forms, simplify using common divisors, and locate positive and negative rational numbers on a number line.
  • Common trap: Comparing numerators only or changing the numerator without changing the denominator.
