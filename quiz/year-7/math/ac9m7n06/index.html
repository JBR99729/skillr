Operations with Rational Numbers

Use all four operations with positive fractions, decimals and percentages, choosing efficient calculation strategies.

Practice now draws from 56 active questions, while Test draws from a separate 40-question bank. The newly authored 56 MCQs are split into 32 Practice and 24 Test questions.

Unit focus

  • Use common denominators for fraction addition and subtraction.
  • Simplify and multiply across; divide by multiplying by the reciprocal.
  • Choose fraction, decimal or percentage form to make calculations efficient.
  • Apply the mathematics to recipes, discounts, paint, land use, nutrition and energy-account contexts.
