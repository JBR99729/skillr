AC9M7N07 • Year 7 Maths

Comparing, Adding and Subtracting Integers

compare, order and solve problems involving addition and subtraction of integers

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • compare, order and solve problems involving addition and subtraction of integers
  • using less-than and greater-than notation in expressions when comparing and ordering integers; for example, negative 5 is less than positive 2 and…
  • discussing language such as “addition”, “subtraction”, “magnitude”, “difference”, “sign” and synonyms of these terms
