Answer guide · AC9M7N05

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. 8.48; the third decimal digit is 6.
  2. 0.095.
  3. 20 × 4 = 80.
  4. $27.49.
  5. Small rounding errors can accumulate. Keep extra precision during the calculation.
  6. 6 complete lengths. Rounding 6.3 up would claim more full lengths than the rope provides.
  7. 3 buses. 93/40 = 2.325, but two buses cannot carry everyone.
  8. The estimate 3 × 10 = 30 shows 313.6 is too large. The correct product is 31.36.
