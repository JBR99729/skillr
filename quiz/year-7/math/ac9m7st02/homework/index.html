Answer guide · AC9M7ST02

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. Mean 7; median 6; mode 6; range 7.
  2. Stem 1: leaves 2, 4, 7. Stem 2: leaves 1, 1, 6. Key: 1 | 2 means 12.
  3. 18 is unusually large compared with the cluster; the mean is affected more than the median.
  4. Both means are 5. A has range 2; B has range 8, so B is more spread out.
  5. 8, the mean of the two middle values 7 and 9.
  6. Old mean and median 6. New mean 12; new median 6.5. The mean changes much more.
  7. Sets can have the same centre but very different variation, as in the two groups above.
  8. Two dots at 1, three at 2 and one at 4. Most values cluster at 1–2; range 3.
