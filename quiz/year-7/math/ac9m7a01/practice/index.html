Variables, Formulas and Substitution

Interpret variables, substitute accurately, solve for unknowns and explain how quantities are related.

Quick preparation

  • Identify what each variable and constant represents before calculating.
  • Substitute values with units, follow the formula's operation order, then interpret the result.
  • When an unknown is not the subject, undo operations carefully and check by substitution.
