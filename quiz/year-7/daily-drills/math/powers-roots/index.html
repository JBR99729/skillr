Powers, Factors and Roots

An exponent tells how many equal factors are multiplied. Square roots reverse squaring for non-negative values.

Key ideas

  • powers
  • squares
  • square roots
  • prime factors
  • order of magnitude

Worked example: 3⁴ = 3×3×3×3 = 81.

Common trap: 3⁴ is not 3×4.

Key words

base — number being repeatedly multiplied
exponent — power showing number of factors
square — number multiplied by itself
square root — value whose square gives the number
prime factorisation — product of prime factors
