Algebraic Expressions

Like terms have the same variable part. Distribute a factor to every term inside brackets.

Key ideas

  • like terms
  • substitution
  • expansion
  • factor common terms
  • form expressions

Worked example: 3(x+4)=3x+12.

Common trap: Distribute to every term inside the bracket.

Key words

term — part separated by + or −
coefficient — number multiplying variable
like terms — same variable part
expand — remove brackets by multiplication
factorise — write as a product
