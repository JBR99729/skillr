Cells and Organisms

Cells are basic living units. Specialised cells can form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs work in systems.

What to remember

  • Cells are basic units of life.
  • Cell structures perform functions.
  • Specialised cells have structures suited to roles.
  • Tissues contain groups of similar cells.
  • Organs contain multiple tissues.

Key words

cell — basic living unit
organelle — specialised structure within a cell
tissue — group of similar cells
organ — structure made of tissues
specialisation — adaptation of structure for function
