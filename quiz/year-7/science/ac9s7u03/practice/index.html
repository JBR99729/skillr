60-second Quick Read

  • Seasons are caused mainly by Earth's axial tilt and orbit, which change Sun angle and day length.
  • Lunar phases come from changing viewing geometry of the Moon's sunlit half; normal phases are not Earth's shadow.
  • Common trap: models preserve useful relationships but often distort real sizes and distances.
