Particle Theory, States of Matter & Diffusion

Build confidence with particle arrangement, motion, heating and cooling, state changes, density, compressibility and diffusion.

60-second Quick Read

  • Solid: particles are close and vibrate about fixed average positions. Liquid: particles stay close but move past one another. Gas: particles are widely separated and move through the available space.
  • Heating generally increases particle motion; cooling decreases it. During a physical change of state, the substance stays the same while particle arrangement and motion change.
  • Common traps: particles do not grow when heated, spaces in diagrams are not automatically air, and diffusion is explained by continual random particle motion.
