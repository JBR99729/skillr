Science communication: viewpoints, policies and regulations

Answer using specific examples and explain how communication of scientific evidence can influence understanding, behaviour, policies or regulations.

  14. How does citizen science improve public understanding of scientific issues?

  15. Why is community engagement important when communicating scientific findings?

  20. Describe one long-term benefit of effective science communication for society.
