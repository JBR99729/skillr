Pure Substances, Mixtures & Separation Techniques

Work through the full curated bank. Questions progress from particle models and solutions to choosing and sequencing separation methods.

60-second Quick Read

  • Pure substance: one chemical substance represented by one repeated particle type; a mixture contains two or more substances physically together.
  • Choose a method from a property difference: particle size → filtration/sieving; density/phase separation → decantation/float-sink; boiling point/volatility → distillation; differing mobile/stationary phase attraction → chromatography.
  • Common trap: a clear solution is not automatically pure, and ordinary filter paper does not remove dissolved salt.
