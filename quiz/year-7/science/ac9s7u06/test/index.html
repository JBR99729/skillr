Pure Substances, Mixtures & Separation Techniques

This is a separate unseen reasoning bank. Choose methods by physical-property differences and evaluate multi-step separation plans.

Before you begin

  • Identify what substances and particle types are present before choosing a method.
  • For a complex mixture, one technique may not be enough: justify the order of each step.
  • Distinguish residue, filtrate and distillate, and do not assume appearance proves purity.
