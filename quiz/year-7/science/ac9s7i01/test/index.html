Investigable questions, predictions and hypotheses

Choose the scientifically strongest answer and distinguish questions, predictions, hypotheses and variables carefully.

60-second Quick Read

  • Question: identifies something that can be investigated through systematic evidence.
  • Prediction: states an expected outcome before testing and gives a scientific reason.
  • Hypothesis: proposes a testable relationship or explanation that evidence can support or challenge.
  • Fair test: change the IV, measure the DV and control other relevant factors.
