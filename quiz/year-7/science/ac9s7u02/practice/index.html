Food Webs, Energy Flow and Ecosystem Change

Work through the full curated bank. Questions progress from core vocabulary to predicting direct and indirect population effects.

60-second Quick Read

  • Food-web arrows point from the organism eaten to the consumer receiving matter and energy.
  • Matter is recycled through organisms and the environment; energy flows through the web and is dissipated as heat.
  • Common trap: one population change can cause several delayed or indirect effects, and a food web is a simplified model rather than a guaranteed forecast.
