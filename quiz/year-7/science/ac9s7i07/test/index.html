AC9S7I07 • Test

Evidence-based arguments, source credibility and ethical use of information

Apply the full skill: weigh evidence, qualify conclusions, evaluate sources and follow cultural protocols.

60-second Quick Read

  • Use multiple relevant evidence types and explain how they support or challenge the claim.
  • Match the strength of the conclusion to the method, sample, limitations and uncertainty.
  • When First Nations knowledge is involved, consider cultural authority, permissions, attribution, consent and benefit-sharing where relevant.
