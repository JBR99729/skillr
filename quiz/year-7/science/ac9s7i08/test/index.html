Scientific communication: purpose, audience and evidence

Apply your understanding to scenario-based questions about reports, digital prototypes, persuasive texts and multimodal explanations.

60-second Quick Read

  • Adapt language, detail, structure and visuals to the audience without changing the scientific meaning.
  • A strong report communicates enough method detail for reproducibility and represents data honestly.
  • Common trap: treating confident or persuasive wording as stronger than evidence. Communication must stay within what the data support.
