What this practice checks

This page checks the specific Year 7 Science skill for AC9S7I06: analyse methods, conclusions and claims for assumptions, possible sources of error, conflicting evidence and unanswered questions.

Topic focus

  • analyse methods, conclusions and claims for assumptions, possible sources of error, conflicting evidence and unanswered questions
  • identifying and considering indicators of the quality of the data when analysing results to identify unanswered questions
  • evaluating the method used in an investigation, identifying assumptions made about variables that should be controlled, suggesting ways it could be improved and giving reasons for…
  • analysing conclusions and claims to identify facts or premises that are taken for granted to be true, and considering their relevance to conclusions

Worked example pathway

  1. Observe the system: Name the feature, change or relationship being investigated: identifying and considering indicators of the quality of the data when analysing results to identify unanswered questions.
  2. Use evidence: Compare the evidence with each answer choice and reject choices that add unsupported claims.
  3. Explain the science: Finish with the science idea from AC9S7I06 using precise vocabulary.

Common mistakes

  • Picking the most dramatic answer instead of the one supported by the observation or data.
  • Using everyday wording when the question needs a science term, pattern or cause-and-effect link.
  • Confusing a prediction, observation, explanation and conclusion.

Assessment guidance

  • Use Practice to learn from feedback; a wrong answer is a useful signal, not a final result.
  • After each question, check whether the explanation names the same idea as the topic guide.
  • Move to Test when you can explain why the correct option is better than the distractors.
