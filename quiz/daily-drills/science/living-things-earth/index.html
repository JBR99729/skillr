Living Things and Our World

Plants and animals are living things. They have observable features and live in habitats. We can compare living and non-living things using what we notice.

What to remember

  • Plants and animals are living things.
  • Rocks are non-living.
  • Plants can have roots, stems and leaves.
  • Animals can have fur, feathers or scales.
  • A habitat is where a living thing lives.

Key words

living — being alive
non-living — not alive
feature — a part you can observe
habitat — place where a living thing lives
plant — a living organism such as a tree
