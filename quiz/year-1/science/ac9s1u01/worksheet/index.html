Answer guide · AC9S1U01

  1. No. The pet also needs water.
  2. It lacks light needed for healthy growth and food-making.
  3. For example, light reaching leaves and water available to roots; air is also acceptable.
  4. Water becomes harder to obtain; the wet habitat also supports the frog's life. Accept another accurate need-to-place connection.

If help is needed: Plants do not eat soil. Green plants use light to make their food and also need water and air; many get support and nutrients from soil.
