SkillrHub · AC9S1I01 · Year 1

Turn noticing into an investigable question

Name: __________________________ Date: ______________

Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

Which can be investigated by observation: ‘Which surface lets this car roll farther?’ or ‘Which car is the nicest?’

2. Try the skill

Complete a prediction: I think the car will roll farther on __ because __.

3. Apply and explain

Notice two objects that roll differently. Write or say one question you could investigate safely.

4. Apply and explain

Why should you keep your original prediction after seeing a different result?

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9S1I01

  1. The surface question. ‘Nicest’ is a preference unless a clear criterion is agreed.
  2. Accept a stated surface with a relevant experience-based reason; the prediction need not later be correct.
  3. For example, ‘Will the cylinder or ball roll farther down the same low ramp?’
  4. It lets you compare what you expected with what was observed.

If help is needed: A prediction is made before the test and includes a reason. An observation reports what actually happens.
