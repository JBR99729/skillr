Punctuation and Capital Letters

understand that written language uses punctuation such as full stops, question marks and exclamation marks, and uses capital letters for familiar proper nouns

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Written language uses capital letters and punctuation to show sentence boundaries, questions, exclamations and familiar proper nouns.
  • Use this routine: Read sentence → Check start/name → Choose end mark → Re-read.
  • Watch for question with full stop: If it asks, use a question mark.
