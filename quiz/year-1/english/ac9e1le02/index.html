Personal Connections to Literature

discuss literary texts and share responses by making connections with students’ own experiences

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Readers can discuss literature by sharing thoughts, feelings and connections to their own experiences.
  • Use this routine: Listen/read → Notice part → Connect → Explain response.
  • Watch for retells only: Add your thought, feeling or connection.
