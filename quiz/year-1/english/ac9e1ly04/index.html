AC9E1LY04 • Year 1 English

Reading with Phonics, Phrasing and Meaning

read decodable and authentic texts using developing phonic knowledge, phrasing and fluency, and monitoring meaning using context and grammatical knowledge

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Readers use phonic knowledge, phrasing, fluency, context and grammar to read words and make meaning.
  • Use this routine: Look at letters → Blend/recognise → Read phrase → Check meaning → Re-read.
  • Watch for only guessing from context: Look at the letters and sounds.
