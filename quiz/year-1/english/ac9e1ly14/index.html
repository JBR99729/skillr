Unit focus

  • High-frequency words appear often in reading and writing. Some can be sounded out; some have a tricky part to remember.
  • Use this routine: Read word → Say word → Map tricky part → Write → Use in sentence.
  • Watch for memorise shape only: Map sounds and tricky letters.
