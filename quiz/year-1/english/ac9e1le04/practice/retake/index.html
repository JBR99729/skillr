Before you restart

  • Poems, chants, rhymes and songs often use sound patterns such as rhyme and alliteration. Students can imitate and invent those patterns.
  • Use this routine: Listen → Find sound pattern → Imitate → Invent → Perform.
  • Watch for alliteration = same letter only: Listen for same sound, not just same spelling.
