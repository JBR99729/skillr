AC9E1LE04 • Year 1 English

Poems, Chants, Rhymes and Songs

listen to and discuss poems, chants, rhymes and songs, and imitate and invent sound patterns including alliteration and rhyme

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Poems, chants, rhymes and songs often use sound patterns such as rhyme and alliteration. Students can imitate and invent those patterns.
  • Use this routine: Listen → Find sound pattern → Imitate → Invent → Perform.
  • Watch for alliteration = same letter only: Listen for same sound, not just same spelling.
