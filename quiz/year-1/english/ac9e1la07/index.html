AC9E1LA07 • Year 1 English

Word Classes in Sentences

understand that words can represent people, places and things (nouns, including pronouns), happenings and states (verbs), qualities (adjectives) and details such as when, where and how (adverbs)

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Words have different jobs: nouns name people, places or things; verbs show happenings or states; adjectives describe; adverbs add detail such as when, where or how.
  • Use this routine: Read sentence → Find word → Ask its job → Name word class.
  • Watch for word meaning only: Ask what job the word does in the sentence.
