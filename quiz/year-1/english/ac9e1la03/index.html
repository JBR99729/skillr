Text Purpose and Organisation

explore how texts are organised according to their purpose, such as to recount, narrate, express opinion, inform, report and explain

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Texts are organised differently because they do different jobs, such as recounting, narrating, informing, reporting, explaining or giving an opinion.
  • Use this routine: Identify purpose → Notice structure → Find key features → Explain.
  • Watch for topic = purpose: Ask what the text is doing, not only what it is about.
