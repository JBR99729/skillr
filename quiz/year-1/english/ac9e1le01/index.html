AC9E1LE01 • Year 1 English

Creating Characters, Settings and Events

discuss how language and images are used to create characters, settings and events in literature by First Nations Australian, and wide-ranging Australian and world authors and illustrators

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Authors and illustrators use language and images to create characters, settings and events in literature.
  • Use this routine: Notice words → Notice images → Name character/setting/event → Explain effect.
  • Watch for opinion without evidence: Point to words or image clues.
