Text Features and Purpose

discuss different texts and identify some features that indicate their purposes

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Different texts have features that hint at their purpose, such as entertaining, informing, instructing or persuading.
  • Use this routine: Look at text → Notice feature → Infer purpose → Explain.
  • Watch for guessing from topic: Use features as evidence.
