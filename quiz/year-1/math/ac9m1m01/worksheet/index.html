Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

A balance tilts down on the side with the stone. Which is heavier: the stone or the pine cone on the other side?

2. Try the skill

Cup A fills 3 identical small scoops; cup B fills 5. Which has greater capacity?

3. Apply and explain

Line up three pencils at one end. Draw them in order from shortest to longest.

4. Apply and explain

Two songs start together. Song A finishes first. Which song has the longer duration?

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9M1M01

  1. The stone is heavier.
  2. Cup B. It holds more of the same-sized scoops.
  3. Accept an ordering supported by aligned starts and the positions of the other ends.
  4. Song B lasts longer because it finishes later after the same start.

If help is needed: An object can be taller yet hold less, or larger yet lighter. Decide whether you are comparing length, mass, capacity or duration.
