Use ten as a stepping stone

Name: __________________________ Date: ______________

Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

Use ten to solve 9 + 4. Show how you split 4.

2. Try the skill

Find 14 - 6 by going back through ten.

3. Apply and explain

Use the double 6 + 6 to find 6 + 7. Explain.

4. Apply and explain

Write one addition fact that helps you check 15 - 7 = 8.

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9M1N04

  1. Split 4 into 1 and 3: 9 + 1 + 3 = 13.
  2. 14 - 4 = 10; two more must be removed, so 10 - 2 = 8.
  3. 6 + 6 = 12. Seven is one more than six, so the total is 13.
  4. 7 + 8 = 15, or 8 + 7 = 15.

If help is needed: Do not use the whole five again after making ten. The two used and the three left must still total five.
