Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

A book costs $7. You pay with $10. How much change should you receive?

2. Try the skill

There are 8 red blocks and 5 blue blocks. Draw a model and find the total.

3. Apply and explain

You have 12 stickers and give away 4. Draw what changes and label what remains.

4. Apply and explain

A toy costs $6. You have $4. Do you have enough? Explain how much more is needed.

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9M1N05

  1. $3. The $7 cost and $3 change together equal the $10 paid.
  2. 13 blocks; 8 + 5 = 13.
  3. 8 stickers remain; 12 - 4 = 8.
  4. No. Another $2 is needed because 4 + 2 = 6.

If help is needed: The words ‘more’ do not always mean add the two numbers given. Ask what is known and which part is missing.
