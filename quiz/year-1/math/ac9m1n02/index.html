AC9M1N02 • Year 1 Maths

Partitioning Tens and Ones

partition one- and two-digit numbers in different ways using physical and virtual materials, including partitioning two-digit numbers into tens and ones

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates through a 56-question learning bank, Test uses a separate 24-question auto-marked bank, and Quiz draws from 100 questions.

Unit focus

  • A number can be split into parts in more than one correct way.
  • Start with tens and ones, then exchange one ten for 10 ones.
  • Check every partition by combining the parts to remake the whole.
