Answer guide · AC9M1N02

  1. 15 ones. 20 + 15 = 35.
  2. 2 tens and 8 ones. The other representation shows 18.
  3. For example, 10 + 9 and 12 + 7. Both pairs make 19.
  4. 2 tens and 12 ones. The total remains 32.

If help is needed: Regrouping does not add or remove objects. Count the value of each bundle as ten, not one.
