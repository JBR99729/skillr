Directions depend on the starting view

Name: __________________________ Date: ______________

Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

Two children face each other. Will their right hands point to the same side of the room? Explain.

2. Try the skill

Why is ‘go forward’ unclear when the starting direction is not given?

3. Apply and explain

Place a toy at a marked start. Give three instructions to move it around a book and check the route with a partner.

4. Apply and explain

A toy must turn left before moving two steps. What could change if the instructions are followed in reverse order?

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9M1SP02

  1. No. Their facing directions are opposite, so their right sides point to opposite sides of the room.
  2. Different facing directions lead to different routes.
  3. Accept ordered directions that state the initial facing direction and lead to the intended place.
  4. It moves two steps in its old direction before turning, so it can finish in a different place.

If help is needed: Turning right changes where you face; it is not automatically a step sideways. Left and right belong to the person following the directions.
