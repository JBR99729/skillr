AC9M1N03 • Year 1 Maths

Skip Counting and Equal Groups

quantify sets of objects, to at least 120, by partitioning collections into equal groups using number knowledge and skip counting

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates through a 56-question learning bank, Test uses a separate 24-question auto-marked bank, and Quiz draws from 100 questions.

Unit focus

  • Equal groups contain the same number in every group.
  • Skip count the full groups by 2s, 5s or 10s.
  • Count any leftover objects separately after the full groups.
