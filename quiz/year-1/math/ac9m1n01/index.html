AC9M1N01 • Year 1 Maths

Numbers to 120

recognise, represent and order numbers to at least 120 using physical and virtual materials, numerals, number lines and charts

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates through a 56-question learning bank, Test uses a separate 24-question auto-marked bank, and Quiz draws from 100 questions.

Unit focus

  • Read numbers by looking at the hundreds, tens and ones.
  • Numbers move up by 1 from left to right on a number line.
  • On a standard hundreds chart, right adds 1 and down adds 10.
