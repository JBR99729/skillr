Answer guide · AC9M1N01

  1. 89, 98, 108. The two numbers below 100 come first; 8 tens is less than 9 tens.
  2. 111. One more than 110 is 111.
  3. One hundred, one ten and four ones; or eleven tens and four ones.
  4. The digits name different places: 9 tens is 90, while 1 hundred is 100. Therefore 102 is greater.

If help is needed: Looking at the ones digit first can give the wrong comparison. Compare the complete quantities; start with the largest place.
