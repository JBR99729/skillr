SkillrHub · AC9M1SP01 · Year 1

A turned shape keeps its features

Name: __________________________ Date: ______________

Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

A closed flat shape has three straight sides. What shape is it?

2. Try the skill

How are a circle and a triangle different? Give a feature of each.

3. Apply and explain

Draw two triangles that look different. Explain why both are triangles.

4. Apply and explain

Find a rectangular face on an object at home. Draw the face and describe its sides.

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9M1SP01

  1. A triangle.
  2. A circle has a curved boundary and no corners. A triangle has three straight sides and three corners.
  3. Accept two closed shapes, each with three straight sides; they may differ in size or side lengths.
  4. For example, a book cover; four straight sides, with opposite sides equal and four square corners.

If help is needed: A shape's name does not change just because it is turned or coloured differently. Use its sides and corners as evidence.
