Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

Today is Tuesday. A game is two days later. On which day is it?

2. Try the skill

Put one day, one year and one week in order from shortest to longest.

3. Apply and explain

A club meets every Friday. Explain why there is one week between two Friday meetings.

4. Apply and explain

Which is a more useful unit for a tree growing from a seedling into a large tree: hours or years? Explain.

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9M1M03

  1. Thursday: Wednesday is one day later and Thursday is two days later.
  2. One day, one week, one year.
  3. The days repeat in a seven-day cycle, so the next Friday is one week later.
  4. Years, because this growth takes a long time, much longer than a few hours.

If help is needed: The number alone does not tell the duration. Two hours and two months are very different lengths of time.
