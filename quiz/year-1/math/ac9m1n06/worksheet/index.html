Sharing and grouping ask different questions

Name: __________________________ Date: ______________

Show your thinking with objects, drawings or words. An adult may read the instructions aloud.

Use the first two questions in class and the last two for homework or independent practice.

1. Try the skill

Put 12 counters into groups of 3. How many groups can you make?

2. Try the skill

Ten counters are shared onto two mats as 6 and 4. Is the sharing equal? Repair it.

3. Apply and explain

Draw 8 objects shared equally between 4 plates. Label how many are on each.

4. Apply and explain

Draw 8 objects with 4 on each plate. How many plates are needed?

Check answers and explanations

Answer guide · AC9M1N06

  1. 4 groups, with 3 counters in each.
  2. It is unequal. Move one counter from the group of 6 to the group of 4 to make 5 and 5.
  3. 2 on each plate; four equal groups of two make eight.
  4. 2 plates. The group size is four and there are two groups.

If help is needed: For ‘three mats’, make three groups first. For ‘three in each group’, place three objects together repeatedly instead.
