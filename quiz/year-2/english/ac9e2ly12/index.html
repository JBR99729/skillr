Prefixes, Suffixes and Word Families

build morphemic word families using knowledge of prefixes and suffixes

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Prefixes and suffixes are morphemes added to a base word. They create word families and change meaning, grammar or both.
  • Use this routine: Find base → Add prefix or suffix → Read new word → Explain new meaning.
  • Watch for any similar spelling is a family: Word-family members share a meaningful base, not just nearby letters.
