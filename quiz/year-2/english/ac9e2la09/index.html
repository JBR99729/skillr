Choosing Precise Vocabulary

experiment with and begin to make conscious choices of vocabulary to suit the topic

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Writers choose vocabulary deliberately. Precise verbs, nouns and topic words help readers form a clearer picture and understand information accurately.
  • Use this routine: Identify topic → Choose precise word → Test meaning → Revise.
  • Watch for choosing the longest word: Choose the most accurate word, not the most complicated one.
