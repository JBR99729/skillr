AC9E2LY09 • Year 2 English

Manipulating Sounds in Words

manipulate more complex sounds in spoken words and use knowledge of blending, segmenting, phoneme deletion and phoneme substitution to read and write words

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Words can be analysed as sequences of phonemes. Readers and writers blend sounds, segment words and create new words by deleting or substituting one sound.
  • Use this routine: Say word → Stretch sounds → Manipulate phoneme → Blend → Check word.
  • Watch for counting letters instead of sounds: Say the word slowly; one phoneme may use more than one letter.
