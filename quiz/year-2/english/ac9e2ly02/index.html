AC9E2LY02 • Year 2 English

Listening, Speaking and Giving Instructions

use interaction skills when engaging with topics, actively listening to others, receiving instructions and extending own ideas, speaking appropriately, expressing and responding to opinions, making statements, and giving instructions

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Effective interaction includes attentive listening, clear speaking, suitable volume and tone, relevant responses, respectful opinions and instructions in a logical order.
  • Use this routine: Listen → Think → Respond → Extend → Check understanding.
  • Watch for waiting only for your turn: Listen for the other speaker's main idea before responding.
