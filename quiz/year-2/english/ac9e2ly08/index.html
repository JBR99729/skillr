AC9E2LY08 • Year 2 English

Legible and Fluent Handwriting

write words legibly and with growing fluency using unjoined upper-case and lower-case letters

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Legible handwriting uses recognisable letter shapes, consistent direction, appropriate size, spacing and placement on the line. Fluency grows through accurate repeated practice.
  • Use this routine: Sit and position → Form letters → Space words → Maintain size → Review.
  • Watch for prioritising speed: Slow down until formation and spacing are secure.
