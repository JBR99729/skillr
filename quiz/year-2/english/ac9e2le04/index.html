AC9E2LE04 • Year 2 English

Rhythm, Rhyme and Repetition

identify, reproduce and experiment with rhythmic sound and word patterns in poems, chants, rhymes or songs

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Poems, chants, rhymes and songs use beat, rhyme, repeated words and sound patterns to make language memorable and expressive.
  • Use this routine: Listen → Tap rhythm → Find pattern → Reproduce → Experiment.
  • Watch for rhyme means same spelling: Rhyme depends on sound, not identical letters.
