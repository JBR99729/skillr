Comparing Texts on the Same Topic

identify how similar topics and information are presented in different types of texts

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Two texts can cover the same topic but select, organise and present information differently because their purposes and audiences differ.
  • Use this routine: Identify shared topic → Identify text type → Compare information → Compare presentation.
  • Watch for comparing unrelated details: Begin with the shared topic and the question being investigated.
