AC9E2LA07 • Year 2 English

Building Noun Groups and Verb Groups

understand that in sentences nouns may be extended into noun groups using articles and adjectives, and verbs may be expressed as verb groups

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • A noun group gives more information about a person, place, thing or idea. A verb group can show an action or state using more than one word.
  • Use this routine: Find noun or verb → Add useful words → Read for meaning → Remove unnecessary detail.
  • Watch for adding random adjectives: Choose details that match the noun and purpose.
