Spelling Patterns and High-Frequency Words

use knowledge of spelling patterns and morphemes to read and write words whose spelling is not completely predictable from their sounds, including high-frequency words

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Spelling draws on phonics, common patterns, morphemes and visual memory. High-frequency words may contain a regular part and a less predictable part that must be remembered.
  • Use this routine: Say word → Find known sounds → Find word parts → Notice tricky part → Practise.
  • Watch for trying sound only: Use sound plus spelling pattern, word part and remembered tricky section.
