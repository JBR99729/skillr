Titles and Commas in Lists

recognise that capital letters are used in titles and commas are used to separate items in lists

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Important words in titles begin with capital letters according to the chosen title style. Commas separate items in a list so the reader can see each item clearly.
  • Use this routine: Identify title or list → Apply punctuation → Read aloud → Edit.
  • Watch for capitalising every word automatically: Follow the title style and focus on important words rather than small joining words when appropriate.
