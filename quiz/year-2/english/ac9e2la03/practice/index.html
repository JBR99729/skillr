Text Structures for Different Purposes

Read the quick preparation notes, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Texts are organised to help them do a job. Stories often follow events, procedures use ordered steps, and information texts group facts under headings.
  • Use this routine: Identify purpose → Notice structure → Find language features → Explain.
  • Watch for identifying by topic only: Two texts can share a topic but have different purposes and structures.
