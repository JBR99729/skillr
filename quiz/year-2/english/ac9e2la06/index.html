AC9E2LA06 • Year 2 English

Joining Ideas in Compound Sentences

understand that connections can be made between ideas by using a compound sentence with 2 or more independent clauses usually linked by a coordinating conjunction

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • A compound sentence joins two complete ideas. Conjunctions such as and, but, or and so show the relationship between the ideas.
  • Use this routine: Find complete idea → Find second idea → Choose conjunction → Check meaning.
  • Watch for joining fragments: Each independent clause must express a complete idea.
