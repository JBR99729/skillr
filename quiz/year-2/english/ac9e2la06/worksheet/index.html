Does ‘The dog barked and the gate opened’ contain two independent clauses? Name them.

4. Apply and explain

Answer guide · AC9E2LA06

  1. I wanted to read, but the room was too noisy.
  2. Yes: ‘The dog barked’ and ‘the gate opened’ can each stand independently.
  3. You can draw a map, or you can write directions.
  4. It names two groups but does not contain two independent clauses stating complete ideas.

If help is needed: Adding ‘and’ does not always join two independent clauses. In ‘red and blue shoes’, it joins describing words, not two complete clauses.
