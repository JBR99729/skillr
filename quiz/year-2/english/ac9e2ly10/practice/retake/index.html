Before you restart

  • A phoneme may be represented by one or more letters. Readers recognise common and less common patterns, preserve sounds in clusters and notice silent letters and word parts.
  • Use this routine: Say word → Identify sounds → Map graphemes → Notice pattern → Check meaning.
  • Watch for one letter equals one sound: Some sounds use digraphs, and some letters are silent.
