Earth in the Solar System & Patterns in the Sky

This test uses a separate authored bank and mixes knowledge, observation, evidence, prediction and misconception questions.

Before you begin

  • Know the relationships among the Sun, Earth and Moon and Earth's place in the solar system.
  • Use observations and models as evidence rather than guessing from colour or size alone.
  • Recognise daily Sun and shadow patterns and longer Moon and seasonal sky patterns.
  • Distinguish regular Moon phases from special named events such as blue, blood and supermoon.
  • For First Nations Australian sky knowledge, recognise that knowledges are diverse and place-based and should be attributed to the relevant People or community.
