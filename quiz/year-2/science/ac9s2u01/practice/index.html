Quick preparation

  • Earth is a planet in our solar system; the Sun is a star and the Moon is Earth's natural satellite.
  • Earth is the third planet from the Sun. Space images show its round shape, land, water and clouds.
  • Repeated observations reveal patterns in the apparent positions of the Sun, Moon, planets and stars.
  • The Sun appears lower in the morning and later afternoon and higher near the middle of the day; shadow length changes with this pattern.
  • The Moon's visible illuminated portion changes in a repeating cycle of about 29–30 days; the Moon itself does not change shape.
  • Stars do not switch off during the day; the bright daytime sky makes most of them difficult to see.
  • Never look directly at the Sun. Use shadows, safe images or approved equipment for investigations.
