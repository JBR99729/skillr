Living Things, Earth and Cycles

Organisms grow and change through life cycles. Earth rotates, producing a regular day/night pattern.

What to remember

  • Living and non-living things can be distinguished.
  • Plants and animals have life cycles.
  • Young organisms grow into adults.
  • Earth rotates.
  • Earth's rotation is linked with day and night.

Key words

organism — living thing
life cycle — stages of growth and change
classification — grouping by shared features
rotation — turning around an axis
cycle — sequence that repeats
How to use this drill: review the short notes when learning, then close them on later attempts and practise from memory. The rotating bank delays a full set repeat for 25 attempts on the same browser.
