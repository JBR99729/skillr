Before you restart

  • Students learn to understand that cooperation with others depends on shared understanding of social conventions, including turn-taking language, which vary according to the degree of formality. They use the visual model, precise vocabulary and evidence to explain and apply the concept.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Read/View → Discuss → Apply → Explain.
  • Watch for formal means unfriendly: Formal language can still be warm and respectful.
