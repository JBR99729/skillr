Oral and Multimodal Presentations

Read the quick preparation notes, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Students learn to plan, create, rehearse and deliver short oral and/or multimodal presentations to inform, express opinions or tell stories, using a clear structure, details to elaborate ideas, topic-specific and precise vocabulary, visual features, and appropriate tone, pace, pitch and volume. They use the visual model, precise vocabulary and evidence to explain and apply the concept.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Read/View → Discuss → Apply → Explain.
  • Watch for presentation means reading slides: Slides support spoken communication.
