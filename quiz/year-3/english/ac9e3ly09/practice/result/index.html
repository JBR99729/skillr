Unit focus

  • Students learn to understand how to apply knowledge of phoneme-grapheme (sound-letter) relationships, syllables, and blending and segmenting to fluently read and write multisyllabic words with more complex letter patterns.   • Use this routine: Notice → Read/View → Discuss → Apply → Explain.
  • Watch for one letter equals one sound: Graphemes may contain several letters.
