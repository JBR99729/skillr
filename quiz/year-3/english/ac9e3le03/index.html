AC9E3LE03 • Year 3 English

Character, Setting and Narrative Mood

discuss how an author uses language and illustrations to portray characters and settings in texts, and explore how the settings and events influence the mood of the narrative

Choose a learning activity. Practice rotates 8 questions from a 24-question bank; Test draws 12 questions from a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students learn to discuss how an author uses language and illustrations to portray characters and settings in texts, and explore how the settings and events influence the mood of the narrative. They use the visual model, precise vocabulary and evidence to explain and apply the concept.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Read/View → Discuss → Apply → Explain.
  • Watch for mood and character feeling are identical: Mood is the atmosphere created for the audience.
