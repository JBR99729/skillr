Clauses and Subject–Verb Agreement

Read the quick preparation notes, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Students learn to understand that a clause is a unit of grammar usually containing a subject and a verb that need to agree. They use the visual model, precise vocabulary and evidence to explain and apply the concept.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Read/View → Discuss → Apply → Explain.
  • Watch for phrase mistaken for clause: A clause usually needs a subject and verb.
