Evaluating Investigations and Drawing Conclusions

Read the quick preparation notes, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Students learn to compare findings with those of others, consider if investigations were fair, identify questions for further investigation and draw conclusions. They use the visual model, precise vocabulary and evidence to explain and apply the concept.
  • Use this routine: Observe → Model → Investigate → Record → Explain.
  • Watch for different result means someone is wrong: Chance variation or method differences may explain it.
