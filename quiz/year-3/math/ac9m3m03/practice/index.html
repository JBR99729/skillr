Time Units, Estimation and Duration

Practise choosing units, estimating and comparing realistic durations, planning activities, and using timers.

Learn AC9M3M03 first: read the full Year 3 lesson on formal units of time, estimating and comparing the duration of events.

Quick preparation

  • 60 seconds = 1 minute, 60 minutes = 1 hour and 24 hours = 1 day.
  • A good estimate is sensible, not necessarily exact.
  • Estimate first, measure with a timer, then compare.
  • Choose a unit that makes the duration easy to understand.
