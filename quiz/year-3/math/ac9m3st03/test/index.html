Guided Statistical Investigations (AC9M3ST03)

Check your understanding of guided statistical investigations in a short shuffled test. Read the purpose below, then work independently and review the feedback afterwards.

About this investigation test

This short test samples how you plan a question, collect suitable data, read a display and support a conclusion. It includes categorical data, such as a choice of activity, and discrete numerical data, such as a count.

Question: be clear about what you want to find out and whose responses are needed.

Collection and display: check that the categories or counts answer the question, and read labels and totals carefully.

Interpretation: support your statement with the data shown. A result from one class does not automatically describe every school.

Work independently once you start. Use the feedback afterwards to identify which stage needs another try. A five-question attempt is a revision check; completing a real guided investigation also requires collecting and representing your own data.

Prepare with the guided statistical investigation lesson and supported Practice. The worksheet gives another way to record written work.
