AC9M3ST03 • Year 3 Maths

Guided Statistical Investigations

conduct guided statistical investigations involving the collection, representation and interpretation of data for categorical and discrete numerical variables with respect to questions of interest

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • A statistical investigation begins with a question and uses collected data to form evidence-based conclusions while recognising variation.
  • Use this routine: Question → Plan → Collect → Represent → Interpret → Report.
  • Watch for question and data mismatch: Collect data that directly answers the question.
