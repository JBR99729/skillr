Multiplication Facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10

recall and demonstrate proficiency with multiplication facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10; extend and apply facts to develop the related division facts

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10 can be built from patterns, arrays and known facts. Each multiplication fact connects to related division facts.
  • Use this routine: Build pattern → Recall fact → Use relationship → Divide → Check.
  • Watch for repeated addition too slow: Use patterns and known facts for recall.
