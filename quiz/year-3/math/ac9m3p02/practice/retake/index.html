Before you restart

  • Repeated chance experiments produce results that can vary. More trials give more evidence about patterns, but exact counts are not guaranteed.
  • Use this routine: List outcomes → Predict → Repeat trials → Tally → Compare results → Discuss variation.
  • Watch for expecting perfect equality: Equal chances do not guarantee equal results in a small trial.
