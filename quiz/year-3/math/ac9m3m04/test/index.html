Telling Time: Analogue, Digital and Clock Angles

Complete a mixed test of nearest-minute clock reading, time language, hand positions and optional Year 4 geometry extension.

Quick preparation

  • Read the minute hand precisely, identify the hour just passed and check that the hour hand has moved a sensible distance.
  • Each clock number represents 5 minutes; exact times may need the small minute ticks and a leading zero such as 7:05.
  • For extension angles: full turn 360°, adjacent numbers 30°, minute tick 6°, and choose the smaller angle when required.
