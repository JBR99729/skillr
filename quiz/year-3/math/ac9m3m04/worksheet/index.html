AC9M3M04 • Year 3 Worksheet

Telling time to the nearest minute worksheet

This worksheet draws 20 questions from the curated AC9M3M04 practice bank so students meet varied clock times and reasoning rather than one repeated template.

Worksheet focus

  • Read analogue clocks to five-minute intervals and exact minute marks.
  • Connect analogue and digital time.
  • Use o’clock, quarter past, half past, quarter to, minutes past and minutes to.
  • Read the hour that has just passed when the hour hand is between numbers.
  • Explain how the hour hand moves as the minute hand travels around the clock.
  • Apply time reading to school, transport, cooking and sport situations.
