AC9S5U04 • Year 5 Science

Weathering, Erosion and Deposition

explain observable properties of solids, liquids and gases by modelling the motion and arrangement of particles

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students distinguish breakdown from movement and settling, model agents such as water, wind and ice and explain how process rate depends on material, slope, vegetation, climate and time.
  • Use evidence to distinguish four processes.
  • Watch for weathering and erosion are the same: Weathering is breakdown in place; erosion removes material.
