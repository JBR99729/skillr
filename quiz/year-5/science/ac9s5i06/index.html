AC9S5I06 • Year 5 Science

Communicating Scientific Ideas and Findings

write and create texts to communicate ideas and findings for specific purposes and audiences, including selection of language features, using digital tools as appropriate

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students construct coherent scientific reports, explanations and presentations, integrate labelled representations, cite relevant evidence and adapt detail for audiences without changing the science.
  • Use evidence to choose a report structure.
  • Watch for results shown without finding: State the pattern and its relevance.
