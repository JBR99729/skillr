AC9S5U01 • Year 5 Science

Structural and Behavioural Adaptations

examine how particular structural features and behaviours of living things enable their survival in specific habitats

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students distinguish structural features from behaviours, connect them to environmental conditions and explain how a feature can improve—not guarantee—survival and reproduction.
  • Use evidence to classify structural/behavioural.
  • Watch for any useful action is an inherited adaptation: Some behaviours are learned or immediate responses.
