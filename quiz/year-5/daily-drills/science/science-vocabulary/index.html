Science Vocabulary

Year 5 introduces formal investigation terms such as independent, dependent and controlled variable.

What to remember

  • Adaptations can help survival and reproduction.
  • Earth orbits the Sun.
  • Light can be reflected.
  • Matter has different states.
  • Independent variables are deliberately changed.

Key words

adaptation — feature or behaviour aiding survival/reproduction
solar system — Sun and orbiting objects
reflection — bouncing of light
independent variable — factor changed
dependent variable — factor measured
How to use this drill: review the short notes when learning, then close them on later attempts and practise from memory. The rotating bank delays a full set repeat for 25 attempts on the same browser.
