Perimeter and Area of Regular and Irregular Shapes

solve practical problems involving the perimeter and area of regular and irregular shapes using appropriate metric units

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students distinguish boundary length from surface coverage, calculate regular shapes, decompose composite regions and approximate irregular spaces with stated assumptions and metric units.
  • Use this routine: Represent → Reason → Calculate → Interpret → Verify.
  • Watch for interior grid lines included in perimeter: Trace only the external boundary.
