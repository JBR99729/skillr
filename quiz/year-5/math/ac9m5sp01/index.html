AC9M5SP01 • Year 5 Maths

Three-dimensional Objects and Nets

connect objects to their nets and build objects from their nets using spatial and geometric reasoning

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students visualise how connected faces fold into prisms and pyramids, test which edges meet and construct objects from accurate nets with suitable tabs or joins.
  • Use this routine: Represent → Reason → Calculate → Interpret → Verify.
  • Watch for any six squares form a cube net: Some arrangements overlap or fail to close.
