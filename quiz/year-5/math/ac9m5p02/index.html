AC9M5P02 • Year 5 Maths

Repeated Chance Experiments and Expected Frequency

conduct repeated chance experiments including those with and without equally likely outcomes, observe and record the results; use frequency to compare outcomes and estimate their likelihoods

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students calculate expected frequency from probability and trial count, conduct or simulate repeated trials, compare observed frequencies and discuss natural variation and sample size.
  • Use this routine: Represent → Reason → Calculate → Interpret → Verify.
  • Watch for expected means guaranteed: It is a long-run prediction.
