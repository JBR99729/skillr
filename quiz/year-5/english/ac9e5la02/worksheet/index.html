AC9E5LA02 · Before you begin

A bare assertion gives a claim without enough support. Check whether statements are facts or opinions, choose reasons and evidence that directly support the claim, keep the claim no broader than its evidence, and consider reliable sources and other viewpoints.

Use this routine: Claim → reason → evidence → source → other view.
