AC9E5LA02 • Practice

Claims, opinions and evidence

Practise moving beyond bare assertions by checking facts and opinions, choosing relevant support, matching claim strength to evidence, recognising other viewpoints and selecting authoritative sources.

Quick preparation

  • Fact: objectively checkable. Opinion: a belief, feeling or evaluation that reasonable people may dispute.
  • Evidence test: relevant → sufficient → checkable → authoritative.
  • Claim test: do not let words such as all, always or best go beyond the evidence.
  • Different views: acknowledge another position respectfully, then respond with a reason.
