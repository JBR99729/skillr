Creating Literary Texts through Figurative Language and Characterisation

Quick preparation

  • Students adapt craft techniques rather than copying wording, develop motivated characters in functional settings and revise figurative language and plot choices for a coherent mood, theme or reader effect.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Interpret → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for adaptation copies plot and wording: Transform technique, context and decisions.
