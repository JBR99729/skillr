AC9E5LE02 · Before you begin

Present an opinion on a literary text using precise terms for literary devices, text structures and language features. Support the opinion with evidence, explain the effect of the feature, and respond respectfully to another reader's viewpoint.

Research boundary: IXL directly supports recognising literary devices, interpreting their effects, point of view and story elements. Extended opinion writing and reflection on another reader's viewpoint are curriculum extensions rather than directly observed IXL task formats.
