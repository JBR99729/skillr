AC9E5LA05 · Before you begin

A complex sentence contains a main clause and at least one dependent clause. Find the subject and predicate, use the stand-alone test, then check what relationship the subordinator expresses.

Routine: clause boundary → main/dependent → subordinator meaning → punctuation → effect.
