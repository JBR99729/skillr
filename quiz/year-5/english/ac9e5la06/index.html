AC9E5LA06 • Year 5 English

Complex Sentences and Logical Relationships

understand how noun groups can be expanded in a variety of ways to provide a fuller description of a person, place, thing or idea

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students identify main and dependent clauses, choose subordinating conjunctions accurately, vary clause order and punctuation and use complex sentences to express precise logical relationships.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Interpret → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for dependent fragment accepted as sentence: Add or connect a main clause.
