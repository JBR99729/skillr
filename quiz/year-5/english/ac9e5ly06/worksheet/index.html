AC9E5LY06 · Before you begin

Writing cycle: purpose + audience → plan → draft and develop → revise meaning → edit conventions → publish.

Remember: a strong paragraph has one controlling idea; supporting details must belong; connectives must show a real relationship; precise vocabulary is better than unnecessarily difficult vocabulary.

Revision ≠ editing: revise ideas, order and detail first. Then edit sentences, spelling and punctuation.
