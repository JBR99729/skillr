Independent writing decisions

The 16-question test bank uses unseen contexts. Apply the writing process rather than memorising examples from Practice.

Before you begin

  • Read for purpose and audience before judging individual sentences.
  • Check whether ideas are relevant, organised and supported.
  • Choose language and punctuation for meaning and clarity, not just formality.
  • Remember: revision improves meaning; editing corrects conventions.
