AC9E5LY06 • Practice

Plan, develop, revise, edit and publish texts

This bank uses real writing decisions in imaginative, informative, persuasive and multimodal contexts.

Quick preparation

  • Purpose + audience first: these guide structure, evidence, vocabulary and tone.
  • Paragraph check: topic sentence → relevant supporting details → logical connectives → useful ending.
  • Revise before editing: improve ideas and organisation before polishing spelling and punctuation.
  • Multimodal rule: diagrams, images, graphs, captions and layout must add meaning.

How to use the feedback

Do not choose an answer just because it sounds formal. Ask which option best serves the purpose, audience and meaning of the whole text.
