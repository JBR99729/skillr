Adverb Groups and Prepositional Phrases

understand how vocabulary is used to express greater precision of meaning, including through the use of specialist and technical terms, and explore the history of words

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students distinguish structural form from function, use groups and phrases to express time, place, manner, cause, condition, degree or viewpoint and place them to avoid ambiguity.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Interpret → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for all adverbials end in -ly: Many are phrases or clauses.
