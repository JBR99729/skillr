AC9E5LA04 • Year 5 English

Paragraph Cohesion and Reference

understand how texts can be made cohesive by using the starting point of a sentence or paragraph to give prominence to the message and to guide the reader through the text

Choose a learning activity. Practice draws from 24 questions, while Test uses a separate 16-question bank.

Unit focus

  • Students organise paragraphs around controlled ideas, use topic and elaboration sentences, maintain clear reference chains and select connectives that express logical relationships across sentences and paragraphs.
  • Use this routine: Notice → Analyse → Interpret → Apply → Create → Review.
  • Watch for every sentence becomes a new paragraph: Group related development around one focus.
