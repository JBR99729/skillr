Quadratics

Use factorisation where possible and connect algebraic roots to x-intercepts of a parabola.

Key ideas

  • factorising quadratics
  • solving
  • roots
  • parabola features
  • applications

Worked example: x²−5x+6=(x−2)(x−3), so roots are 2 and 3.

Common trap: The signs in factors determine the opposite signs of roots.

Key words

quadratic — degree-2 relationship
root — x-value giving y=0
vertex — turning point
parabola — quadratic graph
factorised form — quadratic written as product
