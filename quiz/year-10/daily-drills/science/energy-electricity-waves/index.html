Energy, Electricity and Waves

Energy can be transferred and transformed but is conserved overall. In circuits, current is charge flow, potential difference is energy per charge and resistance opposes current. Waves transfer energy and are described using frequency, wavelength and amplitude.

What to remember

  • Energy is conserved through transfers and transformations.
  • Current is the rate of charge flow.
  • Potential difference is energy transferred per charge.
  • Resistance opposes current.
  • Wave speed depends on frequency and wavelength.

Key words

energy conservation — total energy remains accounted for
current — charge flow per unit time
potential difference — energy transferred per charge
resistance — opposition to current
wavelength — distance between corresponding points on waves
