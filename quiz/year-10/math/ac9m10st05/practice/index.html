60-second revision

Read this first: the Practice questions use these same ideas, representations and common traps, so this 60-second review should make your answers more accurate.

Remember this: A good bivariate investigation asks a focused question, measures two variables consistently, uses an appropriate display and limits conclusions to what the design and data support.

Worked example: 1. Focus question “Is weekly study time associated with mathematics test score among Year 10 students at this school?” identifies two variables and population.

Don’t lose marks here: Projecting far beyond observed years assumes trend continues; explicitly label this limitation.
