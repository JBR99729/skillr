Continuous Distributions and Boxplots

Review the key ideas, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Compare distributions using centre, spread, shape and outliers in the context of the data.
  • A boxplot summarises the five-number summary; a histogram reveals frequency and distribution shape differently.
  • Choose the display that best answers the statistical question, and explain what information it hides.
  • Common trap: Treating an outlier as an error automatically or making a stronger claim than the data support.
