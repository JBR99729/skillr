Why Society Adopts Scientific Practices

Review the key ideas, then test how evidence and social factors influence adoption.

60-second Quick Read

  • Strong scientific evidence matters, but adoption can also depend on perceived benefit and risk, trust, access, cost, values and policy.
  • Examples: GE crops, helmet requirements, genetic screening, climate models, citizen science and Traditional Owner-led carbon farming.
  • Common trap: Low adoption does not prove weak science, and widespread adoption does not prove a claim scientifically valid.
