60-second Quick Read

  • Validity depends on whether the method actually tests the question and controls relevant confounding factors.
  • Repeats help reveal random variation, but they do not repair systematic bias or a poorly designed method.
  • Responsibility matters: safety, animal welfare, ethics, cultural authority and heritage protocols can change or prevent a method.
