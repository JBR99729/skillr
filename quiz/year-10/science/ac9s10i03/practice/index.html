Collect Precise, Replicable Data

Review the measurement essentials, then start when ready.

60-second Quick Read

  • Accuracy is closeness to a reference; precision is closeness among repeated measurements. They are not the same.
  • Choose equipment with suitable range, resolution and calibration. More displayed digits do not guarantee better measurements.
  • Common trap: repeats and averaging can reduce random variation but do not remove a systematic calibration bias.
