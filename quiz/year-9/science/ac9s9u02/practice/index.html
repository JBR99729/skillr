Reproductive systems, gametes and reproduction

Use the Skillr scratchpad or a notebook to connect reproductive structures, gamete adaptations and reproductive strategies before choosing an answer.

60-second Quick Read

  • Reproductive organs work together as systems to produce gametes, support fertilisation and enable development.
  • Sperm and egg cells have different structures because they perform different roles in sexual reproduction.
  • Common trap: Sexual reproduction increases genetic variation; asexual reproduction usually produces genetically identical offspring.
