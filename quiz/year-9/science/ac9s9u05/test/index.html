Conservation of Energy & Efficiency

Analyse unfamiliar energy systems using conservation, efficiency and complete energy accounting.

SkillrHub quiz practice is built for curriculum-aligned revision with answer feedback and a clear review path.

60-second Quick Read

  • For a defined system, total energy in = total energy transferred out or stored. A missing or excess amount signals incomplete accounting or measurement problems.
  • Efficiency can be calculated from energy or from power when input and output rates refer to the same time interval.
  • Common trap: successive efficiencies multiply; they do not add. An efficiency above 100% means the measurement, inputs or system boundary need checking.
