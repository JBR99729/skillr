Collect Precise, Replicable Data

Apply measurement and sampling decisions to unfamiliar scientific data-collection problems.

60-second Quick Read

  • High precision does not guarantee high accuracy. A sensor can give tightly clustered readings that are all systematically wrong.
  • Larger samples and faster logging can reduce sensitivity to random variation or reveal rapid change, but only if the instrument and sampling design suit the investigation.
  • Common trap: resolution, uncertainty, accuracy, calibration and reliability describe different aspects of measurement quality.
