Why Society Adopts Scientific Practices

Apply adoption reasoning to unfamiliar health, environmental and technology scenarios.

SkillrHub quiz practice is built for curriculum-aligned revision with answer feedback and a clear review path.

60-second Quick Read

  • Separate scientific effectiveness from social adoption: a practice can work but remain inaccessible, unaffordable or contested.
  • Adoption can change when incentives, infrastructure, communication or trust change—even if the underlying scientific mechanism stays the same.
  • Common trap: shared facts do not guarantee shared decisions because people and communities may weigh costs, risks, values and local impacts differently.
