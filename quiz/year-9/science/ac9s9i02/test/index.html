Valid and Reproducible Investigations

Evaluate investigation quality, error controls, safety, ethics and fieldwork responsibilities.

60-second Quick Read

  • Consistent results do not automatically mean a method is valid; a systematic bias can be reproduced very consistently.
  • Strong investigations combine question-linked measurements, control of confounders, calibrated equipment, suitable repeats and a method another group can follow.
  • Common trap: PPE does not make risk zero, and scientific value does not override animal welfare, consent, cultural protocols or heritage protections.
