Valid and Reproducible Investigations

Plan fair investigations, diagnose sources of error and manage scientific responsibilities.

60-second Quick Read

  • Validity asks whether the design really tests the intended relationship; reproducibility asks whether others can use the documented method and obtain comparable findings.
  • Random error produces scatter; systematic error produces consistent bias. Repeats help with random variation, while calibration and method redesign address systematic error.
  • Common trap: safety, ethics and heritage responsibilities are part of good investigation design, not extras added after data collection.
