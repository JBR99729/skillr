60-second Quick Read

  • Focus: analyse and connect a variety of data and information to identify and explain patterns, trends, relationships and anomalies
  • Approach: Use observations, models, data or evidence to explain the scientific idea; do not rely on a memorised definition alone.
  • Common trap: Do not confuse a claim with evidence; explain the relationship or mechanism that supports the conclusion.
