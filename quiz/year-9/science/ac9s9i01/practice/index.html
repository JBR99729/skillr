Investigable Questions, Predictions & Hypotheses

Turn broad ideas into measurable scientific questions and reasoned predictions.

60-second Quick Read

  • A strong investigable question identifies a measurable relationship, usually with an independent variable and dependent variable.
  • A reasoned prediction states what you expect and links that expectation to scientific knowledge or evidence.
  • Common trap: a question can sound scientific but still be too broad, unsafe, unethical or impossible to measure reliably.
