Reading Survey Reports

Evaluate unfamiliar survey and media claims using statistical reasoning.

60-second Quick Read

  • Separate sample size from representativeness: a huge biased sample can still estimate the wrong population.
  • For comparisons, check definitions, time periods, base values, inflation and whether percentages or absolute changes answer the claim.
  • Common trap: article sentiment, social-media polls and automated summaries are not automatically population survey estimates.
