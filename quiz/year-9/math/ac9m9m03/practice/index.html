Pythagoras, trigonometry and similarity

Use the Skillr scratchpad or a notebook to sketch right triangles, label opposite/adjacent/hypotenuse, and track scale factors before answering.

60-second Quick Read

  • Pythagoras finds a missing side in a right triangle: a² + b² = c².
  • Use tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent when a problem gives a vertical rise and horizontal run.
  • Common trap: Similar triangles scale lengths by k, but areas by k².
