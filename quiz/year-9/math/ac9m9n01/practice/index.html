Real numbers, exact values and inequalities

Use exact forms such as π and surds where required, and convert to approximations only when asked.

60-second Quick Read

  • Rational numbers can be written as fractions; terminating and repeating decimals are rational.
  • Irrational numbers such as π, √2 and √5 are real numbers but cannot be written exactly as fractions.
  • Common trap: When solving inequalities, reverse the inequality sign only when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.
