Algebraic expressions, expansion and factorising

Simplify carefully, expand every term, and factorise by matching both product and sum.

60-second Quick Read

  • Expand brackets before collecting like terms.
  • For (x + a)(x + b), the middle coefficient is a + b and the constant term is ab.
  • Common trap: In (x + 5)², the middle term is 10x, not 5x.
