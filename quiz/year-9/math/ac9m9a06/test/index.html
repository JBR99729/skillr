Function transformations

Use the Skillr scratchpad or a notebook to sketch the parent graph, then mark reflection, stretch, compression or translation.

60-second Quick Read

  • Multiplying a function by a positive number changes vertical scale; a negative multiplier reflects it in the x-axis.
  • Adding outside the function shifts the graph up or down; changing inside brackets shifts it left or right.
  • Common trap: In (x − h), the graph shifts right h; in (x + h), it shifts left h.
