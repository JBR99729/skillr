📚 Reading Strategy

Reading Comprehension

Read the passage carefully, then answer only from information stated or reasonably implied by the text.

Useful reminders

  • Read for argument, evidence, assumptions and tone.
  • Distinguish strong evidence from a plausible distractor.
  • Use context and structure to infer meaning.
  • Select the answer best supported by the passage.

Key words

main idea — the most important message
detail — specific information in a text
inference — a conclusion based on clues
evidence — words or details supporting an answer
context — surrounding words and ideas that help meaning
Daily practice: these drills focus on useful basics to intermediate English skills. They are designed to build confidence through repeated practice, not to teach every English curriculum outcome.
