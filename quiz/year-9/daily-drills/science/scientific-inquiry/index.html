Scientific Inquiry, Error and Uncertainty

Random error contributes to scatter; systematic error shifts results consistently. Good methods manage variables and quantify uncertainty where appropriate.

What to remember

  • Random error causes scatter.
  • Systematic error can shift all readings.
  • Repeats help estimate random variation.
  • Calibration can reveal systematic issues.
  • Uncertainty limits precision.

Key words

random error — unpredictable variation
systematic error — consistent directional error
uncertainty — measurement limitation
calibration — checking instrument against standard
precision — closeness of repeated readings
