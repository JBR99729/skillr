Science Vocabulary

Key terms span atoms, homeostasis, tectonics, reactions, waves and evidence quality.

What to remember

  • Elements contain one type of atom.
  • Homeostasis regulates internal conditions.
  • Plate tectonics explains large-scale Earth patterns.
  • Reactions rearrange atoms.
  • Uncertainty limits measurement precision.

Key words

atom — smallest unit retaining element identity
homeostasis — regulation of internal conditions
plate tectonics — model of moving lithospheric plates
reaction — rearrangement forming products
uncertainty — limit on measurement precision
