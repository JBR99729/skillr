Word Origins, Morphemes and Unfamiliar Words

Read the quick preparation notes, then start when you are ready.

60-second Quick Read

  • Students decompose words into meaningful parts, connect roots across curriculum vocabulary, account for pronunciation or spelling shifts and verify inferred meanings in context and reference sources.
  • Example: Roots provide clues, not complete definitions. Prefixes, suffixes and context refine meaning, and spelling may reflect origin.
  • Common trap: Root clue treated as full definition. Use affixes and context.
