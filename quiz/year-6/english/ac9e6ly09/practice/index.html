Word Origins, Morphemes and Unfamiliar Words

Review the key ideas, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Break unfamiliar words into meaningful parts: root, prefix and suffix.
  • Use word origins and related word families as clues, then check the meaning in context.
  • Pronunciation and spelling can change even when words share a root.
  • Common trap: Treating the root clue as the complete definition without using affixes and context.
