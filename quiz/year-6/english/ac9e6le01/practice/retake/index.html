60-second Quick Read

  • Students compare how readers may judge character decisions differently, use evidence from action, narration and context and distinguish interpretation from unsupported assumption or stereotype.
  • Example: A response is strengthened by specific textual evidence and relevant context. Context explains possibilities but should not be used to stereotype all people from a time or place.
  • Common trap: Personal reaction treated as self-evident. Explain feature and evidence.
