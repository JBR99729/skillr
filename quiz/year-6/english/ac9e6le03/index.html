60-second Quick Read

  • Students compare several texts by an author, identify recurring patterns and explain how combinations of syntax, imagery, humour, pacing, narration and theme create recognisable style.
  • Example: One feature alone rarely defines style. Use repeated patterns across texts and distinguish author, narrator and genre conventions.
  • Common trap: Narrator and author treated as identical. The narrator is a crafted textual role.
