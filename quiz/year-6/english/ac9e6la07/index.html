60-second Quick Read

  • Students distinguish decorative, organisational, evidential and explanatory visual roles, read labels and scales and explain how visuals combine with captions and surrounding prose.
  • Example: A visual may extend information beyond the prose. Readers should cross-reference, not treat it as decoration or automatically trustworthy evidence.
  • Common trap: All visuals called pictures. Name the form and function precisely.
