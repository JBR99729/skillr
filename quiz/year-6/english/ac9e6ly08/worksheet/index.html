AC9E6LY08 · Before you begin

Students learn to use phonic knowledge of common and less common grapheme-phoneme relationships to read and write increasingly complex words. They move from recognising the feature to explaining how it works in context, then applying it deliberately in their own reading, speaking, writing or multimodal creation.
