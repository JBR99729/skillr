Energy Transfer and Transformation

Energy can move between objects and change form. Useful and dissipated energy should both be accounted for.

What to remember

  • Energy can be transferred by heating.
  • Electrical energy can transform to light.
  • Moving objects have kinetic energy.
  • Energy is conserved overall.
  • Some energy becomes less useful as it spreads to surroundings.

Key words

energy — capacity to cause change/do work
transfer — energy moving between stores/systems
transformation — change of energy form
kinetic — energy of motion
conservation — total energy accounted for
