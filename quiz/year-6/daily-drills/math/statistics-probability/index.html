Statistics and Probability

Use mean, median, mode and range appropriately and represent probabilities from 0 to 1.

Key ideas

  • mean median mode
  • range
  • data displays
  • probability scale
  • simple experiments

Worked example: probability 0.25 = 25%.

Common trap: Mean and median answer different questions about centre.

Key words

mean — sum ÷ number of values
median — middle ordered value
mode — most frequent
range — max−min
probability — likelihood from 0 to 1
