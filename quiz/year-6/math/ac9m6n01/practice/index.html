Integers on Number Lines and the Cartesian Plane

Use zero as the reference point and read each sign as both a value and a direction.

60-second Quick Read

  • Negative integers lie left of zero on a horizontal number line and below zero on a vertical one; positive integers lie right or above.
  • In context, + can represent profit, above sea level, right or up; − can represent loss, below sea level, left or down.
  • For coordinates, read x first, then y. The signs identify the quadrant.
