Organising Ideas to Shape Meaning

Review the key ideas, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Identify how ideas are organised, not only what the text says.
  • Compare structures such as proposition and support, cause and effect, and compare and contrast.
  • Explain how the chosen organisation shapes meaning and affects the text’s effectiveness.
  • Common trap: Naming a structure without explaining its effect on the reader or meaning.
