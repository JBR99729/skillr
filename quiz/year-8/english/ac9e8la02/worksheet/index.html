Answer guide · AC9E8LA02

  1. It is a simile suggesting dependability or guidance, rather than literal resemblance to a building.
  2. It suggests the argument is fragile and may collapse when one part is challenged.
  3. For example, ‘Its pace is like a bicycle with a slipping chain’ suggests uneven progress or repeated interruptions.
  4. One may value clear resolution and dislike exclusion; another may value mystery. Context and evidence should support each interpretation.

If help is needed: Naming a metaphor does not explain its layered meaning. Link its associations to the thing being evaluated and acknowledge context.
