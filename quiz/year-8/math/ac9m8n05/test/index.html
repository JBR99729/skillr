Rational number and percentage modelling

Use the Skillr scratchpad or a notebook to identify the original value, calculate the percentage amount, and check whether the model increases or decreases.

60-second Quick Read

  • For a percentage increase, add the percentage amount to the original value.
  • For a percentage decrease, subtract the percentage amount from the original value.
  • Common trap: Percentage-point change and relative percentage change are different ideas.
