Linear expressions: expand, factorise and simplify

Use the Skillr scratchpad or a notebook to expand brackets, collect like terms, factorise fully and name the algebraic property being used.

60-second Quick Read

  • Expand by multiplying every term inside the bracket by the outside factor.
  • Simplify by collecting like terms only, and watch signs when subtracting brackets.
  • Common trap: Factorising fully means taking out the highest common factor, not just any common factor.
