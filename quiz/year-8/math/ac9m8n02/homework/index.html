Answer guide · AC9M8N02

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. 3⁷; add exponents when multiplying powers of the same base.
  2. 7⁴; subtract the exponents.
  3. 12³/12³ = 1 and 12³⁻³ = 12⁰, so 12⁰ = 1.
  4. The common base remains 2: 2³ × 2⁴ = 2⁷.
  5. 5⁶. Three factors of 5² contain six factors of 5 altogether.
  6. The sum is 16; the product is 64. The multiplication law does not apply to addition.
  7. For example, 10² × 10⁴; 10⁸ ÷ 10²; (10²)³.
  8. Yes: 3 × 3 × 4 × 4 = (3 × 4)(3 × 4) = 12².
