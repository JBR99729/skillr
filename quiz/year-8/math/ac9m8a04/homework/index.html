Question 6

Question 7

A learner concludes two lines never meet after viewing only -1 ≤ x ≤ 1. What should be checked?

Test the conjecture that y = mx + c passes through (0,c) for m = -3, 0 and 4 with c = 2. Explain why it works generally.

Answer guide · AC9M8A04

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. y = -2, 1, 4, 7.
  2. The slope remains 2; every y-value increases by 3, so the graph shifts up 3.
  3. Both cross the y-axis at 5. The negative-slope graph decreases from left to right; the positive-slope graph increases.
  4. Differences are 3, 5, 7, which are not constant, although the values increase.
  5. y = 3n + 1; at n = 4, y = 13.
  6. Values are 1,3,5 and 1,6,11. The intercept is unchanged; the second line has a larger slope.
  7. Expand the viewing window or solve for their intersection. A limited graph window does not prove they never meet.
  8. All give y = 2 at x = 0. In general m × 0 + c = c.
