Part A · Check the skill

Question 1

Two triangles have side lengths 5, 7 and 8 cm. Are they congruent? State the condition.

Question 2

Two triangles have equal angles but corresponding side lengths 3 cm and 6 cm. What can be concluded?

Question 3

A corresponding side changes from 4 cm to 10 cm in similar triangles. Find the scale factor.

Question 4

Does equal area prove two triangles congruent? Give a counterexample.

Part B · Apply and reason

Question 5

Similar triangles have matching sides 6 cm and 15 cm. Another side of the smaller is 8 cm. Find its match.

Question 6

Two triangles each have sides 5 cm and 7 cm with the included angle 60°. Explain why they are congruent.

Question 7

A triangle is reflected in a line. Which lengths and angles change?

Question 8

A learner checks two side ratios for triangle similarity but ignores the third. Why can this be insufficient?

Check answers and reasoning

Answer guide · AC9M8SP01

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. Yes, SSS: all corresponding side lengths match.
  2. They are similar, with scale factor 2 from the smaller to the larger, but not congruent.
  3. 2.5.
  4. No. Right triangles with legs 3 and 4, and 2 and 6, both have area 6 but different side lengths.
  5. 8 × 15/6 = 20 cm.
  6. SAS: two corresponding sides and the angle between them match.
  7. None of the side lengths or angle sizes change; the image is congruent, although its orientation changes.
  8. The unchecked side may break proportionality. Use a complete valid condition such as SSS similarity or two matching angles.
