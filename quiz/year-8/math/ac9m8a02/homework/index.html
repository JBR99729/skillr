Answer guide · AC9M8A02

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. x = 5; subtract 7, then divide by 3.
  2. Points (-2,-3), (-1,-1), (0,1), (1,3), (2,5), joined by a straight line.
  3. x < 13; open circle at 13 with values to its left shaded.
  4. 2x = 12, so x = 6. Substitution gives 12 + 5 = 17.
  5. (0,4) and (4,0). Set x = 0 and y = 0 respectively.
  6. x = 6. Both sides equal 21.
  7. x ≥ -4; division by a negative reverses the inequality.
  8. 3x = 12 has solution x = 4, the x-coordinate of the intersection.
