Question 1

A runner travels 5 km in 25 minutes. Find average speed in km/h.

Question 2

Find the price per kilogram if 750 g costs $6.

Question 3

Part B · Apply and reason

Question 5

Data plans offer 20 GB for $16, 50 GB for $30, or 100 GB for $45. Compare dollars per GB.

Question 6

A cyclist rides 18 km in 45 minutes and 12 km in 30 minutes. Find average speed for riding time only.

Question 7

A tap supplies 9 L in 30 seconds at a steady rate. How much in 2 minutes? State the assumption.

Question 8

A driver averages 60 km/h for one hour and 30 km/h for two hours. Why is the overall average not 45 km/h? Calculate it.

Check answers and reasoning

Answer guide · AC9M8M05

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. 12 km/h, since 25 minutes = 5/12 hour.
  2. $8/kg; $6 ÷ 0.75 kg.
  3. 25 m/s; divide by 3.6.
  4. $8/kg compared with $7/kg. The 1 kg pack has the lower unit price.
  5. $0.80/GB, $0.60/GB and $0.45/GB respectively.
  6. 30 km in 1.25 h gives 24 km/h. Rest time is excluded as requested.
  7. 36 L, assuming the flow rate remains constant.
  8. The times are unequal. Total distance 120 km divided by 3 h gives 40 km/h.
