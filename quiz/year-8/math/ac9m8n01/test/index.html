Irrational numbers, square roots and π

Use exact notation carefully, identify rational approximations, and apply π in circle problems before choosing an answer.

60-second Quick Read

  • Irrational numbers include π, the golden ratio and square roots of non-perfect squares.
  • Historical values such as 22/7 and 355/113 are rational approximations to π, not exact values.
  • Common trap: Circumference uses C = πd or C = 2πr; area uses A = πr².
