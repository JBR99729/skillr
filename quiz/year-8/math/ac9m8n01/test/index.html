Irrational Numbers in Context

Review the key ideas, then start when you are ready.

Quick preparation

  • Irrational numbers have exact values but non-terminating, non-repeating decimal representations.
  • The number π can be located between decimal bounds, and √2 appears as the diagonal of a one-unit square.
  • Keep exact values such as π and √2 until an approximation is requested.
  • Common trap: Treating a rounded decimal, such as 3.14, as exactly equal to π.
