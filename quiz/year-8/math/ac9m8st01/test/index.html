Data collection and sampling

Use the Skillr scratchpad or a notebook to distinguish precision from accuracy and decide whether a dataset or sampling method is representative enough for the intended conclusion.

60-second Quick Read

  • Precision describes how close repeated measurements are to each other; accuracy describes closeness to the true or accepted value.
  • Digital tools and simulations can improve consistency and enable repeated trials, but calibration, design and sampling choices can still introduce error or bias.
  • Common trap: A large or digital dataset is not automatically representative or unbiased.
