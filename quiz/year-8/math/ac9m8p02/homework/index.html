Answer guide · AC9M8P02

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. HH, HT, TH, TT.
  2. 2/4 = 1/2; outcomes HT and TH.
  3. Chess only 7; both 5; music only 10; neither 8.
  4. 22/30 = 11/15; count the overlap once.
  5. Chess row: 5 music, 7 no music. No-chess row: 10 music, 8 no music. Total 30.
  6. The 5 doing both were counted twice; subtract the overlap once to obtain 22.
  7. Red-S, Red-M, Red-L, Blue-S, Blue-M, Blue-L; six combinations.
  8. No. Listing outcomes alone does not determine their probabilities; customers may prefer some combinations.
