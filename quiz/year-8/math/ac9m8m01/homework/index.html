Answer guide · AC9M8M01

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. 60 - 6 = 54 m².
  2. 32 m. Removing the corner replaces lengths 3 and 2 with equal-length inner edges.
  3. Area counts square units covering a surface; perimeter measures one-dimensional boundary length.
  4. The cut-out removes material, so subtract its area rather than add it.
  5. Area 40 + 12 = 52 m². Perimeter 26 + 14 - 2 × 3 = 34 m.
  6. Both original perimeters count it, but after joining it is an internal edge and contributes nothing to the outside boundary.
  7. 24 + 6 = 30 m².
  8. No. A 2 m by 6 m rectangle and a 3 m by 4 m rectangle both have area 12 m² but perimeters 16 m and 14 m.
