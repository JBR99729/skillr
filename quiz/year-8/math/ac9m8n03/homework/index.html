Answer guide · AC9M8N03

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. 0.375, terminating.
  2. 0.666… with the digit 6 repeating indefinitely.
  3. It has rounded the recurring decimal 0.1666… to fit its display.
  4. 0.333 = 333/1000; 1/3 = 0.333… with threes continuing indefinitely.
  5. 7/20 = 0.35 and 11/25 = 0.44 terminate. 5/6 = 0.8333… and 4/9 = 0.444… recur.
  6. 0.4 < 0.44 < 0.444… = 4/9.
  7. Yes. 6/15 = 2/5 = 0.4. Classification uses the simplified fraction.
  8. 1/11 = 0.090909…; block 09 repeats. Repeating remainders in long division establish continuation beyond the finite screen.
