Answer guide · AC9M8SP03

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. (3,2,5).
  2. +3 in x, no change in y, +4 in z.
  3. The y and z values refer to different axes. Swapping them changes northing and height.
  4. It is at height zero on the plane z = 0.
  5. (0,0,0), (3,0,0), (0,2,0), (3,2,0), (0,0,4), (3,0,4), (0,2,4), (3,2,4).
  6. (-2,5,4).
  7. The z-axis; x and y stay constant while z changes.
  8. They can have equal x and y but different z values, for example (1,2,3) and (1,2,6).
