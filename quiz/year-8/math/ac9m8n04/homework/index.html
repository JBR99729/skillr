Answer guide · AC9M8N04

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. 0.
  2. 6; work from left to right for multiplication and division.
  3. 4/6 + 5/6 = 9/6 = 3/2.
  4. -4² = -(4²) = -16. (-4)² = 16 because the negative number is the base.
  5. -$35; -18 + 45 - 62 = -35. The account is overdrawn by $35.
  6. 3.5; multiply first to obtain -2.5 + 6.
  7. -5/4 = -1.25, which is one quarter to the left of -1.
  8. For example, 8 × 1/2 = 4. Multiplying a positive number by a factor between zero and one decreases it.
