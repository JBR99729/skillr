Part A · Check the skill

Question 1

If P(rain) = 0.3, find P(no rain) for the same specified period.

Question 2

A bag has 4 red, 3 blue and 5 green counters. Find P(not red) for one random draw.

Question 3

Is ‘blue’ the complement of ‘red’ in that bag? Explain.

Question 4

If P(not late) = 0.92, find P(late).

Part B · Apply and reason

Question 5

A fair die is rolled. Find the probability of not rolling a multiple of 3.

Question 6

Why must an event and its complement not overlap?

Question 7

A game has probability 7/20 of winning and no draws. Find the probability of losing.

Question 8

If draws become possible, can losing still automatically be called the complement of winning? Explain.

Check answers and reasoning

Answer guide · AC9M8P01

Other valid methods and well-supported explanations are welcome.

  1. 0.7, since complementary probabilities sum to 1.
  2. 8/12 = 2/3.
  3. No. ‘Not red’ includes both blue and green.
  4. 0.08.
  5. Multiples of 3 are 3 and 6, so P(not a multiple) = 4/6 = 2/3.
  6. An outcome cannot both satisfy the event and fail to satisfy it; together they cover the whole sample space.
  7. 13/20.
  8. No. The complement of winning includes losing and drawing.
