Quadrilateral properties and proof

Use the Skillr scratchpad or a notebook to reason from stated side, angle, diagonal and symmetry properties.

60-second Quick Read

  • Squares, rectangles, parallelograms, rhombuses, trapeziums and kites are distinguished by side, angle, diagonal and symmetry properties.
  • Congruent triangles can be used to prove equal sides and angles inside quadrilaterals.
  • Common trap: Never assume a property just because a diagram looks square, parallel or perpendicular.
