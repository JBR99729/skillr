Chemical Reactions

Chemical reactions rearrange particles to form new substances. Evidence can include gas, precipitate, colour or temperature change in context.

What to remember

  • Chemical changes form new substances.
  • Atoms are rearranged in reactions.
  • Mass is conserved in closed systems.
  • Gas formation can indicate reaction.
  • Temperature change can indicate energy transfer in reaction.

Key words

reactant — starting substance
product — substance formed
reaction — process forming new substances
conservation of mass — mass accounted for in closed system
precipitate — insoluble solid formed in solution
