Recent improvements

Newest releases are shown first. The whole number advances on a new release day; decimals mark additional releases on the same day.

Release 11.0

Science assessment banks rebuilt through Year 5

  • Rebuilt and published all Year 2 Science production assessment banks with fully authored Practice and Test questions.
  • Completed the Year 3 Science production rebuild and strengthened assessment quality with improved independent Test banks.
  • Improved validation by reducing duplicate stems, strengthening answer integrity, and preserving Classroom View, worksheets and topic pages.
  • Continued expanding SkillrHub's production-ready Australian Curriculum assessment library with higher-quality, misconception-driven question banks.
Release 10.0

Classroom View and curriculum mapping expanded across F–10

  • Classroom View is now a projection-ready Australian Curriculum teaching resource for interactive whiteboards, projectors and shared classroom screens, with Topic Guide, Practice and Test links kept together.
  • Related Classroom Views now connect nearby curriculum topics so teachers can move between prerequisite, current and extension concepts without breaking the lesson flow.
  • All 676 canonical Foundation–Year 10 Maths, English and Science topic pages now include curriculum mapping for the Australian Curriculum plus equivalent Victorian Curriculum, NSW, USA, Ontario, England and India references.
  • Try Classroom View: Year 3 Maths — telling time, Year 8 English — comprehension, or Year 10 Science — scientific validation.
Release 9.0

F–10 topic-page first pass completed

  • Maths and Science topic pages across Foundation–Year 10 completed a broad first-pass review for cleaner single-column layouts, static teaching content and dependable links between Topic Guides, worksheets, Practice and Tests.
  • All 284 Foundation to Year 10 English topic pages were checked for indexability, curriculum identity, static content, layout and connected learning resources.
  • Resource-link defects were repaired across 89 Year 7–10 English topic pages, including obsolete Homework routes and Practice buttons that incorrectly opened worksheets.
  • New automated checks now protect the F–10 English topic-page set and the shared curriculum layout from future regressions.
Release 8.0

Topic teaching strengthened across Years 2–9

  • Year 2 Maths topic guidance deepened across measurement, fractions, calendar intervals, analog time and turns, with clearer worked thinking, Revision Notes and misconception support.
  • Year 2 Science sound and vibration teaching strengthened with clearer links between actions, vibrations, loudness, pitch, muffling and sound-making investigations.
  • Year 3 Maths and Science topic pages strengthened for more complete self-study, clearer worked models and stronger curriculum coverage.
  • Year 4 Maths, Science, Year 5 Maths, Science, Year 6 Maths and Science received broader topic-teaching improvements with clearer explanations, examples, revision support and misconception checks.
  • Year 7 Science inquiry and science-as-a-human-endeavour content was further strengthened, including evidence, claims, source credibility, communication and evaluation of scientific conclusions.
  • Year 8 Maths, Science, Year 9 Maths and Science topic teaching was strengthened for clearer concept development, self-study and revision.
Release 7.0

Year 7 Science — science as a human endeavour

  • AC9S7H01 strengthened with clearer teaching on how new evidence and different perspectives change scientific knowledge, plus improved Important Q&A and Revision Notes.
  • AC9S7H02 rebuilt around cultural perspectives and world views, with stronger topic guidance, homework and curriculum-specific Practice and Test questions.
  • AC9S7H03 expanded across all seven elaborations with 40 reviewed MCQs, stronger revision guidance, short-answer work and extended-response practice on real-world scientific responses.
  • AC9S7H04 expanded across all six elaborations with 46 unique reviewed MCQs, 20 short-answer questions, Important Q&A and Revision Notes on how science communication shapes viewpoints, policies and regulations.
Release 6.0

Year 7 curriculum content upgrades

  • Year 7 Maths topic pages, homework and assessment banks strengthened across major Number, Measurement, Space, Statistics and Probability topics with more reasoning and less generic question content.
  • Year 7 Science AC9S7U01–AC9S7U06 upgraded with clearer topic teaching, rebuilt homework and substantially expanded Practice and Test banks.
