Release 13.0

More freebies and complete homeschool term packs are on the way

  • The Products page is growing: new free samples are being added, so remember to have a look.
  • Complete term-wise books are in development: planned for homeschooling families and independent learners, with core learning designed to work offline.
  • The first full model is ready: Foundation Maths Term 1 brings together a 10-week planner, diagnostic, parent teaching guides, hands-on activities, worksheets, mastery checks, assessment, answers and evidence records.
  • Designed to support home-education documentation: Australian Curriculum v9 alignment and dated records can make it easier for parents and carers to organise evidence. SkillrHub is not endorsed by an education authority and does not guarantee registration, approval or compliance.
  • Useful across borders: Australian families in Canada, India, the UK, New Zealand, the USA and elsewhere can use the packs to maintain Australian curriculum continuity. Local requirements should always be checked.

Release 12.1

Ben and Vani join the SkillrHub learning journey

Meet our two fictional learning companions, with familiar faces that mature as students progress through school.

  • Go one step at a time: choose Guide me on a topic or Classroom View, follow existing worked steps, and revisit a step with Show me again.
  • Try independently: use Let me try or hide the companions. During an active Test, the guide steps back.
  • Find a starting point in Practice: request an available authored question hint or a general strategy prompt.
  • Meet the faces behind our learning messages: Ben and Vani introduce tips, feedback invitations and updates. Meet the companions and read their story.
  • Age-appropriate presentation: guidance becomes quieter in secondary years, and sensitive science lessons include a factual topic note.

Try a guided Year 4 fractions lesson or open its Practice.

Release 12.0

More learning support for students, families and teachers

  • Learn a topic in more than one way: optional video explanations now sit alongside written lessons, worked examples, worksheets, Practice and Tests.
  • Plan homeschool learning more easily: new planner packs for Foundation, Year 1 and Year 2 organise curriculum resources into a practical sequence.
  • See clearer, stronger topic teaching: topic pages continue to gain improved explanations, examples, quick-learning support, diagrams and worked models.
  • Use reviewed early-years content with greater confidence: Maths, Science and English are now Content Verified from Foundation through Year 3.
  • Year 4 Maths is complete and Content Verified: all 23 curriculum codes now have 48 Practice and 16 Test questions, with strengthened teaching and homework support.
  • More Year 3–4 content has been strengthened: Year 3 English and Science received major reviewed upgrades, while Year 4 Science AC9S4U01–U03 now have reviewed full resource sets and 48 Practice + 16 Test banks.

Release 10.0

Classroom View and curriculum mapping expanded across F–10

  • Classroom View is now a projection-ready Australian Curriculum teaching resource for interactive whiteboards, projectors and shared classroom screens, with Topic Guide, Practice and Test links kept together.
  • Related Classroom Views now connect nearby curriculum topics so teachers can move between prerequisite, current and extension concepts without breaking the lesson flow.
  • All 676 canonical Foundation–Year 10 Maths, English and Science topic pages now include curriculum mapping for the Australian Curriculum plus equivalent Victorian Curriculum, NSW, USA, Ontario, England and India references.
  • Try Classroom View: Year 3 Maths — telling time, Year 8 English — comprehension, or Year 10 Science — scientific validation.
