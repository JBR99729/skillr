Short release notes covering curriculum-aligned question banks, Practice and Test improvements, teacher resources and quality reviews across SkillrHub.

19 August 2026

Year 9 Maths & Science question-bank quality review

  • Year 9 Mathematics question banks reviewed for correctness, clarity, curriculum alignment and useful basic-to-medium practice.
  • Year 9 Science question banks reviewed with focus on scientific accuracy, age-appropriate wording, application and misconception checks.
  • Year 9 Practice and Test questions refined to reduce generic, repetitive and low-value question patterns.

18 August 2026

Year 10 Maths & Science question-bank quality review

  • Year 10 Mathematics question quality review completed across the curriculum question banks.
  • Year 10 Mathematics generic and low-value question patterns replaced with authored calculation, reasoning, modelling and interpretation questions.
  • Year 10 Mathematics reviewed gifted-extension layer added to Practice and Test banks; Year 10 Mathematics question content is now frozen unless reopened for a future revision.
  • Year 10 Science question quality review completed for AC9S10U01–AC9S10U05, with stronger question variety, scientific precision, application and misconception checks.
  • Year 10 Science AC9S10U01–AC9S10U05 banks standardised to 24 Practice and 16 Test questions per curriculum code.
  • Curriculum-code checks strengthened so draft question sets are not published against an incorrect Australian Curriculum code.

16 August 2026

Curriculum resource improvements

  • AC9M7N06 rational number operations expanded with a 25-slide teacher deck, 56-question Practice bank and separate 40-question Test bank.
  • AC9M7N06 question-bank quality reviewed; unsuitable raw floating-point distractors replaced with student-appropriate alternatives and worked explanations.
  • AC9M3M03 units of time and duration expanded with a classroom teacher deck and 80-question Practice bank.
  • AC9M3M04 analogue and digital time expanded with a teacher deck, 52 unique Practice questions and 44 unique Test questions after quality replacements.
  • Release checks strengthened to protect authored topic content, teacher-resource links and assembled question banks during publishing.
