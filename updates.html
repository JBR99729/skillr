SkillrHub Maths & Science updates

Short release notes covering curriculum-aligned question banks, Practice and Test improvements, teacher resources and learning updates across SkillrHub.

20 August 2026

Year 10 Maths & Science learning resources upgraded

  • Year 10 Mathematics topic pages re-authored across Number and Algebra, Measurement, Space, Statistics and Probability, with clearer explanations and a more student-friendly learning structure.
  • Year 10 Science topic pages re-authored across Science Understanding and Science as a Human Endeavour, with clearer concept explanations and stronger revision support.
  • Science teacher resources continue to be upgraded so teacher slides align more closely with the learning content on SkillrHub; Year 7 AC9S7U01 teacher slides were updated as part of this work.

19 August 2026

Year 9 Maths & Science question-bank improvements

  • Year 9 Mathematics question banks improved for correctness, clarity, curriculum alignment and useful basic-to-medium practice.
  • Year 9 Science question banks improved with stronger scientific accuracy, age-appropriate wording, application and misconception checks.
  • Year 9 Practice and Test questions refined to reduce generic, repetitive and low-value question patterns.

18 August 2026

Year 10 Maths & Science question-bank improvements

  • Year 10 Mathematics question banks upgraded with more authored calculation, reasoning, modelling and interpretation questions.
  • A reviewed gifted-extension layer was added to Year 10 Mathematics Practice and Test banks for students ready for greater challenge.
  • Year 10 Science question banks upgraded for AC9S10U01–AC9S10U05 with stronger question variety, scientific precision, application and misconception checks.
  • Year 10 Science AC9S10U01–AC9S10U05 now includes 24 Practice and 16 Test questions per curriculum code.

16 August 2026

Curriculum resource improvements

  • AC9M7N06 rational number operations expanded with a 25-slide teacher deck, 56-question Practice bank and separate 40-question Test bank.
  • AC9M7N06 question content improved with more student-appropriate alternatives and worked explanations.
  • AC9M3M03 units of time and duration expanded with a classroom teacher deck and 80-question Practice bank.
  • AC9M3M04 analogue and digital time expanded with a teacher deck, 52 unique Practice questions and 44 unique Test questions.

Feedback and review

SkillrHub resources are continually reviewed using curriculum checks and feedback from teachers, parents and students. If you find an error, broken link or have an idea to improve a resource, please let us know.
