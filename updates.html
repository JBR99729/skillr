16 August 2026

Year 7 rational number operations: AC9M7N06

Curriculum aligned · Quality reviewed

Fractions, decimals and percentages

AC9M7N06 now includes an expanded topic guide, a 25-slide teacher deck, a 56-question Practice bank and a separate 40-question Test bank covering positive rational numbers and efficient calculation strategies.

Students practise fraction operations, decimals, percentages, equivalent representations, reciprocals, estimation and real-life applications involving shopping, recipes, paint, land use, nutrition and energy accounts.

Topic guide · Teacher deck · Practice · Test

Question-bank quality

Strong questions retained

All sound existing questions remain. Two Practice questions containing unsuitable raw floating-point distractors were replaced with clean, student-appropriate alternatives and worked explanations.

Teacher resource

Expanded classroom teaching sequence

The new teacher deck develops equivalent representations, standard form, all four fraction operations, decimal and percentage strategies, common misconceptions, guided practice and an exit ticket.
