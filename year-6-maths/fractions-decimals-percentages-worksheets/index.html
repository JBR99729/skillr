Parent and teacher guide

Year 6 students need to see fractions, decimals and percentages as different names for related values. Use number lines, hundred grids and conversion tables to compare them. A common mistake is converting by memory without checking whether the value is reasonable. Start with benchmark equivalents such as 1/2, 1/4, 3/4, 0.5, 0.25 and 50%. Then move to less familiar values. Ask students to choose the form that makes a problem easiest to solve.

Common mistakes to watch for

Ask the student to explain their method before moving to a test. If they can only guess, return to one visual model and one worked example. Short practice with feedback usually works better than a long worksheet completed without review.
