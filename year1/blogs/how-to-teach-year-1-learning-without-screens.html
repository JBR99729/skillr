How to Teach Year 1 Learning Without Screens

Screen-free learning works well for Year 1 when it combines movement, talking, practical tasks and short bursts of practice.

Make the lesson active

Use a simple routine: model, do, talk and record. Children can solve a problem with objects, explain their thinking aloud and then complete a short worksheet or drawing.

This approach supports attention, oral language and mathematical reasoning without relying on digital tools.
