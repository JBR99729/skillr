How to Teach Year 1 Addition and Subtraction to 20 Without Stress

Year 1 addition and subtraction can feel tricky because children need to move from counting every object to using known facts and mental strategies. The key is to keep the learning hands-on and short.

A simple approach that works

Start with real objects such as toy animals, counters or blocks. Ask students to make 10, then add or take away a few more. This helps them see quantity before they move to written sums.

Once children are confident with concrete materials, move to drawings and number lines. Finally, introduce simple word problems so children connect the maths to real life.
