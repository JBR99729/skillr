How to Teach Year 1 Maths at Home

Short, practical maths activities can make a big difference at home when they are easy to set up and closely linked to the classroom curriculum.

Choose one small goal

It is better to focus on one idea such as counting on, comparing lengths or recognising patterns than to try too much in one sitting.

Use daily routines

Maths can be built into everyday routines such as setting the table, packing lunch boxes or sorting washing.
