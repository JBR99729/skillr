How to Teach Year 1 Science Investigations

Science investigations in Year 1 can be simple, safe and very effective when they focus on careful observation and clear questions.

Keep the investigation short

A one-step investigation is usually enough. Children can observe, describe and compare what they see before moving to the next idea.

Encourage predictions

Asking children what they think might happen helps them start thinking like scientists. It also builds oral language and reasoning.
