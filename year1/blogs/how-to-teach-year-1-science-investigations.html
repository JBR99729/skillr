How to Teach Year 1 Science Investigations

Science investigations in Year 1 can be simple, safe and effective when they focus on careful observation, clear questions and age-appropriate predictions.

For printable resources across the subject, visit our Year 1 Science worksheets and activities hub.

Keep the investigation short

A one-step investigation is usually enough. Children can observe, describe and compare what they see before moving to the next idea. Keeping the task focused also makes it easier for students to record what happened and discuss the evidence they noticed.

Encourage predictions

Asking children what they think might happen helps them start thinking like scientists. It also builds oral language and reasoning. After the activity, ask students to compare their prediction with their observation rather than focusing only on whether the prediction was correct.

Build observation and recording skills

Year 1 students can record observations with drawings, labels, simple sentences, counts and informal measurements. These methods help children connect hands-on investigations with the inquiry skills used throughout the Australian Curriculum Year 1 Science content.
