What this skill means

Learning goal: I can recognise and use vocabulary that belongs to a learning topic.

  • Use topic-specific words when talking about school learning.
  • Use appropriate words in an Acknowledgement of Country with respect for local protocols.
  • Recognise vocabulary linked to topics such as weather and seasons.

Steps to use

Hear word → Link to topic → Use in sentence → Explain

See it, then try it

Worked example

Question: Which word belongs to science habitats: shelter or chapter? Shelter belongs because it names a living thing’s need.

wordmeaningtopicsentence
Try these
  1. Topic word wall — Sort words into learning areas.
    habitat / character / tens
  2. Word meaning check — Match a topic word to a child-friendly meaning.
    observe → look closely
  3. Use it orally — Say a sentence that uses the topic word accurately.
    The habitat has shelter.
Common mix-ups
  • Knows word sound only — Connect the word to meaning and topic.
  • Everyday meaning only — Check the subject meaning.
  • Does not use in sentence — Practise a sentence, not just a definition.

Curriculum Coverage & Elaborations

Curriculum Coverage & Elaborations

The content description is the required target. The elaborations below are taught as examples and contexts, not as disconnected checklist wording.

  • E1: using appropriate topic-specific vocabulary when discussing a learning area topic
  • E2: using appropriate vocabulary for an Acknowledgement of Country at assemblies and other school events using protocols to recognise the Traditional Owners of Country or Place
  • E3: identifying words for topics studied at school; for example, vocabulary used for weather and seasons
