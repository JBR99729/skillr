  2. Blend and write — Blend a word, then write the sounds in order.
    shop
  3. Two-syllable clap — Clap and read a two-syllable word.
    sun-set

Curriculum Coverage & Elaborations

The content description is the required target. The elaborations below are taught as examples and contexts, not as disconnected checklist wording.

  • E1: using knowledge of letters and sounds to write words with short vowels; for example, “man”, and common long vowel sounds; for example, “time”
  • E2: using knowledge of letter sounds to write single-syllable words with consonant digraphs and consonant blends; for example, “wish” and “rest”

3. Common Student Misconceptions

  • Digraph counted twice: sh/ch/th can make one sound.
  • Guessing from first letter: Look through the whole word.
  • Vowel missing: Every syllable needs a vowel sound.

Practice Thought & Formative Assessment

Use a fresh, code-aligned example. The student should show the exact word, sound, sentence, image feature, audience clue or text evidence that supports the answer.

Quick checks

  • What sound does sh make?
  • Read: frog.
  • Is a in cake short or long?
  • Why check the vowel?

Mastery evidence
