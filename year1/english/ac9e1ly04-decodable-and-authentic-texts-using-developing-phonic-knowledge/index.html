Reading with Phonics, Phrasing and Meaning

What this skill means

Learning goal: I can read with phonics, phrasing and meaning, and I can notice when I need to self-correct.

  • Recognise many high-frequency words while reading.
  • Use phonics, phrasing, context and grammar together to keep meaning.
  • Self-correct or ask for help when the text stops making sense.

Steps to use

Look at letters → Blend/recognise → Read phrase → Check meaning → Re-read

Worked example

Question: In “The frog hops”, if you read frog as flag, re-read and check the picture/context because “flag hops” does not make sense.

Use both word solving and meaning checking

sound outblendchunkcontextgrammar
Try these
  1. Blend and check — Read a decodable word, then use it in a sentence.
    ship → The ship sails.
  2. Phrase scoop — Mark small phrase groups and re-read smoothly.
    The little dog / ran home.
  3. Meaning monitor — Stop when reading sounds wrong and fix it.
    Does it make sense?
Common mix-ups
  • Only guessing from context — Look at the letters and sounds.
  • Sounding without meaning — After decoding, check the sentence makes sense.
  • Robot reading — Practise phrase groups, not one word at a time forever.
Check your understanding

Quick checks

  • What should you do if a word does not make sense?
  • Blend: sh-o-p.
  • Why re-read a sentence?
  • What is phrasing?

I am ready when I can

  • I can recognise many high-frequency words while reading.
  • I can use phonics, phrasing, context and grammar together to keep meaning.
  • I can self-correct or ask for help when the text stops making sense.
