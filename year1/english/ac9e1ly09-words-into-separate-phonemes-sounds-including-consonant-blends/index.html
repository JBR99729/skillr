Segment Words into Phonemes

I can break spoken words into their separate phonemes, including words with blends and clusters.

Topic GuideClassroom ViewPrintable Worksheet40-question PracticeTest
What this skill means

Learning goal: I can break spoken words into their separate phonemes, including words with blends and clusters.

  • Say each phoneme in order in a spoken word, including words with consonant blends or clusters.

Steps to use

Say word → Stretch sounds → Tap phonemes → Blend back

See it, then try it

Tap each sound you hear

frog: /f/ /r/ /o/ /g/best: /b/ /e/ /s/ /t/shop: /sh/ /o/ /p/

Worked example

Question: Segment “frog”. The sounds are /f/ /r/ /o/ /g/; /fr/ has two consonant sounds at the start.

Blend the sounds back to check

saytapcountblend
Try these
  1. Sound boxes — Push one counter for each sound.
    stop = /s/ /t/ /o/ /p/
  2. Blend hunt — Find words with two consonant sounds at the start.
    frog / swim
  3. Stretch and blend — Stretch sounds then say the word normally.
    /b/ /e/ /s/ /t/ → best
Common mix-ups
  • Counting letters not sounds — Listen for phonemes, not letter count.
  • Blend as one sound — /fr/ has /f/ and /r/.
  • Cannot blend back — Always say the word again after segmenting.
Check your understanding

Quick checks

  • Segment “ship”.
  • How many sounds in “frog”?
  • What is a consonant blend?
  • Blend /s/ /t/ /o/ /p/.

I am ready when I can

  • I can say each phoneme in order in a spoken word, including words with consonant blends or clusters.
