Curriculum Coverage & Elaborations

The content description is the required target. The elaborations below are taught as examples and contexts, not as disconnected checklist wording.

  • E1: continuing to develop a functional pencil grip/grasp
Common misconceptions
  • Joined words — Use clear spaces between words.
  • Capital inside word — Use capitals only where needed.
  • Letter reversal — Practise start point and direction.
Try these
  1. Letter family practice — Write letters with similar movements together.
    c o a d
  2. Word spacing check — Rewrite two words with a clear finger space.
    red ball
  3. Readable word test — Read your word back to a partner.
    Can they read it?
Explain and apply

Match upper and lower case

Which letters match: B and b, B and d, or B and p?

Answer and teaching guidance

B and b match because they are the upper-case and lower-case forms of the same letter.

Capital for names

Edit the name ava.

Answer and teaching guidance

The correct form is Ava because a name begins with a capital letter.

Readable unjoined letters

Write the word sun using separate lower-case letters.

Answer and teaching guidance

Letters should be unjoined, correctly shaped and easy to recognise.

Check letter direction

Which letters are easy to reverse: b and d, m and n, or c and o?

Answer and teaching guidance

b and d are often confused, so students should check the stick and circle direction.
