Spell Words with Common Letter Patterns

I can spell one- and two-syllable words using common letter patterns.

What this skill means

Learning goal: I can spell one- and two-syllable words using common letter patterns.

  • Spell one- and two-syllable words using familiar letter patterns and known blends.

Steps to use

Say word → Segment sounds/syllables → Choose pattern → Write → Check

See it, then try it

Use known patterns to spell

playrainshopsunsetjumping

Worked example

Question: Spell “sunset”. Clap sun-set, spell each part, then join the two syllables.

Break the word before writing

soundpatternsyllablewritecheck
Try these
  1. Pattern word sort — Sort words with a common spelling pattern.
    play/day/stay
  2. Syllable spelling — Split a two-syllable word before spelling.
    sun-set
  3. Check aloud — Read your spelling back and listen for missing sounds.
    ship / shop
Common mix-ups
  • Writing from memory only — Use sound and pattern knowledge.
  • Missing second syllable — Clap and spell both parts.
  • Pattern not checked — Compare with a known word.
Check your understanding

Quick checks

  • Spell a word that rhymes with day.
  • How do you spell sunset in parts?
  • Why say the word slowly?
  • Name one common pattern.
