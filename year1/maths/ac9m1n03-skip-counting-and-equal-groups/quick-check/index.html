Skip Counting and Equal Groups: Quick Check

Try each question first. Then open the answer to check the maths and the reasoning.

Topic guide Worksheet Practice Test

1. Count equal groups

Question: There are 6 equal groups of 5 counters. How many counters are there altogether?

Answer and explanation

30 counters. Skip count the six groups: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. Each number names the total after one more group of five.

2. Check that groups are equal

Question: Why is it not safe to skip count groups with 4, 4, 3 and 4 counters?

Answer and explanation

One group has only 3 counters, so the groups are not equal. Make every group the same size before skip counting.

3. Include the leftovers

Question: A collection has 4 groups of 10 counters and 3 loose counters. What is the total?

Answer and explanation

43 counters. Count the groups as 10, 20, 30, 40, then count on 41, 42, 43. The 3 loose counters still belong in the total.

What next?

Remember

Make equal groups, skip count the groups, then include any leftovers.
