Quick questions

  • Partition 68 into tens and ones.
  • Give another partition of 68.
  • What number is 40 + 9?
  • Why is 6 + 2 not the same as 60 + 2?

Return to the concept model if an answer is uncertain. Use the model to explain, not just to guess.

Assessment and reviewReady for practice
  • Only one partition — Tens and ones is useful, but numbers can be split in other ways too.
  • Dropping zero — 50 + 6 is 56; 5 + 6 is only 11.
  • Swapping tens and ones — In 47, the 4 means 4 tens, not 4 ones.
