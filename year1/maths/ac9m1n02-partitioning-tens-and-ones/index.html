Partitioning Tens and Ones

Show how two-digit numbers split into tens and ones, and find different partitions of the same total.

Practice on app ↗Help with gaps ↓10-minute activity ↓

WORKED EXAMPLE

Build 34 with tens and ones

Place value for 34
TensOnes
3 bundles of 104 loose sticks

Three tens are 30. Four ones are 4. Together they make 34.

Learn the skill

1. Tens and ones

A bundle of ten counts as ten objects. 43 is four tens and three ones: 40 + 3.

Try it · What number is six tens and two ones?

62. The six tens have a value of 60.

2. A zero still has a job

50 has five tens and no extra ones. The zero holds the ones place; it does not turn 50 into 5.

Try it · Build 30 with bundles of ten.

Three bundles, with no loose ones.

3. Different parts, same total

Open one of the four tens in 43. Now there are three tens and thirteen ones: 30 + 13 = 43.

Try it · Complete: 35 is two tens and __ ones.

15 ones. Opening a ten changes the grouping, not the total.

Common mistakes

Reading 34 as 3 and 4. The 3 is in the tens place, so it represents 30. Build three bundles of ten and four loose sticks to check.

10-MINUTE HOME ACTIVITY

Bundle and unbundle

Use 34 craft sticks. Make bundles of ten, count the loose sticks, and record 34 as 30 and 4. Untie one bundle and explain why the total stays 34.

Adjust the challenge: Start with 12 sticks. Extend by comparing 3 tens and 4 ones with 2 tens and 14 ones.

FAQ

What does the zero in 40 tell you?

40 has four tens and zero loose ones.

Small steps, clear examples

Open the focus you need for today’s worksheet. Read, try and explain before moving on.

1. Parts make a whole

Split seven counters into three and four. Joining the parts gives seven again: 3 + 4 = 7.

Try it: Find another way to split seven.

See answer and explanation

For example, 2 and 5. Count both parts together to check seven.

5. Check a partition

Add the values of the parts. 28 = 20 + 8 = 10 + 18. All describe the same collection.

Try it: Does 28 = 2 + 8? Explain.

See answer and explanation

No. 2 + 8 is 10. Two tens have a value of 20.

Try a worked example together

Keep the whole when you split it

Work through the example

  1. Three complete groups of ten and four ones make 34.
  2. Exchange one ten for ten ones: now there are two tens and fourteen ones.
  3. Both ways show the same whole: 30 + 4 = 20 + 14 = 34.

Watch for this: Moving objects into new groups does not change how many there are.

Try it together

Show 26 in two ways using tens and ones.

Answer and teaching guidance

2 tens and 6 ones; or 1 ten and 16 ones. Both total 26.

An adult can read the directions, model the idea and accept an oral, pointed or drawn explanation where appropriate. For reading, spelling, handwriting or speaking tasks, observe the child doing the stated skill.

Common mistakes and quick fixes
  • Only one partition — Tens and ones is useful, but numbers can be split in other ways too.
  • Dropping zero — 50 + 6 is 56; 5 + 6 is only 11.
  • Swapping tens and ones — In 47, the 4 means 4 tens, not 4 ones.
