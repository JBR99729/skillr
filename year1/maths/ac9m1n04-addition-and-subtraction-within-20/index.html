Addition and Subtraction within 20

Concept model and worked application

Use part-part-whole and make ten

8 + 58 + 2 + 310 + 3 = 13

For subtraction, think about the missing part or count back carefully.

Common misconceptions and traps
  • Counting all every time — Count on from the larger number when possible.
  • Mixing add and subtract — Read the story: are parts joining or is a part being removed/found?
  • Stopping at 10 — After making ten, remember to add the leftover part.
