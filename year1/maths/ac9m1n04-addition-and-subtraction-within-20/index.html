Addition and subtraction within 20

Build addition and subtraction strategies within 20 using concrete materials, drawings, number bonds and part-part-whole reasoning.

Topic guideTeacher slideWorksheetPracticeTest

What students learn

Students add and subtract numbers within 20 using physical and visual models before relying on mental strategies. They connect number bonds, counting on and back, partitioning and part-part-whole knowledge so they can explain why a calculation works rather than copy a procedure.

  • Represent addition and subtraction with objects, drawings and number sentences.
  • Use number combinations within 10 to calculate within 20.
  • Use counting on, counting back and partitioning strategically.
  • Connect addition and subtraction as related operations.
  • Apply the mathematics to short story problems.

How to teach addition and subtraction within 20

Begin with objects and pictures. Ask students to build the whole, separate it into parts and describe what changed. Move to drawings and number sentences only after the student can explain the concrete model. Use known combinations to 10 to make calculations such as 7 + 5 easier: make 10 first, then add what remains.
