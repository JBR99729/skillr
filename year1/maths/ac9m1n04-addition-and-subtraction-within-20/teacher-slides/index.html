Quick questions

  • Solve 8 + 5 using make ten.
  • Solve 16 − 7 using a strategy.
  • What related fact helps with 13 − 6?
  • When is counting on useful?

Return to the concept model if an answer is uncertain. Use the model to explain, not just to guess.

Assessment and reviewReady for practice
  • Counting all every time — Count on from the larger number when possible.
  • Mixing add and subtract — Read the story: are parts joining or is a part being removed/found?
  • Stopping at 10 — After making ten, remember to add the leftover part.
