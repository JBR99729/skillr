How to use SkillrHub

SkillrHub resources are organised into year levels, learning areas and units, making it easy to find materials for a specific topic or skill. Each unit may include a detailed topic guide, teacher presentation slides and student practice worksheets.

The resources cover a wide range of Australian Curriculum content and curriculum codes. They can be used by teachers, parents, home educators and international learners in four main ways.

1. Classroom teachers

Teachers can open the topic guide within each unit to quickly review teaching tips, explanations, examples and suggested ways to introduce the topic to students.

Teacher slides can be downloaded and displayed in the classroom to support whole-class instruction, revision or discussion. Teachers can also download the related student practice worksheets for students to complete independently or with guidance.

When classroom time is limited, teachers may recommend the worksheets to parents so students can complete or revise the activities at home.

2. Parents and carers

Parents can use the topic guides to understand what their child is learning and to explain the topic in a clear, age-appropriate way.

The teacher slides can be used to introduce or revise key ideas, while the practice worksheets allow children to apply what they have learned. These resources can help parents support their child’s learning and keep practice connected to the skills taught within the Australian Curriculum.

3. Home-education families

Home-education teachers and parents can use the topic guides, teaching slides and practice worksheets to plan lessons, explain new concepts and assess their child’s understanding.

Families may also choose a digital or printed workbook to keep completed activities together. Completed workbooks and worksheets may be retained as supporting evidence of the skills and learning activities completed during the year, subject to the documentation requirements of the relevant state or territory.

4. International teachers and parents

Although SkillrHub resources are structured around the Australian Curriculum, the knowledge and skills covered are relevant to primary-level learning in many countries.

International teachers and parents can use the topic guides, presentation slides and worksheets as supplementary resources. They are suitable for introducing new skills, reinforcing classroom learning, providing additional practice or helping children strengthen areas in which they need more support.

A simple way to begin

Choose your child’s or student’s year level, select a learning area and open the relevant unit. Begin with the topic guide, use the teacher slides to explain the concept and then complete the student practice worksheets to reinforce learning.
