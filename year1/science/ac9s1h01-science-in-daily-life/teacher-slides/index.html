Example 1

Worked example Question: Why might someone take an umbrella after seeing dark clouds? They use the observed weather pattern to predict rain and choose a useful action.

Example 2

Weather decision Use signs in the sky or forecast symbols to choose what to wear or carry. Model: clouds → rain? → umbrella

Example 3

Shadow pattern Observe shadows at two times and predict what may happen later. Model: morning shadow ≠ afternoon shadow

Example 4

Home science List times people use science at home, school or outdoors. Model: cook • clean • garden • travel

Quick questions

  • Give one example of science in daily life.
  • How can weather patterns help people?
  • What makes a prediction scientific?
  • Why is observing useful before acting?

Return to the concept model if an answer is uncertain. Use the model to explain, not just to guess.

Assessment and reviewReady for practice
  • Prediction without pattern — Point to the observation or repeated pattern first.
  • Science only in labs — Science is also used in cooking, weather choices, gardening and safety.
  • Guess equals prediction — A prediction uses evidence or experience.
