Australian Curriculum elaborations

These curriculum examples extend the central model into varied contexts. They are learning contexts, not extra definitions to memorise.

E1

learning from farmers, bush care volunteers, gardeners or nursery owners about how they observe the needs of plants, and how they have designed or managed habitats to meet those needs

Learning connection: learning from farmers, bush care volunteers, gardeners or nursery owners about how they observe the needs of plants, and how they have designed or managed habitats to meet those needs. Connect the example back to this key idea: People use science every day when they observe patterns, make predictions and choose safe or useful actions.

Learning connection: IDENTIFYING ways that science knowledge is used in the care of the local environment and suggesting ways local gardens or parks could better meet the needs of native animals. Connect the example back to this key idea: People use science every day when they observe patterns, make predictions and choose safe or useful actions.

E3

investigating how First Nations Australians use science to meet their needs, such as food and water supply and shelter

Learning connection: INVESTIGATING how First Nations Australians use science to meet their needs, such as food and water supply and shelter. Connect the example back to this key idea: People use science every day when they observe patterns, make predictions and choose safe or useful actions.

Learning connection: RECOGNISING how First Nations Australians use changes in the landscape and the sky to answer questions about when to gather certain resources. Connect the example back to this key idea: People use science every day when they observe patterns, make predictions and choose safe or useful actions.

E5

learning from local ecologists or wildlife carers about native animals’ needs and how they observe animal behaviour to design supports for them to meet those needs, such as building frog and insect hotels and nesting boxes or recycling materials to provide habitat

E6

Avoid this trap
  • Prediction without pattern — Point to the observation or repeated pattern first.
  • Science only in labs — Science is also used in cooking, weather choices, gardening and safety.
  • Guess equals prediction — A prediction uses evidence or experience.
