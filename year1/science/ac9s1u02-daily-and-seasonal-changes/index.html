AC9S1U02: Daily and seasonal changes

describe daily and seasonal changes in the environment and explore how these changes affect everyday life

Topic guideWorksheetPracticeTest

What students learn in AC9S1U02

This unit helps students build a clear, usable understanding of Daily and seasonal changes. The goal is not to memorise one answer pattern. Students should be able to explain the idea, recognise it in a new example and apply it in a short practice or worksheet task.

  • Start with the main idea: describe daily and seasonal changes in the environment and explore how these changes affect everyday life.
  • Use concrete examples, pictures, oral explanation and short written responses before moving to independent practice.
  • making and recording observations of phenomena such as changes to weather, seasonal changes to plants such as colour or dropping of leaves, and growth of flowers or fruit noticing how daily weather indicators and seasonal patterns help us to make plans for activities in our daily lives
  • Finish with a mixed activity so students must choose the correct strategy rather than copy the last example.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

The content description and elaborations below show the curriculum ideas taught in this unit. Items marked as teaching context support lesson planning.

  • Content description: describe daily and seasonal changes in the environment and explore how these changes affect everyday life
  • E1: making and recording observations of phenomena such as changes to weather, seasonal changes to plants such as colour or dropping of leaves, and growth of flowers or fruit
  • E2: noticing how daily weather indicators and seasonal patterns help us to make plans for activities in our daily lives
  • E3: investigating how seasonal changes affect plants and animals, including animals that hibernate and migrate
  • E4: investigating how changes in the weather affect plants and animals, including humans
  • E5: exploring how people make clothing choices using predictions of weather or knowledge of seasonal changes
  • E6: recognising the extensive knowledges of daily and seasonal changes in weather patterns and landscape held by First Nations Australians (teaching context)
  • E7: exploring how First Nations Australians’ concepts of time and weather patterns explain how things happen in the world around them (teaching context)

Common mistakes to watch for

  • Describing what is seen without connecting it to a question, pattern or prediction.
  • Using everyday words only and missing simple scientific vocabulary.
  • Changing more than one thing in an investigation, which makes the comparison unfair.
