Safe investigation procedures: Year 1 Science topic guide

suggest and follow safe procedures to investigate questions and test predictions

What students learn in AC9S1I02

This unit helps students build a clear, usable understanding of Safe investigation procedures. The goal is not to memorise one answer pattern. Students should be able to explain the idea, recognise it in a new example and apply it in a short practice or worksheet task.

  • Start with the main idea: suggest and follow safe procedures to investigate questions and test predictions.
  • Use concrete examples, pictures, oral explanation and short written responses before moving to independent practice.
  • suggesting ways to conduct investigations safely, including being sun safe, using age-appropriate equipment such as plastic goggles and aprons, or following teacher instructions promptly following steps in a guided investigation to determine how different objects move when pushed or pulled
  • Finish with mixed questions so students must choose the correct strategy rather than copy the last example.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Question banks for this unit should draw from the content description and the eligible elaborations below. First Nations-specific notes are kept out of generated question coverage unless they are part of the core concept.

  • Content description: suggest and follow safe procedures to investigate questions and test predictions
  • E1: suggesting ways to conduct investigations safely, including being sun safe, using age-appropriate equipment such as plastic goggles and aprons, or following teacher instructions promptly
  • E2: following steps in a guided investigation to determine how different objects move when pushed or pulled
  • E3: exploring different ways of investigating science questions through guided discussion
  • E4: suggesting steps for setting up and packing away equipment

How to use this unit

Read the topic guide, download the teacher slide for instruction, complete the printable worksheet, then use Practice and Test when the unit's checked 8-question bank is available.

Common mistakes to watch for

  • Describing what is seen without connecting it to a question, pattern or prediction.
  • Using everyday words only and missing simple scientific vocabulary.
  • Changing more than one thing in an investigation, which makes the comparison unfair.
