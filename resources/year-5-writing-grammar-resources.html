A practical writing lesson sequence

  • Model: show one strong sentence or paragraph and identify the deliberate language choice.
  • Compare: contrast it with a weaker version so students can see the effect.
  • Construct together: jointly improve one example.
  • Apply: students write independently using the same focus.
  • Edit: require one targeted revision rather than broad “check your work” instructions.
  • Assess: use a short practice or quick check linked to the same curriculum skill.

Make grammar transfer into writing

After students identify a language feature, ask them to use it for a specific effect: foreground time, clarify cause, add precise description, guide the reader, strengthen an argument or improve flow. This keeps grammar connected to meaning rather than treating it as isolated terminology.
