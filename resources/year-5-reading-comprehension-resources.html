A simple comprehension routine

  1. Preview: identify text type, purpose and likely audience.
  2. Read for gist: summarise the main idea in one or two sentences.
  3. Read for evidence: locate words, phrases or details that support an answer.
  4. Infer: combine clues from the text with what is already known.
  5. Explain: answer using evidence rather than unsupported opinion.
  6. Reflect: discuss how vocabulary, structure, viewpoint or imagery affected meaning.

For intervention or extension

For support, reduce the text length and require one piece of evidence per answer. For extension, ask students to compare two interpretations, identify the strongest evidence and explain why another interpretation is weaker. The same text can therefore serve mixed-readiness groups without changing the curriculum focus.
