Parent and teacher guide

Money problems help students connect number skills to real situations. Start by naming coins and comparing their values, then count small collections using skip-counting. A common mistake is counting the number of coins instead of their total value. Use coin pictures and simple shopping contexts so students can see why value matters. Encourage learners to group coins by value before adding. For word problems, ask what is being bought, how much it costs and whether the question asks for a total, change or comparison.

Common mistakes to watch for

Ask the student to explain their method before moving to a test. If they can only guess, return to one visual model and one worked example. Short practice with feedback usually works better than a long worksheet completed without review.
