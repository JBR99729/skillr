AC9M2M03 • Year 2 Maths assessment task

Calendar dates and days between events assessment

A short assessment task for checking whether students can locate dates and accurately count elapsed days on a calendar.

Assessment task

Give students a one-month calendar containing four marked events such as an excursion, sports day, library visit and class celebration.

  1. Write the date of each event.
  2. Find the event that occurs first and the event that occurs last.
  3. Determine how many days pass between two nominated events.
  4. Find the date exactly 2 weeks after one event.
  5. Explain how you counted the days between two dates.

What to look for

  • distinguishes a weekday from a calendar date;
  • counts the jumps between dates rather than counting both endpoints;
  • uses 7-day week structure correctly;
  • continues counting across a week or month boundary;
  • explains the method clearly.

Simple marking guide

Secure: dates and elapsed-day calculations are accurate and the student explains a reliable strategy. Developing: dates are located correctly but elapsed-day counting has one-off errors. Beginning: the student needs support to locate dates or interpret the calendar structure.
