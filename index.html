How free practice works
  1. Choose your year and try a Maths skill. Start without an email or account.
  2. Check your answer and use the feedback to keep learning. You can create a free account to save your progress.
  3. The app will explain when an adult email is needed to continue. A free account lets you save your work. No credit card required.

Practice follows the selected skill, one question at a time, with answer feedback. The save invitation is optional; no credit card is required.
