    Can I use SkillrHub in the US, UK, New Zealand or Canada?

    Yes. SkillrHub is built primarily for Australian Curriculum V9.0, and learners worldwide can use the available practice and teaching guides. Browse by topic and choose an Australian year that suits your learner. Australian years are not automatic equivalents to local grades. Overseas curriculum references are added only where checked and may support part of a standard; complete coverage of US CCSS, UK, New Zealand or Canadian curricula is not claimed.

    How does free practice work?

    Choose an available skill and answer one question at a time. Guests have 20 free practice questions each day in supported banks. Register and confirm your email for 500 free questions total across Maths, English and Science, including your guest questions. Your account saves your progress. Some banks, including the available Year 6 banks, require a free account.

    Do I need to buy a worksheet pack?

    No. Your 500-question registration offer does not require a worksheet purchase. Topic teaching notes remain free. Printable packs are optional, separate purchases on Teachers Pay Teachers.

    Can I save progress for more than one child?

    Yes. A free adult account lets you create and select learner profiles, so each child’s saved online work stays separate.
