Skillr Education

Skillr Education provides free printable maths and science resources for teachers, parents and students in early childhood and primary school. The collection is growing steadily and is designed to support classroom practice, homework, tutoring sessions and independent revision.

Our worksheets and topic guides are intended to be practical, print-ready and easy to use. Browse by year level, open a topic page and choose the activity that best fits your learner.

Because school year names vary across countries, some pages may suit learners in Foundation, Reception, Prep, Kindergarten or equivalent early years settings. The resources are written to be useful in Australian classrooms while remaining clear and accessible for a wider international audience.

Curriculum note: These resources are primarily aligned to the Australian Curriculum and also include useful reference points for teachers, parents and students in the US, UK, Canada, New Zealand and India.

If you need a worksheet for a topic that is not yet listed, please contact us at skillrhublearning@gmail.com with the year level, subject, topic and curriculum information. We use this feedback to guide future additions and improve the library over time.

Access: Most worksheets and learning resources on this site are free to use. No login, account creation or student information is required to access them.

Who this site is for

Skillr Education is built to give practical value quickly to busy adults and learners. Whether you are planning a lesson, supporting homework, running a tutoring session or revising independently, these resources are meant to be easy to use and ready when you need them.

  • Teachers: use the worksheets and topic guides for class practice, revision, intervention or lesson support.
  • Parents and carers: use them for home learning, homework support and extra practice.
  • Tutors and learning centres: use them for one-to-one sessions, small groups and targeted support.
  • Students: use them to build confidence, revisit concepts and work at their own pace.
