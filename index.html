What’s included, costs and reporting

Purposeful practice, with a clear path: the Year 1 Maths full-year worksheet bundle includes 900 practice questions and separate tests across all 15 codes. Pack sizes, subjects and year-level availability vary; see each listing for what is included.

Affordable, flexible learning: start with the worksheet packs you need and use free topic help and progress tracking alongside them. TpT prices are in US dollars; check the listing for current pricing. Work through the curriculum skills included in your chosen packs, one manageable step at a time.

Useful learning evidence: create separate child profiles, record dated sessions and print summaries to support your education department reporting. For children attending school, use the same records to review extra practice and next steps. Summaries reflect your entries; they do not certify achievement, sufficient learning or department approval.

Buy only what you need: term, yearly and skill-group bundles may contain the same individual packs. Check previous purchases and the listing’s household or classroom licence before buying.
