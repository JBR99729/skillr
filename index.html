    How does free practice work?

    Choose an available skill and answer one question at a time. Guests have 20 free practice questions each day in supported banks. A free email-confirmed account unlocks unlimited available practice, saved progress and assessments. Some banks, including the available Year 6 banks, require a free account.

    Do I need to buy a worksheet pack?

    No. Published digital practice and topic teaching notes are free. Printable packs are optional, separate purchases on Teachers Pay Teachers.

    Can I save progress for more than one child?

    Yes. A free adult account lets you create and select learner profiles, so each child’s saved online work stays separate.
