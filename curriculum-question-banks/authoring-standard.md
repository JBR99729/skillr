# Question-bank authoring standard

## Non-negotiable requirements

1. Every item must directly assess the stated curriculum description.
2. Every scenario, stimulus, stem and assessed sub-skill must be materially
   distinct within the bank. Changing only names or numbers is not sufficient.
3. Language, response length and conceptual demand must suit the target year.
4. Foundation tasks may be teacher-read and may request an oral, drawn, sorted or
   demonstrated response. The delivery mode must be stated where relevant.
5. Multiple-choice items must have exactly four plausible, mutually exclusive
   options and exactly one defensible answer.
6. Short and extended responses must include an observable marking key or sample
   answer. Avoid keys that merely say “answers may vary”.
7. Exam questions must use fresh stimuli and contexts rather than repeating a
   practice question in summative form.
8. Do not include First Nations content references, gamification, badges, points
   or distracting meta-text.
9. Do not reproduce copyrighted passages. All stimuli must be newly authored and
   short enough for the assessed skill.
10. Use Australian English spelling and familiar Australian contexts where a
    location-specific context is helpful.

## Pass 1 distribution

Practice contains 8 questions:

- Tier 1 — Knowledge and Terms: P01–P02
- Tier 2 — Core Skill Practice: P03–P04
- Tier 3 — Guided Application: P05–P06
- Tier 4 — Problem Solving and Reasoning: P07–P08

Exam contains 8 distinct questions:

- Section A — Core Knowledge: E01–E03
- Section B — Application and Problem Solving: E04–E06
- Section C — Extended Response and Analysis: E07–E08

No exam item may reuse a Practice stimulus, scenario or assessed response.

The planned 10-question worksheet samples 5 Practice and 5 Exam items without
replacement. Every item must therefore work as a standalone printable prompt.

## Required item fields

Each question heading is followed by:

- `Type`: Multiple choice, Short response, Extended response, Matching,
  Sequencing, Oral response, Demonstration or Drawing response.
- `Delivery`: only where teacher reading, physical materials or oral delivery is
  required.
- `Question`: the complete prompt and any original stimulus.
- Options `A.` through `D.` for multiple choice only.
- `Correct answer` for closed items, including a brief reason.
- `Marking key` for constructed responses, with explicit evidence and marks.
- `Coverage`: the distinct sub-skill assessed.

## Quality gates

A batch is publishable only when it passes:

- manifest and count validation;
- ID and option validation;
- varied MCQ answer positions so static worksheets do not reveal a pattern;
- exact and near-duplicate detection;
- banned-content scanning;
- independent answer/rubric audit;
- year-level language review;
- curriculum-coverage review;
- final Markdown and spelling review.
