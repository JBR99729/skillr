# Teacher Slides Branding QA Standard

Applies to every SkillrHub teacher-slide / presentation resource.

## Required QA rule

**Teaching content must always be the top visual layer.**

- Do not place `SKILLRHUB.COM`, SkillrHub logos, watermarks, promotional text or other branding across the background or centre of teaching slides.
- Do not place branding behind or over teaching text, diagrams, examples, illustrations, tables, questions, student prompts or interactive elements.
- Branding is allowed only in unobtrusive unused space, preferably a small footer such as `SkillrHub • [curriculum code]`.
- A small logo may be used in a clear corner or on a title/end slide only when it does not compete with instructional content.
- Avoid diagonal, oversized, repeated or high-contrast watermarks.
- Student readability and visual teaching take priority over brand visibility.
- Printable slides must use especially subtle branding.

## QA check

Before approving any teacher deck, verify:

1. No branding crosses the instructional canvas/background.
2. No branding overlaps or visually competes with teaching content.
3. Footer/corner branding is small and unobtrusive.
4. Teaching text and visuals remain the strongest visual layer.
5. Fullscreen and projected classroom views remain clean and readable.
6. Branding does not reduce accessibility or contrast.

**Pass condition:** branding identifies SkillrHub without obstructing, distracting from or visually dominating the lesson.
