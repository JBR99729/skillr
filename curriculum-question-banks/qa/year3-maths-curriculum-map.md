# Year 3 Maths production-bank curriculum map

Each code contains five context sets. Sets 1–3 form the 24-item Practice bank; sets 4–5 form the separate 16-item Test bank. Every set samples all eight listed question-family intentions with changed values and setting language.

| Code | Curriculum intent and question-family coverage | Misconceptions tested | Visual choice | Practice/Test distinction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AC9M3A01 | Connect addition and subtraction as inverse operations; fact families, missing parts, checks and contextual reconstruction | starting subtraction with a part; changing a fact-family value; inaccurate inverse check | part–part–whole equations and reversible number paths | classroom cards/shells/steps vs shop and pathway checks |
| AC9M3A02 | Extend known addition/subtraction facts to tens and hundreds; bridge tens, partition and estimate mentally | treating tens as ones; losing place value; counting inefficiently; unreasonable result | known-fact transformation and partition cards | concrete number stations vs larger shop and challenge values |
| AC9M3A03 | Recall and apply multiplication/division facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10; arrays, groups and related facts | adding factors; reversing division incorrectly; mismatched arrays | equal-group and array models | counters/bikes/class facts vs applied group and division contexts |
| AC9M3M01 | Choose metric units and estimate length, mass and capacity using benchmarks | unit too large/small; mixing attributes; implausible estimate | familiar object beside metric-unit/benchmark labels | classroom objects vs playground and estimation challenges |
| AC9M3M02 | Measure and compare with metric instruments; read intervals, non-zero starts and liquid levels | counting marks rather than intervals; assuming zero start; parallax; comparing unlike units | ruler/scale/jug reading diagrams | direct instrument readings vs broken-zero and error-analysis cases |
| AC9M3M03 | Relate seconds, minutes, hours, days and weeks; calculate and compare durations | base-10 time; comparing unlike units; wrong finish time | timelines and unit-conversion chains | routine schedules vs longer event and conversion contexts |
| AC9M3M04 | Read and connect analog/digital time to the minute; elapsed time and hand positions | swapping hands; treating 60 minutes as 100; hour-hand position | accurate clock faces and clock pairs | school-day times vs home/event times with distinct minutes |
| AC9M3M05 | Recognise angles as measures of turn and compare with right angles | judging arm length; confusing direction with turn size; reversed turn | turn arrows and angle openings | movement/dance contexts vs direction challenges |
| AC9M3M06 | Relate dollars and cents; convert, compare, total and reason about change | omitting cents zero; treating decimal notation as whole numbers; adding unlike places | receipts, price labels and dollar/cent conversions | class-shop purchases vs canteen and savings contexts |
| AC9M3N01 | Read, represent, rename, compare and order numbers beyond 10 000 | dropping placeholder zero; comparing from ones; incorrect renaming | large-number/place-value cards | concrete place-value contexts vs large-number challenge contexts |
| AC9M3N02 | Represent unit fractions and multiples; equal parts, same denominators and completing a whole | unequal parts; swapping numerator/denominator; adding denominators | fraction bars with equal partitions | food/folding models vs cake/pancake completion and error analysis |
| AC9M3N03 | Add/subtract two- and three-digit numbers using partitioning, regrouping, compensation and inverse checks | misaligned places; lost compensation; operation producing unreasonable direction | partition equations and calculation cards | classroom calculation contexts vs applied library/challenge values |
| AC9M3N04 | Solve multiplication/division situations with arrays, grouping, sharing and related facts | adding factors; unequal groups; wrong division orientation | equal groups and arrays | chair/counter contexts vs applied sharing and array checks |
| AC9M3N05 | Estimate quantities and calculations using grouping, rounding and reasonableness | wild guesses; confusing exact/estimate; scale-insensitive answer | grouped collections and rounded-number comparisons | jars/rows vs larger collection and critique contexts |
| AC9M3N06 | Model practical additive, multiplicative, sharing and money problems; interpret and check solutions | choosing operation from keywords alone; failing to interpret remainder/change; no reasonableness check | group, receipt and equation models | class-shop/bag contexts vs multi-step fair/challenge contexts |
| AC9M3N07 | Follow, create and debug ordered number algorithms and patterns | changing step order; vague instruction; wrong intermediate output | input–step–output flow diagrams | robot/number stations vs shop and pathway algorithms |
| AC9M3P01 | Describe everyday chance as impossible, unlikely, likely, equal or certain and justify comparisons | unlikely means impossible; colour preference affects chance; ignoring outcome counts | bags, spinners and chance scales | spinner/weather contexts vs bag and chance-table comparisons |
| AC9M3P02 | Conduct repeated chance trials; record, compare and discuss variation from predictions | fairness means identical short runs; changing conditions; rewriting data to fit prediction | tally/result tables and trial sequences | coin/spinner trials vs number-cube and class experiments |
| AC9M3SP01 | Classify 3D objects by faces, edges, vertices, curved surfaces and suitability for use | classifying by size/colour; cylinder as prism; one group only | labelled 3D-object feature/use diagrams | classroom models vs design, stacking and error-analysis contexts |
| AC9M3SP02 | Interpret/create top-view maps; follow ordered paths and use positional language | reversing direction; ignoring order; treating map as side view | grid maps with start, route and endpoint | classroom/playground paths vs library and treasure-map challenges |
| AC9M3ST01 | Acquire categorical/discrete data by survey, observation and tally; organise accurately | overlapping categories; double counting; unsuitable question/method | labelled tables, tallies and simple bars | familiar surveys vs observation and dataset questions |
| AC9M3ST02 | Interpret and compare tables, picture graphs and bar graphs; preserve data across displays | ignoring key/labels; inconsistent counts; comparing decorative features | labelled bars/picture keys | fruit/pet displays vs weather/book displays and error checks |
| AC9M3ST03 | Conduct a guided statistical investigation from question through conclusion | unsupported claim; inconsistent collection; assuming samples must match | investigation cycle, tables and conclusion/evidence links | garden/travel investigations vs playground/recycling studies |
