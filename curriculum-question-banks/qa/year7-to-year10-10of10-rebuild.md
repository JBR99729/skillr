# Years 7–10 assessment-bank rebuild

Status: in progress

## Locked scope

- Years 7, 8, 9 and 10
- Mathematics, Science and English
- 24 Practice + 16 independent Test questions per AC9 code
- Daily Drills and unrelated quiz systems are out of scope

## 10/10 quality gates

1. Directly assess the AC9 curriculum description; never ask students to identify curriculum wording.
2. Practice and Test must use separate files, prompts, stimuli, contexts and assessed responses.
3. Exactly 24 Practice and 16 Test items per code.
4. MCQ items use four plausible, mutually exclusive options with one defensible answer.
5. Use constructed-response items where MCQ would weaken the intended reasoning/analysis.
6. Practice difficulty progresses from knowledge/fluency through application to multi-step reasoning.
7. Test includes unseen application, misconception/error analysis and higher-order reasoning.
8. No number/name swaps masquerading as new questions; scenarios and sub-skills must be materially distinct.
9. Maths requires authentic calculation, modelling, representation and reasoning where required by the code.
10. Science requires concept application, evidence/data interpretation, investigation reasoning and calculations where appropriate.
11. English requires original text/language stimuli and genuine analysis, interpretation, comparison or creation decisions.
12. Every item has useful answer feedback; constructed responses have explicit marking criteria.
13. Australian English and age-appropriate contexts/language.
14. Visuals must be instructionally relevant and accessible when the assessed skill needs them.
15. Independent QA for answer correctness, curriculum coverage, duplicates, difficulty spread and Practice/Test separation.

## Re-authoring policy

- Year 7 Maths production-v1 material may be retained only when it clears these gates; repetitive or shallow items are replaced.
- Year 7 Science/English generic curriculum-description banks are full-rewrite candidates.
- Years 8–10 generic/shared-source banks are full-rewrite candidates unless live inspection proves a newer production bank exists.

## Completion definition

A code is complete only after both 24-question Practice and 16-question Test banks exist, are independently checked, and pass the quality gates above. A subject/year is not complete merely because pages or placeholder questions exist.
