# AC9M5N01 — Year 5 Mathematics digital question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** This Year 5 skill is about interpret, compare and order numbers with more than 2 decimal places, including numbers greater than one, using place value understanding; represent these on a number line.
- **Method:** Read the question carefully, identify the values or relationships, choose the most efficient strategy, then check whether the answer makes sense in the context.
- **Worked example:** For a screen task, first match each number, model or condition to its mathematical meaning. Then use the tool or answer box only after you have compared the key place values, units, operations or features.
- **Digital model:** Online questions may use movable cards, selectable diagrams, number-line widgets, grid tools, tables, graphs or constrained answer boxes. Units should sit outside numeric input boxes.
- **Common trap:** Do not answer from the surface pattern only. Check the operation, unit, direction, scale, denominator, coordinate order or likelihood before selecting your final answer.
