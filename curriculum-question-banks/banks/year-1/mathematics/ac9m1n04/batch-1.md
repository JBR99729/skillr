# AC9M1N04 — Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Addition combines or increases; subtraction removes, compares or finds a missing part.
- **Method:** Use known parts to 10, doubles, count-on or make-10. Check subtraction by adding the answer back.
- **Worked example:** 8 + 7 becomes 10 + 5: move 2 from the 7 to the 8, so the total is 15.
- **Visual model:** Use counters, ten-frames, part-part-whole diagrams and number lines with draggable, tappable and keyboard choices.
- **Common trap:** Read what changes in the story; a larger starting number does not always mean add.

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Complete ten

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** What part joins 6 to make 10? Write the related addition fact.

**Marking key:** 4; 6 + 4 = 10 (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Using a part-part-whole fact to 10.

### P02 — Match related facts

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match 7 + 3, 10 − 3 and 10 − 7 to 10, 7 and 3.

**Marking key:** 7 + 3 ↔ 10; 10 − 3 ↔ 7; 10 − 7 ↔ 3 (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Connecting fact-family addition and subtraction.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Remove five counters

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Show 13 counters, remove 5 and state how many remain.

**Marking key:** 8 remain; demonstration starts at 13 and removes exactly 5 (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Modelling subtraction as removal.

### P04 — Make ten to add

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Solve 9 + 6 by making 10. Show the split you used.

**Marking key:** Split 6 into 1 and 5; 9 + 1 + 5 = 15 (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Applying the make-10 addition strategy.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Count on a number line

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order the jumps for 7 + 5 using a make-10 route: land on 12; jump 3 to 10; start at 7; jump 2 more.

**Marking key:** Start at 7 → jump 3 to 10 → jump 2 more → land on 12 (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Sequencing number-line addition steps.

### P06 — Draw a fact family

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Draw a part-part-whole diagram with whole 14 and parts 8 and 6. Write one addition and one subtraction fact.

**Marking key:** Diagram shows 14, 8 and 6; examples 8 + 6 = 14 and 14 − 8 = 6 (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Representing related facts with a diagram.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Find the missing amount

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Solve 17 − __ = 9 and explain an addition check.

**Marking key:** 8; because 9 + 8 = 17 (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Finding a missing part and checking by addition.

### P08 — Compare two strategies

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Solve 8 + 8 using a doubles fact and a make-10 strategy. Explain why both agree.

**Marking key:** Double 8 is 16; 8 + 2 + 6 is also 16; both rearrange the same two parts without changing the total (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Comparing valid addition strategies.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Use a near double

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Find 7 + 8 using a doubles fact.

**Marking key:** Double 7 is 14, then add 1 to get 15, or double 8 minus 1 (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Using a near-double strategy.

### E02 — Match equations and answers

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match 16 − 7, 6 + 9 and 18 − 8 to 9, 15 and 10.

**Marking key:** 16 − 7 ↔ 9; 6 + 9 ↔ 15; 18 − 8 ↔ 10 (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Calculating mixed facts within 20.

### E03 — Order a subtraction strategy

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order the steps for 15 − 6: subtract 5 to reach 10; start at 15; subtract 1 more; state 9.

**Marking key:** Start at 15 → subtract 5 to reach 10 → subtract 1 more → state 9 (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Sequencing subtraction through 10.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Compare two towers

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** A red tower has 12 cubes and a blue tower has 7. How many more cubes are in the red tower? Write an equation.

**Marking key:** 5 more; 12 − 7 = 5 (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Modelling subtraction as comparison.

### E05 — Show an unknown start

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Use counters to solve __ + 6 = 18 and describe the missing part.

**Marking key:** 12 counters are the missing part because 12 + 6 = 18 (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Solving an addition equation with an unknown part.

### E06 — Draw an addition route

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Draw a number-line strategy for 6 + 7 that touches 10 and ends at the answer.

**Marking key:** Shows 6 → 10 with +4, then → 13 with +3; answer 13 (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Recording make-10 addition on a number line.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Correct a sign error

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Lina has 14 stickers and gives away 5. She writes 14 + 5 = 19. Explain the error and solve the story.

**Marking key:** Giving away decreases the collection, so subtraction is required; 14 − 5 = 9 stickers (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Selecting the operation from story meaning.

### E08 — Prove a calculation

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Choose a strategy for 19 − 8, solve it, then check your result using addition.

**Marking key:** A valid strategy gives 11, such as 19 − 9 + 1; check 11 + 8 = 19 (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Solving and independently checking subtraction.
