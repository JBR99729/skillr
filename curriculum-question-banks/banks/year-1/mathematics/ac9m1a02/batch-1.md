# AC9M1A02 — Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** A repeating pattern cycles through the same smallest unit again and again.
- **Method:** Find where the sequence begins to repeat, circle the smallest complete unit, then copy that unit in order.
- **Worked example:** red, blue, blue, red, blue, blue has the unit red–blue–blue; the next item is red.
- **Visual model:** Use colour, shape, sound and movement cards. Every colour pattern must also use labels or shapes.
- **Common trap:** Name the smallest repeating unit, not the whole strip that happens to be shown.

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Find the next symbol

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Continue: circle, square, circle, square, __. Name the repeating unit.

**Marking key:** Circle; unit is circle–square (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Continuing an AB repeating pattern.

### P02 — Match patterns and units

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match red-blue-red-blue; clap-stamp-stamp-clap-stamp-stamp; and 1-2-3-1-2-3 to units red-blue, clap-stamp-stamp and 1-2-3.

**Marking key:** Each pattern matches the stated smallest cycle (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Identifying units in varied representations.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Build three repeats

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Use shape cards to make three repeats of triangle, triangle, star.

**Marking key:** Shows triangle-triangle-star three times in the same order (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Constructing a repeated unit with materials.

### P04 — Repair a broken pattern

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** The pattern is A, B, B, A, B, A. Which final item should change, and to what?

**Marking key:** The final A should be B to repeat A-B-B (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Detecting an error in an ABB pattern.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Order one cycle

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** A pattern strip shows moon, sun, star repeated. Order the loose cards sun, moon, star to form the unit from its beginning.

**Marking key:** Moon, sun, star (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Sequencing the repeating unit.

### P06 — Draw the continuation

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Draw the next four items after square, circle, circle, square, circle, circle.

**Marking key:** Square, circle, circle, square (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Extending an A-B-B visual pattern.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Test a claimed unit

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Mila says the unit in 2,4,2,4,2,4 is 2,4,2,4. Give the smallest unit and explain.

**Marking key:** Smallest unit is 2,4 because it alone repeats to make the full sequence (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Distinguishing a smallest unit from multiple repeats.

### P08 — Create with two features

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Create a repeating pattern that changes both shape and size, show three repeats and mark the unit.

**Marking key:** A valid consistent unit uses both features, repeats three times and is clearly marked (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Designing and identifying a multi-feature pattern.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Continue a movement pattern

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** A movement cycle is jump, clap, turn, jump, clap, turn. State the next two movements and the unit.

**Marking key:** Jump, clap; unit jump-clap-turn (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Interpreting a repeating movement pattern.

### E02 — Match new cycles

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match X-Y-Y-X-Y-Y; tap-tap-pause-tap-tap-pause; and 3-1-3-1 to X-Y-Y, tap-tap-pause and 3-1.

**Marking key:** All three are matched to their smallest repeating units (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Recognising repeating units across symbols and sounds.

### E03 — Sequence scattered cards

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order these cards to continue cat-dog-bird: bird, dog, cat, cat, bird, dog.

**Marking key:** Cat, dog, bird, cat, dog, bird (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Reconstructing repeated cycles from unordered elements.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Locate a missing term

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Complete red, red, blue, red, __, blue, and state the unit.

**Marking key:** Red; unit red-red-blue (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Using the repeating unit to fill a gap.

### E05 — Demonstrate two full cycles

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Invent a three-action pattern and perform exactly two full repeats without changing its order.

**Marking key:** Uses a clear 3-action unit and performs it twice in identical order (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Creating and demonstrating a movement pattern.

### E06 — Draw and label a unit

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Draw a pattern with the unit small star–large star–circle repeated twice. Box one complete unit.

**Marking key:** Accurate two repeats with one small-star/large-star/circle unit boxed (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Recording and labelling a specified pattern unit.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Compare same-length units

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Pattern A repeats red-blue-green. Pattern B repeats red-green-blue. Explain why they are different even though they use the same items.

**Marking key:** Order is part of the unit; A and B arrange the colours differently, so their repeats differ (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Reasoning about order within repeating units.

### E08 — Prove whether it repeats

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** The strip is circle-square-circle-triangle-circle-square-circle-triangle. Decide whether it repeats, identify the smallest unit and use it to predict item 10.

**Marking key:** It repeats circle-square-circle-triangle; positions 9 and 10 begin the unit again, so item 10 is square (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Analysing a longer pattern and predicting by position.
