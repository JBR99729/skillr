# AC9E1LY13 — Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Rule:** Spell by saying the word, segmenting sounds or syllables, choosing known patterns, writing, then rereading to check.
- **Worked example:** sunset splits sun/set. Each syllable uses a familiar short-vowel pattern, so the parts can be spelled and joined.
- **Pattern clue:** Word families share chunks such as -at, -ake or -ight; blends such as bl and st keep both consonants.
- **Visual interaction:** Use sound tiles, word-family chunks and syllable cards with audio, keyboard and paper alternatives.
- **Common trap:** A word that rhymes may use a different spelling, so check the known pattern and visual word memory.

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Spell a word family member

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Write the word made by adding m to the beginning of -at.

**Marking key:** Mat (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Using a common rime pattern to spell.

### P02 — Match patterns and words

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match -ake, -ight and -ain to cake, light and rain.

**Marking key:** Each pattern matched correctly (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Recognising common letter patterns.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Build a blend word

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Use letter tiles to spell stop, keeping both beginning sounds.

**Marking key:** s-t-o-p in correct order, with st blend retained (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Spelling a one-syllable word with initial blend.

### P04 — Split a two-syllable word

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Split and spell picnic by its two spoken syllables.

**Marking key:** Pic/nic → picnic (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Using syllable chunks to spell a two-syllable word.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Order spelling checks

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order: segment word; say word; write patterns; reread/check.

**Marking key:** Say → segment → write → reread/check (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Sequencing a spelling strategy.

### P06 — Draw a word-family house

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Create a -op word-family house containing hop, mop and stop. Highlight the shared chunk.

**Marking key:** All three words spelled correctly and -op highlighted (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Organising spellings by shared pattern.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Explain a missing blend letter

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** A student spells black as back. Which sound/letter from the blend is missing?

**Marking key:** /l/ and letter l are missing from bl (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Detecting an omitted consonant in a blend.

### P08 — Spell from two chunks

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Spell bedroom by joining two familiar one-syllable words. State both parts.

**Marking key:** Bed + room = bedroom (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Building a two-syllable compound from known words.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Use a long-vowel pattern

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Write the word cake from the sounds /k/ /ā/ /k/ using a common pattern.

**Marking key:** Cake (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Applying the a_e letter pattern.

### E02 — Match blends and words

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match bl, st and cr to blue, nest and crab, identifying where each blend occurs.

**Marking key:** Bl ↔ blue beginning; st ↔ nest ending; cr ↔ crab beginning (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Recognising initial and final blend spellings.

### E03 — Sequence syllable cards

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order bow, rain to spell rainbow and write the whole word.

**Marking key:** Rain + bow = rainbow (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Ordering syllables in a compound word.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Choose between patterns

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Which spelling fits the long /ī/ word: tim or time? Explain.

**Marking key:** Time; i_e represents the long /ī/ sound here (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Selecting a common long-vowel spelling pattern.

### E05 — Demonstrate say-segment-write

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Use the routine to spell frog aloud and in writing.

**Marking key:** Says frog, segments /f r o g/, writes f-r-o-g and rereads (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Applying a full spelling routine to a blend word.

### E06 — Draw syllable boxes

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Write rabbit in two boxes for rab and bit. Underline the vowel pattern in each.

**Marking key:** Rab | bit, complete spelling with a and i identified (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Representing two-syllable spelling structure.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Correct a pattern error

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** A student writes nite for night in a taught -ight word family. Explain how the known family helps correct it.

**Marking key:** Recognises shared -ight chunk from light/right; correct spelling night (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Using visual pattern knowledge to edit spelling.

### E08 — Spell and justify a mixed list

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Spell plant, wish, sunset and boat. For each, name the blend, digraph, syllable split or vowel pattern that helped.

**Marking key:** All four correct; pl blend, sh digraph, sun/set, oa pattern, each explanation accurate (8 marks).

**Coverage:** Applying common patterns across one- and two-syllable words.
