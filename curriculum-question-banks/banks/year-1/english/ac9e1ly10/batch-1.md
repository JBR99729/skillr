# AC9E1LY10 — Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Rule:** Phoneme manipulation changes one spoken sound: add a sound, delete a sound or substitute one sound while keeping the others in order.
- **Worked example:** Change the middle /i/ in pin to /e/: /p/ /e/ /n/ makes pen. Only the medial phoneme changes.
- **Sound strategy:** Say the word, map one token per sound, change the named token, then blend the new sounds and check the word.
- **Visual interaction:** Use oral audio and sound tokens without requiring spelling, with tap, keyboard and paper-counter alternatives.
- **Common trap:** Change only the named phoneme; replacing or reordering extra sounds creates a different task.

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Add an initial sound

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Add /k/ to the beginning of at. What word is made?

**Marking key:** Cat (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Adding an initial phoneme.

### P02 — Match changes and words

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match delete /s/ from stop, change /m/ in map to /t/, and add /s/ before pin to top, tap and spin.

**Marking key:** Stop minus /s/ ↔ top; map /m/→/t/ ↔ tap; /s/+pin ↔ spin (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Connecting addition, deletion and substitution outcomes.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Move a middle-sound token

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Use tokens for cat. Replace /a/ with /o/ and say the new word.

**Marking key:** Cot; /k/ /o/ /t/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Substituting a medial vowel phoneme.

### P04 — Change a final sound

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Replace the final /p/ in map with /t/. What word is made?

**Marking key:** Mat (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Substituting a final phoneme.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Order a deletion

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order: say stop; identify /s/; remove first sound; blend /t/ /o/ /p/.

**Marking key:** Say stop → identify/remove /s/ → blend /t o p/ → top (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Sequencing initial-phoneme deletion.

### P06 — Draw sound-change boxes

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Draw three sound boxes for pin, cross out /i/, add /a/ and label the new spoken word.

**Marking key:** Shows /p/ /i/ /n/ changing only middle to /a/; pan (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Representing medial substitution.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Identify changed position

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Sun changes to sum. Which sound position changed and what are the two sounds?

**Marking key:** Final position; /n/ changed to /m/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Locating a final phoneme substitution.

### P08 — Make three new words

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Start with pan. Make one new word by changing the first sound, one by changing the middle sound and one by changing the final sound.

**Marking key:** Three valid examples such as can, pin and pat, with only named position changed each time (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Generating words through substitutions in all positions.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Delete a final sound

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Say farm without the final /m/. What spoken word remains?

**Marking key:** Far (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Deleting a final phoneme.

### E02 — Match medial substitutions

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match pin with /e/, /a/ and /o/ in the middle to pen, pan and pon/nonword. State which two are familiar words.

**Marking key:** /e/ ↔ pen; /a/ ↔ pan; /o/ ↔ pon; pen and pan are familiar words (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Exploring medial phoneme changes including a possible nonword.

### E03 — Sequence sound addition

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order: blend plant; say lant; add /p/ at the beginning; identify new first sound.

**Marking key:** Say lant → add/identify initial /p/ → blend plant (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Sequencing addition to an initial cluster.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Substitute in a cluster word

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Change the /l/ in slip to /k/. Say the new word.

**Marking key:** Skip, using /s/ /k/ /i/ /p/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Substituting one phoneme within an initial blend.

### E05 — Demonstrate a sound chain

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Say cat, then change one phoneme each step to make cot, dot and dog. Name each change.

**Marking key:** Cat→cot middle /a/ to /o/; cot→dot first /k/ to /d/; dot→dog final /t/ to /g/ (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Performing sequential single-phoneme substitutions.

### E06 — Draw addition and deletion

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Use sound boxes to show adding /s/ to lip to make slip, then deleting /s/ to return to lip.

**Marking key:** Accurate 3-to-4 and 4-to-3 phoneme representations, with only /s/ changed (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Representing reversible phoneme manipulation.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Correct a two-sound change

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** The task says change one sound from pet to peg, but a student says pig. Explain the error.

**Marking key:** Pet→peg changes only final /t/ to /g/; pig also changes medial /e/ to /i/ (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Checking that only one phoneme was substituted.

### E08 — Solve a manipulation set

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Starting with best: delete /s/; then change final /t/ to /d/; then change medial /e/ to /a/. Say each result and describe each change.

**Marking key:** Best→bet (delete /s/)→bed (/t/→/d/)→bad (/e/→/a/), with each operation named (7 marks).

**Coverage:** Applying deletion and substitutions in a controlled word chain.
