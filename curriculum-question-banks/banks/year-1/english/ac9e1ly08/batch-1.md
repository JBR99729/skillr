# AC9E1LY08 — Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Rule:** Unjoined letters are formed separately, in the correct direction, with consistent size and spacing so each word is easy to read.
- **Worked example:** In ‘Mia’, capital M begins the name, lower-case i and a sit on the line, and a small space follows before the next word.
- **Formation check:** Use a comfortable functional grip, steady paper, correct starting points, clear capitals and lower-case letters, and spaces between words.
- **Visual interaction:** Show animated stroke direction with pause/replay, keyboard-labelled alternatives and printable tracing/start-dot models.
- **Common trap:** A capital is not just an oversized lower-case letter; many capital and lower-case forms differ.

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Choose the capital

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Which letter should begin the name maya when written correctly?

**Marking key:** Upper-case M (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Using an upper-case letter for a familiar proper name.

### P02 — Match upper and lower case

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match A, G and T to a, g and t.

**Marking key:** A ↔ a; G ↔ g; T ↔ t (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Connecting upper-case and lower-case letter forms.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Write a clear word

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Write the word map using separate lower-case letters on the line.

**Marking key:** Letters m-a-p are unjoined, correctly ordered, recognisable and placed consistently (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Forming a short lower-case word.

### P04 — Add word spacing

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Rewrite thereddog as three readable words.

**Marking key:** the red dog, with clear spaces and recognisable letters (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Using spacing to separate handwritten words.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Order letter formation checks

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order: check spacing; choose correct case; form each letter separately; reread the word.

**Marking key:** Choose case → form separate letters → check spacing → reread (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Sequencing a handwriting self-check.

### P06 — Copy with case

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Copy ‘Ben has a kite’ using a capital at the start/name, lower-case letters elsewhere and spaces.

**Marking key:** Accurate case, unjoined legible forms and three word spaces (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Copying a sentence with case and spacing.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Find a case error

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** A student writes aPPle in the middle of a sentence. Explain and correct the case.

**Marking key:** apple; ordinary word uses lower-case letters unless at sentence start, not internal capitals (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Editing inconsistent letter case.

### P08 — Compare readability

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Explain why equal spacing inside letters, clear word spaces and consistent size help a reader.

**Marking key:** They distinguish letters/words, prevent crowding or splitting and make recognition easier (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Reasoning about legibility features.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Write an upper-case word

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Write ACT in separate upper-case letters.

**Marking key:** A-C-T, all recognisable and unjoined with consistent size (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Forming a short upper-case abbreviation.

### E02 — Match case uses

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match capital letter, lower-case letter and word space to name/sentence start, most other letters and boundary between words.

**Marking key:** All three matched correctly (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Connecting handwriting features with functions.

### E03 — Sequence a short sentence

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order and handwrite: swims / Ava / fast.

**Marking key:** Ava swims fast. with capital A, separate legible letters, spaces and end mark (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Combining ordering with readable handwriting.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Repair crowded writing

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** The words blue boat touch each other. State and show the one change needed.

**Marking key:** Adds a visible word space: blue boat (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Correcting missing inter-word spacing.

### E05 — Demonstrate grip and posture

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Show a comfortable functional pencil grasp and paper position, then write sun without joining the letters.

**Marking key:** Grip allows controlled movement without strain; paper is stable; s-u-n forms are legible and separate (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Applying functional handwriting setup.

### E06 — Write paired forms

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Write the pairs Bb, Dd, Rr and circle the upper-case form in each.

**Marking key:** All six forms recognisable and correct upper-case letters circled (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Comparing distinct upper/lower-case forms.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Edit a handwritten label

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** A map label reads canBerra. Rewrite it correctly and explain two changes.

**Marking key:** Canberra; capital C and lower-case b, with legible unjoined formation (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Editing case within a proper noun.

### E08 — Produce a legible mini-text

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Handwrite two short sentences about a pet or imagined animal. Use at least one proper name and then self-check case, formation, size, spacing and line placement.

**Marking key:** Two complete legible sentences; correct name capital; five handwriting checks applied and one improvement identified (7 marks).

**Coverage:** Sustaining readable unjoined handwriting across sentences.
