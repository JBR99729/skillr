# AC9E1LY09 — Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Rule:** Segmenting means stretching a spoken word and saying every phoneme in order. In a consonant blend, each sound remains separate.
- **Worked example:** stop has four phonemes: /s/ /t/ /o/ /p/. The letters s and t form a blend because both sounds are heard.
- **Sound strategy:** Say the word normally, stretch it slowly, move one counter for each sound, then blend the sounds back to check.
- **Visual interaction:** Use sound boxes and counters with audio, tap, keyboard and paper-token alternatives; assess sounds, not letter names.
- **Common trap:** Count phonemes, not letters: ship has three sounds /sh/ /i/ /p/ because sh represents one sound.

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Segment a CVC word

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Say every phoneme in map.

**Marking key:** /m/ /a/ /p/ — 3 phonemes (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Segmenting a three-phoneme word.

### P02 — Match words and sound counts

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match sun, stop and fish to 3, 4 and 3 phonemes.

**Marking key:** Sun ↔ 3; stop ↔ 4; fish ↔ 3 (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Counting phonemes in words with a blend or digraph.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Move sound counters

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Say frog slowly and move one counter for each phoneme.

**Marking key:** /f/ /r/ /o/ /g/ with 4 counters in order (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Segmenting an initial consonant blend.

### P04 — Hear a final cluster

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Segment tent and state the final two sounds.

**Marking key:** /t/ /e/ /n/ /t/; final sounds /n/ /t/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Segmenting a final consonant cluster.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Order spoken sounds

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order the sounds /l/ /k/ /m/ /i/ to make milk.

**Marking key:** /m/ /i/ /l/ /k/ (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Sequencing phonemes in a word with final blend.

### P06 — Draw sound boxes

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Draw one box per sound in clap and place a dot in each.

**Marking key:** Four boxes/dots for /k/ /l/ /a/ /p/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Representing an initial blend with phoneme boxes.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Explain letters versus sounds

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Why does wish have three phonemes even though it has four letters?

**Marking key:** sh works together to represent one /sh/ sound: /w/ /i/ /sh/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Distinguishing grapheme count from phoneme count.

### P08 — Compare two blends

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Segment slip and list. Identify the blend at the beginning of one and the end of the other.

**Marking key:** Slip /s/ /l/ /i/ /p/ starts /s//l/; list /l/ /i/ /s/ /t/ ends /s//t/ (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Comparing initial and final consonant clusters.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Segment a digraph word

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Say every phoneme in chop.

**Marking key:** /ch/ /o/ /p/ — 3 phonemes (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Segmenting a word containing a consonant digraph.

### E02 — Match clusters and words

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match /b/ /l/, /s/ /t/ and /n/ /d/ to blue, star and hand.

**Marking key:** /b//l/ ↔ blue; /s//t/ ↔ star; /n//d/ ↔ hand (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Recognising consonant clusters in spoken words.

### E03 — Sequence brush sounds

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order /sh/ /r/ /b/ /u/ to segment brush.

**Marking key:** /b/ /r/ /u/ /sh/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Sequencing a blend and digraph in one word.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Count sounds in spoon

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Segment spoon and count the phonemes.

**Marking key:** /s/ /p/ /oo/ /n/ — 4 phonemes (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Segmenting an initial cluster with a vowel digraph sound.

### E05 — Demonstrate continuous segmenting

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Say best, stretching each sound without inserting extra ‘uh’ sounds.

**Marking key:** /b/ /e/ /s/ /t/ cleanly, 4 phonemes (3 marks including no added vowels).

**Coverage:** Producing accurate phoneme segments in a final cluster.

### E06 — Draw two sound maps

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Draw sound boxes for flag and ship, then compare the number of letters and sounds.

**Marking key:** Flag: 4 sounds/4 letters; ship: 3 sounds/4 letters; boxes accurately represent /f l a g/ and /sh i p/ (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Comparing blend and digraph sound structures.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Correct an inserted sound

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** A student segments frog as /f/ /uh/ /r/ /o/ /g/. Explain the error.

**Marking key:** The blend has adjacent /f/ and /r/ sounds; no vowel phoneme occurs between them (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Detecting schwa insertion in consonant blends.

### E08 — Segment a mixed set

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Segment sprint, milk and chest. Give each sound in order, count phonemes and identify one initial or final cluster/digraph in each.

**Marking key:** Sprint /s p r i n t/ 6; milk /m i l k/ 4; chest /ch e s t/ 4; features correctly identified (8 marks).

**Coverage:** Applying phoneme segmentation to varied clusters and digraphs.
