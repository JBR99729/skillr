# AC9E1LY11 — Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Rule:** Short and long vowel patterns help read and spell words. Blends keep each consonant sound; digraphs use two letters for one sound, such as sh, ch and th.
- **Worked example:** man has short /a/. time has long /i/ with silent e. wish ends in the /sh/ digraph, while rest begins and ends with separate consonant sounds.
- **Reading strategy:** Spot the vowel pattern and any digraph, say sounds through the word, blend, then split a two-syllable word into spoken beats if needed.
- **Visual interaction:** Use grapheme tiles, sound buttons and syllable arcs with audio, tap, keyboard and paper alternatives.
- **Common trap:** A blend is not one sound: in stop, /s/ and /t/ are both heard; in shop, sh is one /sh/ sound.

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Read a short vowel word

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Blend /m/ /a/ /n/ and write the word.

**Marking key:** Man (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Reading and spelling a short-a CVC word.

### P02 — Match patterns

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match time, wish and frog to long vowel, consonant digraph and consonant blend.

**Marking key:** Time ↔ long vowel; wish ↔ digraph; frog ↔ blend (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Recognising common Year 1 phonic patterns.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Build a digraph word

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Use letter tiles to build shop and say each phoneme.

**Marking key:** s-h-o-p in spelling; /sh/ /o/ /p/ in sounds (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Encoding a word with initial consonant digraph.

### P04 — Change short to long

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Add final e to kit. Read and explain the new word.

**Marking key:** Kite; final e changes the vowel from short /i/ to long /i/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Using a common split-digraph long-vowel pattern.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Order blend sounds

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order /r/ /f/ /o/ /g/ to read frog.

**Marking key:** /f/ /r/ /o/ /g/ → frog (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Blending an initial consonant cluster.

### P06 — Draw sound buttons

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Write fish and draw one sound button for /f/, /i/ and /sh/.

**Marking key:** Correct spelling and 3 sound markers, with sh grouped (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Mapping a final digraph to one phoneme.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Compare blend and digraph

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Explain why br in brush is a blend but sh is a digraph.

**Marking key:** Both /b/ and /r/ are heard separately; sh letters work together for one /sh/ sound (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Distinguishing consonant blends from digraphs.

### P08 — Read a two-syllable word

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Split sunset into spoken syllables, read each part and blend the whole word.

**Marking key:** Sun/set; reads sun + set → sunset (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Decoding a two-syllable compound with known patterns.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Spell a long-a word

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Write the word made by /k/ /ā/ /k/ using the common a_e pattern.

**Marking key:** Cake (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Encoding a common long-a split digraph.

### E02 — Match digraphs and words

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match sh, ch and th to ship, chop and thin.

**Marking key:** sh ↔ ship; ch ↔ chop; th ↔ thin (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Connecting consonant digraphs with example words.

### E03 — Sequence a two-syllable read

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order: blend each part; find a syllable break; read whole word; inspect letter patterns in picnic.

**Marking key:** Inspect patterns → find pic/nic → blend parts → read picnic (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Sequencing two-syllable decoding.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Choose the vowel pattern

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Which word has a long-o pattern: hop or hope? Explain.

**Marking key:** Hope; o_e commonly signals long /o/, while hop has short /o/ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Comparing short and long vowel spellings.

### E05 — Demonstrate continuous blending

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Blend the sounds in rest without adding extra vowel sounds between /s/ and /t/.

**Marking key:** /r/ /e/ /s/ /t/ blended as rest, with both final blend sounds retained (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Reading a word with final consonant blend.

### E06 — Draw a two-syllable map

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Write rabbit, mark rab/bit and underline the short vowel in each syllable.

**Marking key:** Rabbit split rab/bit; a and i identified as short vowel sounds (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Representing vowel patterns across two syllables.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Correct a digraph split

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** A reader sounds ship as /s/ /h/ /i/ /p/. Explain and correct.

**Marking key:** Sh represents one /sh/ phoneme; correct segmentation /sh/ /i/ /p/ (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Correcting misuse of a consonant digraph.

### E08 — Decode and encode a mixed set

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Read and then spell from memory: flag, chin, home and sunset. Name the blend, digraph, long-vowel pattern or syllable split used in each.

**Marking key:** All four words accurate; fl blend, ch digraph, o_e long /o/, sun/set syllables, with explanations (8 marks).

**Coverage:** Applying varied phonic knowledge to reading and spelling.
