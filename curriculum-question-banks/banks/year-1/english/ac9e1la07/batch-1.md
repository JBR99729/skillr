# AC9E1LA07 — Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Rule:** Nouns name people, places or things; pronouns can replace nouns; verbs show actions or states; adjectives describe nouns; adverbs add details such as how, when or where.
- **Worked example:** In ‘She carries the heavy box carefully’, She is a pronoun, carries is a verb, heavy is an adjective and carefully tells how.
- **Word check:** Ask: Who or what? noun/pronoun. What happens or is? verb. What kind? adjective. How, when or where? adverb detail.
- **Visual interaction:** Use colour-independent labelled word cards and sentence slots with tap, keyboard and paper sorting choices.
- **Common trap:** A word's job depends on its sentence; ‘fast’ can describe a car or tell how it moves.

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Find the verb

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** What is the verb in ‘The ducks paddle’?

**Marking key:** Paddle (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Identifying an action verb.

### P02 — Match words and jobs

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match teacher, they and gently to noun, pronoun and adverb detail.

**Marking key:** Teacher ↔ noun; they ↔ pronoun; gently ↔ adverb detail (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Connecting words with grammatical functions.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Sort sentence cards

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Sort cat, sleeps, furry and quietly into noun, verb, adjective and how detail.

**Marking key:** Cat noun; sleeps verb; furry adjective; quietly adverb/how (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Classifying words by function.

### P04 — Replace repeated noun

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Rewrite ‘Lena found Lena's hat’ using a pronoun for the second Lena.

**Marking key:** ‘Lena found her hat.’ (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Using a pronoun to avoid repetition.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Order a detailed sentence

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order: quickly, the small crab, moves.

**Marking key:** The small crab moves quickly. (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Arranging noun, adjective, verb and adverb.

### P06 — Draw word roles

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Draw ‘A tall tree bends slowly’ and label tree, tall, bends and slowly by word job.

**Marking key:** Tree noun; tall adjective; bends verb; slowly adverb/how (4 marks).

**Coverage:** Connecting word classes with sentence meaning.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Distinguish two jobs

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** In ‘The fast train moves fast’, explain the job of each fast.

**Marking key:** First describes train, so adjective; second tells how it moves, so adverb detail (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Recognising one form in different grammatical roles.

### P08 — Improve a plain sentence

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** Expand ‘Birds fly’ with one adjective and one detail telling where or how.

**Marking key:** Example: ‘Colourful birds fly quietly above the lake.’ with both required additions (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Adding descriptive and circumstantial detail.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Find a state verb

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** What is the verb in ‘The soup is warm’?

**Marking key:** Is; it links the soup with its state (2 marks).

**Coverage:** Recognising a verb of being/state.

### E02 — Match questions and details

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students draw lines between matching entries.

**Question:** Match how, when and where to carefully, tomorrow and outside.

**Marking key:** How ↔ carefully; when ↔ tomorrow; where ↔ outside (1 mark each).

**Coverage:** Classifying adverbial details by meaning.

### E03 — Sequence a pronoun sentence

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Online, provide movable cards with drag, tap-to-place and keyboard controls. On paper, students number or rewrite the cards in order.

**Question:** Order: They, after school, practise, together.

**Marking key:** They practise together after school. (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Building a sentence with pronoun, verb and details.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Choose a precise adjective

**Type:** Short response

**Question:** Replace nice in ‘a nice shell’ with an adjective that tells colour, texture or size.

**Marking key:** Any precise suitable adjective such as striped, smooth or tiny (1 mark).

**Coverage:** Selecting informative noun description.

### E05 — Demonstrate action and state

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Provide the named classroom materials or an equivalent labelled visual. Accept a teacher-observed demonstration or a written description.

**Question:** Act or say one sentence with an action verb and one with a state verb. Name both verbs.

**Marking key:** Two complete examples, such as jumps and is/seems, correctly identified (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Contrasting action and state verbs.

### E06 — Draw a word-class map

**Type:** Drawing response

**Delivery:** Online, provide a simple drawing or selectable-diagram tool with a keyboard-labelled alternative. On paper, students draw and label.

**Question:** Create five labelled boxes for noun, pronoun, verb, adjective and adverb detail. Add one original example to each.

**Marking key:** Five suitable examples placed in correct boxes (5 marks).

**Coverage:** Organising vocabulary by grammatical function.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Correct a label error

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** A student labels blue as a verb in ‘The blue boat rocks’. Correct all three main word jobs.

**Marking key:** Blue adjective; boat noun; rocks verb (3 marks).

**Coverage:** Revising word-class analysis from sentence context.

### E08 — Analyse and revise

**Type:** Extended response

**Question:** In ‘He slowly opens the rusty gate at dawn’, identify the pronoun, verb, adjective, how detail and when detail, then replace one with another suitable word.

**Marking key:** He pronoun; opens verb; rusty adjective; slowly how; at dawn when; replacement preserves grammar and meaning (6 marks).

**Coverage:** Analysing several word functions in context.
