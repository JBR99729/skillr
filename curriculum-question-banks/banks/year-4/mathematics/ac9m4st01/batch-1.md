# AC9M4ST01 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Design questions and collect categorical or numerical data.
- **Worked example:** For data collection, use the numbers, labels, diagram or data in the task before choosing an answer.
- **Common trap:** Do not answer from a keyword alone; check the full context and any labels, units or evidence.
- **Visual model:** Use diagrams, maps, graphs, tables, measurement tools, apparatus drawings or real images only when they carry information needed for the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
