## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Students use problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions by modelling the situation, calculating or comparing, and explaining why the answer makes sense.
- **Worked example:** Read the labels first, choose a representation for problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions, calculate carefully, then check the size of the answer.
- **Common trap:** A common error is using a familiar operation without checking the whole, the unit, the scale, or the data display.
- **Visual model:** Use concrete materials, number lines, arrays, graphs, rulers, maps, grids or diagrams when they carry mathematical information.
