# AC9S3U04 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Concept answer:** Solids keep their own shape. Liquids flow and take the shape of their container while keeping the same amount unless added or removed.
- **Key vocabulary:** solid, liquid, flow, container, property.
- **Example:** Water changes shape in a cup or bowl, but a wooden block keeps its shape.
- **Common misconception:** A soft solid, such as playdough, is still a solid because it does not flow like a liquid.
- **Visual model:** Use real objects, labelled diagrams, simple data tables, photos or short investigation cards. Drag tasks must also work with tap and keyboard controls.
