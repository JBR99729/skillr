# AC9S3I05 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Concept answer:** Scientists compare results with others and decide whether the investigation was fair enough to trust.
- **Key vocabulary:** finding, compare, fair, reliable, improve.
- **Example:** If two groups get very different results, they should check whether they used the same method.
- **Common misconception:** Different results do not always mean someone is wrong; the method may have changed.
- **Visual model:** Use real objects, labelled diagrams, simple data tables, photos or short investigation cards. Drag tasks must also work with tap and keyboard controls.
