# AC9S3U03 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Concept answer:** Heat energy can move from warmer objects to cooler objects. Temperature measures how hot or cold something is.
- **Key vocabulary:** heat, temperature, thermometer, insulator, conductor.
- **Example:** A metal spoon in warm soup heats faster than a wooden spoon because metal conducts heat well.
- **Common misconception:** Heat and temperature are related, but they are not exactly the same idea.
- **Visual model:** Use real objects, labelled diagrams, simple data tables, photos or short investigation cards. Drag tasks must also work with tap and keyboard controls.
