# AC9S3I04 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Concept answer:** Representations organise information so patterns are easier to see. Tables, column graphs and labelled diagrams each have a purpose.
- **Key vocabulary:** table, column graph, diagram, label, scale.
- **Example:** A column graph can show which soil held the most water.
- **Common misconception:** A graph without labels or a scale is hard to interpret accurately.
- **Visual model:** Use real objects, labelled diagrams, simple data tables, photos or short investigation cards. Drag tasks must also work with tap and keyboard controls.
