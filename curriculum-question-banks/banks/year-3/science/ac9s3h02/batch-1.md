# AC9S3H02 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Concept answer:** Scientific explanations can help people design solutions, choose materials and solve everyday problems.
- **Key vocabulary:** need, solution, material, design, explanation.
- **Example:** Knowing that dark surfaces warm faster can help design warmer winter clothing.
- **Common misconception:** Science is not only facts in a book; it helps people make decisions.
- **Visual model:** Use real objects, labelled diagrams, simple data tables, photos or short investigation cards. Drag tasks must also work with tap and keyboard controls.
