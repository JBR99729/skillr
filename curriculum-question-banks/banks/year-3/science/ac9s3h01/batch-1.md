# AC9S3H01 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Concept answer:** Scientists use observations and data as evidence to develop explanations about patterns in the world.
- **Key vocabulary:** data, evidence, observation, explanation, pattern.
- **Example:** If plants near sunlight grow taller in repeated observations, the data supports an explanation about light and growth.
- **Common misconception:** One observation is useful, but repeated data is stronger evidence.
- **Visual model:** Use real objects, labelled diagrams, simple data tables, photos or short investigation cards. Drag tasks must also work with tap and keyboard controls.
