# AC9M3P01 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Chance words describe how likely outcomes are: impossible, unlikely, equally likely, likely and certain.
- **Worked example:** Rolling an even number on a fair six-sided die is equally likely to rolling an odd number.
- **Common trap:** Possible does not mean certain.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
