# AC9M3A02 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Known facts to 20 can be extended to larger numbers by place value.
- **Worked example:** 8 + 7 = 15 helps with 80 + 70 = 150 and 38 + 7 = 45.
- **Common trap:** Do not forget the place value when extending a basic fact.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
