# AC9M3A01 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Addition and subtraction undo each other. Use fact families to check missing numbers.
- **Worked example:** 37 + 18 = 55, so 55 - 18 = 37 and 55 - 37 = 18.
- **Common trap:** The total belongs in a different place in subtraction than in addition.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
