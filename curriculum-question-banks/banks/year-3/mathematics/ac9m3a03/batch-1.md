# AC9M3A03 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Use skip counting, arrays and known facts to recall multiplication facts efficiently.
- **Worked example:** 4 x 6 can be doubled twice: 6 doubled is 12, doubled again is 24.
- **Common trap:** Counting all objects one by one is slow and can hide the equal-group structure.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
