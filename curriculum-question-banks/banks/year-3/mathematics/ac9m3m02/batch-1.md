# AC9M3M02 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Use the same unit when comparing measurements. Read scales carefully from zero or from the marked starting point.
- **Worked example:** 45 cm is longer than 38 cm because both are in centimetres.
- **Common trap:** Do not compare numbers alone when the units are different.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
