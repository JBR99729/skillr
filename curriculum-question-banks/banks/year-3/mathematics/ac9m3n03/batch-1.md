# AC9M3N03 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Use place value to add or subtract hundreds, tens and ones. Regroup when ones or tens cross a ten or hundred.
- **Worked example:** 247 + 38 = 247 + 30 + 8 = 277 + 8 = 285.
- **Common trap:** Line up digits by place value; ones under ones, tens under tens and hundreds under hundreds.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
