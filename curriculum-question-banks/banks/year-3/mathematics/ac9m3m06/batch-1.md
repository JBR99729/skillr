# AC9M3M06 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** One dollar equals 100 cents. Money amounts can be represented with coins, notes and decimal notation.
- **Worked example:** $3.45 is 3 dollars and 45 cents, or 345 cents.
- **Common trap:** $3.05 needs a zero in the cents place.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
