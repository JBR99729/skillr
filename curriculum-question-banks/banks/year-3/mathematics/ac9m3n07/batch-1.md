# AC9M3N07 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** An algorithm is a clear sequence of steps that can be followed to solve a problem or complete a task.
- **Worked example:** To make 36: start at 4, multiply by 8, add 4 gives 36.
- **Common trap:** Steps must be in order; changing the order can change the result.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
