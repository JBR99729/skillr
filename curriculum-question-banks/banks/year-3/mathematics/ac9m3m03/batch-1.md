# AC9M3M03 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Time units are connected: 60 seconds = 1 minute, 60 minutes = 1 hour, 24 hours = 1 day and 7 days = 1 week.
- **Worked example:** 2 hours 15 minutes is 120 + 15 = 135 minutes.
- **Common trap:** Time is not base ten; 1 hour is 60 minutes, not 100 minutes.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
