# AC9M3M05 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** An angle measures the amount of turn. A right angle is a quarter turn.
- **Worked example:** A half turn has 2 right angles; a full turn has 4 right angles.
- **Common trap:** Longer arms do not make a bigger angle; the opening or turn matters.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
