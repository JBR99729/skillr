# AC9M3P02 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Repeated trials help show patterns in chance, but results can vary from the expected outcome.
- **Worked example:** A coin might land heads 6 times in 10 flips, even though heads and tails are equally likely.
- **Common trap:** Small experiments do not always match the expected pattern exactly.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
