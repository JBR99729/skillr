# AC9M3N02 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** A unit fraction has numerator 1. The denominator tells how many equal parts make one whole.
- **Worked example:** If a sandwich is cut into 4 equal parts, one part is 1/4 and three parts are 3/4.
- **Common trap:** Unequal parts do not make correct fractions, even if there are the right number of pieces.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
