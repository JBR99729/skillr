# AC9M3N05 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** An estimate is a sensible close answer. Use benchmarks, grouping, rounding and known facts to judge reasonableness.
- **Worked example:** If each row has about 10 shells and there are about 6 rows, estimate about 60 shells.
- **Common trap:** An estimate should be close enough to be useful, not a wild guess.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
