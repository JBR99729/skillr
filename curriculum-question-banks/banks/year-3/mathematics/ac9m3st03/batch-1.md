# AC9M3ST03 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** A statistical investigation asks a question, collects data, represents it, interprets it and reports what was found.
- **Worked example:** Question: How do Year 3 students travel to school? Collect tallies, draw a graph and describe the most common method.
- **Common trap:** Do not make a conclusion that the data does not support.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
