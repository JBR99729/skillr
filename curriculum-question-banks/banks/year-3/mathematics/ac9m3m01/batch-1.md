# AC9M3M01 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Choose units that fit the object: millimetres, centimetres, metres, kilometres, grams, kilograms, millilitres and litres.
- **Worked example:** Measure a pencil in centimetres, a room in metres and a road trip in kilometres.
- **Common trap:** A unit can be correct for one object but silly for another.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
