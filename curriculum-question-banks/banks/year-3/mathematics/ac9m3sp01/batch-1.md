# AC9M3SP01 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Classify objects by features such as faces, edges, vertices, curved surfaces and whether faces are flat.
- **Worked example:** A cube has 6 square faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices.
- **Common trap:** A cylinder has curved surface; it is not a prism.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
