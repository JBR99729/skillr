# AC9M3M04 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** On an analog clock, the minute hand shows minutes past the hour and the hour hand sits between hours.
- **Worked example:** 3:45 means 45 minutes past 3, or quarter to 4.
- **Common trap:** At half past, the hour hand is halfway to the next hour.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
