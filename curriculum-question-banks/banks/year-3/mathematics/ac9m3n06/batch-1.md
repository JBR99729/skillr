# AC9M3N06 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** A model turns a practical situation into a number sentence. Choose operations that match the story and check whether the answer makes sense.
- **Worked example:** Four teams of 6 students and 3 extra students is 4 x 6 + 3 = 27 students.
- **Common trap:** Read the question carefully; some problems need more than one operation.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
