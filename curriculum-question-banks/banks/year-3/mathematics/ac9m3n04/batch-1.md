# AC9M3N04 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Multiplication joins equal groups. Division either shares a total equally or finds how many equal groups fit.
- **Worked example:** 6 bags with 4 apples each is 6 x 4 = 24 apples; 24 shared between 6 bags is 4 each.
- **Common trap:** Only use multiplication when the groups are equal.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
