# AC9M3ST02 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Different graphs can show the same data. Compare labels, scales and category heights before interpreting.
- **Worked example:** A column graph and picture graph can both show that soccer has the most votes.
- **Common trap:** A picture graph key may mean each picture stands for more than one item.
- **Visual model:** Use arrays, number lines, clocks, maps, measuring tools, graph cards or manipulatives that match the question. Online tasks must include tap and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop.
