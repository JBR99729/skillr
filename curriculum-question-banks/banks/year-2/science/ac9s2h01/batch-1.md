# AC9S2H01 - Pass 1 question bank

## 60-second Quick Read

- **Key idea:** Students learn about how people use science in their daily lives by connecting observations, models and data to evidence-based explanations.
- **Worked example:** Use the observation, diagram, table or investigation plan to make a claim about how people use science in their daily lives, then support it with evidence.
- **Common trap:** A common error is making a claim from memory instead of using the evidence, controls, labels or measured results.
- **Visual model:** Use labelled diagrams, apparatus drawings, observation cards, tables, graphs or photographs when the evidence must be inspected. For early years, make the task visual-first and keep wording short.
