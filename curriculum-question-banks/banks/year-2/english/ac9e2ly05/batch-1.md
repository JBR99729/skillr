- **Key idea:** Students use comprehension strategies such as visualising, predicting, connecting, summarising, monitoring and by noticing language, structure, audience, purpose and visual choices.
- **Worked example:** Read the short text or visual carefully, identify the purpose of comprehension strategies such as visualising, predicting, connecting, summarising, monitoring and, and support the answer with a word, sentence or feature from the text.
- **Common trap:** A common error is giving a personal opinion without linking it to wording, structure, image detail, audience or purpose.
- **Visual model:** Use text snippets, layout mock-ups, image frames, speech bubbles or word cards when the student must inspect them to answer. For early years, make the task visual-first and keep wording short.
