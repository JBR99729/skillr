# AC9EFLY06 — Pass 1 question bank

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — A sentence that records an event

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Read the context and options aloud.

**Question:** The class watched an ice cube melt. Which sentence clearly records what happened?

A. Where is lunch?

B. Ice and a cup.

C. Look at this!

D. The ice got wet and small.

**Correct answer:** D — It reports an observed change in a complete sentence.

**Coverage:** Recognising a written event record.

### P02 — Topic vocabulary in a report

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Say, “The class planted bean seeds,” then read the options.

**Question:** Which word would be most useful in a report about the activity?

A. soil

B. spaceship

C. trumpet

D. pillow

**Correct answer:** A — “Soil” is relevant learned vocabulary for planting.

**Coverage:** Selecting vocabulary that fits a reported topic.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Writing and spelling a CVC caption

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Describe a picture of “a cat sitting on a mat”. Ask the child to write independently before prompting.

**Question:** Write one sentence that records the picture. Spell **cat**, **sat** and **mat** using their sounds, and show the sentence beginning and end.

**Marking key:** Expected form such as “The cat sat on the mat.” Award 1 mark for complete relevant sentence, 1 mark for capital/end mark, and 1 mark for correctly sound-mapped CVC target words; 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Coordinating CVC spelling with a punctuated caption.

### P04 — Shared editing marks

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Show: “we put the lid on the pot”

**Question:** Work as an editor: name and make two changes that show the sentence boundary.

**Marking key:** Capital W (1 mark) and full stop after “pot” (1 mark). Corrected: “We put the lid on the pot.”

**Coverage:** Applying basic boundary punctuation during shared editing.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Reporting events in order

**Type:** Sequencing

**Delivery:** Provide these sentences: (a) “At last, the paper was dry.” (b) “First, we painted blue lines.” (c) “Next, we left the picture in the sun.”

**Question:** Put the report in order and copy or say it as a short text.

**Correct answer:** (b), (c), (a). Award 1 mark for order and 1 mark for maintaining complete sentence boundaries in the produced text; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Organising a short event report chronologically.

### P06 — Replacing a vague word

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Show: “We put the thing in the tank. The fish hid inside it.”

**Question:** The “thing” was a small log. Edit the first sentence so the report is clearer.

**Marking key:** “We put the small log/a log in the tank.” Award 1 mark for replacing “thing” with “log” and 1 mark for a correctly bounded complete sentence; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Editing for precise learned vocabulary and clarity.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Creating a three-sentence observation record

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say, “A toy car rolled down a ramp, crossed the mat and stopped at a box.” Provide writing support as appropriate but record the child’s choices.

**Question:** Write or dictate three short sentences that report the events in order. Include at least two accurate topic words.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for each ordered event sentence (3 marks), 1 mark for at least two relevant words such as ramp, rolled, mat, stopped or box, 1 mark for visible/audible sentence boundaries, and 1 mark for plausible phonetic spelling including any attempted CVC words; 6 marks total.

**Coverage:** Composing an ordered, punctuated short report from an observed sequence.

### P08 — Editing a complete draft

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Show: “on monday we fed the hen it had corn in a pan”

**Question:** Edit the draft into two clear sentences. Correct the name of the day, sentence starts, end marks and the CVC words **hen**, **corn** and **pan** where needed.

**Marking key:** A correct version is “On Monday, we fed the hen. It had corn in a pan.” Award 1 mark for Monday capital, 1 mark for two sentence starts, 1 mark for correct boundary between ideas/end punctuation, 1 mark for hen, 1 mark for pan, and 1 mark for preserving corn accurately; 6 marks total. Do not require the comma for full Foundation credit.

**Coverage:** Conducting a multi-feature shared edit of a short event record.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Choosing reporting language

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Say, “The class measured a plant,” then read the options.

**Question:** Which sentence belongs in an observation report?

A. Once a plant flew to the moon.

B. The stem was ten blocks tall.

C. Please come to my party.

D. What is your favourite song?

**Correct answer:** B — It records a measured observation using topic vocabulary.

**Coverage:** Identifying an informative recording sentence.

### E02 — Editing a missing capital

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Show: “the seed split.”

**Question:** Which letter should change, and why?

**Marking key:** Change t to capital T (1 mark) because it begins the sentence (1 mark); 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Explaining initial-capital editing.

### E03 — Spelling from phonemes

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say the word **mud**, stretch its phonemes /m/ /u/ /d/, and show a picture/context if needed.

**Question:** Write the word **mud**.

**Marking key:** m-u-d in correct order. Award 1 mark.

**Coverage:** Encoding a regular CVC word for use in reporting.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Writing a result sentence

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say, “A paper boat was placed in water and stayed on top.”

**Question:** Write one sentence that records the result. Use the learned word **float** or **floated**.

**Marking key:** A complete accurate sentence such as “The paper boat floated.” Award 1 mark for result/topic word and 1 mark for capital plus suitable end mark; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Producing a concise result statement with learned vocabulary.

### E05 — Combining notes into a report

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Give notes: **sun came out / wet path dried / puddle got small**.

**Question:** Turn the notes into two complete sentences that report the changes clearly.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for sunlight/weather event, 1 mark for path/puddle change, 1 mark for logical connection/order, and 1 mark for sentence boundaries; 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Transforming fragmentary notes into coherent reporting sentences.

### E06 — Participating in an edit

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Show: “The bug sat on a rok.”

**Question:** Find one correctly spelled CVC word and correct the misspelled final word.

**Marking key:** Identifies **bug** or **sat** as correct (1 mark) and changes **rok** to **rock** (1 mark). Accept discussion that rock uses two letters for final /k/; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Reviewing known phoneme–grapheme patterns during editing.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Writing from an investigation sequence

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Read: “First, a class put one ice cube in sunlight and one in shade. The sunny cube melted first. The shaded cube stayed solid longer.”

**Question:** Create a three-sentence report with a setup, result and comparison. Use at least two topic words and correct sentence boundaries.

**Marking key:** 1 mark each for setup, sunny result and comparison/shade result; 1 mark for logical sequence, 1 mark for at least two relevant terms such as ice cube, sunlight, shade, melted, solid; 1 mark for capitals/end marks; 6 marks total.

**Coverage:** Synthesising an investigation into a structured written report.

### E08 — Explaining and applying edits

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Show: “we got a big box sam put six red peg in it the box tipt”

**Question:** Edit the text into clear sentences, correcting boundaries, the name, word choice for more than one peg, and the final action word. Explain two edits.

**Marking key:** A strong version is “We got a big box. Sam put six red pegs in it. The box tipped.” Award 1 mark for three clear sentence units, 1 mark for capitals including Sam, 1 mark for pegs plural, 1 mark for tipped, and up to 2 marks for explaining any two changes; 6 marks total.

**Coverage:** Editing morphology, spelling and sentence boundaries in a connected text.
