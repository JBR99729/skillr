# AC9EFLY02 — Pass 1 question bank

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Showing active listening

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Read the scenario and options aloud.

**Question:** A classmate is explaining a drawing. Which action best shows listening?

A. Call out a new idea over the speaker.

B. Start a different conversation.

C. Cover the drawing and sing.

D. Face the speaker and wait until they finish.

**Correct answer:** D — Attention and waiting allow the classmate’s message to be heard.

**Coverage:** Recognising observable listening behaviour.

### P02 — Choosing a suitable volume

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Read the situations and volume labels **quiet**, **comfortable conversation voice**, **loud enough for the group**.

**Question:** Match: (a) speaking to one partner in the library, (b) telling news to the whole class, (c) chatting with a friend outside at lunch.

**Marking key:** (a) quiet, (b) loud enough for the group, (c) comfortable conversation voice. Award 1 mark each; 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Matching voice volume to audience and setting.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Listening and responding

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Say, “I built my tower with a wide base so it would not fall.”

**Question:** Give a response that shows you listened to the idea.

**Marking key:** Refers to the speaker’s content through a relevant comment or question, such as “Did the wide base make it steadier?” Award 1 mark for relevance and 1 mark for waiting/responding clearly; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Building a response from another speaker’s contribution.

### P04 — Informal or structured talk

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Read all options aloud.

**Question:** Which situation is most structured, with an expected order for speaking?

A. Each child shares one observation during morning circle.

B. Two friends invent a game at recess.

C. Siblings chat while drawing at home.

D. Children laugh together on the swings.

**Correct answer:** A — Morning circle has an organised purpose and turn sequence.

**Coverage:** Distinguishing a structured speaking situation from informal interaction.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Entering a conversation

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Role-play two people discussing which blocks they need. Pause after finishing a sentence.

**Question:** Join the conversation politely with one relevant idea, using a volume that suits the small group.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for entering at a pause/without interrupting, 1 mark for a relevant contribution and 1 mark for an appropriate conversational volume; 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Applying timing, relevance and voice control in informal group talk.

### P06 — Repairing an interruption

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say, “Toby begins speaking while Alina is still answering.”

**Question:** What could Toby do and say to repair the interaction?

**Marking key:** Stops/waits (1 mark) and gives a suitable repair such as “Sorry, please finish” or “I spoke over you” (1 mark); 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Restoring turn-taking after an interruption.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Diagnosing why a message failed

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Read: “During a class sharing circle, Indi looks at the floor and whispers. Children at the back begin asking what was said.”

**Question:** Identify the communication problem and suggest two changes that would help the whole class listen.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for identifying insufficient volume/unclear audience delivery; 1 mark each for two useful changes such as louder clear voice, facing the group, slower pace or waiting for quiet; 1 mark for explaining how a change helps listeners; 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Analysing and improving voice use in a structured situation.

### P08 — Managing three speakers fairly

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say, “Three children all begin explaining their science observations at once.”

**Question:** Propose a fair speaking plan, including what listeners do and how each speaker should use their voice.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for a turn system such as an order/object/list; 1 mark for listeners waiting and attending; 1 mark for each speaker using audible but not shouting volume; 1 mark for a transition cue or relevant response between turns; 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Designing a structured interaction with turn and voice expectations.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Listening during partner talk

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Read all options aloud.

**Question:** While a partner speaks, which behaviour makes it easiest to understand them?

A. Practise your own sentence aloud.

B. Attend to their words and keep other objects still.

C. Walk away before the ending.

D. Ask unrelated questions repeatedly.

**Correct answer:** B — Focused attention supports comprehension.

**Coverage:** Identifying effective listening in a close interaction.

### E02 — Volume for a presentation

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Ask the question aloud.

**Question:** How should your voice change when speaking to twenty classmates instead of one nearby partner?

**Marking key:** It should be louder/stronger and clear enough to reach the whole group without shouting. Award 1 mark.

**Coverage:** Explaining audience-related volume adjustment.

### E03 — Recognising a speaker’s turn

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Read the scenario and options.

**Question:** The teacher says, “After Lena finishes, Max will share.” What should Max do now?

A. Tell Lena to stop immediately.

B. Speak at the same time as Lena.

C. Listen to Lena and wait.

D. Begin a game elsewhere.

**Correct answer:** C — The stated order gives Lena the current turn.

**Coverage:** Following an explicit turn-taking structure.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Responding to the actual message

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Say, “Our bean plant grew two centimetres this week.”

**Question:** Ask one question that shows you heard the measurement information.

**Marking key:** A relevant question such as “How did you measure it?” or “How tall is it now?” Award 1 mark for reference to growth/measurement and 1 mark for a clear question; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Demonstrating listening through a content-linked follow-up.

### E05 — Adapting from playground to assembly

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say, “Nora tells a friend, ‘I found your ball!’ outside. Later, Nora must announce the same information to a large assembly.”

**Question:** Show how Nora should change her words or voice for the assembly and explain two changes.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for a complete public announcement; 1 mark for increased audible volume; 1 mark for clearer/slower or more identifying wording; 1 mark for explaining audience size/formality; 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Adapting message delivery between informal and structured contexts.

### E06 — Solving a quiet-pair problem

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Read: “In partner talk, one child speaks so loudly that nearby pairs cannot hear each other.”

**Question:** Give one respectful reminder and one voice change that would solve the problem.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for a respectful reminder such as “Please use a partner voice”; 1 mark for reducing volume while remaining audible to the partner; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Applying volume regulation to a shared environment.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Analysing a failed group discussion

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Read: “Four children discuss a class pet. Alex shouts every idea. Bea never looks up from a toy. Chen begins answering before others finish. Dani speaks clearly but no one responds to her idea.”

**Question:** Identify one interaction problem for each named child and propose a group rule that addresses all four.

**Marking key:** 1 mark each for correctly identifying Alex’s volume, Bea’s inattention, Chen’s interruption and lack of response to Dani (4 marks); 1 mark for a coherent combined rule or plan covering turns, listening, suitable volume and responding; 5 marks total.

**Coverage:** Diagnosing multiple listening and speaking breakdowns.

### E08 — Leading a structured sharing round

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say, “You will help lead a round in which every child names one object that floats or sinks.”

**Question:** Explain how you will begin, signal turns, listen, manage voice levels and finish. Give one sentence you could use at two different stages.

**Marking key:** 1 mark each for a clear opening/purpose, fair turn signal, listener expectation, suitable group-speaking volume and closing/summarising move; 1 mark for two appropriate leader sentences tied to stages; 6 marks total.

**Coverage:** Planning and language-managing a complete structured interaction.
