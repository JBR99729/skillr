# AC9EFLY04 — Pass 1 question bank

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Blending a CVC word

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Show **s-a-t** with the letters separated. Say the common sound for each letter only if the child needs the prompt.

**Question:** Say each sound, then blend the sounds to read the whole word.

**Marking key:** Produces /s/ /a/ /t/ and blends to “sat”. Award 1 mark for sound sequence and 1 mark for blended word; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Using letter–sound knowledge to blend a regular CVC word.

### P02 — Decoding for meaning

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Ask the child to read the bold word in “The dog can **dig**.” Then read options only if needed.

**Question:** Which action matches **dig**?

A. Sleep on a mat

B. Make a hole in soil

C. Sip from a cup

D. Run to a gate

**Correct answer:** B — The decoded word “dig” names making a hole.

**Coverage:** Connecting a decoded CVC word to meaning.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Reading a decodable sentence

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Show: **Sam can pat the cat.** Do not read it first.

**Question:** Read the sentence, then tell who pats the cat.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for substantially accurate reading with blending as needed and 1 mark for identifying Sam; 2 marks total. Do not penalise accent variation.

**Coverage:** Reading a simple decodable sentence and checking literal meaning.

### P04 — Using grammar to self-correct

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Show: **The hen is in the pen.** If the child reads “The hen is in the pet,” ask the question below without giving the answer.

**Question:** Does “in the pet” sound right here? Look through the final word and try the sentence again.

**Marking key:** Recognises the mismatch and rereads “pen”. Award 1 mark for monitoring and 1 mark for using final letter/sound plus sentence grammar to correct; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Coordinating phonic detail and grammatical sense during self-correction.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Choosing between similar-looking words

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Show: **The pig is in the ___.** Options: **pen**, **pin**, **pan**, **pet**. Ask the child to read each option.

**Question:** Which word makes the most likely sentence and matches all its letters?

A. pan

B. pin

C. pen

D. pet

**Correct answer:** C — /p/ /e/ /n/ blends to “pen”, a plausible enclosure for a pig.

**Coverage:** Cross-checking phonics with sentence context.

### P06 — Monitoring an authentic sentence

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Show: **Please put your hat on before we go outside.** Allow the child to attempt using known words, phonics and context.

**Question:** What should the reader do? If one word was difficult, tell what helped you work it out.

**Marking key:** Identifies putting on a hat before going outside (1 mark); names a useful strategy such as first sound, known word part, rereading, sentence meaning or grammar (1 mark); 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Reading functional authentic text and reflecting on monitoring strategy.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Diagnosing a reading mismatch

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Show: **A red fox ran up the hill.** Say, “A reader said, ‘A red fox ran up the hat.’”

**Question:** Explain two clues that show **hat** is not correct, then give the correct word.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for noticing letters/sounds in **hill** do not match **hat**; 1 mark for noting that “up the hill” makes contextual/grammatical sense; 1 mark for correcting to **hill**; 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Analysing a miscue through visual and meaning checks.

### P08 — Reading and checking a tiny text

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Show: **Kim has a red cap. The cap fell in the mud. Kim had to scrub it.** Ask the child to read independently, supporting only after an attempt.

**Question:** Why did Kim scrub the cap? Name one word you checked carefully and explain how you knew it was right.

**Marking key:** Because it fell in mud/was muddy (1 mark); identifies a word from the text (1 mark); gives a valid phonic/context/grammar check for that word (1 mark); reading conveys the main text sufficiently (1 mark); 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Sustaining reading and metacognitive meaning monitoring across sentences.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Segmenting and blending

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Show **m-a-p**.

**Question:** Say the sounds and blend the word.

**Marking key:** /m/ /a/ /p/, “map”. Award 1 mark for sound sequence and 1 mark for blending; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Independently decoding a regular CVC word.

### E02 — Reading a high-utility sentence

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Show: **I can hop on one leg.**

**Question:** Read the sentence and show or explain the action.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for accurate/substantially accurate reading and 1 mark for demonstrating/explaining hopping on one leg; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Connecting sentence reading with embodied meaning.

### E03 — Detecting nonsense in context

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Show: **Dad put the milk in the sock.** Read only if decoding prevents comprehension.

**Question:** The sentence is grammatical, but one idea is unlikely. Which word should the reader check first?

A. the

B. Dad

C. put

D. sock

**Correct answer:** D — Milk is not normally stored in a sock, so the reader should recheck that word/source text.

**Coverage:** Monitoring semantic plausibility during reading.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Correcting from all letters

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Show: **The duck sat on a rock.** Say, “A reader looked only at the first letter and said ‘road’ for the last word.”

**Question:** What should the reader do to correct the word?

**Marking key:** Look through all letters/sounds and blend r-o-ck to read **rock** (1 mark), then reread/check that a duck can sit on a rock (1 mark); 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Correcting partial-cue guessing with full phonic and meaning checks.

### E05 — Reading an authentic label

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Show a simple label: **Wet paint — do not touch.**

**Question:** Read the label and explain what action it asks you to avoid and why.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for conveying the warning accurately; 1 mark for “do not touch”; 1 mark for linking the reason to wet paint/mess/damage; 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Reading and acting on a functional authentic text.

### E06 — Choosing the grammatically fitting word

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Show: **Two cats ___ on the mat.** Options: **sit**, **sits**, **sat**, **sip**. Ask the child to read the options.

**Question:** Which option best completes a sentence about what the cats are doing now?

A. sit

B. sits

C. sat

D. sip

**Correct answer:** A — “Two cats sit” matches plural grammar and the intended present action.

**Coverage:** Using grammatical knowledge alongside decoding to select a word.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Explaining two self-corrections

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Show: **The crab hid in the sand. It was hard to spot.** Say, “A reader first said ‘cab’ for crab and ‘soft’ for spot.”

**Question:** Correct both miscues and explain which letter/sound and meaning clues help each correction.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for **crab**, 1 mark for using the /r/ or full letter sequence; 1 mark for **spot**, 1 mark for using /sp/ or full sequence; 1 mark for explaining that “hard to spot” means hard to see/find and fits the context; 5 marks total.

**Coverage:** Analysing multiple miscues through coordinated decoding and comprehension.

### E08 — Reading, inferring and monitoring

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Show: **Ben fed the hen. Then he shut the gate. “Now she is safe,” he said.** Ask for an independent reading attempt.

**Question:** Who does **she** refer to, and why did Ben shut the gate? Explain one check you used when reading a difficult word.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for the hen; 1 mark for explaining gate closure keeps the hen safe/in; 1 mark for connecting pronoun/context; 1 mark for naming a word checked; 1 mark for an appropriate decoding/context/grammar strategy; 5 marks total.

**Coverage:** Integrating decoding, pronoun resolution, inference and self-monitoring.
