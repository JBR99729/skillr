# AC9EFLY12 — Pass 1 question bank

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Building map from sounds

**Type:** Demonstration

**Delivery:** Say the sounds /m/ /a/ /p/ separately. Provide the letter cards **m, a, p, t**.

**Question:** Choose three cards to show each sound in order, then read the word you made.

**Correct answer:** **m-a-p, map**. The letters represent the three sounds in order.

**Coverage:** Mapping three spoken phonemes to letters in a CVC word.

### P02 — Blending sit

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Show **s-i-t**, point to each letter and say /s/ /i/ /t/ without adding extra vowel sounds.

**Question:** Blend the three sounds smoothly. What word do they make?

**Correct answer:** **sit**.

**Coverage:** Blending letter sounds to read a regular CVC word.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Segmenting and writing sun

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say **sun** naturally, then repeat it slowly if needed.

**Question:** Say the three sounds in **sun** and write one letter for each sound.

**Marking key:** Segments /s/ /u/ /n/ (1 mark) and writes **sun** in the correct order (1 mark). 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Segmenting a spoken CVC word before encoding it.

### P04 — Finding a CVC word

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Read each option as written only after the child attempts to blend it.

**Question:** Which word is written with exactly three letters in the pattern consonant letter–vowel letter–consonant letter?

A. dog

B. ship

C. tree

D. stop

**Correct answer:** A — **dog** follows the consonant-vowel-consonant pattern; the other options include a digraph, vowel team or consonant blend.

**Coverage:** Recognising the structural pattern of a CVC word.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Completing fan from a picture cue

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Show or describe a picture of a hand-held fan beside **_ a n**. Say the target word **fan**.

**Question:** Which letter completes the word so it matches the picture and the sounds you hear?

A. v

B. f

C. s

D. r

**Correct answer:** B — /f/ is the first sound in **fan**, so the completed CVC word is **fan**.

**Coverage:** Selecting an initial letter using phoneme and meaning cues.

### P06 — Sorting by the middle vowel

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Show the words **hen, lip, red, fin**. Ask the child to blend each word.

**Question:** Sort the words into two groups: middle sound /e/ and middle sound /i/.

**Marking key:** /e/: **hen, red**; /i/: **lip, fin**. Award 1 mark for each correctly completed group; 2 marks total.

**Coverage:** Attending to medial short-vowel sounds while decoding CVC words.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Repairing an incomplete spelling

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say: “A child wants to write **cat** but writes **ct**.”

**Question:** Which sound and letter are missing? Rewrite the complete word and explain where the missing letter belongs.

**Marking key:** Identifies the missing medial /a/ sound and letter **a** (1 mark); writes **cat** (1 mark); explains that **a** belongs between **c** and **t** because it represents the middle sound (1 mark). 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Diagnosing and correcting an omitted medial vowel in a CVC word.

### P08 — Making a word chain

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Provide the starting word **pan** and the letter cards **c, m, t**.

**Question:** Change only the first letter to make three new CVC words. Read every word and tell which sound changes each time.

**Marking key:** Produces **can, man, tan** in any order (1 mark each); blends all 3 accurately (1 mark); explains that the initial sound changes while **-an** stays the same (1 mark). 5 marks total.

**Coverage:** Manipulating an initial grapheme while preserving and blending a CVC rime.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Blending pen

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Show **p-e-n** and point to each letter.

**Question:** Say each sound, then blend the sounds to read the whole word.

**Correct answer:** /p/ /e/ /n/, **pen**.

**Coverage:** Independently blending a new CVC word.

### E02 — Encoding rug

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Show a picture of a rug and say **rug** once without stretching the sounds.

**Question:** Write the three letters that represent the sounds in **rug**.

**Correct answer:** **rug**.

**Coverage:** Encoding a spoken CVC word from a meaning cue.

### E03 — Choosing the letters for hot

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Say the target sounds /h/ /o/ /t/ and then read the options as letter names.

**Question:** Which group of letters shows those sounds in the same order?

A. o-h-t

B. h-t-o

C. h-o-t

D. t-o-h

**Correct answer:** C — **h-o-t** represents /h/ /o/ /t/ in sequence and blends to **hot**.

**Coverage:** Preserving phoneme order when selecting a CVC spelling.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Matching spoken and written CVC words

**Type:** Matching

**Delivery:** Say **van, mud, jet** in a different order from the printed list **mud, jet, van**.

**Question:** Point to the written word that matches each spoken word, then blend it to check.

**Marking key:** Correctly matches **van**, **mud** and **jet** (1 mark each). Award a fourth mark if all 3 are checked by accurate blending. 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Coordinating spoken-word recognition with CVC decoding.

### E05 — Solving a missing middle letter

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Show a picture of a lid beside **l _ d** and say **lid**.

**Question:** Which letter completes the word?

A. a

B. e

C. o

D. i

**Correct answer:** D — The middle sound in **lid** is /i/, represented by **i**.

**Coverage:** Selecting a medial short vowel in context.

### E06 — Reading mixed CVC words

**Type:** Oral response

**Delivery:** Show the words one at a time: **cab, pig, gum, web**.

**Question:** Blend and read each word. After reading, choose the word that names an animal.

**Marking key:** 1 mark for each accurately read word (4 marks); identifies **pig** (1 mark). 5 marks total.

**Coverage:** Decoding CVC words with varied vowels and checking meaning.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Explaining a final-sound error

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say: “The picture shows a child who is **fit**. A writer spells the word **fid**.”

**Question:** Read the written word, identify the incorrect letter and repair the spelling. Explain how the final sound proves your answer.

**Marking key:** Blends **fid** as written (1 mark); identifies **d** as incorrect (1 mark); writes **fit** with **t** (1 mark); explains that the final /t/ sound is represented by **t**, not **d** (1 mark). 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Using blending and segmentation to diagnose a final-consonant substitution.

### E08 — Encoding and decoding two linked words

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say: “Write the words **log** and **leg**. Then another reader will use your spellings.”

**Question:** Segment and write both words. Underline the letter that changes, read each completed word and explain how that one letter changes the spoken word.

**Marking key:** Writes **log** (1 mark) and **leg** (1 mark); underlines **o/e** as the changing medial letters (1 mark); accurately blends both (1 mark); explains that the changed letter represents a different middle vowel sound and therefore a different word (1 mark). 5 marks total.

**Coverage:** Encoding, contrasting and decoding CVC words distinguished by one vowel.
