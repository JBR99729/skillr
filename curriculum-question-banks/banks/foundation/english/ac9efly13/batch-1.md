# AC9EFLY13 — Pass 1 question bank

## Practice questions

## Tier 1 — Knowledge and terms

### P01 — Writing the first sound in moon

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say **moon** and ask the child to repeat its first sound.

**Question:** What sound begins **moon**? Write the letter that usually represents that sound.

**Correct answer:** /m/, written **m**.

**Coverage:** Representing an initial consonant phoneme with a letter.

### P02 — Recording the final sound in leaf

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say **leaf**, slightly emphasising but not distorting its final sound.

**Question:** Say the last sound in **leaf** and write the letter that represents it.

**Correct answer:** /f/, written **f**.

**Coverage:** Isolating and representing a final consonant phoneme.

## Tier 2 — Core skill practice

### P03 — Choosing a spelling for ship

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Say **ship** and show a picture of a ship. Tell the child that **sh** can work together to represent one sound.

**Question:** Which spelling best matches all the sounds in **ship**?

A. sip

B. ship

C. shop

D. chip

**Correct answer:** B — **sh-i-p** represents /sh/ /i/ /p/ in order.

**Coverage:** Applying a taught consonant digraph when selecting a spelling.

### P04 — Segmenting and spelling frog

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say **frog** and provide an image cue if needed.

**Question:** Stretch **frog**, say its sounds in order and write the word.

**Marking key:** Segments /f/ /r/ /o/ /g/ (1 mark) and writes **frog** with all four sound positions represented in order (2 marks). 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Encoding a CCVC word from a complete phoneme sequence.

## Tier 3 — Guided application

### P05 — Checking the spelling of nest

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Say **nest**. Ask the child to sound out each option before choosing.

**Question:** Which spelling records the beginning, middle and final consonant sounds in the correct order?

A. net

B. nset

C. nest

D. nesp

**Correct answer:** C — **nest** includes /n/ /e/ /s/ /t/ in order.

**Coverage:** Selecting a spelling that preserves every phoneme in a CVCC word.

### P06 — Repairing a missing sound in plan

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say: “A child wants to write **plan** but writes **pan**.”

**Question:** Which sound is missing? Add the correct letter and rewrite the word.

**Marking key:** Identifies missing /l/ (1 mark), adds **l** after **p** (1 mark) and writes **plan** (1 mark). 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Correcting an omitted consonant in an initial blend.

## Tier 4 — Problem solving and reasoning

### P07 — Making a plausible spelling choice

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say the sentence: “The **fish** swam fast.” Repeat the target word **fish**. Do not display it.

**Question:** Spell **fish**. Point to the letters for the first, middle and final sounds, and explain why two letters are used at the end.

**Marking key:** Writes **fish** (2 marks); maps **f** to /f/ and **i** to /i/ (1 mark); explains that **sh** is a 2-letter pattern representing the single /sh/ sound (1 mark). 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Explaining grapheme choices in a word containing a final digraph.

### P08 — Spelling from a sound map

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Show four sound boxes. Say **drum** and ask the child to move one counter for each phoneme before writing.

**Question:** Fill the sound boxes with letters, write the whole word and check it by blending your spelling.

**Marking key:** Maps /d/ /r/ /u/ /m/ to **d-r-u-m** in order (2 marks); writes **drum** (1 mark); blends the written form accurately to check (1 mark). 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Using a phoneme map to plan, record and verify a spelling.

## Exam questions

## Section A — Core knowledge

### E01 — Representing the first sound in van

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say **van** once and show a picture cue.

**Question:** Write the letter for the sound at the beginning of **van**.

**Correct answer:** **v** for /v/.

**Coverage:** Independently encoding an initial consonant sound.

### E02 — Finding the beginning digraph in chin

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Say **chin**. Remind the child that some sounds are represented by two letters.

**Question:** Which two letters represent the first sound in **chin**?

A. sh

B. th

C. wh

D. ch

**Correct answer:** D — **ch** represents the initial /ch/ sound in **chin**.

**Coverage:** Selecting a digraph for a spoken consonant phoneme.

### E03 — Writing flag

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say **flag** and ask the child to repeat it slowly.

**Question:** Write **flag**, including every sound you hear.

**Correct answer:** **flag**. Award 1 mark for **fl**, 1 for **a**, and 1 for final **g**; 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Encoding a CCVC word with an initial blend.

## Section B — Application and problem solving

### E04 — Selecting the sentence spelling

**Type:** Multiple choice

**Delivery:** Say: “The **best** hat is red.” Emphasise only the target word **best**, then read the options.

**Question:** Which spelling fits the spoken word in the sentence?

A. best

B. bes

C. bet

D. bist

**Correct answer:** A — **best** represents /b/ /e/ /s/ /t/ and matches the meaning of the sentence.

**Coverage:** Coordinating phoneme order and sentence meaning when choosing a spelling.

### E05 — Correcting a substituted consonant

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say: “A picture shows a **crab**, but the label says **crap**.”

**Question:** Which letter is wrong? Rewrite the label and explain how the last sound helps you choose.

**Marking key:** Identifies final **p** as wrong (1 mark); writes **crab** (1 mark); links final /b/ to **b** (1 mark). 3 marks total.

**Coverage:** Using final-phoneme analysis to repair a near-plausible spelling.

### E06 — Spelling two rhyming words

**Type:** Short response

**Delivery:** Say **shop** and **chop**, with a pause between them.

**Question:** Write both words. Underline the part they share and circle the letters that make their first sounds different.

**Marking key:** Writes **shop** (1 mark) and **chop** (1 mark); underlines **op** in both (1 mark); circles **sh/ch** and links them to different initial sounds (1 mark). 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Using onset-rime comparison and digraph knowledge to spell related words.

## Section C — Extended response and analysis

### E07 — Evaluating two spellings

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say **tent**. Show two attempts: **tet** and **tent**.

**Question:** Blend both attempts, choose the better spelling and explain what the other attempt leaves out.

**Marking key:** Blends both attempts as written (1 mark); chooses **tent** (1 mark); identifies the missing /n/ in **tet** (1 mark); explains that **n** must appear before final **t** to represent every spoken phoneme (1 mark). 4 marks total.

**Coverage:** Evaluating spelling completeness through segmentation and blending.

### E08 — Planning and checking a spelling

**Type:** Extended response

**Delivery:** Say: “Write the word **milk** for a shopping label.” Provide no printed model.

**Question:** Say each sound, write a plausible spelling, blend what you wrote and describe one check you made.

**Marking key:** Segments /m/ /i/ /l/ /k/ (1 mark); writes **milk** with all phonemes represented in order (2 marks); blends the result accurately (1 mark); describes a valid check such as listening for the medial vowel or final /k/ (1 mark). 5 marks total.

**Coverage:** Independently planning, encoding and verifying a familiar one-syllable word.
