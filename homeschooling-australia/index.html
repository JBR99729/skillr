Extra support for teaching at home

Need a hand explaining a topic?

Short on preparation time, or unsure how to explain an idea? Selected SkillrHub topic guides include free video explanations that play within the lesson. Use one to help you and your child get started, then build understanding with the written guide and practice activities.

  1. Choose a topic. Open your child's year and topic guide, then look for Watch a video.
  2. Watch and talk together. Load the player and press Play. Pause to discuss an example or replay a difficult part.
  3. Put it into practice. Choose Back to the lesson, explore the worked examples and try the short task, worksheet or practice questions.

This can be especially helpful in Foundation, Year 1 and Year 2. Preview the chosen video and stay nearby to help younger children with the controls and check their understanding.
