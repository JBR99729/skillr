Platform capabilities

Built for everyday classrooms and home learning

The useful details are collected here so the homepage can stay focused on getting learners into the right resource quickly.

🎓 Teaching & practice

  • 50,000+ curriculum-aligned questions: Structured Australian F–10 coverage across Maths, Science and English.
  • Daily Drills: Short retrieval-practice sets for warm-ups and daily learning habits.
  • Immediate feedback: Learners can check responses and review available explanations.
  • Optional read-aloud: A learner can press the speaker control on supported questions; sound stays off by default.
  • Practice, tests and review: Connected activities make it easier to move from learning to assessment.

🛠️ Classroom tools

  • Custom break timer: Remembered 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60-minute reminders with a gentle chime.
  • Digital maths scratchpad: On-screen working space for a finger, mouse or stylus.
  • Quick-code QR cards: Print or copy a direct link to an eligible lesson, drill, worksheet or slide deck.
  • Keyboard controls: Faster answer selection and navigation for supported drills and slides.
  • Quick Class Warm-Up: Open one question from the learner’s latest local practice pattern.

🔒 Privacy & flexibility

  • No learner account needed: Core resources are available without a sign-up or password.
  • Privacy by design: Progress, names, avatars and streaks are kept in that browser’s local storage.
  • Installable PWA: Supported browsers can add SkillrHub to a home screen or app window for one-tap access.
  • Print and PDF friendly: Worksheets, QR cards and qualifying certificates have print-focused layouts.
  • Portable progress: Learners can export a backup file or a visual progress snapshot without uploading it.
  • Three-question embeds: Teachers can copy a small practice-widget snippet for a class page or blog.

Designed for classroom privacy

Learning progress stays local

SkillrHub does not require a personal learner account for core resources. Saved progress belongs to the browser and device being used, and can be cleared through that browser’s storage controls.
