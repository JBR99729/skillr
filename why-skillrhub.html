The SkillrHub learning loop

Resources connect instead of living in separate folders

A learner can move through one curriculum skill from explanation to independent checking.

1LearnTopic guides, key concepts and worked examples introduce the skill.
2PracticeFocused questions build confidence with feedback and review support.
3TestA separate question bank checks what the learner can do independently.
4ReviewUse progress and results to decide which skill should come next.

What is included

Tools for learning, teaching and checking progress

📚 Curriculum learning

Navigate Australian Curriculum v9.0 skills across Foundation–Year 10 Maths, Science and English.

  • Topic guides: curriculum-specific explanations and examples.
  • Teacher resources: slides and supporting classroom material where available.
  • Worksheets & homework: printable or structured follow-up activities.

✍️ Practice & assessment

Give learners something meaningful to do immediately after learning the concept.

  • Practice modules: focused questions with immediate feedback.
  • Separate tests: independent checking rather than repeating the practice flow.
  • Review support: explanations and hints on supported questions.

🔥 Habits & progress

Make SkillrHub useful between lessons as well as during them.

  • Daily Drills: short mixed retrieval sessions.
  • Dashboard: see locally saved activity and progress.
  • Portable progress: supported progress can be exported without a learner account.

🛠️ Classroom tools

Small utilities reduce friction when SkillrHub is used in a classroom or tutoring session.

  • Scratchpad: on-screen working space on supported activities.
  • Timers & controls: practical session tools where supported.
  • QR and sharing tools: make it easier to open the same resource on another device.
🔒

Privacy-first access

No learner account required for core resources

Learner progress, names, avatars and streak information used by the local experience stay in that browser's local storage. This keeps the core learning path quick to access at school, at home or during tutoring.
