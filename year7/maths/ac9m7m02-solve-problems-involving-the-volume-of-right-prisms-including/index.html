Volume of Right Prisms

Build volume from unit cubes, use cross-sectional area × perpendicular prism length, solve rectangular and triangular prism problems, and interpret volume models carefully.

PracticeHomeworkTest

Core idea

V = B × h

B is the area of the constant cross-section and h is the perpendicular prism length. For a rectangular prism this becomes V=lwh. For a triangular prism, first calculate B=½bh, then multiply by prism length.

Worked examples

Rectangular prism

9×5×4 = 180 cm³.

Triangular prism

Triangle area: ½×12×6=36 cm². Length 10 cm → V=36×10=360 cm³.

Reverse problem

V=864 cm³, B=48 cm² → h=864÷48=18 cm.

Unit cubes

A 3×4 layer has 12 cubes. Five layers contain 60 cubes, showing why volume is base area × height.

Cheat sheet

  • Prism: V=Bh
  • Rectangular prism: V=lwh
  • Triangular prism: V=(½bh)L
  • Unknown length: h=V÷B
  • Volume uses cubic units: cm³, m³, mm³.
  • Convert units before multiplying.
  • Doubling B with h fixed doubles V; doubling h with B fixed doubles V.
  • Doubling B and halving h keeps V unchanged.

Important questions

  1. Why does stacking equal unit-cube layers lead to V=Bh?
  2. How do you find the volume of a triangular prism?
  3. Why must the prism length be perpendicular when using V=Bh?
  4. How can two differently shaped prisms have equal volume?
  5. How do you find an unknown cross-sectional area or prism length from volume?
  6. What happens to V when B increases 50% and h decreases 20%?
  7. Why is total surface area not the B in V=Bh?
  8. Why is floor area × number of floors not automatically a volume?
  9. How do cm² and metres need to be handled before calculating volume?
  10. What assumptions are made when a room, tank or building is modelled as a prism?

Common mistakes

  • Using triangle area as bh instead of ½bh.
  • Using a slanted edge instead of perpendicular prism length.
  • Writing cm² for a volume answer.
  • Multiplying quantities with incompatible units.
  • Assuming any shape change keeps volume fixed: both cross-sectional area and perpendicular length must remain unchanged.
