Why Shadows Change (E5)

Shadows change because the sun's position in the sky changes.

TimeShadow pattern
MorningLong
MiddayShort
AfternoonLong again
Safety: Do not look directly at the sun. Observe shadows, not the sun itself.

Sky Patterns (E8)

Short-term

  • Sun rises every morning
  • Sun sets every evening
  • Moon rises and sets daily

Longer-term

  • Moon changes appearance over a month
  • Stars appear in different positions across seasons

First Nations Sky Knowledge (E10)

First Nations Peoples read the sky to understand Country.

The sky tells us about

  • Seasons
  • Weather
  • Animal behaviour
  • Navigation
  • Cultural stories

Examples

  • The Emu in the Sky constellation
  • Yolŋu moon stories
  • Torres Strait Islander star calendars
Before teaching: check whether the sky knowledge of your local Country is published, and share local knowledge alongside these examples.

Investigation Ideas

  1. Track shadows across the school day.
  2. Create a 30-night moon diary.
  3. Observe and sort the day sky and night sky.
  4. Identify planets using a sky app.
  5. Watch time-lapse videos and describe what moves.
  6. Draw and label a solar system model.
Support: provide a partly-filled record sheet.
Extend: compare shadow lengths across two days.
