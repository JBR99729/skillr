Properties of Quadrilaterals

Quadrilateral properties can be established by drawing diagonals and using congruent triangles and angle relationships.

What students learn

Quadrilateral properties can be established by drawing diagonals and using congruent triangles and angle relationships.

Learning intention: Quadrilateral properties can be established by drawing diagonals and using congruent triangles and angle relationships.

Success criteria

  • Explain split a quadrilateral with a diagonal using the approved model.
  • Apply explain a quadrilateral result step by step to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • establish properties of quadrilaterals using congruent triangles and angle properties, and solve related problems explaining reasoning

Prerequisites

Do not overteach

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • A diagram is trusted by appearance instead of stated conditions — Mark givens and name the theorem or transformation used.
  • The properties of quadrilaterals model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘The sum of the exterior angles of a polygon is equal to a complete turn or 360°.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
