Representing Two Events

Two-way tables, tree diagrams and Venn diagrams organise combinations and reveal different probability relationships.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesHomeworkPracticeTest
What students learn

Two-way tables, tree diagrams and Venn diagrams organise combinations and reveal different probability relationships.

Learning intention: Two-way tables, tree diagrams and Venn diagrams organise combinations and reveal different probability relationships.

Success criteria

  • Explain list every two-event combination using the approved model.
  • Apply use a venn diagram without double-counting to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • determine all possible combinations for 2 events, using two-way tables, tree diagrams and Venn diagrams, and use these to determine probabilities of specific outcomes in practical situations

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 7 probability representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • The sample space or dependency changes without being noticed — Rebuild the outcomes and label replacement or conditioning explicitly.
  • The representing two events model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Describe events using language of “at least”, exclusive “or” (A or B but not both), inclusive “or” (A or B or both) and “and”.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
