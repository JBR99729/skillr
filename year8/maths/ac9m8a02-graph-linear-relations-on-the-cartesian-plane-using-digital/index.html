Step 1 · Learn the idea

Tables, graphs and algebra are connected representations of the same linear relationship.

Learning intention: Tables, graphs and algebra are connected representations of the same linear relationship.

Success criteria

  • Explain the central relationship in linear relations, equations and inequalities using correct mathematical language.
  • Use the worked methods for connect table, graph and rule and solve and verify in unfamiliar examples.
  • Check the result using substitution, estimation, an inverse operation or another representation.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • graph linear relations on the Cartesian plane using digital tools where appropriate; solve linear equations and one-variable inequalities using graphical and algebraic techniques; verify solutions by substitution

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 7 algebra representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Avoid this trap
  • A rule is applied without preserving equivalence or its conditions — Return to the linked table, graph or expanded form and verify by substitution.
  • The linear relations, equations and inequalities model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Recognise that in a table of values, if the first difference between consecutive values of the dependent variable is constant, then it is a linear relation.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.

Important questions and answers

1. What is the central idea in Linear Relations, Equations and Inequalities?

Answer: Tables, graphs and algebra are connected representations of the same linear relationship.

2. How can you connect table, graph and rule?

Answer: For y=2x−1, first differences are constant at 2 and plotted points form a straight line.

3. How can the graph verify the solution of 3x+7=6x−9?

Answer: Graph y=3x+7 and y=6x−9. Their intersection has x=16/3, which makes both expressions equal.
