Important questions and answers

1. What is the central idea in Linear Expressions?

Answer: Equivalent expressions can look different while producing the same value for every permitted substitution.

2. How can you connect structure and properties?

Answer: 3(x+4)−2x=3x+12−2x=x+12

3. Is 4x+8 equivalent to 4(x+8)? Justify.

Answer: No. 4(x+8)=4x+32. The factorised form of 4x+8 is 4(x+2).
