AC9S8U03 • Year 8 Science

Plate Boundaries Shape Earth

Plate movement produces different geological features, and multiple evidence lines support plate tectonic theory.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

We are learning to explain plate boundaries shape earth using models and evidence.

Success criteria

  • Identify and connect divergent, convergent, transform.
  • Use a model to explain rather than only describe the concept.
  • Apply the central idea to a curriculum example and justify the connection.
  • Evaluate one model limit, misconception or evidence boundary.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • Divergent, convergent and transform boundaries have distinct relative motions and features.
  • Spatial and historical evidence supports plate tectonic theory.
  • Tectonic activity affects people and can be mitigated through engineering and warning systems.

Prerequisites

  • Read a labelled scientific diagram or data display.
  • Distinguish an observation from an explanation.
  • Use evidence to support a short scientific statement.

Do not overteach

  • Do not show plates floating on liquid magma.
  • Do not treat continental drift and plate tectonics as identical theories.
