AC9S8H02 • Year 8 Science

World Views Shape Scientific Knowledge

Culture and world views influence which questions are asked, whose expertise is recognised and how knowledge develops.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

We are learning to explain world views shape scientific knowledge using models and evidence.

Success criteria

  • Identify and connect world view, question and priority, knowledge practice.
  • Use a model to explain rather than only describe the concept.
  • Apply the central idea to a curriculum example and justify the connection.
  • Evaluate one model limit, misconception or evidence boundary.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • World views influence priorities, methods, interpretation and recognition.
  • Collaboration across perspectives can develop knowledge.
  • Cultural knowledge requires accurate attribution and appropriate protocols.

Prerequisites

  • Read a labelled scientific diagram or data display.
  • Distinguish an observation from an explanation.
  • Use evidence to support a short scientific statement.

Do not overteach

  • Do not generalise First Nations Australian knowledge as one uniform system.
  • Do not use cultural examples as decoration detached from their scientific contribution.
