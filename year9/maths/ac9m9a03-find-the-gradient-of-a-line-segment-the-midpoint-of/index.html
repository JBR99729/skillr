Coordinate Geometry

Gradient, midpoint and distance describe different invariant relationships between two Cartesian points.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesHomeworkPracticeTest
What students learn

Gradient, midpoint and distance describe different invariant relationships between two Cartesian points.

Learning intention: Gradient, midpoint and distance describe different invariant relationships between two Cartesian points.

Success criteria

  • Explain show that gradient is constant using the approved model.
  • Apply see coordinates inside navigation algorithms to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • find the gradient of a line segment, the midpoint of the line interval and the distance between 2 distinct points on the Cartesian plane

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 8 algebra representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • A rule is applied without preserving equivalence or its conditions — Return to the linked table, graph or expanded form and verify by substitution.
  • The coordinate geometry model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Recognise that the gradient of a line is calculated using the gradient of a line segment on that line.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
