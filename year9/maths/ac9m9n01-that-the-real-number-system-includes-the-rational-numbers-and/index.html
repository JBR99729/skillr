Real Numbers and Exact Values

Rational and irrational numbers share one real number line; exact forms preserve value while decimals may approximate it.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesHomeworkPracticeTest
What students learn

Rational and irrational numbers share one real number line; exact forms preserve value while decimals may approximate it.

Learning intention: Rational and irrational numbers share one real number line; exact forms preserve value while decimals may approximate it.

Success criteria

  • Explain map the real number system using the approved model.
  • Apply construct √2 on a number line to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • recognise that the real number system includes the rational numbers and the irrational numbers, and solve problems involving real numbers using digital tools

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 8 number representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Exact and approximate values are treated as interchangeable — Keep exact notation until the requested approximation step.
  • The real numbers and exact values model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Explore the real number system by representing the relationships between irrationals, rationals, integers and natural numbers and discussing the difference between exact representations.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
