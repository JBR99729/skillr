Learning intention: Scientific notation records extremely large or small measurements efficiently while preserving scale and appropriate accuracy.

Success criteria

  • Explain the central relationship in scientific notation and scale using correct mathematical language.
  • Apply the worked methods for write extreme measurements in scientific notation and match instrument precision to recorded values to unfamiliar examples.
  • Check the result using substitution, estimation, an inverse operation or another representation.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • solve problems involving very small and very large measurements, time scales and intervals expressed in scientific notation

Prerequisites

Avoid this trap
  • Dimensions, units or accuracy are mixed — Label every measure and convert before calculating.
  • The scientific notation and scale model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘The approximate geological age of the earth is 4.6× 10^9 years, the mass of a sugar molecule is 5.68× 10^(-21) grams.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
