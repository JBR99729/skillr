Modelling Proportion, Rate and Scale

Direct proportion keeps a constant multiplicative relationship; a useful model preserves units, scale and assumptions.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesHomeworkPracticeTest
What students learn

Direct proportion keeps a constant multiplicative relationship; a useful model preserves units, scale and assumptions.

Learning intention: Direct proportion keeps a constant multiplicative relationship; a useful model preserves units, scale and assumptions.

Success criteria

  • Explain model direct proportion using the approved model.
  • Apply connect environmental rates and conditions to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • use mathematical modelling to solve practical problems involving direct proportion, rates, ratio and scale, including financial contexts; formulate the problems and interpret solutions in terms of the situation; evaluate the model and report methods and findings

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 8 measurement representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Dimensions, units or accuracy are mixed — Label every measure and convert before calculating.
  • The modelling proportion, rate and scale model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Hooke’s law and other science contexts involving wave lengths and frequencies.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
