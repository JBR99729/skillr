Spatial Problem Solving

Angle facts, similarity, scale, Pythagoras and trigonometry work together when a spatial problem is decomposed deliberately.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

Angle facts, similarity, scale, Pythagoras and trigonometry work together when a spatial problem is decomposed deliberately.

Learning intention: Angle facts, similarity, scale, Pythagoras and trigonometry work together when a spatial problem is decomposed deliberately.

Success criteria

  • Explain combine pythagoras and trigonometry using the approved model.
  • Apply model spatial navigation algorithms to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • solve spatial problems, applying angle properties, scale, similarity, Pythagoras’ theorem and trigonometry in right-angled triangles

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 8 measurement representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Dimensions, units or accuracy are mixed — Label every measure and convert before calculating.
  • The spatial problem solving model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Explore the applications of Pythagoras’ theorem in authentic problems, including applying Pythagoras’ theorem and trigonometry to problems in surveying and design.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
