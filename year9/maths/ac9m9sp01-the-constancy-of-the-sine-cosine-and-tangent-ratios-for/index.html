Trigonometric Ratios

Similar right triangles with the same acute angle have constant side ratios, giving sine, cosine and tangent.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesHomeworkPracticeTest
What students learn

Similar right triangles with the same acute angle have constant side ratios, giving sine, cosine and tangent.

Learning intention: Similar right triangles with the same acute angle have constant side ratios, giving sine, cosine and tangent.

Success criteria

  • Explain name sides relative to an angle using the approved model.
  • Apply connect tangent and gradient to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • recognise the constancy of the sine, cosine and tangent ratios for a given angle in right-angled triangles using properties of similarity

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 8 space representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Worked examples

Name sides relative to an angle

Understand the terms “base”, “altitude”, “hypotenuse”, and “adjacent” and “opposite” sides to an angle, in a right-angled triangle, and identifying these for a given right-angled triangle.

Teacher prompt: For name sides relative to an angle, which labelled relationship justifies the conclusion?

Find constant ratios in similar triangles

Explore patterns to reason about nested similar triangles that are aligned on a coordinate plane, connecting ideas of parallel sides.

Teacher prompt: For find constant ratios in similar triangles, which labelled relationship justifies the conclusion?

See sine and cosine on a unit hypotenuse

Establish an understanding that the sine of an angle can be considered as the length of the altitude of a right-angled triangle with a hypotenuse of length one unit.

Teacher prompt: For see sine and cosine on a unit hypotenuse, which labelled relationship justifies the conclusion?

Connect tangent and gradient

Connect the tangent of an angle to the altitude and base of nested similar right-angled triangles.

Teacher prompt: For connect tangent and gradient, which quantities must scale or compare by the same factor?

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • A diagram is trusted by appearance instead of stated conditions — Mark givens and name the theorem or transformation used.
  • The trigonometric ratios model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Understand the terms “base”, “altitude”, “hypotenuse”, and “adjacent” and “opposite” sides to an angle, in a right-angled triangle, and identifying these for a given right-angled triangle.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
