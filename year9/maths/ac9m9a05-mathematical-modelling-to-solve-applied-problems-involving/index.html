Modelling Change

A defensible model matches the pattern of change, interprets features in context and states where its assumptions stop being reasonable.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

A defensible model matches the pattern of change, interprets features in context and states where its assumptions stop being reasonable.

Learning intention: A defensible model matches the pattern of change, interprets features in context and states where its assumptions stop being reasonable.

Success criteria

  • Explain model constant change using the approved model.
  • Apply model a quadratic hunting strategy to a new case.
  • Verify a conclusion with a second representation, estimate or test case.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • use mathematical modelling to solve applied problems involving change including financial contexts; formulate problems, choosing to use either linear or quadratic functions; interpret solutions in terms of the situation; evaluate the model and report methods and findings

Prerequisites

  • Relevant Year 8 algebra representations and vocabulary.

May support informally

  • Digital tools may test, visualise and verify after the mathematical structure is explicit.

Do not overteach

  • Do not introduce later-year formalism that is unnecessary for this content description.

Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • A rule is applied without preserving equivalence or its conditions — Return to the linked table, graph or expanded form and verify by substitution.
  • The modelling change model is copied without interpretation — Connect every label in ‘Cooking times that include resting or cooling times, or water leakage from water tanks, using tables and graphs or digital tools and algebraically.’ to its mathematical role.
  • The result is not verified — Use a second representation, boundary case, inverse operation or simulation check.
Support, core and extend

Support: Reduce numerical or representational load while retaining the same decision and explanation.

Core: Connect at least two representations, justify the method and verify independently.

Extend: Test a boundary case, counterexample or model limitation without introducing later-year procedures.
