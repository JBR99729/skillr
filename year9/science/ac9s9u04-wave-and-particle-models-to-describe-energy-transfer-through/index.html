What students learn in AC9S9U04

This unit helps students build a clear, usable understanding of Wave and particle models to describe energy transfer through different.... The goal is not to memorise one answer pattern. Students should be able to explain the idea, recognise it in a new example and apply it in a short practice or worksheet task.

  • Start with the main idea: use wave and particle models to describe energy transfer through different mediums and examine the usefulness of each model for explaining phenomena.
  • Use concrete examples, pictures, oral explanation and short written responses before moving to independent practice.
  • describing the processes underlying convection and conduction of heat in terms of the particle model modelling the transfer of sound energy as waves using slinky springs and relating to the medium through which the sound is transferred
  • Finish with mixed questions so students must choose the correct strategy rather than copy the last example.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Question banks for this unit should draw from the content description and the eligible elaborations below. First Nations-specific notes are kept out of generated question coverage unless they are part of the core concept.

  • Content description: use wave and particle models to describe energy transfer through different mediums and examine the usefulness of each model for explaining phenomena
  • E1: describing the processes underlying convection and conduction of heat in terms of the particle model
  • E2: modelling the transfer of sound energy as waves using slinky springs and relating to the medium through which the sound is transferred
  • E3: examining how the particle model of electricity explains static electricity and electrical current and relating this to voltage, conductors and insulators
  • E4: discussing the wave and particle models of energy transfer, including the concept of photons, and how they are useful for understanding aspects of light and other forms of electromagnetic radiation
  • E5: investigating aspects of heat transfer and conservation in the design of First Nations Australians’ bedding and clothing in the various climatic regions of Australia (not used for generated questions)
  • E6: investigating the impact of material selection on the transfer of sound energy in First Nations Australians’ traditional musical, hunting and communication instruments (not used for generated questions)
  • E7: examining the forms of electromagnetic radiation that are used in different modern communication technologies and identifying any limitations

How to use this unit

Read the topic guide, download the teacher slide for instruction, complete the printable worksheet, then use Practice and Test when the unit's checked 8-question bank is available.

Common mistakes to watch for

  • Describing what is seen without connecting it to a question, pattern or prediction.
  • Using everyday words only and missing simple scientific vocabulary.
  • Changing more than one thing in an investigation, which makes the comparison unfair.
