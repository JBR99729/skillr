AC9S9U05 • Year 9 Science

Conservation of energy in systems

Analyse energy stores, transfers and transformations using the law of conservation of energy and clearly defined system boundaries.

PracticeTestHomeworkTeacher Slides

Core understanding

  • Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it is transferred or transformed.
  • A system boundary determines what is included in an energy analysis.
  • Energy often becomes less useful when dissipated to the surroundings as thermal energy or sound.
  • Total energy before and after an interaction is conserved when the complete system is considered.
  • Efficiency compares useful output with total energy input.

Worked reasoning

Falling ball: gravitational potential energy decreases while kinetic energy increases. Air resistance can transfer some energy to the surroundings as thermal energy and sound. The energy has not disappeared; the distribution of energy has changed.

Common misconceptions

  • Avoid saying energy is “used up” or “lost” without identifying where it was transferred.
  • Conservation of energy does not mean every device is 100% efficient.
  • Always define the system before deciding whether energy has entered or left it.
