What students learn in AC9S9H03

This unit helps students build a clear, usable understanding of Analyse the key factors that contribute to science knowledge and.... The goal is not to memorise one answer pattern. Students should be able to explain the idea, recognise it in a new example and apply it in a short practice or worksheet task.

  • Start with the main idea: analyse the key factors that contribute to science knowledge and practices being adopted more broadly by society.
  • Use concrete examples, pictures, oral explanation and short written responses before moving to independent practice.
  • researching citizen science projects related to public health and examining why people would choose to be involved investigating how the practices adopted by society based on research by Australian Dr Helen Mayo led to a reduction in infant mortality
  • Finish with mixed questions so students must choose the correct strategy rather than copy the last example.

Curriculum coverage and elaborations

Question banks for this unit should draw from the content description and the eligible elaborations below. First Nations-specific notes are kept out of generated question coverage unless they are part of the core concept.

  • Content description: analyse the key factors that contribute to science knowledge and practices being adopted more broadly by society
  • E1: researching citizen science projects related to public health and examining why people would choose to be involved
  • E2: investigating how the practices adopted by society based on research by Australian Dr Helen Mayo led to a reduction in infant mortality
  • E3: examining how assisted reproductive technologies have become widely used since their initial development
  • E4: examining how government initiatives such as Landcare support adoption of effective land restoration practices that improve soil quality and increase carbon sequestration in soils
  • E5: investigating how First Nations Australians’ fire management practices are informing and being adopted in contemporary fire management (not used for generated questions)
  • E6: analysing factors that have led to the adoption of solar panels and battery storage by individuals, industries and communities
  • E7: investigating how an understanding of materials and concern for the environment have led to the adoption of widespread recycling practices

How to use this unit

Read the topic guide, download the teacher slide for instruction, complete the printable worksheet, then use Practice and Test when the unit's checked 8-question bank is available.

Common mistakes to watch for

  • Describing what is seen without connecting it to a question, pattern or prediction.
  • Using everyday words only and missing simple scientific vocabulary.
  • Changing more than one thing in an investigation, which makes the comparison unfair.
