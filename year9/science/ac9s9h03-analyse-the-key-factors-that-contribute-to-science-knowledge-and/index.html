AC9S9H03 • Year 9 Science

Why Society Adopts Scientific Practices

Evidence, trust, access, values, policy and perceived benefit influence whether science-related practices are adopted.

Topic GuideTeacher SlidesPractice SheetPracticeTest
What students learn

We are learning to explain why society adopts scientific practices using models and evidence.

Success criteria

  • Identify and connect evidence strength, benefit and risk, trust.
  • Use a model to explain rather than only describe the concept.
  • Apply the central idea to a curriculum example and justify the connection.
  • Evaluate one model limit, misconception or evidence boundary.
Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • Adoption is influenced by scientific and social factors.
  • Evidence quality is essential but not sufficient.
  • Adoption patterns can vary among groups and over time.

Prerequisites

  • Read a labelled scientific diagram or data display.
  • Distinguish an observation from an explanation.
  • Use evidence to support a short scientific statement.

Do not overteach

  • Do not label communities as anti-science without evidence.
  • Do not treat adoption as proof that a claim is scientifically valid.
Teaching progression
  1. 1. Define the practice
    Use the shared model to foreground evidence strength.
    Students: Annotate or explain the link involving evidence strength.
  2. 2. Evaluate evidence
    Use the shared model to foreground benefit and risk.
    Students: Annotate or explain the link involving benefit and risk.
  3. 3. Map adoption factors
    Use the shared model to foreground trust.
    Students: Annotate or explain the link involving trust.
  4. 4. Explain variation
    Use the shared model to foreground access and cost.
    Students: Annotate or explain the link involving access and cost.
Worked examples

Observe → connect → conclude

A vaccination program may have strong effectiveness and safety evidence.

Use how government initiatives to make the role of access and cost visible and connect the evidence to the unit’s central explanation.

E5: Investigate: values and policy

investigating how First Nations Australians’ fire management practices are informing and being adopted in contemporary fire management

analysing factors that have led to the adoption of solar panels and battery storage by individuals, industries and communities

E7: Investigate: benefit and risk

investigating how an understanding of materials and concern for the environment have led to the adoption of widespread recycling practices

Use how an understanding of materials and concern to make the role of benefit and risk visible and connect the evidence to the unit’s central explanation.

Common misconceptions
  • Strong evidence guarantees rapid adoption.
  • Low adoption proves the science is weak.
