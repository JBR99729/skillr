Year 9 Science • AC9S9H02

How technology enables scientific advances

New technologies extend human senses, improve measurement and reveal evidence that was previously unavailable. Scientific discoveries can also drive new technologies, so the relationship works in both directions.

Teacher SlidesHomeworkPracticeTest

Core understanding

  • Microscopes, telescopes, sensors and imaging systems extend observation.
  • Improved precision can reveal patterns and evidence that change scientific explanations.
  • Digital technologies allow large data sets to be collected, modelled and shared.
  • Technology has limitations and can introduce measurement or interpretation errors.

Worked example: microscopes and cells

  1. Before microscopes, cells could not be directly observed.
  2. Improved lenses allowed scientists to observe cells and tissues.
  3. This evidence supported cell-based explanations of living things.
  4. Later technologies revealed organelles and microorganisms in greater detail.
