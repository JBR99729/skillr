Year 9 Science • AC9S9H01

Validating and refining scientific knowledge

Scientific claims become stronger when evidence is checked, methods are repeated, experts review the work and explanations are refined when better evidence appears.

Teacher SlidesHomeworkPracticeTest

Core understanding

  • Scientific knowledge is based on evidence rather than opinion alone.
  • Replication and reproducibility help test whether results are reliable.
  • Peer review allows experts to question methods, data and conclusions.
  • Scientific explanations can be refined when new evidence becomes available.

Worked example: why peer review matters

  1. A scientist makes a claim.
  2. Other experts examine the method, measurements and interpretation.
  3. They test whether the evidence supports the conclusion.
  4. Weaknesses can lead to revised methods or explanations.
  5. This process increases confidence in accepted scientific knowledge.
