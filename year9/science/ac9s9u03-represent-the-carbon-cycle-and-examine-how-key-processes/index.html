Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • Carbon exists in major stores across four Earth spheres.
  • Photosynthesis, respiration, decomposition, dissolution and combustion transfer carbon.
  • Changing one transfer rate can alter multiple stores.

Prerequisites

  • Read a labelled scientific diagram or data display.
  • Distinguish an observation from an explanation.
  • Use evidence to support a short scientific statement.

Do not overteach

  • Do not imply every carbon pathway operates at the same speed.
  • Do not confuse matter cycling with energy flow.
Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Carbon is created by combustion.
  • The carbon cycle is one simple circle.
Revision notes

  • Carbon exists in major stores across four Earth spheres.
