AC9S9U04 • Year 9 Science

Wave and particle models of energy transfer

Use wave and particle models to explain how energy moves through materials and systems, and recognise the strengths and limits of each model.

PracticeTestHomeworkTeacher Slides

Core understanding

  • Waves transfer energy without transporting matter overall.
  • Sound is a mechanical wave and requires particles in a medium.
  • Light can transfer energy through a vacuum.
  • Thermal conduction can be modelled as kinetic-energy transfer through particle interactions.
  • Scientific models simplify systems and therefore have limitations.

Worked reasoning

Why can sound not travel through space? Sound depends on vibrating particles passing energy to neighbouring particles. In a vacuum there is no continuous particle medium, so the mechanical disturbance cannot propagate in the usual way. Light is different because electromagnetic waves do not require a material medium.

Common misconceptions

  • Waves do not necessarily carry matter with them.
  • Sound and light do not require the same transmission conditions.
  • A model is not a literal picture of reality; it is a tool for explaining observations.
