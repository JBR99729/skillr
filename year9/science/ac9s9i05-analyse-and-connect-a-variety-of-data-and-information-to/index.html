Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • Comparable data can reveal converging patterns or conflicts.
  • Anomalies and uncertainty affect interpretation.
  • Explanations connect data patterns to scientific mechanisms or models.

Prerequisites

  • Read a labelled scientific diagram or data display.
  • Distinguish an observation from an explanation.
  • Use evidence to support a short scientific statement.

Do not overteach

  • Do not combine incompatible data without transformation or caveat.
  • Do not infer cause from pattern alone without design or mechanism.
Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Different sources can be averaged immediately.
  • An anomaly invalidates the whole data set.
Revision notes
  • Analysis explains patterns, relationships and anomalies by comparing data sources and scientific models.
