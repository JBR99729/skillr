Concept boundary and prerequisites

Must teach

  • Purpose and audience guide content and form.
  • Scientific representations and conventions remain accurate.
  • Editing checks evidence, uncertainty, accessibility and ethical attribution.

Prerequisites

  • Read a labelled scientific diagram or data display.
  • Distinguish an observation from an explanation.
  • Use evidence to support a short scientific statement.

Do not overteach

  • Do not simplify by removing essential uncertainty or units.
  • Do not use visual drama that exaggerates effects.
Common misconceptions and quick fixes
  • Audience adaptation permits changing the conclusion.
  • More technical vocabulary is more scientific.
Revision notes
  • Content, language, structure and digital features are selected to communicate evidence accurately to an identified audience.
  • Purpose and audience guide content and form.
  • Scientific representations and conventions remain accurate.
