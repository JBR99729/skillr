# Practice: question | correct answer | distractor | distractor | explanation
The school yard is bright at lunchtime and dark after sunset. What has changed?|day to night|winter to summer|rain to wind|Day and night change within one day. Seasons change over much longer periods.
Rain starts during lunch. Which change helps keep the outdoor reading books dry?|move them under cover|spread them on the grass|leave their covers open|Cover protects the books from the rain.
