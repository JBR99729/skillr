# Year 7 Mathematics topic modules v2: curriculum and preservation map

Scope: all 30 active Australian Curriculum v9 Year 7 Mathematics content descriptions. This is a curriculum-review companion to the v2 source and validator, not a substitute for schema validation or rendered-page inspection.

## Preservation contract

For every code, retain and repurpose the existing `assets/year7-maths-data-*.js` fields `title`, `subtitle`, `desc`, `core`, `learn`, `modelTitle`, `modelVisual`, `modelNote`, `applyTitle`, `applyVisual`, `applyNote`, `terms`, `mistakes`, `quick`, `mastery`, `activities`, `correctExample`, `fillTemplate`, `enrichment1` and `enrichment2`. Preserve the legacy topic page's curriculum identity, elaborations, international mapping, official references and navigation as the reference layer. Preserve each one-page teacher PDF as an optional legacy resource. Preserve the worksheet route, print workflow and SkillrHub branding while replacing assessment-bank reuse with one independent nine-question 3/4/2 source presented across Topic Practice 1 and Topic Practice 2. Preserve every question exactly once across the 5/4 split, and keep each sheet's answer key limited to its own questions.

The v2 mapping is:

- `core`, `learn`, `modelNote` and `applyNote` → deep-dive prose and reasoning.
- `terms` → visible vocabulary definitions.
- `mistakes` → misconception corrections, slide responses and remediation.
- `model*`, `apply*` and `correctExample` → two explicit worked examples and accessible visuals.
- `mastery` → student-friendly success criteria.
- `quick` → the 60-second check, with a concealed expected response.
- `activities`, `enrichment1` and `enrichment2` → teacher notes, transfer tasks and Tier 3 questions.
