Read 'you'. Cover it, write it from memory, and read your writing back.|Check accurate reading and y-o-u in order. This is supported retrieval after reading, not a timed fluency measure.
Read 'are' aloud. Hide the model and write the word.|Check accurate reading in the child's accent and a-r-e in order. A silent letter in an accent still belongs in the spelling.
Read 'said'. Cover it and write it, then check the letters in the middle.|Check reading with the usual short e vowel and spelling s-a-i-d. Do not teach ai as always representing long a.
Read 'have' aloud. Cover it, write it and compare with the model.|Check accurate reading with its usual short a and spelling h-a-v-e. Not every final-e word has a long vowel.
Read 'they'. Cover the word and write it on paper.|Check accurate reading and t-h-e-y in order. The th sound is voiced in this word; spelling should retain both letters.
Read 'come'. Cover it and write it, then read your word back.|Check accurate reading and c-o-m-e. Its vowel does not follow the common long-o final-e example.

Read 'I have some paper.' Read 'some' again, then cover and write it.|Check accurate reading and s-o-m-e. Its usual vowel is not the long o suggested by some other final-e words.
