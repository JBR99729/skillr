SKILLRHUB

Year 3 Time: Read It, Measure It, Explain It

Sample from the classroom teaching slides for AC9M3M03 and AC9M3M04.

LESSON FLOW

Three short lessons

Lesson 1: time units and duration. Lesson 2: analogue clocks to the minute. Lesson 3: explain and correct time mistakes.

WARM-UP

What can happen in 1 second, 1 minute and 1 hour?

Students sort events by sensible time unit, then justify their choice.

WORKED EXAMPLE

A bus leaves at 3:25 and arrives at 3:50. How long is the trip?

Count on from 3:25 to 3:50. 25 minutes have passed.

CLOCK READING

Read the minute hand first

The long hand shows minutes. The short hand shows the hour it is moving towards.

COMMON MISTAKE

If the short hand is between 4 and 5, the hour is still 4 until the minute hand reaches 12.

Students explain why 4:45 is not 5:45.

GUIDED PRACTICE

Explain your answer

What time does this clock show? How do you know?
