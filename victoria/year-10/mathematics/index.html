Victorian Curriculum Level 10 Mathematics

Use this mapping to find one complete SkillrHub lesson for each Australian Curriculum Year 10 topic while seeing how that learning relates to Victorian Curriculum Mathematics Version 2.0. Victoria does not always organise content one-to-one with ACARA, and optional Level 10A content is identified where it genuinely provides the closest match.

How the Victorian mapping works

SkillrHub keeps the Australian Curriculum lesson as the canonical learning page. This Victoria hub uses Level terminology, current VCAA codes and the structure of Version 2.0 to point teachers, students and families to those lessons without duplicating them.

Some Australian descriptors split concepts that Victoria combines, and some digital/spatial extension content aligns most closely with optional Level 10A. Those differences are stated explicitly.
