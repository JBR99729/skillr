Poems, chants, rhymes and songs lesson plan

A ready-to-use lesson sequence for listening to, discussing and creating sound patterns using rhyme and alliteration.

Learning intention

Students listen to and discuss poems, chants, rhymes and songs, then imitate and invent simple sound patterns.

Success criteria

  • I can hear words that rhyme.
  • I can notice repeated beginning sounds.
  • I can join in with a chant or rhyme.
  • I can invent a short rhyming or alliterative line.

20–30 minute lesson sequence

  1. Warm-up: read or perform a short rhyme and ask students what sounds repeat.
  2. Explicit teaching: model rhyme and alliteration with two simple examples.
  3. Guided practice: students identify matching end sounds and repeated first sounds.
  4. Create: pairs invent one rhyming line or short alliterative phrase.
  5. Exit check: each student explains which sound pattern they used.

Assessment evidence

Look for accurate identification of rhyme or alliteration and a student-created example that follows the same sound pattern.
