What makes a science worksheet useful?

A useful science worksheet focuses on a concept or investigation skill and asks students to explain, compare, classify, predict or interpret evidence. It should connect to a teaching explanation when the learner gets stuck.

Foundation to Year 2 science

Younger students need resources about observing, describing, sorting, asking questions and noticing changes in everyday materials, living things, weather and movement.

Years 3 to 6 science

Primary science expands into life cycles, materials, forces, Earth and space, heat, light, sound and structured inquiry. Topic guides help students understand the concept before completing practice.

Years 7 to 10 science

Secondary science needs clearer links between ideas, evidence and models. SkillrHub's Science pathways support biological, chemical, physical, Earth and space science, plus inquiry skills.
