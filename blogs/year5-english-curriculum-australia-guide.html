Year 5 English Curriculum Australia: Reading, Writing and Language

Year 5 English develops stronger analysis and more purposeful composition. Students examine ideas and language across genres, expand vocabulary and produce persuasive, informative and imaginative texts with clearer structure.

Support interpretations with evidence

Reading questions should increasingly require students to explain how they know. Ask them to quote or point to a detail, compare two language choices or explain how a text feature influences the reader.

Plan before drafting

Year 5 writers benefit from a clear purpose, audience and structure before they begin. A short plan can prevent repetitive paragraphs and make editing more useful. After drafting, revise for ideas and organisation before correcting surface errors.
