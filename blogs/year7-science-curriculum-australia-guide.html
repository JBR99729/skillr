Year 7 Science Curriculum Australia: What Students Learn and How to Practise

Year 7 Science introduces more formal scientific models, terminology and investigation. Classification and ecosystems, mixtures and substances, Earth systems, forces and motion are explored alongside evidence evaluation and experimental design.

Learn concepts through models and evidence

Students should explain what a model represents and use observations or data to support claims. Definitions are useful, but they should be applied to classify examples, interpret results or predict what will happen in a new situation.

Use the exact curriculum unit

The Year 7 Science curriculum units provide a clear topic map. Each unit connects the Topic Guide, Teacher Slides, Worksheet, Practice and Test so students can revisit the same concept after feedback.

Build investigation discipline

Students should identify variables, choose suitable measurements, record units, display data clearly and consider sources of uncertainty. A conclusion should answer the question and refer to evidence rather than simply restating the prediction.
