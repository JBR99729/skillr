Choose the learning need before choosing the app

A child who understands a classroom explanation but needs more practice has a different need from a child who needs someone to teach the idea again. Before adding a subscription or tuition session, choose one current difficulty: a fraction calculation, a short reading task or a Science concept. Ask the child to explain what they already understand. That gives you a better starting point than selecting a programme only because it has a large question bank.

Use the school's learning goals as the reference. Singapore's MOE Primary Mathematics syllabus includes number, fractions and mathematical problem-solving, but the sequence and expected difficulty can differ from another country's course. Choose by the actual topic, wording and readiness. Primary 3 is not automatically the same as Australian Year 3.

“Asian-style teaching”: look for a clear learning sequence

There is no single teaching style used across Asia. If “Asian-style teaching” or “Singapore-style teaching” means structured steps, visual models and a secure understanding of the basics to you, inspect those features directly. SkillrHub's available guides include worked examples and conceptual or visual support where appropriate. That is not a claim to reproduce a full Singapore Maths method or to have complete MOE alignment.

A concrete-to-calculation example: three quarters of 20

  1. Start with objects: put 20 counters into four equal groups. There are five counters in each group.
  2. Make the structure visible: draw four equal boxes and write 5 in each. Shade three boxes. The shaded amount is 5 + 5 + 5 = 15.
  3. Write the calculation: one quarter of 20 is 20 ÷ 4 = 5, so three quarters is 5 × 3 = 15.
  4. Check understanding: ask why dividing by four comes before taking three groups. Then try one half of 18: 18 ÷ 2 = 9.

This is an original supplementary example, not an MOE level mapping or an exam-style claim. For another explanation, open the fractions, decimals and percentages guide. Its Australian year label describes the source curriculum, not a placement recommendation for a Singapore child.

When tuition may be the better fit

If your child remains confused after an explanation and a supported attempt, ask the teacher what the difficulty involves. A tutor may provide live dialogue, tailored instruction or specialist help that an independent-practice app cannot.
