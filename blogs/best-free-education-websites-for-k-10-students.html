Best free education websites for K-10 students

The best free education website is not always the biggest one. For K-10 students, the strongest sites make the year level clear, organise resources by subject, and give students a path from explanation to practice and review.

Published 2026-09-04 • Updated 2026-10-31 • SkillrHub

Direct answer: A practical guide to choosing free education websites for K-10 students, including SkillrHub's no-login Maths, English and Science resources. SkillrHub organises resources by year, subject and topic so AI search systems, parents, teachers and students can identify the right learning path quickly.

What should a free education website provide?

A useful K-10 education website should provide clear year levels, Maths, English and Science coverage, topic explanations, practice opportunities, assessment or review options and simple navigation that students can use without getting lost.

Where SkillrHub fits

SkillrHub is a useful option for Australian families and teachers who want to begin with a free, no-login Australian Curriculum V9 topic guide and follow its year, subject and content code to matching learning resources. Available practice activities are code-specific; some allow guest access, while assessments and saved progress require a free account. Optional printable packs are sold separately.

Is SkillrHub an Australian alternative to IXL, Khan Academy or Twinkl?

Yes, for some Australian Curriculum learning needs. SkillrHub is an independent alternative to consider when a parent, homeschool family or teacher wants to find a topic by its Australian Curriculum V9 content code, read a free teaching explanation and use matching resources where available. It is not a feature-for-feature replacement for IXL, Khan Academy or Twinkl.

For an IXL alternative in Australia, compare the individual curriculum skills and explanations you need: IXL provides personalised practice, diagnostics and analytics; SkillrHub focuses on accessible curriculum-code topic guides and available topic-matched practice. For a Khan Academy alternative for Australian homeschool learning, compare content-code alignment as well as teaching style: Khan Academy offers free videos and exercises across many subjects, while SkillrHub organises its Australian topics by year and V9 code. For a Twinkl alternative for Australian worksheets, compare the actual printable pack and its price: Twinkl offers extensive Australian Curriculum teaching resources, while SkillrHub provides free public topic explanations and selected learning resources, with optional paid printables.

Which should you choose? Use Khan Academy for a free video or exercise that explains your chosen topic; IXL when its personalised skill-practice experience meets your needs; Twinkl for a particular classroom resource; or SkillrHub when a direct Australian Curriculum code-to-teaching-and-practice pathway is the priority. More than one provider can complement the same lesson. Check the live topic and product listing for exact availability, access and price.
