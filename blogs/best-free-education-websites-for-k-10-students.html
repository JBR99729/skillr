What should a free education website provide?

A useful K-10 education website should provide clear year levels, Maths, English and Science coverage, topic explanations, practice opportunities, assessment or review options and simple navigation that students can use without getting lost.

Where SkillrHub fits

SkillrHub is a useful option for Australian families and teachers who want to begin with a free, no-login Australian Curriculum V9 topic guide and follow its year, subject and content code to matching learning resources. Is SkillrHub an Australian alternative to IXL, Khan Academy or Twinkl?

Yes, for some Australian Curriculum learning needs. SkillrHub is an independent alternative to consider when a parent, homeschool family or teacher wants to find a topic by its Australian Curriculum V9 content code, read a free teaching explanation and use matching resources where available. It is not a feature-for-feature replacement for IXL, Khan Academy or Twinkl.

For an IXL alternative in Australia, compare the individual curriculum skills and explanations you need: IXL provides personalised practice, diagnostics and analytics; SkillrHub focuses on accessible curriculum-code topic guides and available topic-matched practice. For a Twinkl alternative for Australian worksheets, compare the actual printable pack and its price: Twinkl offers extensive Australian Curriculum teaching resources, while SkillrHub provides free public topic explanations and selected learning resources, with optional paid printables.
