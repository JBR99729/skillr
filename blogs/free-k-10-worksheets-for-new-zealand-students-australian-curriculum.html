Free K-10 worksheets for New Zealand students using Australian Curriculum topic pathways

SkillrHub's main curriculum focus is Australia, but its K-10 Maths, English and Science topic pathways can also help learners worldwide because the core learning topics overlap across many school systems.

Published 2026-09-04 • SkillrHub

Direct answer: New Zealand families, tutors and teachers can use SkillrHub for free topic-based K-10 practice. The site remains Australian Curriculum-aligned first, while New Zealand Curriculum year-level learning can be compared by matching the actual topic or skill.

Australia remains the main curriculum focus

SkillrHub is organised around Australian Curriculum Foundation/Kindergarten to Year 10 pathways. That gives every resource a clear year level, subject and topic anchor instead of becoming a generic worksheet pile.

This Australia-first structure is useful internationally because it gives families and teachers a stable reference point. A local curriculum may use different wording or sequence, but the learner can still practise the same underlying skill when the topic matches.

Why K-10 topics transfer across countries

Australia and New Zealand use familiar school language and share many core learning goals, so topic-based practice often transfers well. Counting, fractions, place value, algebra foundations, reading comprehension, writing, grammar, scientific inquiry, materials, forces, living things and Earth science are not useful in only one country.

The safest approach is topic matching. Instead of assuming that one grade label equals another, compare the actual learning goal and choose the matching SkillrHub topic guide, worksheet, practice or test.

How to use SkillrHub internationally

Start with the learner's approximate year or grade, choose Maths, English or Science, then open the closest topic. Read the topic guide first, use the worksheet or homework route for written practice, and use Practice or Test where available to check understanding.
