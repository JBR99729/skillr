Year 1 Science Curriculum Australia: What Students Learn and How to Practise

Year 1 Science helps children move from simply noticing the world to describing patterns, features, materials and changes more carefully. They begin to use observations as evidence and communicate what they discover.

What students work on

Learning can involve observable features of living things and materials, everyday changes, patterns in the environment and simple investigation skills. The exact sequence is easiest to see in the Year 1 Science curriculum units.

Science needs observation, not just facts

A useful science question asks students to look, compare, predict or explain. Encourage children to say what they observed before telling them the scientific answer. When possible, connect the topic to a safe real-world example: an object, a plant, a material, a weather observation or a simple classroom demonstration.

Use worksheets as records of thinking

A science worksheet is strongest when students draw, classify, label, compare or explain evidence. It should support the investigation or concept rather than replace it. After the worksheet, online practice can check whether the idea transfers to a different example.
