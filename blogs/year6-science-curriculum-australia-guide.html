Year 6 Science Curriculum Australia: What Students Learn and How to Practise

Year 6 Science prepares students for more formal secondary investigation and explanation. Organisms and environments, changes in matter, Earth and space, energy and electricity are explored alongside planning, recording and evaluating evidence.

Use models carefully

Models help students explain systems that cannot always be observed directly. Ask what a model represents, what it leaves out and how it supports an explanation. This builds scientific literacy rather than simple diagram memorisation.

Plan investigations with purpose

Students should identify what they are testing, choose relevant measurements, control important variables where appropriate and record results in a form that can be compared. They should use evidence—not expectation—to support conclusions.

Find the exact curriculum unit

The Year 6 Science curriculum units connect each concept with Topic Guide, Teacher Slides, Worksheet, Practice and Test. This makes targeted revision easier before the move into secondary science.

Practise data interpretation

Use short tables, graphs, diagrams and experimental descriptions. Ask what conclusion is supported, what evidence is missing and whether another explanation is possible. These questions strengthen the habits students will need in later practical work.
