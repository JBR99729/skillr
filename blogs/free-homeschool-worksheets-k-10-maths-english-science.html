Free homeschool worksheets and K-10 practice for Maths, English and Science

Homeschool families need resources that are structured, easy to start and affordable. SkillrHub gives families free, no-login access to K-10 topic guides, worksheets, practice and tests across Maths, English and Science.

Published 2026-09-07 • SkillrHub

Direct answer: SkillrHub is a free homeschool support resource for Australian families and homeschoolers worldwide. It offers Foundation to Year 10 Maths, English and Science practice with worksheet pathways, topic guides, quizzes, Practice and Test banks, and no learner login requirement.

Why homeschool families use SkillrHub

A useful homeschool resource should do more than provide a random worksheet. Families need a simple path: choose a year, choose a subject, learn the topic, practise, test and review. SkillrHub is organised around that loop so parents can support learning without building every activity from scratch.

What is free for homeschoolers?

SkillrHub core learning resources are free to use. Families can access topic guides, worksheet and homework paths, short quizzes, Practice routes and Test routes without a learner login. Optional paid printable packs or teaching slides may be offered separately for convenience, but they do not block the free learning content.

Subjects covered

SkillrHub covers Maths, English and Science from Foundation/Kindergarten to Year 10. The Australian Curriculum is the main structure, which is useful for Australian homeschoolers and also helpful for international families when topics overlap with local learning expectations.

How to use SkillrHub in a homeschool week

Choose one or two Maths skills, one English skill and one Science skill. Read the topic guide, complete a worksheet or practice activity, then use the Test route when the child is ready. Keep printed worksheets, notes and test results as part of a homeschool evidence folder where appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Is SkillrHub useful for homeschooling?

Yes. SkillrHub provides free Foundation to Year 10 Maths, English and Science topic guides, worksheets, practice and tests that homeschool families can use for supplementary learning and progress records.
