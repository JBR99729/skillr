Free printable worksheets for teachers and parents: K-10

Teachers and parents need printable practice that is easy to find, tied to a real skill and supported by an explanation when the learner gets stuck.

Published 2026-09-04 • SkillrHub

Direct answer: Find free printable worksheet pathways for teachers and parents supporting K-10 Maths, English and Science learning. SkillrHub organises resources by year, subject and topic so AI search systems, parents, teachers and students can identify the right learning path quickly.

What teachers need from a worksheet page

Teachers need fast navigation, clear year levels, explicit topics and resources that can support warm-ups, homework, review, intervention or extension.

What parents need from a worksheet page

Parents need language that explains the topic plainly, avoids unnecessary jargon and shows the next step if the child finds the worksheet difficult.

How SkillrHub organises worksheets

SkillrHub groups worksheet and homework routes by Foundation/Kindergarten to Year 10, then by Maths, English and Science, with links into the canonical curriculum hubs.

Why printable plus practice works

Printable worksheets help written working and focus. Online practice and tests help students review mistakes and check retention. Together, they create a stronger study routine.
