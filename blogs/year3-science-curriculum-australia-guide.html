Year 3 Science Curriculum Australia: What Students Learn and How to Practise

Year 3 Science begins asking students to connect observations with broader explanations. They study living and non-living systems, materials, heat, Earth processes and forces while improving the way they plan, record and communicate investigations.

Move from description to explanation

Students should increasingly answer not only “what happened?” but “why might it have happened?” Encourage comparisons, predictions and evidence. A labelled diagram, data table or short explanation can show much more understanding than a list of memorised definitions.

Use the exact curriculum unit

The Year 3 Science curriculum units organise the subject into precise learning goals. SkillrHub links each one to teaching and practice resources so a learner can review the concept before attempting the worksheet or online questions.

Keep investigations connected to the concept

A practical activity is valuable when students know what they are observing and what evidence matters. Before an activity, ask for a prediction. During it, record observations. Afterwards, compare the result with the prediction and explain what the evidence suggests.
