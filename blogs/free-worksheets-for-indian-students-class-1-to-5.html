Free worksheets for Indian students: Class 1 to Class 5

Find a useful SkillrHub topic or worksheet by the skill your child needs to practise. SkillrHub follows the Australian Curriculum; these resources can support Indian learners where the topic and difficulty match their schoolwork.

Choose by skill, then check the level

Indian Class numbers and Australian Year levels are not a syllabus equivalence. The class sections below are browsing suggestions. Check the linked explanation against your child’s textbook, teacher’s instructions and current understanding before using a worksheet.

  1. Choose one skill from the sections below.
  2. Read the topic explanation and work through an example together.
  3. Open a published worksheet from the topic page, or use one of the direct worksheet links below.
  4. Check the responses and revisit the explanation for anything unclear.

Class 1: Counting, simple sentences and living things

Start with the skill your child is practising at school. Use objects for counting, talk through a simple sentence, or observe the needs of a familiar plant or animal before choosing a written task.

  • Numbers to 120 Australian Curriculum AC9M1N01

Class 3: Multiplication, comprehension and heat
