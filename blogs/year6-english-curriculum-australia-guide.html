Year 6 English Curriculum Australia: Reading, Writing and Language

Year 6 English expects students to read more critically, compare perspectives and create sustained texts with greater control. Vocabulary, figurative language, evidence, cohesion and editing become increasingly important.

Read for viewpoint and effect

Students should identify not only what a text says but how language and structure shape the reader’s response. Ask them to compare perspectives and support interpretations with details from the text.

Write sustained, connected responses

Planning becomes increasingly valuable as texts grow longer. Students should organise paragraphs around a clear purpose, select evidence or detail and revise for cohesion. Editing should improve meaning and style as well as spelling and punctuation.

Free-first learning

Try the free resources first

Before committing to another monthly or yearly learning subscription, use the free curriculum-linked resources available for the student’s current topic. Start with the Topic Guide, use Practice, review mistakes and then take the separate Test.

A larger paid question bank or learning program can be useful, but paying for more volume does not guarantee that a student will use it consistently. If the learner is already working through the free resources and still needs extra tutoring, marking, adaptive practice, reporting or more question volume, compare paid options for that specific need.
