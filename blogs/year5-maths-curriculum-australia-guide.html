Year 5 Maths Curriculum Australia: What Students Learn and How to Practise

Year 5 Maths broadens students’ number sense and expects more flexible problem solving. Whole-number operations, factors and multiples, fractions and decimals, measurement, geometry, probability and data are applied in increasingly unfamiliar situations.

Look for relationships, not isolated rules

Students benefit from seeing how multiplication connects to factors, how fractions connect to decimals and how measurement formulas connect to diagrams. Ask them to represent the same problem in more than one way and explain why the methods agree.

Target the curriculum unit

The Year 5 Maths curriculum units give a precise starting point. SkillrHub links each unit to the topic explanation, teaching presentation, printable worksheet, online Practice and Test, making it easier to diagnose gaps without mixing unrelated content.

Include estimation and checking

Before calculating, students should predict the approximate size of an answer. After calculating, they should decide whether the result is reasonable. This habit catches many common errors and supports later work with decimals, percentages and algebra.

Move from routine to transfer

Once standard questions are secure, vary the representation, context or information given. A student who understands a concept should be able to recognise it when the question looks different from the worked example.
