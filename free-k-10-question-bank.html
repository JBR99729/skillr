20 free questions each day for guests · No email or credit card to start · Free accounts unlock unlimited available practice and saved progress

What is free? Published digital practice, topic teaching notes and available skill assessments. Paid printable packs are optional.

Who is it for? Australian primary students, homeschool families, parents, teachers and tutors looking for practice aligned to a specific curriculum skill.

Current coverage: Published Maths banks span Foundation–Year 8, with selected English banks from Foundation–Year 5 and Science banks from Foundation–Year 4. The code list shows exact availability; the F–10 topic-guide directory is broader than the app bank.

How free practice supports learning

  1. Read the matching free topic guide or choose a skill you have learned.
  2. Answer one practice question at a time using the supported controls.
  3. Check the answer feedback, revisit the concept and continue practising.
  4. Use the separate assessment where available. Sign in with a free account and select the learner before starting.

Saved dashboards help families review their recorded progress. Community answer totals include genuine guest activity; account progress stays attached to the selected learner.

Research-informed, originally authored questions

Our question-building workflow maps the Australian Curriculum skill, inspects relevant teaching and task research, then authors original questions with purposeful variation and suitable conceptual scaffolds. Checked answers, feedback and teacher/software review gates support quality. Read our editorial standards or report an issue.

Questions and review are part of a learning tool, with no guaranteed school result or claim of independently proven effectiveness. Families can try a bank and tell us what helps or feels unclear.

Questions about free learning

Is SkillrHub practice free?

Yes. Guests can answer 20 free practice questions each day in guest-enabled banks without email or a credit card. A free email-confirmed account provides unlimited available practice and saved progress. Paid printables are optional.

Which years and subjects can I practise?

Published Maths banks span Foundation–Year 8, with selected English banks from Foundation–Year 5 and Science banks from Foundation–Year 4. The code list shows exact availability; the F–10 topic-guide directory is broader than the app bank.

How are the questions developed?

Questions are originally authored using curriculum mapping and inspected teaching/task research. The workflow includes purposeful variation, conceptual scaffolds, checked answers and feedback, and teacher/software review gates. It does not guarantee school marks.

How do assessments work?

Available Assessment links open the separate Test bank for the same curriculum code in the app. A free account, learner selection where applicable and acceptance of the test rules are required.

Do guest answers contribute to the community counter?

Yes. Submitted genuine guest and signed-in answers contribute, whether correct or incorrect. QA/test sessions are excluded. The counter measures answer events, not unique questions or curriculum mastery.
