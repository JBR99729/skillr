Free K-10 Question Bank: 50,000+ Maths, English and Science Questions

SkillrHub includes 50,000+ questions across Foundation/Kindergarten to Year 10, with worksheet, quiz, practice and test pathways aligned to Australian Curriculum Maths, English and Science topics.

Direct answer: SkillrHub is a free Australia-first K-10 learning resource with 50,000+ questions, no learner login requirement for core resources, and connected topic guide, worksheet, quiz, practice and test pathways.

What the question bank covers

The SkillrHub question bank supports Foundation/Kindergarten to Year 10 learning across Maths, English and Science. Questions are organised by year level, subject and Australian Curriculum topic so students, parents, teachers and tutors can find the right resource without searching through unrelated worksheets.

How the pathways work

SkillrHub is built around a connected learning loop. Learners can read the topic guide, use a worksheet or homework sheet, complete a quiz or practice activity, then take a separate test where available. This structure helps the question bank work as a learning system rather than a loose pile of printable pages.

Why this matters for free worksheet searches

Many parents and teachers search for free printable worksheets, but the most useful resource is usually a complete pathway: explanation, worksheet, practice, test and review. SkillrHub's 50,000+ questions make those pathways broad enough to support regular homework, revision, classroom practice and tutoring.

Australia-first, useful worldwide by topic

Australia is SkillrHub's main curriculum focus. International learners can still use the site when Maths, English or Science topics overlap with local curriculum expectations. The safest approach is to match the actual topic or skill rather than relying only on country-specific grade labels.
